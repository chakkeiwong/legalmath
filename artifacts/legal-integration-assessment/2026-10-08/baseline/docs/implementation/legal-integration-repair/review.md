## Traced execution and disposition

The retained baseline needed 420 scoped pairs. The configured four-action stage
could not cover the required 70 calls even without errors. Combined generation
returned zero candidates but proceeded to three invalid question assignments.
These are execution defects, not failures of the legal theory. Both original
executions remain failed and all their evidence is preserved.

Broad fidelity previously omitted the author's statement that TRUE represented
a duty trigger. The repaired request exposes statement, subject, distinction,
assumptions and questions separately from the executable representation. The
validator still rejects prose as an executable quote. This repairs information
loss; it does not prove the author's source interpretation or force the model
to interpret it correctly. Exact question assignments remain provisional and
the broad matrix remains a coverage check.

Scoped completion now requires the full expected Cartesian matrix, two validated
perspectives and assessment of all four dimensions. UNASSESSED agreement cannot
skip a remaining review round or promote completion. Supported uncertainty and
explicit not-applicable judgments remain possible; legal resolution is not
required to report that a dimension was assessed.

Optional tools now have explicit product checkpoints after validated generation
and criticism. Product requests are augmented before the existing compact and
source/executable-reference transports. The journal binds the full source packet,
backend identity and checked inputs; each request's context is frozen for exact
reuse. A checkpoint is only a check of the retained proposal and computation.
No proposed preference is accepted by the adapter.

The external graph uses eligible arguments and effective defeats from the
product's typed criticism evaluation. PyArg and Carneades agree on grounded and
preferred semantics; product grounded extensions are compared directly. Review
found that the old clingo sidecar checked raw attacks. A final revised control
explicitly feeds the normalized graph to clingo and compares stable extensions
with the product. Preliminary R2 evidence is retained, and final R2 uses a new
directory and identity. This was a correction within I5, not a new program phase.

Successful exact model requests reuse the existing evidence cache. Unknown
outcomes from a process interruption cannot silently dispatch again. The narrow
existing transient-error retry and circuit breaker remain. New tool journals
preserve failures and require reviewed recovery. The old ProductProvider trial
wrapper remains historical; new execution uses the product interface directly.

**PASS for the bounded diagnostic.** The full live product remains under-budgeted.
The local checks passed 157 regression cases plus two focused accounting/matrix
tests added during review. The complete controlled product used ten scripted
provider actions and then resumed with zero new requests. Installed tools checked
all 12 retained candidates: six supported Booleans and six explicitly unformalized
records. These 12 are generated product candidates, not the separate 35 original
readings. All original denominators are retained.

The strongest alternative explanation for a useful live answer is that the model
already knew the distinction; the small diagnostic cannot attribute improvement
to tools. Conversely, a timeout says nothing about legal usefulness. The weakest
evidence remains the legal interpretation itself: unlabelled development sources
and proposed judgments. Source-supported legal review, target-specific labelled
evaluation and real factual records are still required.
