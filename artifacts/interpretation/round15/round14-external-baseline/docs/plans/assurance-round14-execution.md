# Round 14: decisions across surviving interpretations

## Purpose and frozen comparator

The question is whether the system can give useful, traceable decisions without
silently choosing one English interpretation. The comparator is round 13:
741 passing engineering tests, 883 completed required pair outcomes, eight
unencoded readings, 272 proposed exclusions, and 339 remaining PDF differences.
These are development observations, not a measured legal error rate. Round 13's
final delivery manifest and post-execution review must be verified before work.

The mechanism under test has three parts: preserve all retained alternatives;
separate different legal questions before comparison; and report a decision only
when every retained alternative answering that question has the same known
result on the supplied facts. An unencoded alternative blocks that conclusion.
Agreement is conditional on the recorded hypothesis universe and source version;
it is never a statement that all possible readings have been discovered.

## Evidence contract and research intent

| Role | Predeclared criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Primary engineering acceptance | Exact inventory conservation; executable alternative-sensitive decisions; fresh generated Java agrees with Python and an independently implemented evaluator on declared cases; at least one positive and one negative known result on a preselected new circular, plus correct abstention for missing facts. |
| Source-fidelity veto | Changed actor, unit, polarity, qualifier, scope, deadline, unsupported extra fact, or an adverse source assessment prevents promotion of the affected reading. |
| Engineering veto and repair trigger | Missing IDs, malformed proposals, stale hashes, invalid fact alignment, lost parent qualifications, unsound PDF normalization, or Java disagreement require repair and rerun of affected checks. |
| Continuation veto | Corrupt retained evidence, invalid harness, unavailable necessary authority after the bounded search, or the declared resource ceiling stops dependent actions. Other phases still proceed. |
| Explanatory diagnostics | Number of proposals, model labels, disagreement counts, syntax recovery, test counts, processing time and accepted/abstained case counts. None estimates a legal error probability. |
| Prohibited inference | General English correctness, complete case-law semantics, statistical independence of fresh contexts, reduced legal-review cost, production approval or bank integration. |

Success of a scoped decision does not close every provision of its circular.
Full-source coverage and unimplemented questions remain separately visible.
An all-abstention result fails the useful-decision demonstration but may validate
the refusal mechanism. A failed candidate triggers its planned repair; it does
not invalidate the whole research direction.

## Skeptical review before implementation

Reviewed against the successor specification and actual round-13 artifacts.
Three material flaws in a naive continuation were repaired in this plan:
(1) null formalizations are unresolved meaning, not syntax bugs; (2) fewer
comparisons can conceal missed qualifications; every exclusion is challenged;
(3) same-input Java/Python agreement cannot validate the English, so source
reference cases are frozen separately and their authorship stays explicit.
The ensemble must not discard an inconvenient unencoded branch or mix
applicability with satisfaction. It must produce no probability of correctness
from votes. A new source's absence does not become an assumed predicate.

Audit verdict: executable for the scoped engineering and development-evidence
claims above. It cannot close empirical legal accuracy or broad jurisprudential
coverage without additional evidence. Acceptance requires useful generated
decisions as well as correct refusals. Documentation-only edits are separately
bound so they do not invalidate already tested code and consume live calls.
