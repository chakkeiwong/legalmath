6.28 From complementary checks to a working
investigation
Return to the offer containing a fee waiver and a separate voucher. A programme can
implement its conditions exactly and still miss the voucher if the input record describes
only whether the offer contains a discount. In that situation, adding another compiler
leaves the missing distinction untouched. The necessary repair is to reconsider what
the facts describe. The proposed investigation therefore treats the factual model as a
revisable interpretation of the source. One proposal may describe the whole offer; an-
other may distinguish each benefit, its recipient and its connection to a product. Both
proposals retain their source passages and classification assumptions. They cannot be
treated as equivalent merely because both use a variable named discount.
This change gives breadth-first investigation a substantive purpose. Before ranking
readings of the exception, separate readers propose the actors, components and dates
needed to express it. A challenger then asks which relevant distinction all those pro-
posals might have missed. Subsequent search investigates cases that separate the pro-
posals. For the combined offer, assessing the voucher separately is such a case under
the interpretation developed earlier in this book. Whether a particular benefit is legally
a gift remains a classification question; a mathematical comparison cannot supply that
classification.
The source itself must also remain open to challenge. Suppose the discount exception
appears in a footnote recovered by OCR but omitted by the embedded PDF text. Com-
paring whole-page strings identifies a difference but does not explain it. The investiga-
tion needs the footnote’s page region, the text recovered by each method and its attach-
ment to the relevant paragraph. Additional work then has a specific purpose: inspect
that region, recover its wording, or locate the corresponding oﬀicial text. Ordinary line
wrapping can be reconciled without changing words. A missing negation or uncertain
table header cannot. Agreement among text parsers does not settle a disagreement with
the rendered page because the text parsers may share the same omission.

factual model and proposed rule it affects, which action was taken, and whether new
evidence answered the question. A retry that produces the same unsupported asser-
