### Two acceptance issues requiring explicit review

The current product sets review_accepted only when every assigned review is
VALIDATED/ACCEPT. The fixed-correction continuation additionally appends a failed
dependency when any original warrant is unresolved. These checks protect against
unresolved material defects, but a whole-product completion decision also needs
to distinguish an unresolved proposition from correct handling of that
uncertainty. The installed context-disposition mechanism already supports a
source-backed NOT_REQUIRED_FOR_CONDITIONAL_ANSWER decision. Use it where its
conditions actually hold. Audit whether historical objections that no longer
apply to the corrected answer still veto it; do not assume they are obsolete.

Keep separate decisions for software execution, adequacy of the conditional
answer, resolution of the underlying legal proposition, and tool contribution.
A necessary unresolved premise blocks an unconditional claim. It need not imply
that a properly supported conditional explanation is a software failure. An
incorrect citation, lost condition, unsupported inference or unknown material
defect still blocks acceptance. No automatic UNRESOLVED-to-ACCEPT conversion.

The corpus consumer currently requires all four answer references before it can
evaluate its combined context and only reports success if every applicability,
later-treatment and proposition-use item has a positive status. Preparation of
source research need not wait for all answers. Any change to recorded completion
statuses must distinguish research completed with a documented limitation from
positive source qualification; it must not label an unchecked source qualified.
