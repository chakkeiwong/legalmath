authenticated parent request with identical non-record fields and identical
source records. It does not combine arbitrary subsets from unrelated requests.
Joint dependency packets require their original exact hash. Successful parent
responses and complete recovery children are validated under the existing rules.

whose second attempt failed before transport. That attempt and its failure stay
in history. This is a finite correction under the user's instruction to complete
the current program, not an automatic attempt reset. All other original owners
use existing allocations. Register cumulative detour 61 without rewriting the
preceding 60. Preserve successful owners and every unrelated veto. Exact sealed
predecessor bytes are archived for the existing historical validation mechanism.

uses the existing reviewed five-minute adapter, authenticating that adapter's
original grant against sealed predecessor files and the separately reviewed

still governed by the provider's normal authority check. No service probe or
additional replacement is added.
