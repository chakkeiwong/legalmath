# Proposed R10: exact P14 account continuation

16 October 2026. Round six completed Gatecoin's first synthesis level with
116/117 successes and one explicit provider safety refusal. P14 then reused
35 saved successful account responses before provider slot 2333 disconnected
without completing `detail-87`. Its authenticated event says the service is
temporarily unavailable. The shared circuit opened with zero automatic
replacements. Gatecoin's policy refusal must not be retried, transformed or
rerouted. P10 and evidence remain independent but require available service.

R10 is the last of the ten authorized repairs. The existing master supports
an exact synthesis continuation, but not this exact account continuation.
Extend only the continuation validator and binding construction to accept an
authenticated account failure. Reuse the existing account-recovery mechanism
and its unchanged checkpoint. Require an actual production replay to reproduce
all 35 saved successes and the exact failed request/schema/wire binding, with
that request the first missing dispatch. No provider call or reservation is
permitted in local replay. Preserve every old failure, source, grant and charge.

Planned cumulative allocation: R1-R10, nineteen of twenty additional task
attempts. The last unallocated task attempt remains reserved; one attempt
alone cannot justify another binding/acquisition/review cycle. The original
8,100-call ceiling, 41 separate historical calls, all 48 spent replacements and
both original deadlines remain unchanged. No automatic second continuation is
authorized by this repair. No provider switch or safety-refusal retry is part
of the proposal.

Evidence contract: the engineering question is whether the exact interrupted
P14 account can proceed without resampling its completed predecessors or
reopening Gatecoin's refused branch. The comparator is the actual round-six
request, phase, dispatch, receipts and settled program. Exact 35-success reuse,
exact missing request/wire equality, complete charge identity, zero local
dispatch, preserved refusal and original dependency graph are pass criteria.
Unknown reservations, changed source/history, a policy refusal in the proposed
retry, failed replay, exhausted authority/deadline or failed binding are
continuation vetoes. Provider availability is an unproven prerequisite; the
first needed request tests it once. Capacity and test totals explain execution
only. Four original legal questions, 46 obligations, 35 readings, full reviews
and matched tool effects remain the substantive acceptance criteria.

Default audit: the unchanged P14 recovery is usable only if production replay
proves all successes and the missing request still match. The receipt, not the
generic `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT` summary, establishes the no-output service failure.
The one-request service bound and zero replacements derive from the user's
finite authorization and spent replacement ledger. Existing token/output
profiles remain reviewed constraints, not a new capacity claim. Historical
mission refresh exceeds its 300-second child timeout; retain that operational
failure and reconcile with an exact direct refresh after settlement. R10 does
not silently weaken validation or add an unrelated performance repair.

Skeptical review: PASS for unbound candidate design and conditional adoption
after settlement and actual replay. A naive extension would reopen every
question, accidentally admitting Gatecoin's refusal, or mistake a generic
local circuit error for proof that no provider call occurred. Require explicit
P14 ownership, slot 2333 and authenticated no-output service events. A blanket
retry is rejected. The closed-loop result must be retained even if the service
fails again. This may close the execution prerequisite; it cannot establish
complete legal interpretation or full tool evaluation.

Keep candidate code in /tmp while the current supervisor runs. Do not change
bound production files, freeze a moving baseline, register a revision or start
a duplicate supervisor. Retain exact commands, environments, hashes, elapsed
times, actual replay and final decision under this directory.

Pre-adoption repair: reused successes have request, schema, result and retained
provenance; they do not acquire a new reference-binding file. The initial
prototype wrongly required such files for all responses. Correct the checker
and fixture to require them only for the newly dispatched failed request.
The production replay proves 36 exact logical contracts, 35 exact retained
successes and the failed request's exact wire contract. Do not manufacture
wire evidence in historical directories. Missing failure bindings or invented
success bindings fail the checkpoint checks.

Actual evidence also exposes two ordering dependencies in wire encoding:
record-column order and the first encounter of hash identities. Canonical JSON
sorts object fields, so restoring columns alone does not recreate hash aliases.
The actual production replay must retain its failed request with constructor
order intact in an additional evidence file. Its contents must equal the saved
logical request, and normal `prepare` must reproduce the entire recorded wire
binding exactly. Receipt checks then use that reconstruction. Do not change
the encoder, guess an ordering, relax wire equality or rewrite old evidence.


Execution review: PASS for adoption and one exact P14 continuation. Installed
regression checks and actual production replay pass. All 35 saved successes
are reused through unchanged provenance; all 36 logical requests and schemas
match. The first missing request is detail-87, with its exact wire request and
schema authenticated to slot 2333. No provider completion or reservation occurs
during replay. The failed stage contains exactly one charged request.

Decision: permit the single interrupted account attempt, then unsent work and
independent eligible P10/evidence owners. Preserve Gatecoin's failed branch and
refusal. Source/history integrity, original dependencies and cumulative charges
remain mandatory. Availability is unproven until the first needed request;
failure closes the new scope without an automatic replacement. No extra call
allowance or deadline is granted.

Post-run red team: exact transport and saved-response identity prove execution
continuity only. Later account coverage, synthesis meaning, source reviews and
matched answer effects remain unevaluated. The refusal is a provider limitation
for that request, not proof of an impossible legal question. An unavailable
service defers independent live owners without consuming their task attempts.

This adoption uses ten of ten repairs and nineteen of twenty additional task
attempts cumulatively. One task attempt remains unallocated; it does not cover
another binding/acquisition/review cycle. The program must retain and report
actual results even if this last repair cannot complete the integration.
