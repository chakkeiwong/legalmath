Hong Kong Court of Final Appeal - Decided Cases






    
    
    
    
  
  
    
Recent CFA judgments in decided cases handed down within the current or last month can be found on the Recent Judgments page.

Older judgments can be found on the Judgments in Decided Cases page. This page links to the searchable HKLII database. There is usually a time lag of a few days between the date of handing down of a CFA judgment and its publication on the HKLII website. Accordingly, on a particular date, this page may not contain all of the CFA judgments. You are advised to check the Recent Judgments page for those judgments that may not yet have been included in the searchable HKLII database.

Archived Judgments contains a separate database of CFA judgments handed down since 1 January 2015 after transfer from Recent Judgments. It is not a comprehensive database of all CFA judgments.
