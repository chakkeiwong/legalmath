(B) Device binding

4. Device binding creates a secure association between the internet broker or VASP’s
internet trading platform and the client’s device based on securely enrolled device
attributes. This approach enables the internet broker or VASP to verify that any login
request originates from a device previously registered to the client’s account. This
reduces reliance on credentials alone and helps mitigate unauthorised account access
from unregistered devices.
