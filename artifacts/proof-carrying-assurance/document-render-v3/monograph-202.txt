Verification also checks the report that a reader sees. Every disagreement produced by
the recorded comparisons must appear with its material severity and unresolved dispo-
sition. A caller cannot delete it or mark it explained without a corresponding evidence
procedure. The same check preserves the proposed readings’ objections, the critical
questions and their required evidence, and the exact proposition and domain attached
to each obligation. Consequently, evidence for a finite replay cannot be relabelled as a
result about every future circular while keeping its successful checker status.
Concurrent document changes also invalidated a later attempt even though its focused
tests passed. The combined revision is therefore validated in an isolated repository
copy. Its controller records the actual execution root, archives input bytes, and com-
pares source hashes before returning the results to the working repository. Each cam-
paign retains a fixed retry ceiling; the earlier exhausted campaign remains closed with-
out current acceptance. This distinction prevents a successful command on one revision
from being presented as evidence for another revision.
The resulting engineering evidence can be reproduced and reviewed. It does not es-
tablish legal accuracy, statistical independence, complete future-circular coverage or
production readiness. Those claims require a separate evaluation with independently
adjudicated cases and a bank-approved operating environment.
