6.33.3 One downloaded file can contain incompatible editions
The acquired 23EC44 appendix pack supplies a concrete source-selection test. Its clean
October 2023 terms occupy Appendix 7, PDF pages 74–94. Appendix 7a, pages 95–135,
shows amendments and can yield old and replacement wording in the same extracted
stream. The new selection manifest binds the original PDF, each extracted page, both
edition ranges and the exact selected provisions. An active source packet requires a
clean edition; attempting to select marked changes as the active edition is rejected. Mix-
ing provisions from the two editions is rejected even though both came from the same
genuine download.
Selection within the clean edition must preserve qualifications as well. The executed
example takes the definitions on page 76, the footnote about the structure of the collec-
tive investment scheme, the continuation on page 77 about persistent price appreciation
and notification, and the application paragraphs on that page. The manifest explicitly
links these provisions. Selecting the definitions while omitting the declared footnote or
continuation fails with a missing-dependency result. Each excerpt has a page and exact
character offsets; changed source bytes, changed extracted text or a changed manifest
invalidate the earlier selection.
These structural checks operate on an inspected manifest. They do not discover every
legally relevant qualification automatically. The packet therefore keeps unresolved au-
thority questions as unresolved dependencies. The file also carries a notice that the
circular was superseded and updated in December 2023; the printed October date can-
not establish current applicability. The program records the clean historical edition
and leaves effectiveness unresolved. This separates successful acquisition and selection
from the further task of determining which provisions govern a particular assessment
date.
All restored pairs, retained parents, source questions and unresolved PDF-materiality
identities remain available for the next investigation. Estimating legal error or savings in
review cost still requires source-grounded answers and independent reference cases.
6.34 A proof-carrying investigation joins the methods
without joining their claims
The preceding sections establish why a single program, a majority vote or a successful
search cannot settle the meaning of a circular. They leave a practical question for the im-
plementation team: how should source recovery, alternative readings, legal arguments,
formal checks and Java generation be joined so that each result remains attached to the
question it actually answers? The answer in the current prototype is a typed assurance
dossier. The dossier is a record of evidence and limits. It is not a new legal language
and it is not a certificate that the regulator would accept a proposed reading.
