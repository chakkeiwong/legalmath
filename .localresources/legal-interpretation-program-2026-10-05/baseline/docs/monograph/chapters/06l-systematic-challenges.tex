\section{Challenge each interpretation before narrowing the alternatives}
\label{sec:systematic-challenges}

The UCITS continuation makes the central difficulty concrete. Two readers can
return valid source references for the same claim while leaving its executable
meaning unsettled. Increasing the number of replies does not tell us whether
both readers overlooked the same exception. The challenge method therefore
asks what could falsify each proposed reading and records the evidence needed
to act on that challenge. The implementation joins source-bound questions,
independent Boolean checks and bounded reader follow-ups inside the complete
investigator. Its execution record distinguishes these mechanisms from the
unresolved question of whether the proposed legal premises are right.

\subsection{A challenge is a question with a complete denominator}

Consider a simplified, invented provision: ``An intermediary must make the
report when condition A or condition B holds, unless exemption E applies.''
This sentence teaches the mechanics without purporting to reproduce a circular.
One proposed program computes $(A\lor B)\land\neg E$; another computes
$A\lor(B\land\neg E)$. At $A=\mathrm{true}$ and $E=\mathrm{true}$, the second
program requires a report even though the first does not. The difference is
mathematical. Deciding whether the exemption applies to both conditions remains
an interpretation of the sentence and its legal context.

Every retained candidate is consequently examined along nine declared
dimensions: actor, scope, quantification, negation, exception, time, definition,
authority and output meaning. The dimensions are a useful working vocabulary,
not a theorem that all legal ambiguities have these forms. A reader can add a
new concern or an alternative that the formal vocabulary cannot express. The
report keeps those concerns beside the original candidates.

A challenge binds the exact source edition, original question, candidate and
dimension. Its identifier is calculated from those substantive inputs, so a
different batch number cannot make the same challenge new. A source scanner
also locates words such as ``unless'', ``each'' and ``before''. These locations
nominate questions for investigation. Their absence does not prove that the
corresponding exception, quantifier or temporal qualification is absent: it may
be expressed differently or incorporated from another document.

The denominator is the full set of required candidate--dimension pairs. An
invalid reply, an unassessed dimension and an expired attempt remain in that
set. Otherwise a reader could appear more reliable merely by answering the
easy questions. Every response retains its exact evidence and a proposed
disposition, while the program separately records which questions have actually
received the required perspectives.

\subsection{What a counterexample permits us to reject}

Return to the exemption. Suppose an investigation proposes the explicit formal
premise that exemption E precludes this particular reporting duty. Write
$P(x)$ for the premise that E holds in factual situation $x$, and $R(x)$ for the
candidate's decision that the duty applies. The proposed obligation is
\[
  \text{for every }x\in D,\qquad P(x)\Longrightarrow \neg R(x),
\]
where $D$ is the declared domain of complete Boolean assignments to A, B and E.
There are eight such assignments. The checker evaluates them independently of
the production expression evaluator. A candidate that returns true at an
assignment with E true supplies a replayable counterexample to this obligation.

For a finite collection of declared obligations $\mathcal O$, let
$C(h,o,x)$ mean that candidate $h$ satisfies obligation $o$ at assignment $x$.
The candidates surviving these checks are precisely
\[
  H_{\mathcal O,D}=\{h\in H:\ \forall o\in\mathcal O\;
                              \forall x\in D,\ C(h,o,x)\}.
\]
If a checker finds $o$ and $x$ with $\neg C(h,o,x)$, elementary logic gives
$h\notin H_{\mathcal O,D}$. The executed Lean theorem checks this conditional
elimination rule. It assumes the stated domain and obligations; it does not
prove that the exemption sentence warrants the proposed formal premise.
The original candidate and the counterexample both remain available in the
investigation. A false premise can invalidate an otherwise useful elimination,
so the legal conclusion cannot be silently copied from the mathematical result.

Breadth-first enumeration provides a second, distinct check. Within an explicit
grammar, the program constructs Boolean alternatives before assigning search
priority. It records the grammar, enumerated expressions and remaining
frontier. Exhausting that finite grammar establishes only that its expressions
were considered. It says nothing about an omitted actor, a different factual
abstraction, an unrepresented date or a relevant authority outside the packet.
Search priority therefore describes which distinction to inspect next, never a
probability that a reading is legally correct.

\subsection{Different opportunities to notice the same error}

The source scanner, independent Boolean evaluator and language-model readers
encounter different failure opportunities. A scanner can preserve an explicit
``unless'' that both readers ignored. A mathematical evaluator can replay a
violating assignment that a persuasive explanation obscures. A reader can
propose that the factual vocabulary itself omits a relevant condition, which
neither of the other checks can infer from Boolean structure alone.

These differences justify combining the methods, but they do not establish
statistical independence. All may depend on the same incomplete source packet.
Two fresh conversations may use the same model family. Two compiled programs
may derive from the same mistaken interpretation. The investigation therefore
records actual mechanism and provider identities and their shared dependencies.
Changing a reader's name cannot create another model family. If an alternative
configured provider is unavailable, that absence remains visible.

Controlled faults test the connections. For example, remove the exemption from
a proposed Boolean program while retaining the declared formal obligation.
The counterexample checker must expose a violating assignment, and the complete
investigator must retain it. Such a test checks a specified mathematical fault.
It neither supplies a human legal answer nor estimates the frequency of future
legal mistakes. Detection counts describe this declared collection of faults;
they cannot establish superiority among stochastic interpretation methods.

\subsection{Bound the answer while preserving the investigation}

The earlier 263-concern diagnostic showed why output size must be controlled
separately from source access. The new profile requests a bounded group of
challenge responses while preserving the complete source, question and candidate
context. Its first round obtains blind perspectives. Follow-up rounds receive
the same retained prior-round evidence and investigate differences in status,
explanation or proposed alternatives. All original versions remain available.

The joining check accounts for every required challenge exactly once in each
completed group and exposes missing groups. Equal status words alone are not
semantic agreement: two readers can use the same status while proposing
different exception scopes. The report therefore preserves substantive response
differences and unresolved cross-group interactions. No majority vote may remove
an interpretation. After the declared maximum number of attempts, the unresolved
question and its history remain part of the result.

The profile must execute inside the complete investigator, and its actual stage
files must be checked when the dossier is verified. A successful standalone
helper does not establish that the product called it. Changing the input codec,
challenge vocabulary or follow-up policy changes the method identity and
requires a later prospective window. The earlier tested method remains a
separate historical record.

Finally, evidence acquisition changes particular premises. A newly obtained
legislative edition can answer whether a referenced provision is available;
its effective date and application to the transaction may remain disputed.
Installed-package file hashes can establish which local files were inspected;
they cannot attest remote model weights or the publisher's clock. These limits
belong beside the affected result. The useful product is a progressively more
checkable investigation that explains exactly what was established and what
still prevents a legal conclusion.

\subsection{The implemented rule and its assumptions}

The mathematical rule needs a slightly more precise treatment of applicability
than an ordinary Boolean program. Each candidate supplies a scope function
$S_h:D\to\{0,1\}$ and a decision function $R_h:D\to\{0,1\}$. A proposed
obligation supplies a guard $G_o:D\to\{0,1\}$ and an expected decision
$e_o\in\{0,1\}$. In this implementation the obligation is satisfied at $x$ when
\[
 C(h,o,x)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 G_o(x)=1\ \Longrightarrow\
 \bigl(S_h(x)=1\ \land\ R_h(x)=e_o\bigr).
\]
Thus an active obligation that the candidate excludes from its scope is a
violation of the declared obligation. This convention matters: otherwise a
candidate could pass every check by declaring every relevant transaction out of
scope. Whether an obligation really applies to that transaction is still a
premise supplied by the investigation.

The independent checker supports total Boolean assignments to at most eight
named facts. It parses the stated expressions separately and enumerates all
$2^n$ assignments. For each active obligation it records the first violating
assignment, together with the source, question, candidate and obligation hashes.
Verification recomputes the certificate from those original inputs. Unsupported
numeric, temporal, unknown-value or conflict semantics receive an explicit
qualification; they do not produce a vacuous passing certificate.

Three propositions are checked by Lean. The first says that one active
obligation and a violating assignment contradict universal satisfaction. The
second says that quantifying over every item in every declared batch is
equivalent to quantifying over their joined index set. The third says that two
different reported values contradict a uniform report across that set. These
propositions are deliberately stated over supplied functions and index sets.
They do not prove that the Python parser implements the Lean definitions; the
independently replayed finite checks supply separate evidence for the supported
Python fragment. Nor does disagreement among explanations establish which
explanation is legally right.

\subsection{What the executed checks establish}

The first complete-investigator integration run passed 107 focused checks.
It exercised the new stage, retained its actual responses and checked those
files when the investigator's report was verified. A further 18 checks covered
the executed Lean rules, bounded panel and complete dispatcher together. These
counts describe software checks, not independently established legal answers.
In the controlled exception experiment, two unanimous reader proposals could
not suppress the Boolean check's counterexample to the supplied exemption
obligation. Altering the question, replacing an actual response, deleting an
executed action or changing the kernel receipt was rejected by the respective
verification boundary.

The source work exposed another practical source of false confidence. A request
for Chapter 571 as a PDF returned an HTML browser-check page. The government's
bulk legislation archive was available, but its approximately 560 MB response
exceeded the declared five-minute acquisition limit. The repair used the ZIP
directory and HTTP byte ranges to recover the complete selected Chapter 571 XML
member. It retained the range bytes, required the same strong ETag throughout,
checked each Content-Range and let the ZIP reader verify the member's CRC.
About 716 kB of range responses recovered a 3.47 MB XML member. This establishes
which bytes were retrieved, not which historical provisions govern a particular
transaction.

The original failed requests remain alongside that repair. In particular, the
SFC circular endpoint returned the correct circular as HTML despite the initial
expectation of JSON. Correcting its media-type interpretation did not rewrite
the original expectation or response. This is why the execution record must
distinguish successful accounting of an attempt from successful acquisition of
the requested authority. The repair phase requires a nonempty complete chapter
member before it can pass.

The XML identifies its consolidated edition as 24 August 2025 and exposes
separate parent elements for Part IV and Schedule 1. These are useful structural
anchors for the retained authority question about the 2023 circular. The date
also supplies a reason for caution: a later consolidated edition is not, by
itself, evidence of the law applicable at an earlier transaction date. The full
member remains available for a provision-by-provision temporal investigation;
the retrieval result does not close that question.

\subsection{The live capacity test and its unresolved work}

The test on circular 26EC22 encountered a different obstacle from the synthetic
exception example. Its fourteen retained interpretations generated 126
candidate--dimension obligations. The first request occupied 270,261 bytes,
above the existing 200,000-byte input limit, so neither reader received it.
A recorded investigation could therefore contain all the proposed questions
without having obtained any challenge response. Recording the failed request
established what went wrong; it did not demonstrate that the readers had
examined the interpretations.

The repair stored each repeated passage once and represented the obligations
as rows with named columns. Every reference expands to its complete original
text. The complete source, all fourteen interpretations, their original
questions and the mathematical checks were preserved. Exact reconstruction
was checked before dispatch for all twenty-two planned requests; the largest
occupied 194,723 bytes. This establishes preservation of the input and its
fit within the declared bound. It does not establish that a reader understands
the compact representation as reliably as ordinary prose.

The continuation reused the retained interpretation and question proposals
under the implementation that produced them. It did not ask the models to
generate those proposals again. The new challenge requests inherited the
original attempt counts and start times, so changing the representation did
not renew an exhausted issue. Earlier failures remained available beside the
new responses.

Nineteen requests reached the model service. Sixteen returned responses that
passed the structural, identity and quotation checks; two connections failed,
and another response carried a wrong binding identity. The latter was rejected,
but an error in rejection handling interrupted preparation of the final report.
An offline repair reconstructed that report from the recorded actions without
making another model call. Three planned requests remained undispatched.
The report retains 42 obligations missing a required perspective, 75 unresolved
obligations and 21 proposed alternative-reading records. The alternatives have
not all been investigated, and repeated proposals are not independent findings.

The responses also make the substantive uncertainty clearer. For paragraph 10,
readers distinguished retained accountability from effective performance or
discharge of responsibility. An operational defect might be evidence bearing
on responsibility without, by itself, determining whether this particular
requirement has been breached. These are competing proposals about the source,
not settled legal interpretations. Eleven original candidates lacked a
supported formal program. The other three supplied no declared formal
obligations for a counterexample check, so their absence of a found violation
does not support their legal correctness.

The repaired software passed 2,243 regression tests. That result and the
validated responses support different conclusions: the tests exercise stated
software properties, while the live responses show that the repaired input
reached the readers and yielded usable proposals. The investigation remains
incomplete. Its next substantive work is to investigate the missing perspectives
and new alternatives, obtain the required authority and define justified formal
premises. Reusing this development circular cannot supply evidence about
performance on genuinely later publications.

\subsection{The supplemental review and what remains unresolved}

The supplemental review completed the missing perspectives. Six calls supplied
all sixty missing reviewer assignments, and thirty-six further calls assessed
each of the fourteen original readings and twenty-one alternative records from
both roles. Every record was retained. The alternatives are still proposals
about meaning, and repeated proposals are not independent discoveries. The
active source is the revision of circular 26EC22 dated 20 April 2026; the older
authority questions about the 2023 circular belong to a separate historical
inquiry.

The resulting seventy assessments proposed twenty-four conditional Boolean
programs. Eight original readings and five alternative records received at least
one such proposal; forty-six assessments remained explicitly unformalized.
Each accepted program had satisfiable positive and negative obligations, so an
empty premise could not produce a passing result. Exhaustive checks found no
violation of those declared obligations. Separately, the existing conditional
expression compiler agreed with native execution on twenty-four bounded probes
per program, including unknown facts. Production translation remained blocked
by the original source questions and dependencies. None of its proposed runtime
cases was executed.

Passing these checks did not make the competing readings equivalent. One
reading separates retained responsibility for management, operational soundness
and ownership records from achieved performance. Another additionally requires
an adequately managed, sound arrangement and proper ownership records. Under
an explicit illustrative identification of their responsibility predicates,
exhaustive enumeration produced three assignments in which the retention
program returned true and the outcome program returned false. For example,
responsibility could be classified as retained while the records were
classified as improper. This is a conditional distinction between programs.
The identification of the predicates, the factual possibility of the example
and the legally correct interpretation remain separate questions.

The authority review also caught two errors in selecting source text. Several
parts of the Chapter 571 XML use the label P4; the first extraction allowed
Schedule 10's transitional provisions to replace the operative Part IV on offers
of investments. In the 2024 Unit Trust Code, a search for the Chapter 6 heading
selected its contents entry. Selection now checks the statutory parent and the
presence of sections 103--105, and requires the chapter's substantive numbered
clauses. The failed selections and the responses that exposed them remain
available.

Both reviewers then examined the corrected sources for the current tokenisation
question. Section 6.8 expressly permits a trustee-appointed person to maintain
the register. Its wording agrees across the retained 2018, 2024 and current
Code editions after declared whitespace normalization. Circular 26EC22
separately retains the Product Provider's responsibility despite outsourcing.
These texts make the distinction between an appointed performer and retained
responsibility more concrete, but they do not by themselves determine legal
title, settlement finality, the complete liability standard or effective
performance in a particular arrangement. The statutory consolidation is dated
24 August 2025, with section 103 amended from 23 May 2025. It establishes
neither the complete historical 2023 law nor uninterrupted applicability through
April 2026. The two historical structured-product re-reviews exhausted their
allowed attempts after quoting passages that occurred more than once in the
supplied statutory unit; their results remain unaccepted.

Twenty-four follow-up calls reconsidered all fifty-three obligations still open
after the missing perspectives had been supplied. Their complete requests
included the original circular, readings and questions, both relevant reading
assessments, and the corrected current-authority findings. Repeated field names
were stored as named table columns, alongside the exact text pool. Independent
reconstruction preserved every field and value, and the largest request occupied
193,268 bytes. The two pilot responses initially failed because quotation
validation still admitted only the original circular's source identifiers.
Checking the additional quotations against their retained, hashed authority
sources recovered those unchanged responses without another model call.

The follow-up reduced the unresolved count from fifty-three to forty-six:
fourteen questions each about time, definitions and authority, and four about
the meaning of the output. Relative to the earlier seventy-five unresolved
obligations, this is a narrower and fully reviewed set of questions, not a count
of legally settled propositions. No new reading was proposed. Fifteen new
formal-premise records yielded thirty-seven nonvacuous checks without a
violation; eight other program--premise combinations used incompatible factual
vocabularies and remained unsupported. No inferred renaming converted those
combinations into passes.

The continuation used seventy-nine of the one hundred additional calls.
Every reservation has a final record, including rejected responses and the two
offline recoveries. The old spending records and all earlier failures remain
unchanged. The revised extraction and encoding implementation passed 2,331
regression tests; the later authority-citation and recovery changes passed
fifty-one focused checks and replay of the actual provider receipts. These
results establish the reported software behavior and the extent of the review.
Resolving the remaining questions requires applicable legal standards and
editions, the identities and roles of the actual parties, the governing scheme
documents, and evidence about ownership records, controls and regulatory
requests. Additional model agreement alone would not supply those facts.
