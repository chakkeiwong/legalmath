# Preserved prospectuses and related sources

Original bytes are retained in `originals/`; page text in `text/` is a derivative.
`catalog.json` records every requested source, including unsuccessful downloads.
`manifest.json` records URLs, acquisition attempts, versions and SHA-256 checksums.
These are development sources. Document preservation does not verify a legal interpretation.

Additional [senior and junior bond comparison prospectuses](classification-controls/README.md)
are preserved for the dedicated loss-absorption classification plan: four final
PDF bundles covering eight bond series, with original-byte and extraction hashes.
Their classifications and source-linked reasons are now in the
[bond-by-bond report](../implementation/bond-loss-absorption-classification/results.md).

Additional [UK, EU and Australian senior bank issue documents](bank-senior-controls/README.md)
cover NatWest Markets, BPCE and CBA. Their source assessment and an executed
diagnostic expose explicit statutory principal-loss provisions in the UK/EU
examples and the need to distinguish CBA repayment, restructuring and benchmark
clauses. The dedicated binary classifier has now been executed.

[Additional corporate and capital-bond sources](classification-additions/README.md)
include Tesco, Veolia, Deutsche Bank, Sogécap and the executed Standard Chartered
conditions. Further source-family challenges are preserved under
[classification-holdout](classification-holdout/README.md) and
[classification-holdout-v2](classification-holdout-v2/README.md). The report
contains 26 issue rows: 12 positive, 13 negative and one unresolved. All source
interpretations remain qualified; formal execution does not prove English law.
