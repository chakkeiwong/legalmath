1.      Recent Developments



1.1     Banco Espírito Santo informs about the resolutions of the extraordinary general meeting
        On 9 June 2011, BES informed about the following resolutions of the Shareholders’ General
        Meeting held on the same day:

        Item One - approval of the partial amendment of the articles of association, namely the amendment
        of Article 4 as follows:
                                                  Article 4.º Share Capital

        1. The company’s share capital is fully paid up and is 3,499,999,998.00 and it is represented by
        1.166.666.666 shares with no par value.

                                                                                                            1
        2. The Board of Directors is authorized, upon favourable opinion of the Audit Committee, to
        increase the share capital, one or more times, through the issuance of ordinary shares or preferential
        shares, redeemable or nonredeemable, under the terms and conditions to be defined.

        3. The maximum amount authorized, in addition to the share capital amount, is of
        €5.000.000.000,00, being this authorization valid for a period of five years.


        Item Two - approval on the suppression of shareholders’ pre-emption rights, in case the Board of
        Directors resolves on a capital increase, under the terms of Article 4 (2) of the Company’s Articles
        of Association.

        The purpose of this resolution is to fulfil legal provisions following the request by Banco Espirito
        Santo of a personal guarantee of the Portuguese State, under the terms of Law no. 60-A/2008, of 20
        October, and Portaria no. 1219-A/2008, of 23 October, as amended by Portaria no. 946/2010, of 22
        September, for a financing operation through the issuance of non subordinated bonds up to the
        amount of € 1,250,000,000 with a maturity of three years, if that guarantee were to be executed.

        Item Three - ratification of the co-optation, on May 2nd 2011, of Mr. Vincent Pacaud as a member
        of the Board of Directors.



1.2     Moody’s places Portuguese banks’standalone ratings on review for downgrade

        On 9 June, 2011 Moody’s placed Portuguese banks’standalone ratings on review, including Banco
        Espirito Santo standalone credit strength D+ and long term rating Baa2, for possible downgrade.
