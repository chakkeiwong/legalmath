This supplement (the “Supplement”) to the prospectus (the “Prospectus”) dated 17 July 2013
and supplemented by the first supplement to the Prospectus dated 2 of September 2013, by the
second supplement to the Prospectus dated 6 of November 2013 and by the third supplement to
the Prospectus dated 24 of February 2014, constitutes a fourth supplement to the base
prospectus for the purposes of article 13 of Part II of the Luxembourg Act dated 10 July 2005
relating to prospectuses for securities (the “Prospectus Act”) and is prepared in connection with
the €20,000,000,000 Euro Medium Term Note Programme (the “Programme”) established by
Banco Espírito Santo, S.A. (the “Bank” or “BES”) and BES Finance Ltd. (“BES Finance”).
Terms defined in the Prospectus have the same meaning when used in this Supplement.

This Supplement is supplemental to, and should be read in conjunction with the Prospectus, the
first supplement dated 2 September 2013, the second supplement dated 6 November 2013 and
the third supplement dated 24 February 2014.

The purpose of this Supplement is to include a new risk factor in the section of the Prospectus
headed “Risk Factors”, to incorporate by reference (i) the Bank’s Management Report and (ii)
the Financial Statements and Notes to the Financial Statements as at 31 December 2013, which
are still to be approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders to be held on 5 May 2014 and
to amend the summary of the Prospectus accordingly, to update the section on recent
developments of the Bank and to update certain statements in the “General Information” section
of the Prospectus.

Supplement. To the best of the knowledge of each of the Bank and BES Finance (who have

2.     Risk Factors
       In the section “Risk Factors” commencing on page 31 of the Prospectus, after the risk
       factor numbered 1.16, a new risk factor will be added with the following wording:
       “1.17     2013 KPMG AUDIT REPORT
       In its audit report of the BES’ consolidated financial statements for the financial year
       ended on 31 December 2013, KPMG included an emphasis of matter with respect to note
       46 therein, which makes reference to the BES Group's placement of certain debt
       instruments issued by Espírito Santo International, S.A. ("ESI")              and certain of its
       subsidiaries. As at 31 December 2013, the total amount of these debt instruments was
       euro 3,035 million, of which euro 1,565 million were held by retail customers of the BES
       Group and euro 1,470 million by its institutional customers. As at 14 March, 2014 the
       total amount of these debt instruments held by retail customers of the BES Group was
       euro 867 million. Although ESI has put in place reorganisation and deleveraging
       programs aimed at rebalancing its financial position, given the challenges associated
       with ESI's ability to fully implement these programs and its financial position generally,
       Espírito Santo Financial Group S.A. has unconditionally and irrevocably guaranteed the
       obligations of ESI under the debt instruments distributed to BES Group retail customers.
       In the event that BES Group customers suffer losses from debt securities issued by ESI,
       this may have negative reputational consequences and, as a consequence, could have a
       material adverse effect on the BES Group's financial condition and results of
       operations.”
