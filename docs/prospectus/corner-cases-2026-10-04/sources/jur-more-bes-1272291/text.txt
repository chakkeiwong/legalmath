2.      Recent Developments

2.1     Qualified shareholding

        On 3 December 2010, pursuant to and for the purposes of Article 17 of the Portuguese Securities
        Code, BES announced it had received a notification from Silchester International Investors LLP
        ("Silchester") informing that, on 30 November 2010, Silchester increased its stake in BES from 4.17
        per cent. to 5.02 per cent., controlling 58,559,605 BES shares.

        Silchester is a UK based investment management firm. Silchester invests client assets in publicly
        traded non-US equity securities. As of 30 November 2010, Silchester had in excess of $20 billion
        (€15.3 billion) of assets under management primarily from US institutional clients.

2.2     Completion of acquisition of Gespastor

        On 23 December 2010, BES announced that ESAF Spain, through ES Gestión, had fully acquired
        Banco Pastor’s fund management firm, Gespastor SGIIC, S.A. on 23 December 2010, under the
        terms and conditions announced on 6 August 2010.

2.3     Completion of acquisition of 25.1% Moza Banco

        On 20 January 2011, BES announced the acquisition of 25.1 per cent. of Moza Banco, SA ("Moza
        Banco") for approximately €7.1 million, through its subsidiary BES Africa, SGPS, SA. ("BES
        Africa"). The remaining capital is held by Moçambique Capital SA, with 50.4 per cent. and
        Geocapital - Gestão de Participações, SA with 24.5 per cent..

        Simultaneously, Moza Banco has increased its share capital from €8.6 million to €12.3 million (at a
        MZM / EUR exchange rate on the date of 30 January 2011).This share capital increase was
        subscribed by the shareholders in the proportion of their stakes, raising the investment of BES Africa
        to approximately €8.1 million.

        Moza Banco is a Mozambican bank which began operating in June 2008 and focuses on Corporate
        Banking and Affluent / Private Banking.

        The two institutions combine (i) the recognized competence of the management team and knowledge
        of the Mozambican market, by the current shareholders of Moza Banco and (ii) the experience of
        Banco Espirito Santo Group, with strong know-how in Affluent, Private and Corporate Banking.
