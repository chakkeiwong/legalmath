# Corporate senior and capital-bond source additions

Ten original PDFs, 682 pages, are preserved with URLs and original/extraction
hashes in [manifest.json](manifest.json). The completed classifier uses these
sources together with the earlier archive. These are dated offering documents;
source meaning and the relevance of all incorporated material remain qualified.

Corporate issues are described by their actual senior unsecured terms;
“senior preferred” is not imposed as a banking classification on them.
The [Tesco financial statements](originals/tesco-2025-financials.pdf) were an
initial wrong-base candidate and are preserved as financial reference material.
Two truncated Veolia transfers remain in `acquisition/`; only the complete
197-page PDF is used. The earlier Standard Chartered circular contains a
subject-to-completion warning; its executed deed supplies final conditions.

[Issue inventory](issue-inventory.json) records document scopes, exact editions,
identifiers and qualifications without expected answers. The separate
[Lloyds inventory](lloyds-inventory.json) recovers the already-retained final
HTML filing mirror and records its weaker authenticity boundary.
