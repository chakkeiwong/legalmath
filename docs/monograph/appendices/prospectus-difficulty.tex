\section{How to reproduce and interpret this record}

The protected run used commit \texttt{2d6b737b} plus 293 archived source files,
including then-uncommitted repairs. It completed in 16.063 seconds on the
existing CPU environment, with a 60-second guard per document and no
classifier tuning after selection. Across all 24 documents there were
21 abstentions, three conditional positives and no runtime errors. The
three focused harness checks and 909 prospectus regression tests passed;
those checks establish software and preservation properties, not independent
legal correctness.

The source set has 24 distinct hashes and 16 issuer families, below the
original preference of at least 20 families. Orange had previous catalogue
discovery exposure. The engine received whole-document diagnostic selections;
that choice deliberately tests irrelevant text and unselected options, but
does not establish their legal applicability. These distinctions prevent an
abstention count from being presented as an error rate.

The convenient PDF index is
\path{docs/prospectus/jurisdiction-2026-10-04/prior-24-pdf-index.json}.
Each copy was checked against its retained original and SHA-256 hash.
Original responses, HTTP receipts and extracted pages remain preserved;
the new filenames do not replace them. The reviewed identities, dates,
page anchors and raw-result links are in
\path{docs/implementation/prospectus-difficulty-2026-10-04/reviewed-catalogue.json}.
The same directory contains \path{review-errata.json} and
\path{next-repairs.md}. The corrections include TenneT's issuer, EDF's
replacement-note scope and proposed/future insurance-resolution law.
Frozen pre-run metadata is retained so that the original run remains
reproducible.

The immutable reader outputs and execution manifest are stored here:
\begin{quote}\small
\path{docs/implementation/prospectus-difficulty-2026-10-04/run-001/}
\end{quote}
The method archive is \path{docs/prospectus/difficulty-2026-10-04/method.zip}.
Integrity is checked with the following command from the worktree:

\begin{lstlisting}

freeze; subsequent jurisdiction retrievals must therefore be distinguished
from the original study's 40 requests. The original study manifest and its
dated verification preserve the earlier balance. The acquisition limit is
a resource control, not a measure of legal completeness.

\subsection{BES: repayment promises and resolution measures}

The retained final terms are dated 6 May 2014, Series 36, tranche 1,
EUR 750 million of 2.625 percent senior notes due 8 May 2017. Rendered
page 4 confirms ISIN PTBEQKOM0019 and Common Code 106485003.
Rendered PDF pages 1--3 establish the issue, senior status, 100 percent
redemption and EUR 100,000 final redemption amount per calculation amount.
Page 5 says that intended Eurosystem eligibility does not establish actual
eligibility: that depends on the Eurosystem criteria.

The terms require the 17 July 2013 base prospectus and supplements dated
2 September 2013, 6 November 2013, 24 February 2014 and 23 April 2014.
The extended search retained that base and all four supplements. The
Portuguese measure with its exact instrument list remains missing; the
new judgments discussed below concern distinct claims that must be matched
separately. The original
five-page PDF has no extracted text layer; silent empty-text classification
would lose even the repayment promise. Selected fields were transcribed
from rendered pages, with their transcription status recorded.

The local source is
\path{docs/prospectus/jurisdiction-2026-10-04/sources/jur-bes-2014-ptbeqkom0019/source.pdf}.
A proposed test first establishes the exact promise, then adds a reviewed
measure tied to that identifier and date. It must distinguish an authority's
power, its exercise, a liability's transfer or retransfer, recognition in
another forum, and the eventual payment outcome. A judgment concerning a
different loan or note cannot fill the identifier link.

\subsection{Greece: English-law principal protection and enforcement limits}

The source is the Hellenic Republic's offering circular of 28 March 2008:
EUR 600 million of 2.085 percent inflation-linked notes due 25 July 2057,
consolidated with the EUR 1 billion 2007 issue. PDF page 25 gives permanent
ISIN XS0292467775 and temporary ISIN XS0347909490. Condition 4, page 10,
floors redemption at principal. Condition 10, pages 14--16, contains
creditor-voting modification provisions. Condition 14, pages 17--18, selects
English law and qualifies execution/attachment by international conventions
and Greek law.

This is a useful control against silently applying a domestic-law statutory
restructuring rule based only on the issuer country. It is also a positive
source example of a contractual immunity waiver with an express
local-enforcement qualification. The applicable law, actual attachment
proceeding and exact restructuring history remain separate evidence needs.
A readable official copy of \emph{Kuhn}, C-308/17, is now retained. Its
jurisdiction ruling concerns a statutory collective-action-clause exchange;
it does not establish the treatment of these English-law Notes.

The source is
\path{docs/prospectus/jurisdiction-2026-10-04/sources/jur-hellenic-2008-candidate/source.pdf}.

\subsection{Judgments, notices and the limits of their holdings}

The official \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-c498-judgment/source.html}
{2024 \emph{Novo Banco} judgment, C-498/22 to C-500/22}
records a bond retransfer and nonpayment (paras.~58--63), and addresses
recognition of measures adopted before proceedings (paras.~89--97).
The \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-novo2021-judgment/source.html}
{2021 judgment, C-504/19}
concerns a share-sale liability changed during pending proceedings
(paras.~49, 59--66).
\href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-novo-generated/source.pdf}
{\emph{Goldman Sachs v Novo Banco}, [2018] UKSC 34}
concerns the Oak loan and recognition of a determination that its liability
had never transferred (paras.~21--34, PDF pp.~9--12).
These are three distinct disputes.

In \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-kotnik-judgment/source.html}
{\emph{Kotnik}, C-526/14, ECLI:EU:C:2016:570},
the Banking Communication cannot impose independent obligations on Member
States (para.~44). The burden-sharing discussion preserves qualifications,
including the no-creditor-worse-off principle (para.~77), and distinguishes
authority-adopted measures from private arrangements for the Directive
2001/24 classification (para.~110).
\href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-kuhn-judgment/source.html}
{\emph{Kuhn}, C-308/17, ECLI:EU:C:2018:911},
addresses the civil-and-commercial-matters jurisdiction boundary, not
the merits of a particular English-law bond's haircut (para.~44).

\href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-santander-judgment/source.html}
{\emph{Banco Santander}, C-410/20, ECLI:EU:C:2022:351},
addresses specified prospectus damages and nullity claims after a total
\emph{share} write-down (para.~51). Its discussion of compensation compares
resolution with normal insolvency; it does not guarantee the original
investment's value (paras.~48--50).
\href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-indel-judgment/source.html}
{\emph{Indėlių ir investicijų draudimas}, C-671/13, ECLI:EU:C:2015:418},
makes negotiability material to deposit-certificate exclusion
(paras.~38--40). Exclusion from deposit protection does not automatically
exclude investor protection, whose own conditions still apply
(paras.~47--52). In particular, article 2(2) of Directive 97/9 concerns
the inability to return money or instruments held in connection with
investment business. The judgment is not a general guarantee against
an issuer's credit default.

The retained \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-dana-judgment/source.pdf}
{Dana Gas judgment}, \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-lloyds-generated/source.pdf}
{Lloyds ECN judgment} and
\href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-ukraine-judgment/source.pdf}
{Ukraine judgment} supply the controls developed in the main text:
assumption versus finding, separate governing laws, regulatory redemption
versus conversion, majority versus dissent, and permission to defend versus
final discharge. Their original offering documents remain missing.

HETA's \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-heta-2232299/source.pdf}
{November 2014 rename notice}, \href{../prospectus/corner-cases-2026-10-04/sources/jur-more-heta-2356087/source.pdf}
