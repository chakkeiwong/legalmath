\subsection{What the executed repairs establish}
\label{sec:prospectus-executed}

The ordered repairs of 6--7 October 2026 connect source intake, contract
construction, interpretation, law and financial calculations to the installed
application. They correct demonstrated software errors, including a legal
evaluator that ignored an additional necessary premise and a BASF bank request
that inherited a UBS instrument classification. The complete interpretation of
the BASF contract remains unfinished. This distinction matters: the application
now consumes the intervening work, so the next construction and interpretation
repairs can change its report.

The earlier execution passed 50 development tests but left 18 adversarial
findings. Its ten BASF edits left 163 bracket branches and an unmatched opening
bracket. Those receipts remain preserved as historical observations. The repaired
service uses versioned products with exact parent identities. A changed selected
contract invalidates its interpretation; separately admitted legal facts and
financial scenarios replace their corresponding results. The installed command
rejects corrupted products from each of the five upstream stages.

Three paired checks establish actual dataflow. Making a fully interpreted
synthetic repayment clause unresolved changes Q1 from no to unknown. Changing
a necessary synthetic legal premise from true to false changes Q4 from yes to
no, without rebuilding the source inventory. In the Deutsche hypothetical
write-down calculation, increasing the required loss from 50 to 60 with the
same principal pool changes the note's allocated loss from 25 to 30.
Installed and worktree reports agree exactly in these checks. They test software
consumption and arithmetic, not an independent reading of a prospectus.

The legal evaluator now executes the supplied Boolean expression over exact
source occurrences. Each fact records its instrument, jurisdiction, validity
interval, knowledge interval and observation date. Missing facts remain unknown;
contradictory active observations remain conflicts. Necessary false premises
cannot disappear behind a fixed checklist. The clause evaluator likewise
preserves repeated and multiline occurrences, executes supplied conditions and
exceptions, and distinguishes compulsory common conversion, possible common
shares and common shares as the sole conversion outcome. Conditional repayment
no longer supplies unconditional evidence of absence.

Source intake retains the same four BASF documents, 519 pages, 43,626 text units
and 489 detected references. The supplement's date is recovered from its first
page, and the annual report's publication date from page 296. Existing OCR
supplies word geometry for four further retained documents; profile pages from
Deutsche, BBVA and SEB preserve their native word boxes. Corrected OCR
transcriptions remain separate from raw coordinates until their character
mapping is reviewed. These inventories support inspection; they do not establish
reading order or meaning.

Visual inspection of BASF's printed page 112 confirms that the long-coupon
alternative lacks an outer closing bracket. Its marginal instruction and the
next definition delimit the unselected branch. The repair excludes that branch
under the final terms' annual, no-stub selection without inventing punctuation.
The same margin says the reference-period definition applies to every
Actual/Actual option. It therefore survives, while its inapplicable short- and
long-coupon additions are removed. Treating an unchecked optional field as
authority to delete that common definition would be wrong.

Further selected branches retain their contractual wording after removal of
the selection delimiters. Every copied character and substituted value has a
recorded origin. There are now 83 unresolved brackets and no unmatched delimiter
in the retained candidate. The smaller count measures construction progress;
it cannot prove completeness. Clause numbering, other fields, marginal
attachments, incorporated agreements and the annual-report range disagreement
still require work. The supplement really prints both the overall range
195--209 and the detailed notes range 209--290; neither may be silently changed.

The production law stage also executes 25 retained development scenarios with
checked source quotations, and the financial stage executes the three named
Deutsche, BBVA and SEB subcalculations. Under their declared hypothetical
premises, the results include a Deutsche loss allocation of 25, a BBVA conversion
price of EUR5.01 and three shares, and a SEB price of USD3 and three shares.
Allocation now groups by an explicit legal holder identifier rather than a
display name. These calculations do not establish actual exercise, complete
settlement or the correctness of every translated legal premise. Corpus
availability dates also do not establish when a statute or judgment applied.

Q6 now uses a sourced senior-bond classification and an explicit mapping between
the issue-date contract question and a hypothetical current purchase
investigation. All 14 bank obligations are compared by their actual catalogue
records, rather than their number alone. Client, entity, capacity and policy
facts remain unresolved, and the report grants no transaction permission.

The broader deterministic regression run passed 983 tests. Subsequent focused
checks include the source-specific BASF correction. Passing these tests and
reusing verified receipts establish the recorded engineering behavior; the
automatic grammar remains a bounded English implementation. The general
construction representation supports text, sequences, choices, fields,
references and dated overrides, but it has not replaced every BASF-specific
operation or completed the other agreement sets.

Occurrence coverage, partial Boolean completion, temporal admission and exact
holder allocation have conditional correctness arguments for the supplied
representations. Their production implementations now have regression evidence.
The arguments still require faithful translation of the source contract and
complete dependencies; neither requirement follows from passing these tests.

Independent readers have not supplied an unexposed cohort, adjudicated labels
or accepted sign-offs. P7 consequently records no independent evaluation, and
P8 blocks legal release. The remaining program includes substantial engineering:
complete and review the selected contracts, attach governing exceptions and
indirect references, evaluate the same source through reviewed and automatic
interpretation, and supply the contractual conventions needed for settlement.
Actual dated law, event and client facts must then support the particular
application. The new receipts, regression records and unresolved obligations
are preserved under \path{docs/implementation/prospectus-repair-2026-10-06/}.
