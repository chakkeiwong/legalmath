\section{Assurance through methods with different opportunities to fail}
\label{sec:diverse-assurance-round11}

The bank needs a reason to trust each transformation between a circular and a
client decision. Consider the gift provision in circular 23EC46. A distributor
may read a prohibition on gifts, encode it as a Boolean condition and deliver a
Java class that implements that condition exactly. If the source packet omitted
the qualification concerning discounts of fees or charges, every subsequent
step could agree while the control remained wrong relative to the selected
passage. The converse mistake matters too: a broad interpretation of the
discount qualification could exempt something whose classification has not been
established. Accuracy therefore depends on discovering missing qualifications,
challenging factual definitions and checking executable behaviour separately.

There are at least three competing proposals worth distinguishing in this
example. One prohibits any classified gift linked to promotion; a second excludes
classified fee discounts but considers only a specific product; a third also
considers a particular type of investment product. The first can miss the
exception; the second can miss the wider product-type route; the third can still
misclassify a voucher as a discount. These are illustrative formal proposals,
not findings that all three are defensible readings of the retained circular.
Their purpose is to expose where evidence is needed. The exact source passage,
the referenced Code edition and the relevant FAQ must remain attached to the
question. A solver cannot supply an absent authority or establish the historical
applicability of a later FAQ revision.

The additional assurance layer makes these opportunities for error visible. It
requires methods that observe different parts of the problem: an image-based
source reading, a structural text reading, alternative interpretation search,
an argument solver, a second symbolic solver, and executable fault challenges.
Each method has a precise responsibility. Failure of one method does not erase
the work of another, but it prevents the affected claim from receiving a stronger
status than its evidence supports.

\subsection{Why independent executions need not mean independent errors}

Separate implementations are useful because they can expose different mistakes.
They do not justify multiplying error probabilities without evidence.
\citet{knight1986diversity} studied independently developed programs and observed
joint failures inconsistent with a simple independence assumption. The experiment
concerned its particular programming task; it does not estimate a regulatory
interpretation error rate. Its lesson for this product is methodological:
separate development is a design choice whose effect must be measured.

\citet[section 4.1]{littlewood2001diversity} explain one reason for correlated
failure. Let $X$ denote a randomly encountered input and let $\theta(X)$ be the
probability that an independently sampled implementation fails on that input.
Assume, within this illustrative model, that the two implementations fail
independently conditional on the particular input. Across inputs of differing
difficulty, the probability of joint failure becomes
\begin{equation}
 \Pr(F_1\cap F_2)
 = \mathbb{E}[\Pr(F_1\cap F_2\mid X)]
 = \mathbb{E}[\theta(X)^2]
 = \mathbb{E}[\theta(X)]^2+\operatorname{Var}[\theta(X)].
 \label{eq:round11-shared-difficulty}
\end{equation}
The last equality follows from the definition of variance. Both programs are
more likely to fail on the same difficult input, so the joint probability exceeds
the product of their marginal probabilities when difficulty varies. A distant
footnote or an ambiguous actor definition is a plausible shared difficulty in
our setting. The equation is an explanation of a mechanism, not a fitted model
of LegalMath. We have not measured its parameters.

The language-model study by \citet{kim2025correlated} provides a related reason
to avoid counting provider labels as independent evidence. Their evaluation
examines agreement on incorrect answers in specified benchmark tasks, including
cross-provider comparisons. Those tasks are not Hong Kong regulatory
interpretation. We borrow the need to examine shared errors, and retain the
distinction between a benchmark result and a claim about this bank's controls.
If there are no observed errors in an evaluation cell, the shared-error fraction
has no denominator. The report should say that the quantity is not estimable,
not record zero correlation.

\subsection{The source must be checked before interpretations are compared}

An interpretation ensemble can inspect only the material it receives. The source
stage therefore retains the original bytes, their hash, the source location,
the selected edition and a page inventory. The new execution round uses the
seven-page and three-page annexes retained for 23EC35 and the two-page appendix
retained for 23EC49. These twelve pages are a bounded public-source integration
corpus. Scanned exceptions and table footnotes are tested separately with
explicitly synthetic fixtures; we do not assert that the retained circulars
contain every difficult layout pattern.

The first extraction route uses the existing PDF text layer. ResearchAssistant
supplies a second orchestration route with parser timeouts, error diagnostics and
explicit partial-extraction states. Its available extractor is Poppler's
\texttt{pdftotext}. Calling Poppler directly and through ResearchAssistant checks
configuration and orchestration, but it is one extraction family. A count of
three command invocations must not turn it into three independent readers.
ResearchAssistant's reconciliation confidence is a diagnostic, not a legal
reference, and missing equations or citations remain missing.

Docling adds a structured representation of document text, layout and provenance
\citep{doclingsoftware}. The adapter preserves its document JSON, text-item page
references and bounding boxes where present. A Markdown export alone would lose
the connection needed to investigate a footnote or table cell. The selected
CPU configuration and model files are identified in the tool manifest. Features
such as table reconstruction or picture description that are disabled in the
pilot remain explicit limitations; their absence cannot certify a table's
semantics.

Optical character recognition (OCR) converts the visible letters in a page image
into machine-readable text. It supplies a different observation of a PDF from
reading its embedded text layer, which can omit words visible to a person.

The OCR route renders every retained page, then invokes Tesseract directly
\citep{tesseractsoftware}. It saves the raster hash, recognised text and TSV word
coordinates and confidence values. This route observes visible pixels rather
than relying on the PDF's hidden text layer. It can consequently expose a scanned
qualification omitted by text extraction. It can also confuse digits, negation,
superscripts and punctuation. Word confidence is used to locate uncertain
regions, never to authorise a control. Two OCR routes that both use Tesseract
must identify that shared backend.

Reconciliation operates page by page. It checks page coverage, empty pages,
material token differences and exact text differences after whitespace handling.
Negation, numbers, dates, punctuation and order are not silently removed to make
the routes agree. A token comparison may help locate an omission but cannot
establish that reordered words have the same meaning. Raw route text remains
available beside the finding. A missing route, missing page or extraction error
opens an unresolved source issue. A low discrepancy score cannot close it.

Suppose a synthetic page contains an image of the words ``other than a discount
of fees or charges'', below otherwise machine-readable text. The text routes
agree because they see the same incomplete text layer. The OCR route supplies
additional words. The controller links this difference to the page and requests
a second rendering or OCR configuration within its declared action budget. If
the result persists, the report retains the qualification as disputed source
material. It does not discard the minority route. This is a useful positive
result even when the controller cannot settle which extraction is correct.

\subsection{Alternative models and the scope of formal checking}

The existing interpretation engine already generates multiple candidates,
preserves dimensions of disagreement and uses bounded search to investigate
counterexamples. This round adds independent checking mechanisms around that
engine. It does not replace search with a vote. Initial source-based candidates
remain separate from fault mutants and from a backend reconstruction of an
already chosen formula. These three kinds of object answer different questions:
what the source might mean, whether a checker catches a deliberately changed
rule, and whether a translation preserved its input.

For a small Boolean illustration, let $G$ mean that the offered benefit is a
gift, $D$ that the fee-discount qualification applies, $S$ that promotion concerns
a specific product, and $T$ that promotion concerns a particular product type.
An illustrative prohibition condition is $G\land\neg D\land(S\lor T)$. A
candidate using only $S$ differs when $G$ and $T$ are true while $D$ and $S$ are
false. This witness identifies exactly which factual question matters. It does
not establish that the supplied definitions of $G,D,S,T$ are legally justified.

cvc5 supplies a separately implemented SMT solver alongside Z3
\citep{cvc5software}. The adapter accepts an explicit supported expression
fragment and enumerated factual domains. It asks whether two expressions differ
at any permitted assignment, and saves a concrete assignment when they do.
Unsupported expressions, inconsistent domains, timeout and solver unknown have
different statuses. None means equivalence. The first adapter covers Boolean
conditions with explicit unknown handling and bounded integer threshold checks;
the full RuleIR arithmetic, dates and host data mapping remain covered only by
their separately declared checks until additional adapters are written.

The two solver encoders must be inspected separately. If both receive a shared
translation that accidentally drops $T$, agreement exposes no such error.
Accordingly the formal record distinguishes solver identity, encoder identity,
fact definitions, supported domain and input formula hash. Exhaustive small
truth tables provide a third diagnostic for the bounded fixture. Python and Java
replay connect a solver witness to the executable control when that expression
belongs to the generated-code profile.

clingo supplies a different formal mechanism: answer-set programming
\citep{clingosoftware}. In the argumentation adapter, facts name arguments and
attacks, and the program chooses a possible extension. Constraints exclude a set
containing arguments that attack each other. A further condition requires every
argument outside a stable extension to be attacked by an argument inside it.
The solver enumerates all stable extensions within the declared finite bound.
The existing exhaustive Python graph evaluator provides the comparator.

Mutual attack illustrates why a single result is inadequate. For arguments $a$
and $b$ attacking each other, the stable extensions are $\{a\}$ and $\{b\}$.
If both conclude the same proposition $p$, that proposition is supported in every
extension even though neither particular argument appears in every extension.
Conclusion-level skeptical support therefore requires, for every extension,
some argument for $p$. Reversing those quantifiers would answer a different
question. For an odd attack cycle there may be no stable extension; the absence
of extensions is not universal acceptance. The adapter consequently requires a
nonempty, complete extension set before making its conclusion-level statement.
It preserves truncation and empty-set outcomes explicitly.

This finite graph interface is deliberately narrower than full ASPIC+ or legal
argument construction. It checks the supplied attack relation. It does not
establish the authority of a premise, infer a legally justified preference, or
decide whether two real cases are analogous. TweetyProject, a collection of
libraries for logical reasoning and argumentation, and s(CASP) remain
possible extensions once their premise, preference and negation semantics are
mapped to a specific product question. Adding every library before specifying
that question would increase integration work without a corresponding claim.

\subsection{Evaluation and faults in delivered Java}

Inspect AI is an evaluation host with a scorer interface
\citep{inspectsoftware}. Its role here is to execute frozen challenge cases and
retain target, response and score records. The first integration uses deterministic
responders, so it tests the evaluation plumbing and reference separation without
spending live model allowance. The responder receives the public case while the
target is kept in the scorer. A shared wrong answer must fail against the target
even if every responder supplies it. A missing answer remains missing. This
does not estimate a live language model's accuracy or justify selecting a model.

PIT mutates Java bytecode to challenge tests \citep{pitsoftware}. A threshold can
change from greater-than-or-equal to greater-than; a branch can be negated; or a
Boolean result can change. The selected pilot control and its tests are compiled
with the retained Java 17 toolchain. The first run establishes that the baseline
tests actually pass. PIT then reports which mutants the tests kill and which
survive. The report preserves mutator identity, source line, test coverage and
status. A surviving material mutant prevents acceptance of that test claim.

The PIT pilot is not a whole-application Java proof. Existing LegalMath tests
already compile generated classes, replay symbolic witnesses, compare Python
and Java over specified states and challenge runtime outcomes. Those tests remain
mandatory and are run alongside the new pilot. In particular, a true prohibition
predicate must not become a generic permission flag in the host. Passing an
isolated Boolean test does not establish the output convention, fact freshness,
time-zone handling, decimal units or workflow composition of the bank system.

The proposed calibration work would use separately labelled examples to assess
the coverage of sets of possible answers. MAPIE is a software library for
constructing such prediction sets. Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} explains why
their coverage guarantees require assumptions about the reference labels and
the relationship between calibration and future examples.

At the time of this assurance round, the Catala experiments were in a separate
worktree. Their separately authored examples examined specification choices,
while a generated adapter checked translation from a common input. The completed
Catala campaign has since been merged into main; Chapter~\ref{ch:implementation}
describes its shared frontend and supported targets. This earlier round retains
its original tool identities and does not claim to have tested the later
translator. KeY proofs of selected Java/JML obligations and MAPIE calibration
are later work: the former needs specific specifications and closed proof
obligations; the latter needs an appropriate separately labelled calibration
corpus. Neither tool is useful as an empty completion checkbox.

\subsection{A common result and a bounded discrepancy investigation}

Each assurance result names its source and claim, method family, implementation
and tool versions, shared dependencies, input hashes, supported scope, completion
status and evidence location. Its finding is supported, contradicted or
unresolved relative to that stated claim. The controller also records whether
the method actually ran. An unavailable executable is not a negative finding
about the law; it is missing evidence. A complete execution with a discrepancy
is successful detection, but the affected semantic claim remains unresolved.

The controller opens one issue for each material difference or unavailable
required check and records the action before dispatch. A source issue may request
a second OCR configuration; a formula difference requests a replayable witness;
an argument difference requests complete extension comparison; a Java fault
requests a narrower regression case. Each action has an immutable identifier,
input hash, timeout and outcome. Repeated attempts do not increase a confidence
score. The budget limits investigation work; it cannot lower acceptance criteria.

Repairs fall into two classes. A mechanical repair, such as repeating a page
extraction with a declared OCR segmentation mode or regenerating a stale plan,
can execute automatically. A semantic revision, such as changing what counts as
a fee discount, needs new source-backed evidence and must return through the
interpretation process. The controller never edits the rule merely to make
backends agree. Exhausted investigation produces an explicit uncertainty report
containing the remaining alternatives, consequences, missing evidence and the
next discriminating question.

A crash is also an outcome. Issued actions remain consumed, partial evidence
remains visible, and recovery cannot reset the budget. Evidence from a previous
source or formula version cannot close the current issue. The implementation
therefore binds decisions and plans to input hashes and verifies predecessor
evidence before continuing. Tests must deliberately change a source, truncate
an extension list, remove a required route, interrupt a subprocess and exhaust
the final action to ensure these protections actually execute.

\subsection{The executable implementation round}

The round-11 master program starts with this documentation phase. It then installs
the selected tools into a separate CPU environment and a local OCR directory,
records exact versions, and runs small import and fixture checks. It next runs
the source routes on all twelve retained pages, tests alternative-model and
symbolic mechanisms, evaluates the deterministic Inspect cases, executes the
PIT pilot and runs the existing regression suite. The final phase joins evidence
without upgrading the scope of any component result.

Before each phase, the program writes a plan containing current implementation
hashes, predecessor outcomes, acceptance conditions and unresolved findings. A
failed phase produces a repair request and retains its attempt directory. A
retry requires a recorded executed repair and regression result. Successful
completion refreshes the successor plan automatically. Fixed subprocess argument
vectors and a narrowly scoped installation approval prevent model-generated
commands from changing the toolchain or invoking arbitrary services.

There are two final decisions. Engineering completion asks whether the declared
tools ran, failure controls behaved as specified and required evidence was
preserved. The interpretation decision asks whether the particular legal question
has sufficient source-backed support. The first can pass while the second remains
unresolved. This distinction makes a useful product possible: the bank can obtain
reproducible evidence and sharply stated residual questions without purchasing a
false guarantee that every future circular has only one correct machine reading.

The next empirical study must measure missed material conditions, unsafe
acceptance, correctly accepted coverage and residual review effort on distinct
source families. Artificial faults help verify detection mechanisms but cannot
estimate the prevalence of legal mistakes. The current round is intended to make
that study executable and its evidence harder to misinterpret. Its results are
reported in the round-11 execution record, with tool identities, retained failures
and the exact next-phase proposal.
