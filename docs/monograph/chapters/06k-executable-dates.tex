\section{Checking executable quotations and calendar comparisons}
\label{sec:executable-dates}

The processing repairs leave two precise engineering questions. Can an
interpretation proposal point to the exact executable expression it discusses?
Can the date comparisons in the retained UCITS proposals receive a formal
preservation check? Both questions can be addressed without making a model's
legal judgment an answer key. They require different evidence: one concerns
the identity of a quotation, the other a declared mathematical meaning.

\subsection{A quotation must identify the actual expression}

A source quotation and a program quotation have different subjects. The first
identifies words in a circular. The second identifies the expression proposed
to implement a reading of those words. A model may quote the right circular
while describing a formula that was never encoded, or quote an older formula
after its exception changed. Neither quotation supports a claim about the
current executable expression.

The first optional executable-reference protocol, version~1, binds each
selectable passage to the complete request, candidate reading, exact question
and representation. It provides separate references for the scope expression,
result expression and complete representation. That first design leaves a
subtle problem: the representation also contains freely written fact
descriptions. A description containing a line headed \texttt{Scope:} can look
like the start of a second program expression to a text-matching routine.
Accurately quoting that description would still fail to identify the proposed
program. Likewise, a hash supplied alongside a representation cannot establish
that the representation came from the retained reading. Both can be changed
together.

Version~2 therefore starts with the reading and question retained by the
investigation, independently of the request being checked. It parses the
reading's scope and result, renders those two expressions using the existing
controlled language, and locates their program block after the fact
descriptions. Only these expressions receive selectable references. The entire
representation remains visible so that fact meanings and qualifications are
available for reasoning, but explanatory prose does not become executable
evidence merely by resembling a heading. The transmitted reading hash,
question and representation must match the retained originals before a model
is called and again when its response is resolved.

Each reference records its expression kind, the reading and question hashes,
the formalization and representation hashes, and the exact Unicode character
positions of the quoted expression. The model selects the identifier;
deterministic software retrieves the original text. Changing the request,
reading, question or expression invalidates the old selections, even when a
short expression looks identical. An expression owned by another candidate
cannot be borrowed. The parser and renderer remain software assumptions in
this transport check; the separate preservation proofs address their stated
formal boundaries.

Exact identity includes the type of each recorded value. An integer character
offset of zero and the Boolean value false are different JSON values, although
Python's ordinary equality treats them as equal. A reference-table check based
on that equality can accept altered metadata while returning the same quotation.
The source and version~2 executable resolvers therefore compare canonical
serializations, which preserve this distinction and reject floating-point
offsets. The regression challenges these substitutions before any text is
selected. This check protects the evidence record; it adds no support to the
interpretation attached to that evidence.

An unencoded reading has no executable references. Its correspondence must
remain unassessed, or be declared inapplicable to the question with the
separately required justification. Software rejects a preserving, conflicting
or omitting executable judgment for such a reading. It does not repair a
response by relabelling its substantive judgment. After references are
resolved, the original source, question and correspondence checks still apply.
Exact quotation establishes what was discussed, not whether the discussion
is right.

The investigation uses source references while extracting claims, proposing
readings and defining questions. It adds executable references at the later
correspondence stage, when both a particular reading and its question exist.
This routing is part of the recorded method. The resolver changes quotation
identifiers into text; it preserves every status, rationale, follow-up question
and additional concern. The existing semantic validator then checks source
quotations, question identity and compatibility between the proposed judgments.
For example, an exact result quotation still cannot support a preserving
judgment when the same response says source support is unestablished.

A rejected response retains its original batch, perspective and reservation.
The next bounded attempt receives the diagnostic and prior-round evidence;
the two perspectives in one round receive the same earlier evidence. Failure
at the attempt limit leaves the required pairs unresolved. Version~1 records
remain historical records rather than being silently converted into successful
version~2 observations. Replaying a response under the new selection rules is
possible only where its original quotation names an available expression
exactly. A longer prose quotation or a malformed paraphrase is reported as
unselectable, not replaced with evidence the model never supplied.

\subsection{What a calendar comparison means}

The retained UCITS readings use a date to delimit a proposed regulatory
condition. The legal selection of that date remains a separate question,
but its mathematical comparison can be specified independently. We use valid
dates in the proleptic Gregorian calendar, from year 1 through year 9999.
``Proleptic'' means applying the same calendar rule throughout this range,
without reconstructing historical national changes of calendar. The accepted
spelling is the fixed-width form \texttt{YYYY-MM-DD}.

Let $y$, $m$ and $d$ be a valid year, month and day. A Gregorian leap year is
divisible by four, except for a year divisible by one hundred unless it is
also divisible by four hundred. Thus 1900 has 365 days and 2000 has 366.
The number of days before the start of year $y$ is
\[
 Y(y)=365(y-1)
       +\left\lfloor\frac{y-1}{4}\right\rfloor
       -\left\lfloor\frac{y-1}{100}\right\rfloor
       +\left\lfloor\frac{y-1}{400}\right\rfloor.
\]
The first term counts ordinary days in preceding years. The remaining terms
add four-year leap days, remove century exceptions and restore four-hundred-year
exceptions. Let $M(y,m)$ count the days in the completed months of year $y$;
it is zero in January and includes 29 February after February in a leap year.
The zero-based ordinal of the date is
\[
 O(y,m,d)=Y(y)+M(y,m)+d-1.
\]
Consequently, 1 January in year 1 has ordinal zero. Moving to the next valid
day increases the ordinal by one, including across month and year boundaries.

The checked proposition equates strict comparison of these ordinals with
lexicographic comparison of valid year, month and day triples. The proof first
checks that successive year starts differ by the correct 365 or 366 days.
Month lengths and starting offsets put every valid day inside its year, with
each month ending before the next begins. Dates in different years are
therefore ordered by their year starts; dates within one year are ordered by
their months and then days. The integer rank $372y+31(m-1)+d$ has the same
order: it reserves 31 positions for each month and 372 for each year, enough
to prevent overlap between components. Those deliberately padded positions
express component ordering; they are not elapsed-day counts.

The independent formal-expression parser now accepts canonical date literals
and date facts in supported comparison expressions. It reconstructs the same
constructor tree as the shared model, and Lean checks that equality. The
calendar theorem supplies a declared meaning for date order; the constructor
theorem checks preservation of the expression using it. Neither theorem
chooses the date intended by a legal provision. Evidence timestamps,
business-day calendars and date-arithmetic library functions remain separate.

The implementation also checks the representation boundary. Java compares
canonical date strings, while the Catala adapter uses a day count relative to
1970. Subtracting the same epoch from two ordinals preserves their comparison.
An independent calendar implementation is compared exhaustively with accepted
canonical spellings and the production Python date codec throughout the
declared range. Native challenges exercise equality, inclusive and strict
comparison, leap days, century exceptions and both endpoints through the
actual RuleIR/Java and Catala routes. These are checks of the codecs and
selected runtime cases. The Lean theorem does not prove the Python libraries,
string comparator, Java compiler or Catala compiler.

\subsection{Stronger formal evidence with legal questions retained}

All eight encoded UCITS readings received constructor-preservation certificates.
Their sources, readings and questions are unchanged; the ninth remains
unencoded. The replay supplies no invented dates. Pending-application,
incorporated-authority and legal-date questions remain unresolved.

A later audit reproduced the three Gregorian propositions, all 3,652,059 valid
dates in the declared range and preservation evidence for the eight encoded
readings. Its 49 known-date cases ran on each target, giving 98 native
executions. Eight additional requests with unknown or conflicting inputs
abstained at the shared input boundary before either target executed. These
requests check the common boundary and cannot count as independent native
results. Their behavior also supplies no theorem for arithmetic on unknown or
conflicting dates.

Independent Boolean relations also check the HKMA calculations of
Section~\ref{sec:joined-eligibility} over their full Boolean domains. Omitting
each broker-exception condition produces a counterexample. Both backends
execute the proposals and abstain on incomplete or conflicting input. The
earlier eligibility increment passed 338 affected tests and completed 234 product
and date executions. Every real-document report retains missing bank and legal premises.

These results close specific software and mathematical gaps. Program agreement
does not establish legal correctness. Theorems cannot supply private bank
mandates or account records. Future rules require new observations under a
frozen method, before further repairs. Development cases remain development
evidence; future failures and qualifications must be preserved.

\subsection{Continuing an investigation without erasing its failures}
\label{sec:continued-investigation}

An additional computing allowance can help investigate an unresolved reading,
but it changes neither the source nor the evidence already obtained. The UCITS
investigation illustrates the distinction. Its earlier ensemble had reserved
92 requests under a 120-request task limit. A later allowance of 500 requests
therefore left 28 requests available for that original task. Failed requests
were already included in the 92. Counting only successful answers would make
an unreliable method appear less expensive and give it more opportunities to
produce a convenient result.

The software keeps the old allowance unchanged and records the additional
grant separately. Before dispatch, it checks the original task balance and the
history of the particular source claim, proposed program, question and reader
perspective. Repacking the same question into a smaller batch does not create
a new issue. Nor does adding an executable-reference identifier: that identifier
helps locate evidence but contributes no new substantive question. In contrast,
an amended source must be retained as a new edition whose relationship to the
old reading is investigated explicitly.

Time limits also belong to the original issue. During the continuation, one
pair's deadline expired after the initial eligibility inventory but before
dispatch. The guard stopped the request before charging a model call. This
case requires checking eligibility immediately before dispatch as well as
during planning. The excluded pair remains in the report. It cannot disappear
from the denominator merely because the other pairs can still be processed.

The first live version~2 batch supplied two validated proposals for eleven
pairs; one pair was excluded by that deadline. The two responses retained an
unresolved correspondence assessment: one of the eleven pairs concerned a
reading that had no executable formalization.
These are distinct observations: a response can obey the evidence protocol,
two perspectives can both be present, and the substantive question can remain
unresolved. The same model supplied both fresh contexts, so these observations
do not measure independence or accuracy. The subsequent execution record keeps
the pair counts, disagreements, invalid responses and expenditure separately.

The broader continuation supplied both perspectives for another 47 pairs.
Together, these runs added 58 pairs with two validated responses to the earlier
165, leaving two of the original 225 pairs outside that coverage because their
original deadlines had expired. Among the 58 newly processed pairs, 42 had all
four dimensions assessed by both perspectives. Twenty unresolved or disputed
pairs remained in these new results, including four with differing dimension
judgments. An unassessed dimension and a disagreement are different reasons
for retaining a question. The earlier 42 disputes remain in their original
record; the new totals do not erase them.

A later follow-up lost its upstream connection. Its reservation remained
spent, bringing the continuation to 17 requests and the original task to
109 of its 120-request allowance. Concurrent work also changed the shared
package while this run was active, so the controller refused to certify a
stable method identity for the run. Every returned response was subsequently
rechecked against its unchanged source, reading and question without another
model request. That rechecking establishes the retained reference and response
checks, not a retrospective method freeze. Later phases execute copied source
trees so that work on another module cannot silently change their method.

\subsection{When a correct transport still cannot finish the task}

Exact reconstruction of a compressed request answers a software question:
did the model receive the intended material? It does not answer whether the
model can return the required result within the available time. The
authentication-source diagnostic made this distinction concrete. Its input
contained all 263 source units and 263 deliberately unresolved synthetic
concerns. Both fresh attempts to return a disposition for every concern hit
the five-minute deadline, although the request itself fitted the byte limit.
The concerns were constructed to test accounting; their existence was not
evidence of 263 defects in the circular.

The repair partitions the requested answer while retaining the whole input.
Four groups contain 66, 66, 66 and 65 concerns. Every request still contains
the entire source, the interpretation, all questions and all concerns, so an
exception outside the requested group remains available to the model. Each
answer must account for its selected concerns exactly once and retain the
original questions. The joining check rejects repeated or foreign concerns,
lists missing groups and retains differing question dispositions or proposed
revisions. Accounting for every identifier does not prove that cross-group
legal interactions were understood; that remains an explicit qualification.

The first smaller answers exposed a separate error. The response format
required a cryptographic hash for each question, but the request supplied no
such mapping. A model with no calculation tools could copy a supplied hash
but could not reliably compute the required digest from the question's JSON.
One response substituted zeros. The validator correctly rejected it. Earlier
synthetic tests had calculated the hashes inside their test provider, concealing
the missing input that the actual model faced.

The corrected transport calculates each question's digest before dispatch and
supplies the mapping explicitly. The output schema permits only these supplied
hashes, and the validator still checks which question owns each one. Supplying
this deterministic bookkeeping value introduces no legal answer key: the
question's status, rationale, evidence and uncertainty remain the model's
proposal. The changed request contract also changes the cache identity, so an
old result cannot silently stand for execution of the repaired request.

Both full-output timeouts and both smaller invalid responses remain in the
record. The first 66 concerns have consumed two attempts per perspective;
the repair has only their remaining third attempt. Later groups retain the
attempt spent on the original full response. A failure stops further expansion
of that perspective and leaves every missing concern visible. This example
shows why a repair must address the observed cause while preserving the
history that made the cause visible.

The final bounded run returned eight validated responses, one per group and
perspective. Each perspective accounted for all 263 concerns; the joining
check found no differing question statuses between that perspective's batches
and no proposed replacement interpretation. The twelve-call diagnostic budget
includes the earlier four failed requests. The result demonstrates complete
response accounting for these synthetic concerns under the repaired input
contract. It supplies neither a legal accuracy estimate nor evidence that
the two perspectives are independent.

\subsection{New source evidence changes a particular premise}

The earlier tokenised-securities experiment in
Section~\ref{sec:invariant-decisions} received circular 23EC52 without its
separate appendix. A later source revision joined the retained circular and
the acquired three-page appendix into a 177-unit packet. The revision preserves
the predecessor's six authority questions and its extraction findings. It does
not copy an earlier model judgment into the enlarged context and call the
question settled.

Both fresh first-round proposals distinguished the newly available appendix
from legislation still absent from the packet. They proposed that the appendix
references could now be inspected directly, while complete legislative
provisions, exemptions, relevant editions and transaction applicability remained
missing premises. Their precise explanations and alternative readings were
retained alongside exact source selections. These are proposed answers from
the configured model, rather than a finding that the English meaning has been
proved. The source revision closes an availability gap within this packet;
it does not close all the legal questions that motivated retrieval.

The first follow-up could not be sent within the unchanged 200,000-byte bound.
It contained both earlier proposals as well as the entire revised source.
Lossless table encoding reduced that exact request from 209,707 to 190,946
bytes and reconstructed it without a change. Subsequent processing retains
the original role spending and deadline. If later proposals make the context
too large again, the system reports that limit; it does not discard an
inconvenient reading to make the request fit. Neither successful retrieval nor
successful compression supplies the still-missing legislative premise.

The encoded follow-up returned another proposal from each perspective, leaving
four retained proposals in total. All four reported partial source support and
missing premises. The next round exceeded the byte limit again as the retained
proposal history grew; both requests stopped before a model call. The source
question therefore remains qualified with an explicit processing limit. It
does not become resolved merely because both perspectives named similar gaps.

\subsection{A future observation belongs to the whole interpretation method}
\label{sec:whole-method-observation}

Consider two programs that produce the same formal expression. One receives
the full appendix; the other receives a text extraction that omits an exception.
A proof checker may accept both instances of that expression. Identifying only
the checker and expression would conceal the difference that matters most to
the legal question. A prospective observation must identify how the source
became an interpretation, as well as how the interpretation became Java.

The full method therefore records the extraction software and model locks,
the configured model route, generation and search settings, the mechanisms
for proposing questions and investigating authority, the source and executable
reference protocols, the issue limits, and the qualification software and
toolchains. The dispatcher derives this record from its actual configuration
before beginning an investigation. Each completed investigation carries it
alongside the raw source editions, selected question and individual stage
records. A later verifier compares those records with the frozen method and
rechecks the qualification attached to every retained candidate.

This comparison serves a specific purpose. A changed extractor, search budget
or reference protocol makes the result evidence about a changed method.
Missing authority processing cannot be concealed by a successful Java build.
A different source edition or question cannot borrow another investigation's
qualification. Every claim--candidate pair remains accounted for, and an
unencoded candidate remains visible. A missing stage or failed verification
leaves the submitted task pending or explicitly incomplete; it does not become
a successful observation through a summary label.

Selection precedes observation. The study freezes its method, source families,
publication window and development-source identities. It retains every
submitted task, including an ineligible submission or an unfinished
investigation. The raw primary document identifies the source even when
additional background documents are attached. The retained stage times must
follow admission into the study; a previously completed run cannot count as
a new observation simply because its hashes still match. If observations
motivate a repair, that window becomes
development evidence. The revised method needs a later window whose sources
were not used to make the repair. An older window that bound only the final
qualification checker retains that narrower meaning; it cannot be relabelled
as a study of the entire interpreter.

A first encounter after the frozen window closes remains outside that study,
even if the document was published earlier. Renaming a task does not turn the
same source and question into another eligible observation. Different
questions about one circular may still be investigated, so the report also
counts distinct primary sources. Neither count establishes independence of
legal questions or of circulars issued under the same regulatory regime.

The resulting record can establish that a stated method processed particular
retained inputs and that its mathematical and executable qualifications passed
their registered checks. Locally retained hashes do not authenticate remote
model execution or a fixed remote model revision, establish the publisher's
clock, or prove that every eligible
publication was submitted. Binding dependency lock records also does not
attest the installed contents of every third-party package. Those limits
accompany the study report. Most
fundamentally, an unchanged method and checked program do not establish that
the legal premise was correctly understood. The value of the record is that
future failures remain attributable and challengeable, while the propositions
actually proved keep their precise mathematical scope.
