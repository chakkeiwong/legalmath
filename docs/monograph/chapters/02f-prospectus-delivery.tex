\section{From selected terms to a qualified prospectus answer}
\label{sec:prospectus-delivery}

Outstanding principal falls when an issuer repays a bond, when the contract
extinguishes an unpaid claim, and when an authority imposes a write-down.
These events have different legal and financial meanings. A reader that notices
only the reduction can give the same answer for all three. Interpretation must
preserve the selected obligation, its conditions and exceptions, and the sources
that determine its effect.

The 6 October 2026 investigation traced the actual application. The ordinary
classification command still used the original reader. The repaired candidate
ran from a separately retained source tree; OCR and BASF admissions supplied
evidence through explicit consumers. Success in one path did not establish that
the ordinary application used the repair. Earlier completion notes recorded
bounded campaigns, not delivery of a general interpreter.

\subsection{Corrections established by the evidence}

Seven constructed examples separated interpretation from coverage. Both
preserved readers correctly handled explicit repayment and principal write-down.
Both returned a negative when principal obligations ceased permanently
\emph{without payment}, and when payment depended on an unavailable Condition 14.
Both returned a compulsory-conversion positive for an explicitly non-operative
example and for a conversion sentence governed by the holder's option in the
preceding sentence. The candidate corrected the blank-operative-page case; the
ordinary reader still returned a negative. These are counterexamples to the
respective behaviours, not estimates of their frequency in prospectuses.

Conversion required after exercise of an option does not make exercise itself
compulsory. Interpretation must preserve the election owner and the earlier
sentence supplying its antecedent. Likewise, a repayment promise cannot establish
absence of principal loss while another operative condition remains unread.
Affirmative absence requires a complete scope for that question.

Two historical statements need dated qualifications. The empty BES scan was a
failure of the 4 October intake; later OCR supplied text and source-review
receipts. Legibility did not settle every incorporated document or the precedence
of duplicate risk factor 1.17. The subsequent BASF admission accounted for 55
checkboxes and eight bilingual responses. It expressly did not construct the
complete German contract. Neither result establishes complete interpretation.

\subsection{What the literature contributes}

ContractNLI requires supporting sentences and list items to be self-contained,
even when they occur in separate passages, and distinguishes local from distant
exceptions \cite[Sections 2--5 and Appendix A.1]{contractnli2021}. This supports
retaining a governing paragraph with its subordinate clauses. Its 607
nondisclosure agreements and 17 hypotheses are a different task from prospectus
analysis. Scans and multicolumn documents were excluded during collection.
The results therefore do not validate BES OCR or German marginal instructions,
and the label \emph{not mentioned} cannot prove contractual absence.

Holzenberger and Van Durme connect statutory reasoning to typed spans, events
and argument relations \cite[Sections 3--5 and Appendices B, D and E]{connecting2023}.
A calculation needs the actor, amount and date, not merely an event name.
Their empty-knowledge-base control shows why negation-as-failure can produce
apparently correct answers despite missing extraction. The prospectus service
retains missing premises explicitly and does not adopt their research
implementation's date-filling defaults as contractual dates.

LegalRuleML separates sources, interpretations, contexts and the authority
selecting between alternatives \cite[Sections 3--4]{legalinterpretations2014}.
Structured argumentation distinguishes attacks on premises, conclusions and
inferences \cite[Sections 3 and 6]{aspic2010}. These distinctions represent
conflicting language versions, duplicate provisions and incorporation ranges.
They do not decide which document governs. Priority needs a source-backed
interpretation.

Catala executes exceptions and conflicts after formalization
\cite[Sections 4.1, 4.4--4.5 and 6.1]{catala2021}. Its compiler correctness
argument concerns translation between formal languages; it does not prove a
faithful translation of the prospectus. The same boundary applies to
deterministic calculations and witness replay here. Repeating a recognizer
establishes reproducibility; independent reading tests interpretation.

Month addition can depend on rounding and operation order: two one-month
adjustments need not equal one two-month adjustment
\cite[Sections 2, 3.2 and 4.3]{dates2024}. Business-day movement answers a
different question from calendar-month rounding. Neither a library default
nor a guessed date establishes the contractual convention.
The recent \emph{Know Your Limits} preprint also separates translation from
solver execution and documents unsupported assumptions
\cite[Sections 3--5 and Appendix A]{limits2026}. Its retained version has
inconsistent dataset totals and reports manual program corrections; its
numerical rankings supply no adoption threshold here.

Technical sections and relevant appendices were inspected for all seven papers,
along with retained SARA implementation files, related PyArg code and selected
official Catala excerpts. These sources support explicit context, typed
arguments, alternatives, exceptions and time. None supplies a complete
prospectus engine or a transferable accuracy claim.

\subsection{Constructing the selected contract}

BASF SE's EUR500 million 4.25\% notes due 8 March 2032,
ISIN XS2595418596, select German Option I from the September 2022 base
prospectus. The operative text must be assembled from the main column, marginal
instructions, nested alternatives and completed fields. A checkbox selects an
alternative; it is not itself the operative clause.

Several selections must survive together. The scheduled holder put is excluded;
the change-of-control right retains its rating condition and exercise procedure.
The Finance-only initial guarantee is excluded for BASF SE, while the conditional
successor guarantee in \S10(d) remains relevant to substitution. The clean-up call
requires both acquisition of at least 75\% of original principal and the
corresponding reduction recorded in the global note. It concerns the whole
residual issue, with 30--60 days' notice and repayment at par plus accrued
interest. Removing every guarantee reference, every holder right or the second
clean-up-call condition would construct a different contract.

Every page is inventoried before semantic filtering. Text units retain source
hashes, page coordinates, reading order and language. Assembly records the source
and reason for each deletion and substitution and preserves unresolved
instructions. Continuous text remains a candidate until relevant selections and
references are resolved. BASF's annual-report incorporation exposes a remaining
disagreement: the broad heading ends at page 209, whereas the detailed notes
range ends at page 290. Both references must survive review.

Interpretation separates six questions. The first concerns contractual principal
loss and reports statutory disclosure separately. The second records conversion,
election owners, and possible common shares versus common shares as the sole
outcome. The third concerns question-specific completeness. The fourth combines
a dated legal basis with separately supplied jurisdiction, entity, instrument,
event and procedure facts. The fifth computes a supported conditional financial
quantity. The sixth retains the bank's existing obligations. A product feature
does not discharge those obligations or authorize a transaction.

Completeness is local to the question. An unread payment condition can defeat
a negative answer about principal loss; a missing financial metric need not
defeat a supported statement about a selected option. Excluding a dependency
requires a relevance reason. A positive also needs disposition of exceptions
and amendments that could defeat it. Consistent readings that change the answer
produce uncertainty; contradictory applicable assertions without resolved
priority remain a conflict.

\subsection{Delivery and acceptance}

The executable program freezes questions and baselines, constructs sources and
selected terms, interprets clauses, and joins separate law, fact and financial
paths before producing the application report. Failed attempts are retained.
Changed source or method hashes require reassessment of dependent results; the
next-phase record identifies unfinished work. Missing agreements and reviewers
block affected conclusions while engineering continues.

The first acceptance exercise concerns a finite assisted service with a frozen
scope. It requires useful supported positive and negative answers and no wrong
accepted answer in that set; blanket abstention is insufficient. The ordinary
reader, frozen candidate and automatic successor must answer a common versioned
question when compared. Supplying independently reviewed clauses diagnoses
execution; it cannot count as automatic extraction accuracy.

Independent first readings must be made without engine predictions, with
disagreement resolved by an adjudicator or recorded reasoned agreement.
The 1,467 issue-context records, 1,307 unique spans and 25 review forms are
different counting units whose overlap must be reconciled. Exposed development
counterexamples supply neither population error bounds nor independent
acceptance.
