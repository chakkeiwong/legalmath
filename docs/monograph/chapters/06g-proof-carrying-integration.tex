\section{A proof-carrying investigation joins the methods without joining their claims}
\label{sec:proof-carrying-integration}

The preceding sections establish why a single program, a majority vote or a
successful search cannot settle the meaning of a circular. They leave a
practical question for the implementation team: how should source recovery,
alternative readings, legal arguments, formal checks and Java generation be
joined so that each result remains attached to the question it actually
answers? The answer in the current prototype is a typed assurance dossier.
The dossier is a record of evidence and limits. It is not a new legal language
and it is not a certificate that the regulator would accept a proposed reading.

\subsection{The objects that travel through the investigation}

The dossier starts with a \emph{source packet}. The packet identifies the
official document bytes, edition, selected passages, cited dependencies and
the date and scope of the question. A digest of the packet is part of every
later record. A candidate that quotes the same sentence from a different
edition is consequently a different candidate, even if the visible sentence
has not changed. This is the same discipline used by the authority and
temporal records described earlier in this chapter.

The next object is a set of \emph{interpretation hypotheses}. A hypothesis has
an identifier, an interpretation family, the source passages on which it
depends, controlled English, assumptions, supporting and opposing reasons, and
either a RuleIR bundle or an explicit statement that it cannot yet be encoded.
The family records a choice that changes legal consequences: for example, a
later refund may be treated as a fee discount, or the exception may be attached
only to the fee component of a combined offer. Repeating the same prompt with
different wording does not create a new family.

An \emph{argument record} links those hypotheses to premises, exceptions,
counterarguments and source evidence. ASPIC+ distinguishes attacks on a
premise, an inference and a conclusion; Carneades distinguishes ordinary
premises, assumptions and exceptions \citep[secs.~2--3]{aspic2010}
\citep[secs.~3--6]{carneades2007}. LegalMath borrows these distinctions in its
bounded graph. The graph can show that a reading is supported, opposed or
blocked under the recorded premises. It does not turn an unresolved priority
between two legal reasons into a Boolean answer.

A \emph{method result} says what one assurance route did. It contains a method
family, target question, input hash, implementation hash, result hash, shared
dependencies, status and limitations. The result also names retained files:
the exact request, response, checker output, compiled program and replay cases
where applicable. The verifier reads those files and recomputes their hashes.
A hash of a sentence saying ``the check passed'' is insufficient evidence.
The status vocabulary includes
\texttt{AGREES}, \texttt{DISAGREES}, \texttt{UNRESOLVED},
\texttt{UNAVAILABLE}, \texttt{NOT\_COMPARABLE} and \texttt{STALE}. The
validator requires at least two method families for a required comparison. It
also records shared dependencies: generated Java and the optional Catala backend
share RuleIR and fact preparation, so their agreement is a translation check,
not two independent interpretations.

Finally, a \emph{proof obligation} states a formal proposition, assumptions,
checker and domain. It records one of \texttt{PROVED}, \texttt{CHECKED}, \texttt{DISPROVED},
\texttt{UNKNOWN}, \texttt{UNSUPPORTED} or \texttt{NOT\_RUN}. A proved
obligation must carry a certificate accepted by a registered proof checker;
the historical v3 replay integration accepted on 26 September had no registered
proof-certificate adapter. The continuing-investigation successor adds a
separate, narrowly scoped Boolean Lean certificate, explained in
Section~\ref{sec:successor-assurance}; the historical replay record is preserved.
An unknown or unsupported obligation
must carry a limitation. For example, a finite exhaustive replay can establish
that a generated Java class agrees with a Python evaluator on the listed
snapshots. It cannot establish that the English phrase ``discount of fees or
charges'' includes every future rebate.

The objects are deliberately separate because they answer different questions.
The following table is the implementation boundary.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{0.19\textwidth}Y Y}
\toprule
Object & Question answered & What a passing status does not establish \\
\midrule
Source packet & Which bytes, edition and passages were read? & That the extracted text contains every visual qualification or incorporated authority. \\
Hypothesis & What source-linked readings are being investigated? & That a generated reading is legally tenable or complete. \\
Argument graph & Which premises and objections support or defeat a reading? & That a chosen priority is the regulator's priority. \\
Proof obligation & Does a stated formal proposition follow under its assumptions? & That the assumptions describe the source or the bank's facts correctly. \\
Method result & What did this checker or generator return for this target? & Independence, probability of correctness or a universal answer. \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{The dossier keeps source, interpretation, formal and runtime claims in separate ledgers.}
\label{tab:proof-carrying-boundaries}
\end{table}

\subsection{A dry run on two meanings of a promotional connection}

Take the retained 23EC46 source packet and the paragraph~10 gift-promotion
question. The packet retains the circular, including the references to the Code
and FAQ and the surrounding paragraph~9 classification language. Retaining a
reference is different from acquiring its text: the referenced FAQ answer is
missing from this replay packet. The implementation imports all five candidate
readings and the criticism proposal from round~3, A7 attempt~08. Their origin is
recorded as archived proposals. No fresh model generation occurs in this run.
Two of these readings use the same five fact definitions and differ as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
\item an operational connection between the gift and the investment product
      is necessary for the selected prohibition; and
\item either that operational connection or product-directed promotional
      presentation can satisfy the connection condition.
\end{enumerate}

Both are investigation hypotheses. Both retain gift classification and the
fee-discount exception. The source packet does not decide between them. The
other three readings remain visible, including differences in how product-type
linkage relates to promotion and where the promotion condition enters scope.
Different fact definitions cannot be equated merely because names look alike.

The scheduler first visits all five readings, then compares pairs. For the two
readings above, a separating input has an in-scope distributor offering a gift
that is not a fee discount, with product-directed presentation but no operational
connection. One encoding returns false and the other true. The solver searches
the declared three-valued Boolean domain; a found witness is replayed through
both original programs. The feasibility and legal meaning of those facts remain
questions for the investigation. A separating assignment locates the dispute;
it does not decide which reading the regulator would adopt.

The controller records the following sequence for the same target question:

\begin{enumerate}
\item two extraction methods inspect the retained circular and source anchors
      are checked against its text; absent authority text remains absent;
\item the breadth scheduler retains every imported reading and its assumptions,
      with archived origin explicitly distinguished from fresh generation;
\item an argument graph records the support for each family and the absence of
      a retained authority deciding the disputed connection;
\item RuleIR type checking checks each restricted encoding;
\item a finite solver query finds a permitted input on which the two encodings
      differ, and the witness is replayed in the actual Python and Java
      evaluators;
\item the Java artifact is bound to the exact RuleIR bundle and snapshot set;
      and
\item the installed pinned Catala backend is compiled and replayed over the
      same declared cases; missing tools or unsupported operations are recorded
      as such, and the disabled live provider supplies no fresh evidence.
\end{enumerate}

The result is therefore a useful but blocked dossier. Its formal and execution
checks can succeed for their declared cases, while the authority
question remains open. The host receives an unresolved result and the smallest
question that could change it. It does not receive an approval.

\subsection{Why a formal proof is valuable and still insufficient}

The proof layer is most useful when its proposition is explicit. A Lean proof,
an SMT unsatisfiability result or a checked Java/JML property can establish a
statement such as

\begin{equation}
  \forall x\in D:\quad E_{\mathrm{Python}}(x)=E_{\mathrm{Java}}(x),
  \label{eq:integration-behavioural-obligation}
\end{equation}

where $D$ is the declared finite or symbolic input domain and both evaluators
have the same fact vocabulary, time semantics and result categories. A proof
of~\eqref{eq:integration-behavioural-obligation} is evidence about translation
and execution. It is not a proof that the source intended the predicate
$E$.

The proof assistant can also check a RuleIR property, such as preservation of
the three-valued exception semantics under a lowering step. The statement must
include the meaning of unknown, conflict, scope and dates. If a backend omits
one of these cases, the obligation is \texttt{UNSUPPORTED}; the missing case
cannot be hidden by a successful Boolean test. MathDevMCP is useful at this
layer because it can retain named obligations and checked derivations. Its
success remains conditional on the local RuleIR semantics and theorem target.

Stipula and its Java/JML/KeY route illustrate the same boundary. A contract
language can describe parties, state transitions, permissions and scheduled
events, and KeY can reason about properties of a generated Java model
\citep[secs.~3--6]{stipula2021}\citep[secs.~3--5]{stipulakey2025}. This is a
promising backend for an event-heavy duty, but the translation from an SFC
paragraph into the contract state machine is still an interpretation step. A
successful KeY proof cannot close that step automatically.

\subsection{The future-circular path}

The bank is concerned with future circulars, so the product must fail visibly
when a new clause does not fit its current grammar. The dossier has an
\texttt{EXTENSION\_REQUIRED} question for this case. It names the new source
passage, the absent interpretation family, the RuleIR construct that is needed
and the evidence required from the legal owner. The controller does not select
the closest historical family merely because its Java emitter compiles.

This is a principled extension path rather than an assertion of universal
coverage. A future source is first added to the authority catalog and source
inventory. Its unresolved choices are enumerated before candidate generation.
The new family receives its own controlled-language and argument records. A
formal encoding is accepted only after its types, time model, result categories
and source quotations are reviewed. Existing programs can be reused when the
new rule is proved equivalent under the declared mapping; otherwise a new
artifact is generated. Old decisions remain tied to their old source edition.

ResearchAssistant can supply retrieval, parsing, local paper and source
retention. DynareMCP offers a pattern for recording hypotheses,
dependency graphs and bounded search priorities. The present dossier replay
uses LegalMath's source and search adapters; it does not invoke those adjacent
repositories. Tree-of-Thoughts and Language
Agent Tree Search motivate breadth and environment-feedback operations
\citep{treeofthoughts2023,lats2024}. None of these components supplies an
oracle for an unseen legal meaning. Codex can serve as a bounded candidate or
review-question generator, provided its output is treated as untrusted typed
evidence and is subjected to the same source, argument and proof gates. The
current master records the provider-unavailable state because its live budget
is exhausted.

\subsection{The executable master and its stop conditions}

The implementation contract is in
\path{src/legalmath/interpretation/assurance/integration_contract.py}. The
master controller is
\path{scripts/run_proof_carrying_master.py}; its reviewed plan and allowlist
are in \path{docs/plans/proof-carrying-assurance-integration.md} and
\path{docs/implementation/proof-carrying-assurance/allowlist.json}. From the
repository root, the bounded execution is:

\begin{lstlisting}
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_proof_carrying_master.py audit
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_proof_carrying_master.py execute
\end{lstlisting}

The application also exposes \texttt{assurance-dossier} and
\texttt{assurance-dossier-verify}. The first accepts a repository root, retained
input manifest, output directory, JDK and optional pinned Catala configuration;
the second rereads a saved dossier and verifies its evidence. The input manifest
names the packet, candidate set, criticism proposal and source files by path and
SHA-256, and declares the assessment time, probe and pair limits, required
backends and proposal origin. No executable expression from a model is accepted:
the restricted expression parser and typechecker mediate compilation. A caller
can supply another circular's retained investigation through this same interface.
The interface does not generate fresh readings, acquire missing authority text
or authenticate source acquisition merely by accepting their file hashes.

The six phases freeze inputs, validate the contract, exercise the method
families, build this monograph and process guide, run a deliberate stale-target
repair, and execute the full regression. Each phase writes its exact command,
environment, input hashes, result hash and predecessor manifests. After a
phase succeeds, the controller refreshes the next-phase plan from those actual
hashes. If a source, implementation or plan file changes, that phase and every
successor become stale before another attempt can run. A failed phase cannot be
retried until an executed repair note and focused regression are recorded.

The final report is deliberately capable of saying ``engineering pass with
retained uncertainty.'' The following conditions remain blocking regardless of
how many methods agree: an unresolved authority or source region, an unsupported
future clause, a stale result, a method unavailable for a required target, an
incomparable fact vocabulary, an unknown solver result, an unproved proof
obligation, or a missing independent legal decision. This is the intended
connection between the flow chart and the Java system: Java receives a typed
decision and its evidence, while the bank's host chooses an approved action for
\texttt{UNKNOWN}, \texttt{CONFLICT}, \texttt{OUT\_OF\_SCOPE} or
\texttt{BLOCKED\_UNRESOLVED}.

An execution audit rejected the first adapter because it constructed success
labels from descriptions rather than attaching executed evidence. Its original
attempts are preserved, and its interrupted regression is not an acceptance
result. The current repaired execution writes to
\path{artifacts/proof-carrying-assurance/verified-execution-v3/}.
The evidence verifier checks every referenced file, candidate-to-bundle link,
required obligation and shared dependency; unresolved obligations block a ready
status even when the method records agree. The original controller's exceptions
for changed supervisor files were removed: changed controller, plan, allowlist
or material evidence requires new acceptance.

The original input-manifest file is itself mandatory evidence. The verifier
reparses its bounded schema and compares it with the configuration embedded in
the target before checking any replay. This closes a further audit finding: an
internally consistent embedded configuration had survived removal of the file
that originally declared it. Tests now reject a missing manifest, a differing
embedded configuration and a probe budget outside the permitted range. The
earlier successful regression remains historical evidence for the earlier code;
it is superseded by a new acceptance run after this repair.

Verification also checks the report that a reader sees. Every disagreement
produced by the recorded comparisons must appear with its material severity
and unresolved disposition. A caller cannot delete it or mark it explained
without a corresponding evidence procedure. The same check preserves the
proposed readings' objections, the critical questions and their required
evidence, and the exact proposition and domain attached to each obligation.
Consequently, evidence for a finite replay cannot be relabelled as a result
about every future circular while keeping its successful checker status.

Concurrent document changes also invalidated a later attempt even though its
focused tests passed. The combined revision is therefore validated in an
isolated repository copy. Its controller records the actual execution root,
archives input bytes, and compares source hashes before returning the results
to the working repository. Each campaign retains a fixed retry ceiling; the
earlier exhausted campaign remains closed without current acceptance. This
distinction prevents a successful command on one revision from being presented
as evidence for another revision.

The resulting engineering evidence can be
reproduced and reviewed. It does not establish legal accuracy, statistical
independence, complete future-circular coverage or production readiness. Those
claims require a separate evaluation with independently adjudicated cases and a
bank-approved operating environment.
