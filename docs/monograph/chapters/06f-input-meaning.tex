\section{Repairing the distinctions that a comparison can lose}
\label{sec:input-meaning-repair}

The unresolved comparisons in the preceding section do not all describe the
same problem. A circular may contain a supported duty about advertisements
while the selected program decides whether a gift is prohibited. Reporting
that the gift formula does not implement the advertising duty is accurate as a
description of the formula, but it has not identified a defect in that formula.
The bank still needs to account for the advertising duty elsewhere. Conversely,
an exception to the gift prohibition belongs in the gift decision even if an
earlier reader called it background. A useful comparison must distinguish
these two situations before proposing a repair.

Round 16 implements four separate judgments. The first asks whether the
extracted proposition is supported by the supplied source. The second asks
whether that proposition affects the exact question assigned to the program.
The third compares the proposition with the actual executable expression. The
fourth records the availability of any authority needed to decide the matter.
Each judgment has its own reason. Source support and question relevance carry
source quotations; a proposed preservation or contradiction of executable
meaning also carries an exact quotation from the rendered expression. The
question includes the actor, unit of assessment, temporal basis and meanings
of true and false. Its content hash prevents an assessment of one question
from being reused after the question changes.

Consider an illustrative circular containing both an advertising disclosure
duty and a gift restriction. The disclosure duty may be recorded as supported
by the source, not applicable to the gift question, and consequently not
applicable to the gift formula. That combination no longer needs a fictitious
formalization failure to satisfy the response schema. The claim--reading pair
nevertheless remains in the inventory, with its reasoned relation available for
challenge. If another reader finds that the disclosure paragraph qualifies the
gift restriction, the system retains both proposals and requests another
investigation. Agreement on a relevance label does not establish that no
distant qualification has been missed.

An omitted exception has a different diagnosis: the source proposition is
supported, its relation to the gift question is relevant, and the expression
omits it. A falsely extracted proposition has a different starting point again.
If a reader says that no electronic certificate is required while the retained
footnote states a certificate requirement, the extracted proposition must be
challenged before its supposed requirement is translated into code. A missing
external definition can leave authority availability unresolved even when the
program faithfully preserves the words already supplied. The new contract
keeps these observations separate and rejects a claimed preservation when the
declared question and the expression have opposite result meanings.

Migration cannot recover these distinctions retrospectively. The 140 accepted
round-15 comparisons used a combined label, and their reasons do not constitute
four newly validated judgments. The migration therefore stores each complete
old assessment, its provenance and its hash, while marking the new dimensions
unassessed. All 232 restored pairs survive, including the 91 still pending and
the one with no supported encoding. No new model call or new source judgment
was made in this round. The new interface is an explicit versioned path; older
experiments retain the old contract so that their recorded outcomes remain
reproducible.

The reconciliation procedure does not choose a winner by majority. It retains
the proposals and identifies the dimensions on which they disagree. Missing
authority, an unassessed relation, a contradicted source proposition or an
omission can request further processing even when two readers share the same
diagnosis. The caller supplies an attempt number within a fixed maximum. Before
that maximum, an unresolved case requests repair; at the maximum, it produces
an explicit uncertainty report. This procedure supplies a bounded control for
investigation. It neither supplies new evidence by itself nor consumes a model
call without an authorized execution route.

The new investigation route executes that control with retained or local
providers. Its first two perspectives receive the same source and question
without seeing one another's response. Later rounds receive the earlier
disputes or response-validation diagnostics. Each invocation has a persistent
reservation, and the raw response is saved before validation. In the retained
worked fixture, an applicability disagreement causes six local invocations
across three rounds; all six proposals remain in the final uncertainty report.
A malformed-response fixture exercises an actual additional round. Resuming
the same completed requests reuses evidence, whereas a changed source, question
or setting invalidates the run. The first transport failure stops further
dispatch, including after resume. Agreement requires a validated response from
each declared perspective: repeated answers from one cannot replace a missing
other perspective. These are executable local tests of the
investigation procedure. The route explicitly refuses live providers pending
their integration with the shared allowance; no new live judgment is implied
by the fixture's six invocations.

\subsection{What had happened, and what the bank knew}

The contact example requires two clocks. Suppose the bank asks at noon whether
contact has occurred for STR~S. A contact at 11:00 can answer that factual
question positively; a contact at 13:00 cannot. There is also a knowledge
cutoff: an 11:00 contact entered into the system at 13:00 was not available to a
replay restricted to information recorded by noon. A contact about STR~T
provides no witness for STR~S. Compressing any of these distinctions into one
supplied Boolean can make the rule appear correct while its input answers a
different question.

The implemented event projection receives an event identity, an STR identity,
an event kind, the occurrence time, the recording time, evidence identities and
whether the event is confirmed or disputed. The caller states the start of the
interval being examined, the assessment instant and the knowledge cutoff. A
confirmed event counts only if it belongs to the specified report, falls in
the stated occurrence interval and was recorded by the knowledge cutoff. The
by-now interval includes the assessment instant. This is the chosen factual
question; it introduces no deadline into the circular.

A positive answer can have a particular event as its witness. A negative answer
requires more: a separately supplied assertion that the history is complete
for the report, event kind and whole interval being assessed. That assertion
has its own recording time and evidence. Without an eligible completeness
assertion, absence produces an unknown result. An assertion recorded before
the end of the interval cannot certify its future portion. A relevant disputed
event produces conflict, and two incompatible records using the same event
identity are rejected. These checks make the reliance on event recording
visible; they cannot establish that an operational source captured every real
contact.

Resubmission adds another relation. The resubmission event names the original
submission, and that original must belong to the same STR. A missing original
leaves the linkage unresolved. The adapter rejects an attributed resubmission
that predates its original. A complete history with no linked resubmission can
support a negative answer to this factual existence question; it does not by
itself decide whether the legal performance requirements were satisfied.
Schema validation and technical liaison likewise remain different from the
intention that triggers their associated duties.

The source's effective interval and the generated bundle's interval are kept
separately from these two clocks. Round 16 re-executes the nine retained child
cases without changing their bundles. The three historical assessments still
return the version-time refusal because their requested dates precede the
stored bundle intervals. The added time-context check records that refusal
separately from unresolved source effectiveness. An explicitly supplied
effectiveness assertion needs source anchors and an interval before it can
produce even a conditional applicability result. The code has not supplied the
missing legal date. A pair of trigger and performance records also retains
unencoded performance instead of reporting overall compliance from a true
trigger.

\subsection{An exact number must survive the Java entry point}

Equation~\ref{eq:exact-fractional-exposure} depends on a positive denominator.
Testing that identity inside the rule evaluator does not protect an external
interface that accepts arbitrary numerator and denominator fields. Round 16
adds a packaged Java entry point which accepts the decimal percentage itself.
Its request declares the signed exact-percentage profile, subject, assessment
and knowledge times, development mode and three facts: fund-manager status,
the stated virtual-asset objective, and intended percentage exposure. The
generated entry point supplies the internal rational fields. It rejects a
caller who also tries to supply those fields, a raw snapshot or other
undeclared fields.

The percentage is a decimal string in percentage units. Thus \texttt{"10"}
means 10\%, while \texttt{"0.1"} means one tenth of one percent. Suppose its
signed unscaled integer is $u$ and it has $s$ decimal places. Java computes the
integer numerator $100u$ and the positive denominator $10^s$, then divides
both by their greatest common divisor. The policy receives that reduced exact
ratio in basis points. At 9.999\%, the initial pair is 999900 and 1000, which
reduces to 9999 and 10. The comparison remains false because 9999 is less than
10000. No binary floating-point representation or rounding rule intervenes.
Python provides a separate reference conversion using rational arithmetic.

The accepted syntax has an optional minus sign, an ordinary integer part and
an optional nonempty decimal part, within a 64-character limit. Exponents,
whitespace, redundant leading zeroes, non-ASCII digits, nonfinite values and
JSON numeric inputs are rejected. Very fine decimal scales remain exact when
they fit the limit. A missing percentage is converted to missing rational
facts; a conflicting percentage supplies conflicting facts. Their eventual
effect follows the declared RuleIR uncertainty semantics. For example, the
retained case with no virtual-asset objective and a missing percentage returns
unknown, while conflicting percentage evidence blocks a known result.

Signed input is an explicit arithmetic profile inherited from the earlier
rational experiment. Acceptance of a negative number under this profile is
not a decision that negative intended exposure is permissible for a particular
fund question. A deployment must state its financial domain separately. The
new package is also an explicit interface choice: existing raw-snapshot APIs
remain available for their earlier purposes and do not acquire this protection
merely because the new JAR exists. A host must call the generated percentage
entry point. Its build record binds that class, the actual JAR, the policy and
the Java sources.

Thirty retained host cases exercise this JAR and the Python reference. They
include all seven earlier threshold examples, additional fine-scale and signed
values, missing and conflicting facts, malformed strings and direct attempts
to inject denominators. Seventeen requests are rejected as specified; the
other thirteen agree on the transformed snapshot and the full decision content
apart from the declared engine identity and its associated hash. Separate
focused checks send a floating-point JSON number directly to Java, avoiding
the misleading possibility that Python alone rejects it before the JAR sees
the request. These results establish the tested representation boundary; the
qualifying-asset classifications supplied to the rule still need their own
evidence.

\subsection{One downloaded file can contain incompatible editions}

The acquired 23EC44 appendix pack supplies a concrete source-selection test.
Its clean October 2023 terms occupy Appendix~7, PDF pages 74--94. Appendix~7a,
pages 95--135, shows amendments and can yield old and replacement wording in
the same extracted stream. The new selection manifest binds the original PDF,
each extracted page, both edition ranges and the exact selected provisions.
An active source packet requires a clean edition; attempting to select marked
changes as the active edition is rejected. Mixing provisions from the two
editions is rejected even though both came from the same genuine download.

Selection within the clean edition must preserve qualifications as well. The
executed example takes the definitions on page~76, the footnote about the
structure of the collective investment scheme, the continuation on page~77
about persistent price appreciation and notification, and the application
paragraphs on that page. The manifest explicitly links these provisions.
Selecting the definitions while omitting the declared footnote or continuation
fails with a missing-dependency result. Each excerpt has a page and exact
character offsets; changed source bytes, changed extracted text or a changed
manifest invalidate the earlier selection.

These structural checks operate on an inspected manifest. They do not discover
every legally relevant qualification automatically. The packet therefore keeps
unresolved authority questions as unresolved dependencies. The file also carries
a notice that the circular was superseded and updated in December 2023; the
printed October date cannot establish current applicability. The program
records the clean historical edition and leaves effectiveness unresolved. This
separates successful acquisition and selection from the further task of
determining which provisions govern a particular assessment date.

All restored pairs, retained parents, source questions and unresolved
PDF-materiality identities remain available for the next investigation.
Estimating legal error or savings in review cost still requires source-grounded
answers and independent reference cases.
