## Remaining acceptance and next work

Human comprehension and naturalness remain pending. The new passages teach
the mechanisms before introducing formal or implementation names, but neither
compilation nor author/model review certifies a human voice. Feedback should
test whether a reader can reconstruct the worked distinctions and explain the
statistical assumptions without supplying missing steps.

The evidence still leaves legal interpretation and source coverage questions,
comparative performance on unfamiliar source families, reviewer effort and
institution operation unsettled. Local composition does not establish
whole-circular coverage; official FAQ examples do not supply a general theory
of precedent; and a controlled amendment does not establish independent
interpretation of a new regulation. Bank identities, fact ownership,
confidentiality, audit integrity, real host concurrency and release authority
need evidence from the intended environment. These remain substantive next
work, with their distinctions preserved in the books.

Concurrent implementation changes were preserved. No implementation code,
