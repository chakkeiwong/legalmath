## Notice correction

Base page 132 has two separate alternatives. The Luxembourg-listed alternative
contains both paragraph (1), website publication, and paragraph (2), a clearing
system substitute **subject to the Luxembourg exchange's rules**. The other
alternative starts `[(1) Mitteilungen an das Clearing System` and is explicitly
governed by the margin for securities not listed on an exchange. Final page 7
checks both publication media; final page 13 selects the Luxembourg regulated
market. The old constructor selected both alternatives because it matched the
clearing-system label without its governing condition. That was a local
selection error. The new operation excludes the unlisted alternative and
preserves the listed alternative's qualification. This conclusion concerns the
selected issue configuration, not observed current listing or delivered notices.
