## Question to preserve

Can additional public evidence and more selective questions resolve material
uncertainties without increasing missed interpretation defects? Use the retained
P12 implementation and the completed A7 implementation as explicit comparators.
Hold source access and model budget equal when comparing reasoning methods;
measure the effect of acquiring a missing authority separately. A lower question
count alone cannot establish a better method.

B1 should precede more elaborate voting: a missing incorporated provision can
leave every model reader without the needed evidence. B2b replaces the current
unit-gain repair scheduling heuristic with checked candidate partitions; the
current question list is deduplicated, not a proven minimum set. B3 requires
actual sourced examples. Model-generated case descriptions are proposals until
their identity and applicability have been checked against the retained record.

B2a is motivated by the actual 24EC16 fidelity checks: several generated programs
cover a liquidity or staffing subcontrol while the requested scope includes broader
operational readiness. The current checker correctly leaves omitted duties
unestablished. Treating every partial program as a complete competing interpretation
would inflate both disagreement and the human question queue. Preserve the original
component scope, compose complementary duties under an explicit contract, and only
then compare complete alternatives. Do not implement composition by blindly
conjoining Booleans: preserve distinct modalities, exceptions, applicability and
unknown/conflict outcomes. Unanswered questions must distinguish missing program
coverage from an ambiguity that actually requires additional legal evidence.

The current wire validators check schemas, references and expressions. They do
not establish that every free-text field is coherent prose. In the development
pilot, 24EC16's first candidate response included self-repair chatter in an
explanatory field while remaining valid JSON. The executable-meaning checker
deliberately excludes such candidate narrative as a source of authority. B0 must
address presentation and diagnostic quality without weakening that separation.

The live acceptance also provides a concrete reliability baseline for B0:
attempt 06 repeated incorrect response fields after uninformative repair
feedback; attempt 07's explicit-schema repair fixed that interface, but a later
batch returned one extra claim/candidate pair. The strict checker rejected it
and the model corrected it in its single allowed repair. Extend repair feedback
with exact missing, extra and repeated pair identities. Before changing batch
sizes or timeouts, compare completion and exhaustion on the retained workload;
smaller batches must still cover the whole matrix and respect the same total
resource ceiling. Preserve failed and corrected responses as separate evidence.

B0 must distinguish per-response limits from aggregate investigation limits.
The present `Fidelity` contract caps a response at 1,000 checks and 30 additional
concerns, and the combined batches are validated with the same contract. The
accepted investigations stay within those bounds (the larger has 384 checks and
24 concerns). Larger combinations can therefore fail aggregate validation even
when each batch is valid. Add explicit whole-investigation capacity checks before
dispatch and an aggregation contract with declared bounds; preserve every row and
concern, and test the boundary without silently truncating evidence. Such failure
must remain incomplete execution rather than a supported interpretation.

For long incorporated Codes, B1 also needs a bounded section-retrieval contract.
Retain the complete raw document and its version, identify the cited provision,
and include its definitions, qualifications and references with exact locations.
Do not silently truncate the document to fit a model request or describe an
excerpt as all applicable law. Missing context, uncertain section boundaries and
unresolved cross-references must survive the selection. The current packet audit
checks complete coverage of the retained extraction; changing that contract
requires its own adversarial tests and explicit selection evidence.

B1's source-completeness cases must also include partially scanned PDFs and
image-based qualifications. The current two text extractors do not implement OCR;
agreement between them cannot certify that a visible image-based footnote was
captured. Compare retained page structure and rendered content with the selected
text, and report unsupported visual content rather than interpreting parser
agreement as complete source acquisition.

## Evidence contract for B4

Predeclare source families, transformations, clean controls, seeds or provider
replications, per-arm resources and the primary paired criterion before dispatch.
For seeded defects the label describes the introduced alteration, not a complete
legal interpretation of the circular. Public worked examples with explicit
outcomes provide a different evidence class. Institution-owned decisions may be
used only if authorized and must retain their provenance and uncertainty.

Promotion requires the declared defect-detection and useful-coverage criterion
with uncertainty intervals, and no integrity, stale-source, missing-pair or
silent-budget violation. Descriptive question counts, runtime and agreement can
explain a result but cannot supply an English-correctness score. Cost claims need
observed review work on a comparable task. Few sources or replications cannot
support a population-wide ranking.

Continue engineering when an interpretation remains uncertain but the harness is
valid. Stop dependent claims when required source evidence is unavailable,
corrupted or mislabelled. Repair a missed controlled exception; do not discard
the negative result or redefine success as abstaining on every case. The strongest
alternative explanation for apparent gains is unequal source access or reuse of
development answers; frozen source/response manifests and withholding by source
family are required diagnostics.

## Human questions and spending

First exhaust retained official evidence, exact source-bound prior answers and
bounded automated challenges. Remaining questions should identify the precise
clause, competing outcomes, affected controls and attempted evidence actions.
An institution's fact owner or internal compliance team may answer a narrow
question without commissioning a broad review. External consultancy is a
separately authorized last resort; this plan neither contacts anyone nor commits
funding. Future natural-language error rates remain unmeasured until supported
by suitable outcome evidence, whatever the number of passing software tests.
