## What is executable

The [operator guide](operator-guide.md) gives the complete commands and input
contracts for `interpretation-assurance` and `assurance-monitor`. The first
captures public source versions, compares two extraction methods and checks the
packet against the retained text. It follows bounded official references and
keeps missing, stale or ambiguous dependencies visible.

Two isolated calls then extract source claims before candidate generation. Each
claim records actor, action, modality, conditions, exceptions, time and evidence;
every source unit needs a disposition. The union retains differences. Candidate
generation and bounded BFS/UCT investigation use the existing search engine. The
added semantic checker compares the original source and independent claims with
the actual executable expression and its used fact definitions. An unused
exception declaration cannot masquerade as an implemented exception. Required
claim/candidate pairs are checked in bounded batches without silently omitting
coverage. The entailment judgment itself remains model-generated.

The criticism layer distinguishes ordinary premises, assumptions and exceptions,
strict and defeasible steps, and attacks on premises, conclusions and inference
applicability. Proposed preferences cannot suppress attacks without a separately
accepted preference binding. Cases and problem factors are represented separately;
analogy, distinction and countercase constructors require sourced factors. This
does not verify a model's classification of a passage as precedent or reproduce
full ASPIC+, Carneades or AGATHA semantics.

Repairs target the implicated stage and invalidate dependent checks. Every
dispatch consumes its reservation, including failure; repeated unchanged
commitments are not progress. Original candidates and responses remain available.
Optional derived fact mappings normalize declared output conventions and replay
both original generated Java programs on the stated domain, preserving unknown,
conflict and out-of-scope outcomes. Equivalence is conditional on those mappings
and the domain, not a proof of English meaning.

The monitor runs one schedulable tick. Source, incorporated dependency, control,
implementation/provider/settings, fact-schema and evaluation-date changes create
successor investigations. Unchanged controls can reuse retained results; failed
or interrupted investigations consume bounded retries. Overdue checks are
observable. No scheduler has been deployed and no bank service-level commitment
is inferred from the local tests.

## What the live source checks expose

The first accepted 23EC46 batch checked 32 claim/candidate pairs and returned
25 ENTAILED, two CONTRADICTED and five NOT_ESTABLISHED judgments. It identified
an alternative that requires an operational product link beyond promotional
presentation, another without an executable product-type-link branch, and an
interpretation whose two enumerated categories might exclude other links.
These are source-grounded model criticisms for further investigation, not
independently adjudicated legal errors. All original alternatives remain retained.

The same batch identified issues that program equivalence cannot answer: the
referenced FAQ is missing from the supplied authority set, and the rules require
someone or another service to classify whether an offered benefit is a gift or
a fee discount. A false result from this selected paragraph-10 control also
does not establish compliance with the circular's separate section-103 ground.
The report therefore preserves both code-level differences and unresolved source
and fact-classification questions. It does not collapse them into a correctness
percentage.

The first accepted 24EC16 batch returned five ENTAILED and 27 NOT_ESTABLISHED
judgments. Its criticism identifies generated programs that cover only liquidity
or staffing while the selected operational-readiness scope also contains broader
assessment, operational, technology and communication duties. These results expose
partial program coverage; they do not establish that each omitted duty is legally
ambiguous. The successor plan therefore adds explicit component scoping and
composition before comparing complete interpretations or asking a human to resolve
the remaining questions. Composition must preserve modalities and exceptions;
simply conjoining partial Boolean programs is insufficient.

The most important remaining implementation work is resolving incorporated
public authorities, composing complementary component rules, and turning material uncertainties into questions with checked
candidate partitions. The current scheduler uses relative costs and a unit gain
for an actionable repair; its question list is deduplicated, not optimal. Case
mechanisms need actual retained public cases/examples and verified applicability.
The [successor plan](next-phase-plan.md) gives acceptance cases and an evidence
contract for those tasks. Narrow internal questions and reused official evidence
precede any separately authorized external consultancy.

The strongest alternative explanation for apparent gains is better source access
or reuse of development responses rather than better reasoning. Frozen source
and response manifests make that explanation inspectable. A missed seeded defect,
an incorrectly escalated clean control, stale authority accepted as current or a
changed response replayed as identical would overturn engineering acceptance.
The weakest evidence remains unmeasured correctness on natural future circulars.
