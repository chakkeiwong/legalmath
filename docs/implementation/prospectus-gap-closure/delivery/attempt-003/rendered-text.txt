Source findings
Enel’s final terms name the base prospectus of 20 December 2024 and supplement of 27 January
2025; both retained editions were checked. The final redemption amount is EUR 1,000 per EUR
1,000 Calculation Amount. The base prospectus, p.95, makes the terms subject to separate agency
provisions and identifies the agency, guarantee and covenant agreements available for inspection
through the agents.
Unilever’s final terms name the retained memorandum of 16 May 2025. Its summary refers to a
trust deed dated 22 July 1994 (p.8), while the operative conditions identify a trust deed dated 16
May 2024 (p.32). Amendment history may explain this, but the executed agreements have not been
obtained. The paying agency agreement is separately identified as 22 July 1994, as amended.
Deutsche Bank’s German terms control (p.4). The German and English columns of section 5(4),
pp.52–55, both specify reduction when consolidated CET1 falls below 5.125%, pro-rata allocation
and caps, prompt determination within one month unless shortened, and notice rules under which
missing notice does not invalidate the reduction. Restoration is discretionary and subject to profit,
distribution and continuing-trigger constraints. This local reading does not establish translation
equivalence for the whole prospectus.

open. The planned four-case source challenge is incomplete: two capital families were inspected,
while no corporate bundle was obtained within the retrieval budget. A further unseen-source challenge is needed after the final witness repair.

an incomplete interpretation. The source challenge is too small to establish accuracy in an issuer
population. An applicable omitted condition, wrong edition, or accepted forged witness would overturn the affected result.
