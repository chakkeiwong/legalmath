# Remaining limits and what would close them

The acceptance boundary is a proved formal proposition or an explicit
qualification. Human judgments are not quality labels, correctness authorities
or acceptance gates. Missing evidence must not be treated as false or as bank
clearance.

| Remaining limit | Is the remedy known? | Required work or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual bank, branch, capacity, client, beneficial owner, mandate and route | Yes: retrieve versioned records and run the dependency checks | These private documents are not in the public archive. A guessed JPMorgan entity or synthetic account changes the question. |
| Complete licensing and other client/product rules | A bounded inventory can be expanded provision by provision | Fix the entities, activities and jurisdictions, then retain missing authorities and examine their applicability. Fourteen declared obligations do not prove discovery of all applicable law. |
| Full UBS notice/conversion schedule and issuer-specific resolution law | Known engineering route; not implemented by the completed seven specifications | Formalize the notice, business-day calendar, restoration-before-notice exception, conversion date, alternative loss-absorption date and price adjustments from the complete incorporated documents. Current amounts are conditional on those event premises. UK issues require their own legal basis. This remains source/implementation work. |
| Fund strategy and depositary-share rights | Source-specific formalization is possible; no universal classifier is established | Preserve the legal form, underlying rights, deposit agreement, governing law and strategy. The HKMA FAQ supplies no bright-line fund threshold. `qualifying_wrapper` remains a visible interpretation premise. |
| Source currentness and cross-border legal effects | Amendment, commencement, supersession and proceeding tracking is a known procedure | Matching bytes and download dates do not prove force. The excluded Swiss consolidation stays excluded. Later litigation merits, stays, finality, repayment and foreign recognition require further evidence. |
| General English-to-formal correctness | No general proof method is established here | A quote proves location; a constructor theorem assumes a formal proposal; two backends can share a mistaken proposal. Stronger assurance requires a justified formal semantics for the relevant legal language and incorporated authorities. Ambiguity remains qualified. |
| Unknown future legal changes | Finite observations cannot establish universal future accuracy | Freeze before observations, preserve the denominator and counterexamples, and qualify new situations. The new freeze has no future observations. Universal mathematical claims apply only within their stated domains. |
| Large-source comprehension and irreducible context size | Repeated-representation overflow is repaired; general comprehension/capacity remains unresolved | The retained case fits its bound after exact encoding. Unique explanations can still overflow. Live comprehension remains untested and requires a new explicit allowance; historical grants stay exhausted. |
| Whole compiler/runtime and richer date libraries | Further bounded proofs and independent checks are possible | The new theorem covers Gregorian ordering and supported constructors. Business-day rules, date libraries, whole Java/Catala compilation and evidence-time selection are separate obligations. |

The next instrument-specific contract extension needs an exact issue and edition,
incorporated documents, event types, calendars, proposed transitions, independent
relations, counterexample mutations and explicit refusal conditions. This
campaign's pass cannot certify provisions it did not implement. The unknown
English entailment and future-generalization problems must not be disguised as
missing packages or compiler installation problems.

## Decision and red-team assessment

| Decision | Primary criterion | Veto status | Main uncertainty | Next action | Not concluded |

| Accept the scoped integration | Bindings, retained obligations, independent relations, both targets | Missing evidence still vetoes clearance | Meaning and complete rule discovery | Obtain the specified sources/facts and extend declared scope | Full SFC eligibility or bank permission |
| Accept the date theorem in its domain | Lean checks with disclosed standard axioms | Changed dates, constructors and identities reject old evidence | Legal date selection and codec/compiler boundaries | Apply supported comparisons; retain separate qualifications | Whole compiler correctness |
| Freeze before new observations | Immutable method/source identities | Development cases cannot count as untouched future evidence | No future observations yet | Record observations before repairing the frozen method | Future-law accuracy or backend superiority |

The strongest alternative explanation for agreement is a shared interpretation
error. Independent equations address formal implementation errors, not that
English-meaning problem. A counterexample inside a proved domain would require
inspection of the specification, proof assumptions and executed representation.
A new legal qualification outside it requires a new source-bound investigation.
Source interpretation and coverage remain the weakest evidence, so real-document
results stay qualified.
