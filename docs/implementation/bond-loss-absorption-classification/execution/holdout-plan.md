The freeze recorded in `freeze-v1.json` precedes acquisition and inspection of
the Compass and ING sources. The earlier development planning had mentioned
Compass as a possible issuer; no issue terms were inspected before the freeze.
This is an unseen-document/family transfer challenge, not random sampling or a
legal-accuracy benchmark. Issuer choice and availability limit inference.

Question: can the unchanged implementation read a new corporate senior issue
and a new CoCo issue, emit source-bound reasons, preserve unresolved evidence,
and execute those same formal premises through both backends? Pass requires
unchanged method hashes and intact sources, not agreement with an agent's
anticipated answers. Any unresolved mechanism is retained. Any code repair
would end this holdout attempt and convert its documents to development.

incorporate the 13 June 2025 base and 18 December 2025 supplement. The acquired
2026 base is preserved as a rejected edition. Selecting the documents expressly
named by the final terms is input assembly; the classifier remains unchanged.

April 2025 multi-security shelf; scope is assigned from their headings.

navigation supplied the public sources; failed page routes and retained issuer
pages are recorded in the acquisition directory. No externally hosted model or
human quality label is used.
