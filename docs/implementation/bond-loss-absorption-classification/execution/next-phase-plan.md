# Refreshed next phase after delivery

The current requested Tesco/Veolia negatives, controls and per-bond reasons are
published. This is a qualified software/source demonstration; Shell's fresh
case remains unresolved and no universal legal-generalization claim is made.

The correctness follow-up reproduced three reader failures through
`analyze_issue` using controlled source/extraction fixtures; see
`reader-gap-diagnostic.json`. A notification duty is mistaken for compulsory
conversion, a negated write-down statement becomes positive, and a permanent
principal-forfeiture clause is missed, allowing a negative. The direct
automatic-conversion control succeeds. These invalidate general semantic
reliability of the current reader, not the formal Boolean rule, and do not
independently reclassify the retained real bonds.

Repair priorities now precede expansion of coverage:

1. Bind each extracted action to its subject, affected security, modality,
   trigger, negation, exceptions and governing definitions. Keep a proposed
   parse separate from a validated formal premise. An unrelated `shall` must
   not establish mandatory conversion; discretionary regulator powers must
   not be rejected merely because their wording uses `may`.
2. Bind every premise and supporting reason to an issue/series and applicable
   document edition. Resolve common terms and series-specific terms before
   classification. The current summary's maturity-year filter and phrase
   exclusions are narrow repairs, not general issue or meaning resolution.
3. Stop deriving semantic completeness from repayment evidence plus no
   recognised unresolved candidate. Require explicit disposition of potentially
   operative terms and dependencies within a declared supported domain.
   Unsupported operative language must retain an unresolved state. An absence
   of recognised keywords cannot certify absence of the feature.
4. Generate the decision and its explanation from the same validated derivation.
   A separate checker must reject missing issue/condition/definition links;
   checking that a quote occurs in the source is insufficient. Any semantic
   mapping still assumed rather than proved must remain qualified.
5. Test the whole path with independent formal constructions and adversarial
   transformations: attach `shall` to a different verb, negate the mechanism,
   swap series, remove a definition, substitute a document version and add an
   unrecognised principal-loss clause. Require the decision, supporting reason
   and uncertainty to respond correctly. Freeze a new version before new
   issuer/wording challenges; preserve failures and exposed development data.

Then establish principal repayment when final terms state a cash amount per
calculation amount.
Use exact currency/decimal values and explicit field identities. Require the
final redemption amount to equal the selected calculation amount in the same
currency, with a source-bound repayment clause connecting it to the note's
principal; do not compare an issue price, coupon amount or minimum denomination
instead. A mismatch, unspecified amount, currency mismatch, principal-linked
formula or conflicting final/base term must remain unresolved.

Review before execution must include issuer/series identification, term
precedence, units, currency and the impact on existing negatives. Construct
independent examples for full repayment, partial repayment, missing fields,
different currencies, coupon/issue-price confusion and split-page tables.
Keep Shell as development after the repair. Re-run the retained source corpus
and the six integrity faults, then freeze before selecting fresh issuers.
No human or model answer should become a quality label. A successful exact
amount relation proves that relation under its extracted premises; it does
not establish unrestricted English interpretation or contract completeness.
