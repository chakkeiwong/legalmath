# Deliberate expansion of a disputed source clause

Status: proposed successor to the frozen E2 matrix; no live calls admitted by
this note alone. The existing candidates delegate Q3's business qualification
to supplied judgment facts. This preserves an unknown input but does not make
competing readings of that qualification executable. The next implementation
should expose those alternatives locally before making another overall rule.

Use a source-bound issue with its full context, quoted target sentence, explicit
question and a small declared fact vocabulary. For the retained example, ask
which antecedent of the qualified *ordinary-course guidance* a reading expresses.
Do not ask whether the institution is legally exempt from licensing. The words
“should”, “in the ordinary course” and the possibility of governing statutory
qualifications survive in every interpretation. A Boolean antecedent cannot
represent those qualifications as an unconditional legal exemption.

Two fresh readers should enumerate readings independently before seeing peers.
Each proposal supplies an expression over the declared facts, a plain English
paraphrase, the syntactic attachment it chooses, exact source evidence, and
assumptions required to connect those facts to the source. A reader may report
that the vocabulary is inadequate; the controller must retain that uncertainty.
There is no mandatory quota of three plausible readings: fabricated diversity
would create a false result. Conversely, duplicate formulas do not count as
distinct executable hypotheses merely because their paraphrases differ.

After that breadth pass, a third request receives the union and seeks missing
scope, negation and connective attachments, plus concrete counterexamples to
each. The controller retains parentage and source identity, deduplicates exact
commitments only, and records deferred proposals when capacity is reached. It
must not treat similar prose or an equal sampled output as equivalence.

Compare every admitted executable hypothesis in the full finite T/F/U domain
of the declared Boolean inputs and replay counterexamples in generated Java.
Also exercise conflicting inputs separately. A table should show which readings
disagree on which facts and carry the source quotations, assumptions and open
questions next to those distinctions. Prioritize the next source question by
the number of executed distinctions it addresses per bounded call. This is an
investigation priority, not a posterior legal probability. A final critic checks
the readings and distinguishing facts against the full source; an unsupported
reading may be marked challenged but must not disappear solely on model vote.

Every model action passes through the counted checkpoint mechanism, with at
most three attempts for malformed responses and an explicit phase ceiling.
Missing evidence, an unrepresentable qualification or an unresolved discrepancy
becomes a residual question. Only checked logical impossibility in the declared
expression profile can eliminate an encoded branch mechanically; English
disfavouring remains defeasible evidence. This preserves the useful distinction
between software error, a rejected candidate and unresolved legal meaning.

Admission must specify the actual fact vocabulary and why it does not contain
the disputed answer as an opaque Boolean. That is the earliest veto: a tree of
renamed business-judgment facts could appear broad while still avoiding the
interpretation problem. Tests need duplicated readings, all-readers-same-error,
unsupported source quotes, omitted hypotheses, malformed counterexamples,
unknown/conflict inputs, and budget exhaustion. A live run needs all source and
model-request records, exact graph coverage, complete surviving alternatives,
and no claim of calibrated legal probability or production authorization.
