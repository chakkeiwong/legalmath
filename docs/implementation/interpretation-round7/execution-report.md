## What the checks found

The labels distinguish support for an extracted English claim from coverage of
that claim in a supplied program representation. They are model judgments with
mechanically checked evidence references. They do not form a labelled legal
accuracy dataset. In particular, the 157 NOT_ESTABLISHED results cannot be read
as 157 independently confirmed interpretation errors.

One concrete implementation defect is the imposed choice between compliance and
prohibition labels. The retained candidates compute a three-factor licensing
trigger. A true trigger says neither that a licence is held nor that the office
complies with the law. A successor must attach the actual question and a neutral
predicate meaning to the generated program and its Java package.

A second problem is the scope of the formalization. The conjunction consumes
business status, regulated-activity status and territory as supplied judgments.
It does not implement the definition of related entities, the intra-group
carve-out, the case-specific business assessment or the ordinary-course
qualification. Some claims are background or qualifications rather than
independent Boolean outputs. A single trigger cannot be treated as an executable
account of every sentence in the FAQ.

The repaired argument graph retains questions about statutory carve-outs,
territorial facts, the scope of negation and “or” in the business qualification,
and the authority for proposed argument priorities. Grounded or stable acceptance
within this proposed graph does not verify its legal premises. The evaluator
therefore reports UNRESOLVED and does not authorize candidate pruning.

The strongest alternative explanation for many NOT_ESTABLISHED results is that

judgments. The successor preserves this complete matrix and makes its new,
narrower trigger checks explicit. It must not relabel the old checks to create an
apparent accuracy improvement. The weakest evidence remains source
interpretation by correlated model readers without an independent legal oracle.
