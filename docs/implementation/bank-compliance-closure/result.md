# Bank-compliance implementation result

The reviewed five-phase program executed successfully after the recorded
repairs. The implementation now retrieves evidence, derives conditional findings
against a fixed obligation inventory, preserves source and fact uncertainty,
executes the same formal models through RuleIR and Catala, and records transaction
events and duties. This completes the scoped engineering program, not all
substantive bank-compliance rules.

The final run has **153 passing tests**, with no failures, errors or skips. Of
these, 46 exercise the new investigation path and 107 cover the preceding
compliance/prospectus/qualification work. All **18 independent formal-equation
obligations** passed, and **11 formal-expression-to-model preservation
certificates** were checked by Lean. Both native targets passed **330 generated
executions on 165 identical input cases**. These results establish the recorded
formal and engineering properties; they do not prove legal source meaning.

The integrated evaluation additionally passed all eleven models through each
target on a complete hypothetical factual scenario: **22 native model
evaluations**. With real retained public documents and missing account/policy
facts, both targets abstained on ten models and explicitly left the unsupported
CMIC capacity unresolved. The transaction result remained `QUALIFIED`, with a
formal composition outcome of `UNDETERMINED`. No transaction was cleared.

Two official sanctions snapshots were retained and parsed: 19,391 SDN records
and 481 consolidated-list records, with publication dates of 23 September and
14 September 2026 respectively. These dates describe the downloaded snapshots;
they do not establish complete legal currentness. The public example retains 36
source records, including original/extracted-text pairs. Internal bank policy
and real account information remain unavailable.

The monograph compiled successfully to **328 pages**, with a 78-page technical
companion. The changed pages were rendered and inspected. All thirteen citations
and the displayed equation in the changed section were preserved. The full
document check passed with 129 cited documents and 282 citation occurrences.
These are build, preservation and inspection results, not human quality scores.

## Reproduction and repair

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_bank_compliance_program.py
.venv/bin/python -m legalmath.transaction --help
python3 scripts/build_reader_facing_monograph.py
```

The final dependency-bound attempts are identified by `summary.json` and
`state.json`. Each phase receipt records the command, Python environment, git
commit, uncommitted input identity, exact tool hashes, CPU-only mode, deterministic
seed status, wall time and output hashes. `run-manifest.json` gathers the final
results and points to those receipts. A second unchanged program invocation
verified and reused all five phases; it did not rerun the native experiments.

`review.md` records the skeptical audit and executed repairs, including the
cross-designated SDN record, derived-product inconsistency, insufficient threshold
premise, calendar retrieval and malformed ownership list. Those findings were
implementation failures. They triggered repair and rechecking rather than
abandoning the research direction. Earlier attempts remain historical evidence.

## Remaining gaps

Nine of the fourteen catalog obligations still retain unimplemented rule
families or unresolved coverage: party/scope evidence, complete applicability,
blocking applicability, other sanctions, licensing, other client controls,
other product/issuer-law requirements, internal policy and settlement-route
requirements. The five supported obligation entries evaluate conditional rules;
the additional six formal models supply connected calculations.

Completing those families requires separately sourced rules and their exact
applicability conditions. Actual JPMorgan policies, account mandates, entity
identity and ownership evidence are also missing. Universal natural-language
meaning and arbitrary future-law coverage have not been proved. The strongest
alternative explanation for an apparently correct legal result remains a
faithfully executed but incomplete or wrong interpretation. The system therefore
retains qualified statements; it does not replace these gaps with human grading.

The future-observation series has been initialized and has **zero real
prospective observations**. New identifiers and generated amendments test
engineering behavior, not accuracy on unknown future law. The concrete remaining
work is in `next-implementation.md` and the layer-by-layer implementation guide.
