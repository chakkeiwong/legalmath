# Skeptical review and executed repairs

The initial plan was reviewed before implementation in
`docs/plans/bank-compliance-closure.md`. This record explains the implementation
review and the boundaries of the resulting evidence.

## Baselines, assumptions and stopping rules

The original composition component is the engineering baseline. Human answers,
the old product-only result, and agreement between target languages are not legal
oracles. Exact formal equations are checked independently of the shared AST;
the retained expression interpreter supplies the runtime reference. Both targets
receive identical generated factual cases. No speed or superiority ranking is
made from these deterministic checks.

The selected law models remain proposals based on retained source editions.
The private-account threshold is the required minimum in USD cents, not current
wealth; the existing product amounts use their declared HKD units. Unknown
territorial predicates stay unknown. Actual control adequacy, account facts,
entity identity and internal policy are not inferred from public disclosures.
Board-authorization and training records are business facts where the selected
rule requires them, not human quality labels for the software.

The synthetic integration account supplies complete Boolean and exact monetary
premises solely to exercise the interface. Its one-day fact interval is a
generated-case convenience, not a recommended evidence-refresh policy. Public
source intake supplies no default freshness interval or legal effective date.

A failed test, source check, formal obligation or native comparison requires
repair. Missing confidential information and unresolved legal meaning qualify
their conclusion while allowing the remaining engineering phases to execute.
The observed defects were parser, dependency and evidence-boundary failures;
they did not invalidate the target question or the formal research direction.
Earlier passing attempts are retained, but changed method identities require a
fresh program run.

## Strongest remaining alternative explanation

The system may correctly execute an incomplete or wrongly interpreted legal
specification. Formal preservation, exact arithmetic, finite runtime checks and
source hashes do not eliminate that explanation. The public-document example
therefore remains qualified, with every missing catalog obligation visible.
Nine catalog entries still lack a completed executable interpretation or the
necessary source coverage. This is an implementation limit, not a claim that
all seven substantive legal layers are complete.

The strongest evidence that would overturn the engineering result is a retained
counterexample to an input, dependency, temporal, formal or runtime invariant.
The strongest evidence that could expand a legal conclusion would be an
independently checked source-meaning/applicability argument and complete factual
support for the explicitly bounded question. Neither is replaced by human
grading. The future-observation series currently has zero real observations;
generated changes and unfamiliar names do not establish future legal accuracy.
