# Frozen candidate review

The optional selected-fixed-maturity-calculation.v1 route delegates the complete
unchanged coupon request to fixed_term. The prior module and legacy routes are
unchanged. It adds a separate source-profile calculation with exact principal
eligibility per account. Principal is calculation units times the printed final
amount; coupon and principal entries remain separate, and totals use exact
rationals. Eligibility is not copied from coupon flags.

The full14 source groups equal the reviewed packaged profile, with a digest
comparison as well as structural equality so Boolean/numeric source-coordinate
substitution cannot exploit Python equality. Each complete source quotation must
have exactly one unchanged assembly occurrence in OPERATIVE or UNKNOWN roles;
its actual role is retained. No source applicability is inferred from that role.
The output explicitly preserves conditional applicability, no legal entitlement
finding, no actual cancellation/payment and no transaction permission.

All nine extra conditions require literal True. Payment events have unique IDs,
kinds and UTC timestamp/order pairs; presentation precedes payment, due date and
payment date match the source date and hypothetical calendar. UTC-only dates are
a bounded convenience choice; non-UTC input is refused rather than converted
silently. Unknown/false conditions require another supported analysis; this
calculator cannot resolve those legal branches. Source amounts, calendar and
note-form/holdings remain separately visible.

The independent checks parse declared integral holdings and compute source amounts
in integer pence; they do not call production arithmetic or date functions. The
baseline is the complete current coupon-only answer on the same hypothesis graph,
plus all six original011 complete answers. New tests check source-role preservation,
omitted/duplicate/excluded source copies, numeric profile corruption, entitlement
divergence, event boundaries and alias isolation. The ordinary and installed worker
retain whole answers/P5 products and individual malformed-input/answer refusals.

Pre-run skeptical audit PASS: no hidden par assumption, source role promotion,
coupon-entitlement substitution or aggregate-only comparator. Package/source
byte equality and import-origin checks address the shared interpreter risk.
Tests are deterministic checks, not statistical comparisons or legal acceptance.
The runtime wrapper and its admission review are bound in the task; method freeze
also retains their identities. Failed branches remain available for honest result
analysis, and independent ready stages can proceed under the original limits.
