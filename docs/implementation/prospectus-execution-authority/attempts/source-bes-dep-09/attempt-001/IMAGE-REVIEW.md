# 2010 page range includes distinct report roles

Six original images were inspected: English pp92/94/96/190/191 and Portuguese
p192. Both retained editions number the listed pages physically as printed.
The English statutory consolidated audit is entirely on p190, dated 4 March
2011; p191 is the separate Audit Committee report dated 7 March 2011. The
prospectus table's heading `Auditors' report 190 to 191` therefore spans two
different report roles. A page-range label must not make the committee itself
a statutory auditor or transfer reasonable assurance to its concurrence.

English p190 retains its IFRS opinion and paragraph 8's separate conclusion on
other legal reporting requirements. English p191 retains `to the best of their
knowledge`, its discussion of both individual and consolidated reports, and
concurrence with the statutory opinions. These separate scopes and dates match
the already consumed Portuguese reports. Portuguese p192 contains the statutory
opinion, paragraph 8 and 4 March date; copying pp190–191 there omits these.
The Portuguese committee occupies pp193–194. The English audit states that it
is a free translation. Bilingual legal equivalence remains unadjudicated.

The listed English financial statements are present. A finite image check
confirms 2010 assets of EUR83,655,427 thousand, profit attributable to Bank equity
holders of EUR510,520 thousand (total profit EUR656,991 thousand), and closing
cash/equivalents of EUR1,341,403 thousand. These are distinct accounting totals;
the source relation must preserve their subjects and units. Full table and
cross-edition numerical validation are not established by these checks.

The original 6 June 2011 supplement distinguishes these Bank accounts from BES
Finance accounts and from the May quarterly release. Its pp2–3 retain the
informational-only exclusion for unlisted pages. The current task verifies reuse
of the unchanged Portuguese actual consumer and original unresolved BES-DEP-09
answer, not a new English consumer or full legal-incorporation decision.
