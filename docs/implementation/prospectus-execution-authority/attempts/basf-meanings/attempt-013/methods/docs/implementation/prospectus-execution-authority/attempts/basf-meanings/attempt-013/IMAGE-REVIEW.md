# Default(f) source and hypothesis review

The governing German126–127 and133 originals were inspected during the
immediately preceding body review in this session. The English OptionI82 and
final7 originals were viewed again for this package. All five images and both
PDFs are identified in original-sources.json.

German127(f) joins cessation and disposal with a comma, then prints both
financial consequences with 'dadurch' causal qualifications. English82(f)
prints 'operations or sells or disposes ... and thus (i) ... and (ii) for this
reason ...'. The latter is comparison evidence. The checked final7 selection
is German and English with German controlling; German133 says the German text
is binding and authoritative and the English translation is nonbinding.

German126's issuer block introduces the holder's immediate-redemption demand
plus accrued interest. German127's following cure clause extinguishes the
right if its ground is cured before exercise. Its section9(2) condition applies
to(b)/(c) and evaluates the stated other grounds when the declaration arrives.
The one-tenth fraction is visually complete despite its split extraction.
The written German/English declaration must be delivered personally or by
registered mail to the Fiscal Agent's designated office. This source review
does not establish an office, eligible holder, actual declaration or event.

The three retained constructions are hypotheses from meanings005, now made
explicit. Causal effects of cessation, disposal and combined conduct are kept
separate. This is deliberate: a true disposal-related loss does not prove a
cessation-related loss. Candidate agreement is bounded by this nonexhaustive
set. The receipt-time residual eligibility premise remains explicit and
unknown by default. None of these modeling choices supplies independent legal
construction or changes any ordinary source role/answer.
