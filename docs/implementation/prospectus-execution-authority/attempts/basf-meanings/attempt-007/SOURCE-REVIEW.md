# Four selected governance sentences

The original rendered base130 page was inspected. Its selected German paragraph
states that the common representative has tasks and powers conferred by law or
holders' majority resolution; must follow holders' instructions; must report on
its activities; and is subject to the SchVG for removal and its other rights and
duties. The source-packet.json file retains each complete sentence's selected
occurrence spans. The intervening individual-assertion restriction from
attempt006 is untouched, including its express-permission exception and seven
per-right applications.

The alternative appointment-in-terms text and its ten-times-remuneration liability
cap are excluded by the existing final-term selection. They do not enter this
packet. The selected resolution-appointment branch remains a contractual
possibility, not evidence of an actual appointed person, effective majority
resolution, mandate scope or representative action.

The governance_kind labels are supplied interpretations, tested against this
source. Exact source binding and typed-output validation cannot establish that a
legal reading is true. The ordinary explanation must therefore retain
NOT_ESTABLISHED for actual authority and compliance. It must expose the
statutory-reference edge and leave applicable statutory requirements unresolved.
The retained modern SchVG review provides useful context but does not establish
the controlling historical text or actual legal requirements for this instrument.

Full sentences cross existing line boundaries; instruction/reporting text shares
lines25/28 with the already interpreted restriction. Only four previously UNKNOWN
source roles are promoted to the reviewed operative role. Sentence-level cuts
must preserve all earlier supplied meanings and leave all other unknown text
visible. The final exact-character comparison, rather than a shrinking unit
count alone, determines whether the source representation is acceptable.

The new effect type is a declared rule description. It has no computation of
actual performance or appointment, no principal-loss/conversion effect and no
new financial action. Its ordinary-answer display is required so that supplying
these meanings does not merely relabel them as effect=none and hide them from
the user. The remaining source investigation includes adjacent procedural
paragraphs; this package is not a complete section11 analysis.
