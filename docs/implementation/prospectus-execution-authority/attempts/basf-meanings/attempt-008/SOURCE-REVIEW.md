The issuance permission requires the same terms subject to the listed issue
date, interest commencement and issue-price exceptions, yielding one series.
The purchase permission does not establish that a purchase occurred. After
purchase the issuer may hold, resell or submit notes for cancellation; submission
is not completed cancellation. A public purchase offer must extend to all
holders. The wording establishes the recipients of that offer, not equality of
every subsequent trade price. Cancellation and the ban on reissue/resale apply
to fully repaid notes, not to any purchased or partially repaid notes.

These supplied readings do not compute or prove a debt balance, an issuer-owned
voting exclusion or a quorum denominator. The earlier ICSD-record adjustment
reading and its unresolved German/English discrepancy remain unchanged. A
principal-loss power does not follow from this full-repayment cancellation
sentence. Actual transactions, record entries, statutory requirements and legal
acceptance require separate evidence.
