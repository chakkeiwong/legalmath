# Source reading and conditional derivation

The retained source098 complete WpHG section 2 text, lines 113–129, and source102
complete sections 4 and 5 were inspected again. Their original body/receipt/text
hashes are checked through the inherited issuer/election source context. The
latest complete consumer baseline is source106; its ordinary and installed
answers are equal. Source108 did not obtain the WpAV text. No request was retried.

Let C mean the hypothetical number-1 securities class, S the seat category, A the
issuer-wide organised-market admission category, and L applicability of the
displayed reading. Section 2(13)1(a) supplies a German route exactly when

    C and S=Germany and A≠none and L.

This encodes the three conditions in the displayed paragraph. Section 5(1)
expressly imposes publication on an issuer whose German home state follows from
number 1(a). Section 4(3), in contrast, makes publication constitutive for an
election. Importing that election condition into the automatic route would add a
conjunct absent from the inspected automatic provision. The consumer reports no
finding that the automatic issuer fulfilled its publication duty.

For the provisional route, let D mean German admission. Structural category 1(b)
is C, third-country seat and D. Structural category 2 is the supplied other-
instrument issuer class, with either German seat and any EU/EEA admission, or
non-German seat and D. Section 4(1) permits category 1(b) if there was no prior
other choice or admission in the previously chosen state ceased. Section 4(2)
also permits category 2 when a continuing prior other choice lies outside the
last three years. These histories concern eligibility at evaluation, not the
time of the older choice analyzed in the election106 answer.

Let E be that eligibility and N mean no effective German or other EU/EEA choice
at evaluation. Section 2(13)3 supplies the provisional route exactly when

    E and D and N and L.

The additional D matters: an issuer in category 2 with German seat and foreign-
only admission may be structurally eligible to choose Germany, but fails the
separate German-admission conjunct of number 3. An effective German election
also ends this provisional route; the existing continuing-election route remains
available. Thus a negative provisional result cannot be read as a negative
German home-state classification.

These equivalences are conditional deductions from the stated reading and input
categories. They are not proofs of raw issuer classification, historical law or
foreign election effectiveness. In particular the number-1 securities class is
supplied; the code does not calculate denominations, issue-date FX or mixed-issue
issuer classification. The OTHER_EU_EEA_ONLY category has the same other-state
scope as the downstream foreign-duty premise.

The production evaluator enumerates every completion of missing values over the
declared finite domains. Its YES/NO results require all completions to agree;
mixed completions return UNKNOWN. Explicit conflicting premises return CONFLICT.
The separate checker uses structural and history tables with its own category
constants. Exhaustive complete-world agreement plus explicit missing/conflict
checks establish agreement for the implemented finite reading. Both methods
share this source reading, so agreement cannot independently establish its
historical applicability or exhaustiveness.

A proved German route feeds GERMANY to section 2(14). Failed and unknown routes
leave the home state unknown; they never supply NEITHER or an invented foreign
state. The admission category is projected into section 2(14) from the same
scenario. Therefore a test cannot combine a German-only route with a stale
foreign-only downstream admission. The inherited foreign transparency-duty
premise and the section 2(14) exception remain operative. The derived issuer
result then reaches the existing publication consumer with no selection of an
actual contractual interpretation.

The strongest alternative explanation for a passing test is a shared mistaken
source reading or misclassified supplied premise. An applicable historical
edition, relevant implementing rule, or counterexample to these source
conditions would require revision. Counts and runtime are engineering
diagnostics, not evidence of complete legal coverage.
