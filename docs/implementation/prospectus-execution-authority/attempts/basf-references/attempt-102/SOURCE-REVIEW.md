## What the displayed provisions say

Section4(1) concerns the issuer category in section2(13)1(b). It permits a German
election if no other home state has already been elected, or if the securities
are no longer admitted to any organised market in the previously chosen state.
The election lasts until either the securities cease to have German organised
market admission, instead have other EU/EEA organised-market admission and a
new home state is chosen, or there is no remaining EU/EEA organised-market
admission. The first expiry alternative is a conjunction; delisting in Germany
alone is not the whole stated alternative.

Section4(2) concerns section2(13)2. Election is permitted if no other state was
chosen within the preceding three years, or if the financial instruments are
no longer admitted to any organised market in the previously chosen state. The
choice lasts until the issuer issues admitted securities of section2(13)1, no
EU/EEA organised-market admission remains, or a new home state is chosen under
sentence3. Sentence3 permits a new choice when German admission has ceased and
other EU/EEA admission exists, or when other EU/EEA admission exists and at least
three years have passed since choosing Germany. These two different three-year
conditions concern different elections and cannot share an unexplained timer.
No calendar-anniversary arithmetic or issuer-wide admission facts were proved.

Section4(3) says the choice becomes effective with publication under section5.
Section4(4) authorises more detailed regulations about the election. Thus an
eligible choice, a publication event, continuing choice and complete compliance
are different questions. A downstream model must not silently convert every
section5 duty into an extra independent condition of election effectiveness.

Section5(1) covers the automatic German home-state category in section2(13)1(a)
and issuers choosing Germany under section4(1)/(2). It requires prompt
publication, and additionally prompt delivery to the company-register operator
for registration and forwarding to the European single access point, plus
notification to BaFin. It also requires notification to the competent authority
of an other EU/EEA seat state when applicable and to the authority in an other
EU/EEA state of organised-market admission when applicable. Its references to
Article24 of Directive2004/109/EC do not name the actual receiving authorities.
Section5(2) authorises detailed publication regulations. The parsed links supply
navigation only; they do not identify those implementing rules.

## Consequence for the existing consumer

The section2(14) qualification model already accepts a hypothetical home-state
category. These sources expose how one declared German-election route could
qualify that premise: correct section2(13) category, initial permission, an
actual hypothetical choice, publication under section5 and unexpired choice.
Route category, qualifying publication, prior elections, market admission and
time-period predicates would need explicit inputs. Absent or inconsistent
inputs must remain UNKNOWN or CONFLICT. Failure of this German-election route
does not establish which other home state applies; the automatic/default
section2(13) routes and other states' laws remain distinct.

The source text displays a European-single-access-point forwarding requirement.
This attempt does not establish its commencement date or applicability to
2022/2023. More generally these are downloaded consolidated provisions, not a
proved historical edition or proof that every displayed amendment was in force
at any particular date. Headers establish transfer metadata only. The historical
edition, implementing regulations, actual elections and recipient identities
remain open. No production logic or actual issuer conclusion changes here.

Post-review challenge: a simple conjunction could calculate the declared
premises correctly while selecting the wrong issuer category or treating
publication compliance as known. Any later integration must preserve those
premises explicitly, retain the source and temporal limits, compare the actual
downstream answer, and distinguish route failure from a complete home-state
classification. A count of acquired sections cannot establish legal coverage.
