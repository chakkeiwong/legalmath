## H16 — WpÜG30(2), coordinated action

then supplies its own control threshold: more than50% of BASF voting rights.
The retained WpÜG30(2) includes coordination by agreement or otherwise, the

actual concert facts, selected final terms, rating and notices remain unknown.

and retain both the individual-case alternative and all later prerequisites.

## H28 — BGB801, presentation and limitation

Read BGB801 in full with802.801(1) sentence1 concerns presentation within30years

## A2 — FATCA statutes, agreements and implementation

The Treasury portal lists Germany as Model1 in force11December2013. Its linked

institutions;4(5) can isolate a noncompliant related branch only with multiple
