# Source-to-model design following the twelve readings

This is a design for a separately reviewed implementation, not production
admission or proof of legal application. It preserves all60 original records,
47 reviewed links covering48, and the twoUNKNOWN headings. The new twelve
records remain distinct from contract-to-contract links. Defined-term discovery
and unqualified uses remain unfinished. Source presence is not resolved law.

## Smallest reusable representation

Introduce an optional `statutory_references` input whose entries bind a contract
referring occurrence/span to (a) citation identity, (b) retained legal authority
and provenance, (c) edition evidence, (d) literal extent and onward scope, and
(e) application premises and unresolved alternatives. Do not fabricate OPERATIVE
statute occurrences in the contract graph or route a statute to contract section5.

The reusable validator should reconstruct exact referring spans against the
selected contract. Authority evidence must have its own source namespace and
raw/extracted identities, exact quotations and locations. An official-host
acquisition receipt establishes provenance and availability, not execution,
historical validity or complete interpretation. An HTML portal returned for a
statute is an unavailable substantive target, even with HTTP200.

Keep separate values for retrieved source edition; proven, reconstructed or
unknown historical validity; event/issue dates; legal knowledge date; and local
acquisition. Source acquisition must never populate an earlier knowledge date.
Represent dynamic wording explicitly and retain the event-date premise. A
reconstructed version must preserve the amendment sources and exact transform;
unknown historical validity must survive even when source text is available.

Literal extent distinguishes a named subsection/sentence, a complete numbered
range, multiple loci, `ff.`, and an unspecified statutory set. An explicitly
finite literal citation can still have unresolved onward dependencies. An open
literal extent or a named missing onward authority cannot produce CLOSED scope.
Keep located subsets, onward questions and completeness independently visible.

Application construction remains a reviewed conditional statement with named
jurisdiction, instrument, dates, purpose, premises, exceptions and alternatives.
Use `law_facts` only where its inspected source-bound dated predicate contract
fits. It evaluates supplied constructions; it does not establish them. Do not
use a single `valid_resolution`, `affiliate` or `FATCA_exempt` bit to conceal the
source conditions. Where this package cannot establish a construction, expose
the uncertainty and source reading rather than invent an executable legal rule.

Admission must validate the declared supported source profile by reconstruction,
not merely hash an arbitrary caller's statement. A reusable carrier alone cannot
prove that its supplied legal reading is correct. Distinguish generic schema
checks from the finite source-reviewed profile and from automatic discovery.

## Integration and independently checked consumption

`reviewed_references.admit` currently treats contract targets as OPERATIVE (or
UNKNOWN in v3); leave this invariant intact. `service.dependencies` should admit
legal authorities separately and add typed dependencies plus question-local
uncertainties. Those uncertainties must bind P3/P4 and reach the actual ordinary
Q1/Q2 answer. The independent consumer must reconstruct every admitted source
record and compare the exact dependency set and legal uncertainty set. A
report-only attachment does not meet the integration criterion.

Propagate the optional field consistently through contracts' request allowlist,
service interpretation allowlist, interpretation construction, export/native
preservation, P3 dependency reconstruction and P4/ordinary-consumer verification.
Existing automatic relation discovery must not mark a statutory reference as
closed through a coincidentally numbered contract clause. Covered discovery
replacement needs the same explicit span identity as prior reviewed links.

For initial source availability with unproved2022 editions, the correct supported
answer can be an explained UNKNOWN. It should distinguish missing source,
unproved edition, open scope and missing application facts, rather than retain
only the old EXTERNAL_SOURCE_MISSING label. A known current-law reading must
never promote an unconditional holder entitlement or silently change unrelated
financial or contract answers.

## Exact comparator and acceptance

The comparator is the full saved021 ordinary answers plus022's controls and
reconciliation, not an invented new022 answer. Compare complete unaffected
P4/P5 and final answers under a precisely enumerated semantic projection. Only
new legal-reference evidence and its explicitly caused uncertainties may differ;
IDs, contract terms, financial values and other conclusions must not drift.
Keep actual refusal errors and distinguish a harness failure from a source or
model failure. No new test count closes the original60-record/discovery task.

The source/design seal checks retention only. A later implemented-method review
must freeze schema, finite source profile, independent checker, baseline, trial
commands and bounds before same-task admission. It must challenge unsupported
current-law application, caller-controlled findings, report-only integration,
unknown removal, and uncontrolled source/profile growth. Record measured work
and unknown authoring/controller time honestly; no new directory resets limits.

Skeptical design audit: PASS for this staged representation and retention of
unknowns. Implementation promotion remains pending. The weakest evidence is
historical application and onward law, so no schema or successful execution can
supply those missing propositions. Internal reconstruction and adverse behavior
are build requirements; an external adviser opinion is not required.
