## What the inspected provision says

The complete retained section50(1) begins with an Inlandsemittent. That is a
statutory issuer category; German seat alone is not a proved substitute for
the category. It requires immediate publication of changes to rights attached
to admitted securities. Paragraph1 number1(a) covers specified derivative
rights for admitted shares; number1(b) covers changes to terms of non-share
securities, including interest rates or related conditions, insofar as their
rights are indirectly affected. Number2 covers information the issuer publishes
in a third country that may matter to the public in the EU and EEA.

The same paragraph requires simultaneous notification of the publication to
the Bundesanstalt and simultaneous transmission of the information to the
entity operating the Unternehmensregister, for inclusion and onward delivery
to the central European access portal. The wording describes transmission to
the register operator; it does not say that the issuer itself must perform
the operator's onward delivery. Paragraph2 authorizes more detailed rules on
minimum content, method, language, extent and form. Those rules, the statutory
issuer definition and their historical editions were not acquired in this
phase. Their requirements cannot be invented or marked satisfied.

The contract's citation establishes a source-dependent alternative. It does
not establish that every resolution falls within section50(1), that the issuer
is an Inlandsemittent, or that an actual website publication satisfies the
statutory method and concurrent reporting duties. A correct conditional model
must keep these premises separate. The reach of the statutory alternative
relative to the additional section13 duty needs an explicit interpretation;
report alternative readings if that reach is not justified, instead of
silently dropping section13. The separate website requirement survives either
reading.
