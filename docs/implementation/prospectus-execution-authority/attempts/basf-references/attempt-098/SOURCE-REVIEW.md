WpHG2(1) includes bearer and order debt securities within transferable securities,
subject to the opening definition. Subsection11 defines an organised market;
subsection12 defines a third country. These categories must not be replaced by
ordinary-language notions of exchange trading or foreign incorporation.

Subsection13 distinguishes three German home-state routes. Number1 addresses
shares and debt denominations below EUR1,000 (or equivalent at issue): its
German-seat branch requires admission to an organised market in Germany or the
EU/EEA; its third-country-seat branch requires German admission and a German
home-state election under section4(1). Number2 covers other financial instruments:
the German-seat or German-admission alternatives are qualified by election under
section4(2). Number3 provides a provisional route for issuers eligible under
number1(b) or number2, with German admission, while no effective home-state
election under sections4/5 or the corresponding EU/EEA provisions exists.
Sections4/5 and foreign-law election effectiveness have not been inspected here.

Subsection14 then defines Inlandsemittent by two routes. Number1 includes German
home-state issuers, but excludes the stated case of admission only in another
EU/EEA state, without German admission, insofar as the issuer is subject there to
publication and notification duties under Directive2004/109/EC. Number2 includes
issuers whose home state is another EU/EEA state and whose securities are admitted
to an organised market only in Germany. The exception and exclusive-admission
conditions matter. German seat alone does not establish this status. An unknown
home state or applicable exception must remain unknown in conditional analysis.

The HTML's extracted links are navigation links; sections4/5 are textual cross
references, not captured hyperlinks. Their absence from links.json does not
close those dependencies. Subsections15–18 define distinct issuer/market/service
categories; none may silently substitute for subsection14.
