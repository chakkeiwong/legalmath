# Declared election calculation and its proof boundary

The target is the continuing German-election route under the displayed
section4(1) or section4(2), conditional on the supplied category, choice,
publication and applicability premises. It is not the issuer's complete
home-state classification. The prior-election category refers to the German
choice time; admission and termination facts refer to the evaluation time.
Section4(3)'s publication requirement remains distinct from eligibility and
expiry. Section5's additional notifications are not silently added as extra
effectiveness conditions. Source102's complete operative review supplies the
legal reading; no historical commencement date or actual election is proved.

For route1(b), let P mean category membership and permitted prior history,
C the German choice, U qualifying publication, A applicability, O admission
only in another EU/EEA state, N a qualifying new choice, and Z no EU/EEA
admission. The declared result is P and C and U and A and not((O and N) or Z).
P's permitted prior categories are no prior other choice and ceased admission
in the previously chosen state. This is section4(1)'s two permission alternatives
and two duration endpoints, with section4(3)'s effectiveness condition.

For route2, let F mean other-EU/EEA admission, G mean no German admission,
T mean at least three years since choosing Germany, and I admitted issuance
of section2(13)1 securities. The declared endpoint is
I or Z or (N and F and (G or T)). The continuing result again conjoins
P,C,U,A and negates that endpoint. P also permits the prior-other-admission-
continues category when no other choice was made within the preceding three
years. This historical choice test is different from T. Admission uses four
mutually exclusive categories, preventing independent facts from describing
both no EU/EEA admission and some other EU/EEA admission.

The candidate evaluates these formulas on every completion of the supplied
finite inputs. YES means every completion is true; NO means every completion
is false; UNKNOWN means both occur. An explicit conflict anywhere in the
declared input produces CONFLICT for this review, including explanatory
intermediate statuses. This conservative conflict policy is intentional, not
proof that every contradictory premise is outcome-determinative. Unsupported
keys, categories, Boolean substitutes or routes are refused.

The separate verifier uses a four-entry prior-history permission table and
admission-indexed duration/permission tables. With category choices fixed,
initial eligibility and expiry depend on disjoint Boolean inputs, so its
staged truth-set combination retains their possible outcomes. Its final union
over category choices matches complete-world enumeration. Tests compare all
512 fully specified route1(b) worlds and2048 route2 worlds, four-state
combinations of the common Boolean premises, open categorical inputs, explicit
conflicts and source-specific boundary examples. These deterministic checks
establish agreement for the declared mapping; the source reading and supplied
predicate meanings remain shared assumptions, not independent legal truth.

Only YES supplies GERMANY downstream. NO and UNKNOWN leave home_state unknown;
CONFLICT stays conflict. The implication is sound under the declared premises
because the modeled route is sufficient for its hypothetical German-election
conclusion, while failure does not exclude automatic/default/foreign routes.
The existing section2(14) foreign-admission exception and publication readings
then operate on that derived premise. The actual CLI comparison must verify
this implication and every preceding answer field; a formula test alone cannot
prove successful product integration.
