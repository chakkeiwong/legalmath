## What the sections actually say

BGB206 suspends limitation while force majeure prevents the creditor from
pursuing the right within the last six months of the limitation period. Force
majeure alone is insufficient: prevention of pursuit and the stated temporal
condition are also required. This is a suspension rule, not an automatic
six-month addition. The retained section contains one operative sentence and no
separately stated exception.

BGB210(1) addresses a person who lacks full legal capacity and has no legal
representative. A limitation period running for or against that person cannot
expire before six months after unrestricted legal capacity or remedy of the
representation defect. If the limitation period is shorter than six months,
that shorter period replaces the six months. BGB210(2) excludes paragraph1 to
the extent that a person of limited legal capacity has procedural capacity.
The qualification is expressly about a person of limited capacity; it must not
be generalized to every person addressed by paragraph1. This is protection
against expiry, not a statement that the entire clock stops running.

BGB211 addresses a claim belonging to or directed against an estate. Limitation
cannot expire before six months after the inheritance is accepted by the heir,
estate insolvency proceedings are opened, or the claim can be asserted by or
against a representative. The text also substitutes the shorter limitation
period where it is shorter than six months. These are alternative statutory
triggers. Their interaction on actual facts, including any priority between
events, has not been established here. The text does not establish that every
death suspends every claim or creates a universal six-month extension.

## The antecedent in802 and the corresponding application

The complete retained023 text of802 first suspends the beginning and running
of presentation and limitation in the applicant's favour. Suspension starts
with the application for a payment stop. The next sentence states its ending
conditions, including the case where the stop predates initiation of the
public-notice proceedings: six months have elapsed since removal of the
impediment to initiation and initiation was not previously requested. The final
sentence says that206,210 and211 apply correspondingly to "diese Frist".

The local grammatical reading is that this phrase refers to that immediately
preceding six-month period. It does not itself establish direct application of
all three provisions to every presentation or limitation clock. Corresponding
application could protect pursuit of initiation against force majeure, lack of
capacity/representation or an estate situation. Mapping the creditor,
enforcement act, protected person and estate alternatives onto that procedural
period requires a separate legal construction. The statute's original words
and the local construction must remain separately identified. The current
wording supports a bounded conditional source reading; it proves neither the
historical2022 edition nor applicability to a particular instrument or event.
