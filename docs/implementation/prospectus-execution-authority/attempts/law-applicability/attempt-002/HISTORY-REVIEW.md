# What the primary2024 amendment establishes

The primary Bundesgesetzblatt2024 I No.323 PDF was obtained from recht.bund.de
using the exact publication citation in the consolidation. The candidate URL was
constructed from that citation and checked against the downloaded document; it
was not a followed link from the consolidation. This is the third successful
official GET in the six-request bound. No credentials or third-party uploads
were used. The original35-page PDF and receipt are retained.

Article25 on PDF page15 expressly identifies the SchVG's prior amendment as
Article5 of the Act of3 June2021, BGBl.I p.1423. It changes only:

- §9(1) sentence2: written form becomes text form for the minority request.
- §15(2) sentence1: text form is added for the attendance register.
- §15(2) sentence3: the chair's signature requirement is removed.

Article74(1) on PDF page34 uses the first day of the quarter following
promulgation, subject to paragraphs2–13. Those exceptions were checked in full
through page35; none identifies Article25. The publication is dated29 October2024.
The resulting commencement date for Article25 is therefore1 January2025. That
date is a derivation from the publication and commencement rule, not the signing
date23 October2024. Pages15 and34 were also visually checked against the original
PDF, and the text of paragraph13 on page35 was read.

These primary sources give a concrete, checkable backward-change instruction
for§§9 and15. The current consolidation's substantive majority, representative,
implementation and challenge provisions are not changed by this2024 act. The
stated last prior amendment helps trace2022 wording, but the original2021 act and
an independently authenticated historical consolidation are not retained here.
We therefore do not silently promote the current diagnostic into an actual2022
legal application. A reconstructed historical derivative must be labelled as a
derivation, carry both source identities, reverse exactly these edits and prove
forward equivalence. Actual issue, resolution and proceeding dates remain absent.

The attempted diagnostic-interpreter PDF read failed before creating an output
because PyMuPDF is not installed in that interpreter. The already installed
pdftotext utility successfully extracted the primary PDF; pdftoppm rendered the
reviewed pages. No dependency was installed and no package was declared faulty.
The failure and fallback concern tool availability, not the legal interpretation.
