# Declared majority and quorum thresholds now have exact arithmetic

The production legal evaluator can now derive a declared ratio predicate from
source-bound eligible totals. P4 accepts the optional ratio_facts input and the
actual current final consumer retains the operands, units, event, dates, source
anchors and exact comparison in Q4. Inputs without the option retain their
previous behavior. This closes the specifically identified arithmetic capability
gap; the original legal-applicability task remains incomplete.

The retained BASF/SchVG development model supplies the rule interpretations.
Exact rational comparison distinguishes a simple-majority tie, at least 75
percent, the first-meeting half-principal quorum, and values immediately below
these boundaries. A large integer case remains below 75 percent despite the
rounding risk in binary floating-point division. Vote majority uses eligible
participating voting weight; quorum uses represented versus eligible outstanding
principal. These denominators are supplied observations, not classifications
inferred from raw ballots, abstentions, affiliates or holder claims.

Eighteen real-source conditional cases reach the actual current consumer with
their expected results; five invalid inputs are refused. Exact majority and
quorum pass, simple-majority ties and insufficient quorum fail, missing/future
totals remain UNKNOWN, and inconsistent tallies produce CONFLICT. Passing
arithmetic with missing eligibility or other legal prerequisites remains
UNKNOWN. Expressly including suspended votes still fails the existing legal
screen. The measure-9 example preserves contract-majority YES versus
statutory-majority NO under a simple-majority vote. All twelve necessary families
remain visible; no YES is described as proof of an effective amendment.

The final focused suite passes 176 tests. Final-method revalidation checks all
18 numeric answers and 13 legacy answers through current consumption; all
retain their expected Q1–Q6 content. A correctly rehashed P4 whose calculation
explanation was falsified is refused by current reassessment. Tests also cover
unavailable/altered sources, intervals, missing operands, literal value binding,
wrong identities/units, mixed meetings across different predicates, malformed
numbers/operators and Boolean overrides. Conflicting observations cannot be
silently overwritten by a supplied Boolean for the same computed predicate.

Two driver failures remain retained. Preparing the hypothetical observation
document used a substring match that confused half=50 with abovehalf=50.001;
exact labelled-line selection repaired the ambiguity without a production
change. The first numeric stage then wrongly required all of Q3 to stay equal

facts. A reviewed comparison correction reused nine complete retained answers
and ran only the unfinished cases, checking Q1/Q2/Q5/Q6 equality and exact
propagation of Q4's unresolved list into Q3. Neither failed command is counted
as a successful execution. Final code review also closed cross-predicate event

The claimed numerical target is the comparison of two nonnegative finite
decimal quantities against a declared rational threshold. The implemented cross
multiplication equals that target when the denominator is positive; missing or
zero-denominator evidence supplies no Boolean. CODE-REVIEW.md records the short
derivation and final event-scope repair. It does not establish the legal
interpretation of a quoted rule. The strongest alternative explanation for a
misleading legal answer remains wrong supplied eligibility, event identity,
statutory applicability or threshold selection. Actual records, controlling
historical sources and independent legal review would be needed to resolve it.
