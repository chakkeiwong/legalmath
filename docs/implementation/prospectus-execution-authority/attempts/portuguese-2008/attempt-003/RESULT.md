# Portuguese audit roles: finite optional integration passes

The actual current consumer reproduced a source-role error in our existing
Portuguese profile. It supported a statutory audit opinion when the supplied
conclusion quoted only the board's preparation duties. For both the consolidated
and individual audits, it also supported conclusions with the opinion's
materiality words omitted, conclusions contaminated by board duties, and
assurance facts quoting board duties. Exact quotation binding alone did not
establish that the quotation performed the asserted role.

The explicit optional `portuguese-report-context.v2` repairs that finite
integration gap for the three inspected Portuguese2008 readings. The two audit
readings must retain all nonspace characters of paragraph7 and the paragraph4
assurance sentence in their respective facts. Supplied nonspace characters
outside the corresponding role interval also prevent support. The profile
retains separate source-mapped board duties, auditor responsibility, examination,
concordance, opinion basis, opinion, assurance and materiality passages. Complete
fragmented quotations remain supported. Full text, original PDF edition hash
and ordered raw-page identities bind the inspected readings; unrecognized or
rebound readings remain unresolved. This is finite source-interval validation,
not a general Portuguese parser or an independent judgment of an audit opinion.

source intervals. The implementation computes coverage of every nonspace source
character and rejects supplied nonspace characters outside those intervals.

source anchors are retained in `source-review.json` under reviews/portuguese-
audit-107 and in each actual context output. They do not prove that a fresh
document has the same structure or that the supplied audit classification is an
independently accepted legal construction. V1 remains explicitly available;
this package makes no silent default change.
