# Independent review preparation

Question: can the currently retained source families supply a genuinely
unexposed independent review cohort, and what concrete preparation can proceed
before real readers and a separate adjudicator are assigned?

The baseline is the current `review_records.admit`, `evaluation.score` and
`prediction_execution.execute` workflow, the classification results and retained
source manifests. Historical directory names such as `fresh` are not exposure
evidence. Cases used for development, predictions or source-answer analysis
cannot become unexposed through renaming or a new freeze date.

Retain an exclusion inventory that binds original document hashes to prior
analysis. Distinguish confirmed exposed cases from cases whose exposure is
unknown. Read existing metadata and historical analysis; do not inspect a new
candidate source or release predictions during review preparation. An absent
match cannot prove independence. Include the already exposed BES, BASF, Shell,
Compass, Deutsche and earlier classification families, keeping uncertainties.

Prepare unsigned registry, assignment, first-reading and adjudication templates
with missing values explicit, and a concrete sequence for the administrator.
Do not fabricate people, qualifications, dates, signatures, exports or labels.
Use the existing actual admission functions to demonstrate that the unfinished
packet cannot be accepted. Run the focused existing review-record controls;
their synthetic identities remain engineering fixtures.

Primary criterion for this partial package: every claimed exclusion has retained
evidence; missing prerequisites are actionable; the unfilled packet is rejected
by the actual admission function; historical exposure cannot be called fresh.
Full task acceptance still needs a real unexposed cohort and authentic independent
readings. Positive synthetic tests are explanatory engineering checks only.
Corrupted source hashes, invented attestations, premature prediction exposure
or missing evidence veto acceptance of this package. Missing actual people
blocks human acceptance and invitations, while other ready tasks continue.

Defaults and assumptions: use the existing two-reader/distinct-adjudicator
protocol, supported by the retained evaluation code. HMAC authenticates the
administrator's attestation; it proves neither qualification nor honesty.
Family aliases and source manifests may be incomplete. Unknown families remain
unresolved rather than being silently classified as unexposed. No statistical
accuracy or ranking follows from the finite workflow checks.

Skeptical audit PASS after rejecting reuse of the historically named fresh
cohorts: the proposed artifacts answer readiness and prevent a false independence
claim. They cannot complete independent legal acceptance. One bounded admitted
package, local CPU-only checks under 120 seconds per command, one-second yields,
under 10 MB new evidence. Preserve command, method/input hashes, results and
decision table in the admitted attempt; record, refresh and continue ACTUS
prerequisites or the next independent ready task.
