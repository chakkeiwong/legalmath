The defect was in our admission model: valid individual anchors were treated as
sufficient for a reference's known complete scope. The repair does not identify
a PDF package defect. Its strongest limitation is intentional dependence on one
reviewed edition and native reading. New source content or segmentation needs
review, not automatic promotion. Exact native coverage still does not prove
that every financial cell or legal implication has been interpreted correctly.
