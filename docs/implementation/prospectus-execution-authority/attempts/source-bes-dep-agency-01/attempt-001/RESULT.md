# Principal Agency Agreement: public search retains an open source requirement

The original base prospectus's printed pp76–78 distinguish the 18 January 2008
amended and restated Principal Agency Agreement from the 18 February 2009
Interbolsa amendment and 3 November 2010 Common Representative agreement.
They name the Issuers, Guarantor, Trustee, Bank of New York Mellon as Agent and
other paying agents. Inspection copies are described as available at the
specified offices of the Trustee, Agent and paying agents; this does not supply
the executed agreement or establish present availability at those offices.

Two bounded archive queries returned eight issuer-domain captures and no
exchange-domain captures. All eight issuer search snippets identify a prospectus
or offering circular, including entries whose titles are merely `untitled` or
typesetting filenames. The 2003 and 2007 records predate the required restatement.
No separately identified executed agreement was nominated. Metadata is retained
as discovery evidence; it cannot prove that agreements are absent from all
archives or attachments. No content download was justified by these results.

The independently different request routes are an exact agreement filing in
the exchange/issuer archive or a supplied inspection copy from the named Agent,
Trustee or paying agent, with date, parties and amendment chain checked. No
external message was sent. Availability and authenticity remain unresolved.

Verified reuse of the retained actual P3/final answer confirms that
BES-DEP-AGENCY-01 still appears unresolved in Q1 and Q2. Existing positive and
adverse consumer evidence remains hash-bound. This was not a new consumer run.

| Decision | Primary criterion | Veto | Main uncertainty | Next justified action | Not concluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain the original-source requirement | No exact agreement identified in two bounded queries | Prospectus mention is insufficient for agreement identity | Original may survive outside indexed public files | Continue Interbolsa-agreement source work; retain specific inspection/archive route | Source nonexistence, package failure or legal completion |

The failure is missing source evidence within this search. It does not
invalidate the engine or establish a fundamental package problem. An executed
agreement with matching date, parties and full amendment history would overturn
the source disposition. The weak part is archive search coverage, so the
disposition is local and conditional. Independent ready work continues.
