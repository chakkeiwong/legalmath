# Optional source-linked explanations through the actual legal consumer

The optional subset-minimal.v1 path now supplies one sufficient, subset-minimal
set of observed Boolean premises for each supported YES/NO legal-basis result.
It includes the exact authority occurrence, all active observation or successful
calculation supports for each retained premise, and an opposite-valued Boolean
counterexample after deleting each retained premise. It reaches both P4 and
the normal full answer in current and frozen modes.

This repairs a local missing explanation capability. No external package defect
was found or inferred. The method specializes Algorithm1 of Ignatiev et al.
(AAAI2019) to the existing finite domain. The retained paper and original-author
XReason code were inspected; the latter is a later tree-ensemble implementation,
not a replication of the paper's NN experiment. No new package was installed.

## Actual evidence

- Single focused process:282 tests; zero failures, errors or skips.
- Independent bitset corpus:4096 cases,16 declared expressions, all four-state
  assignments of four facts;1722 explanation proofs,485 UNKNOWN and1889 CONFLICT.
- Original BASF cohort:21 full legacy answers unchanged and2 original refusals
  preserved with and without the option;42 option-enabled current/frozen answers.
- Calendar cohort:19 full legacy answers unchanged;38 current/frozen answers.
-136 source-bound basis explanations independently checked for sufficiency,
  subset-minimality, deletion witnesses and source support;16 basis results
  preserve typed non-explanation. The two modes carry the same proofs.
- Adverse tests reject insufficient/nonminimal sets, invented literals, missing
  or false witnesses, incorrect conclusion/identity, extra claims and source
  forgery. Current consumption rejects a rehashed forged explanation.
-12-fact support and13-fact refusal, depth/node limits, irrelevant and relevant
  conflicts, empty supports, negative conclusions, request/overlay routes and
  inactive/future observations were exercised.

The original favorable-deposit example explains notice_registration using
meeting_notice14=True and registration_required=False. This follows from the
declared expression and hypothetical observations; the proof does not establish
that the historical legal interpretation or actual meeting facts are correct.
The unequal-cardinality control returns {b,c} for a OR (b AND c), even though
{a} is shorter. That directly tests the stated subset-minimal claim and its
limit rather than labelling the result 'smallest'.

Strongest alternative explanation: a mathematically correct explanation can
still describe the wrong supplied legal formula. Source occurrences and full
consumer comparisons address attribution and integration, not interpretation
truth. A verified counterexample to sufficiency/minimality, stale/misattributed
support, or changed non-explanation content would overturn this engineering
result. The weakest evidence is semantic completeness of supplied law; it is
explicitly unresolved rather than replaced by an external-adviser requirement.

Explicit Z3 use, more than12 facts, inactive authority, UNKNOWN and CONFLICT
produce typed non-explanation. No minimum-cardinality, unique, causal, aggregate
cross-basis or Z3/core-minimality claim. Frozen consumption preserves a sealed
product's content; current consumption recomputes it. The independent verifier
separately checks the explanations in both actual consumer results.
