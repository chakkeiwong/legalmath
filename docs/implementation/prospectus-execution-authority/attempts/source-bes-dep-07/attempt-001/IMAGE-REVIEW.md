# Edition-specific incorporation boundaries

Seven images were inspected: English pp241 and 245–248, Portuguese pp242 and
249. Printed and physical numbering agree in the examined 2008 editions, unlike
the 2009 English offset. The English consolidated audit is pp245–246 and the
individual audit pp247–248, each dated 25 February 2009. The former uses
EU-adopted IFRS; the latter uses Banco de Portugal NCA, with explicit exceptions
under Regulations 1/2005 and 4/2005. Both English reports identify themselves as
free translations of Portuguese originals.

The retained Portuguese complete reports were already image-reviewed and
consumed: consolidated pp245–247, individual pp248–249, committee pp250–251.
The newly inspected p249 confirms the individual opinion and date. Applying the
prospectus's English auditor range 245–248 to the Portuguese edition omits that
opinion. The English notes end on p241 with Note 46; its Portuguese counterpart
is on p242. Copying the prospectus notes range through p241 to Portuguese also
omits a substantive subsequent-events note. These are two distinct edition
boundary counterexamples, not grounds for silently extending legal incorporation.

The Note 46 images reveal a further translation issue. Portuguese p242 says the
9 January 2009 guaranteed debt issue was `1,5 mil milhões de euros`, whereas
English p241 says `euro 1 500 000 million debt`. The literal monetary scale is
inconsistent. Both discuss 80% international placement and demand of 1.9 billion
(English `1,900 million`), which suggests a translation/unit error but does not
authorize a silent correction. Keep the exact original strings and unit conflict
for the existing finance/relation repair. This is a source discrepancy visible
in the PDF image, not established as an OCR-package error.

Reuse current Portuguese actual-consumer evidence only after source, method and
sealed-output verification. That evidence preserves NCA exceptions, both audit
dates, the separate committee date and conditional scope. It does not mean the
English report or whole incorporation relation was newly consumed in this task.
The original dependency must stay unresolved in the retained Q1/Q2 answer.
All-table numeric equivalence and independent legal acceptance remain open.
