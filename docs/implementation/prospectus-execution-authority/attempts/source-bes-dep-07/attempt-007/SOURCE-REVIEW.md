# Five original images and the complete EPS notes inspected

The five 1800-pixel grayscale images are legible. Original English and
Portuguese pages 105–106 contain the full 2008 Note 14; physical page 112 of
the 2009 report is printed page 111. Its full Note 14 includes the preference
dividend footnote beneath the table. These are report images, not the unrelated
page 112 of the prospectus also present in the extraction graph.

Both 2008 notes show 2008 then 2007. Numerators are 402,284 and 607,069 thousand
euros; gross weighted shares are 500,000/500,000 thousand, treasury shares
2,698/4,090 thousand, and outstanding shares 497,302/495,910 thousand. EPS is
0.81/1.22 euros per share. Portuguese uses decimal commas in this note,
whereas its earlier primary statement used decimal dots. The image resolves
the English displaced native labels: outstanding shares and EPS labels follow
their values in extraction order but occupy their own final two table rows.

The 2009 note shows 2009 then 2008: adjusted profit 488,634/368,804 thousand
euros, gross weighted shares 1,000,000/500,000 thousand, treasury 1,826/2,698
thousand, outstanding 998,174/497,302 thousand and EPS 0.49/0.74 euros per
share. Its numerator label carries footnote (1): net profit adjusted by the
dividend from preference shares. The retained primary statements show
Bank-holder profit 522,114/402,284 and preference dividends of 33,480 in both
years. Both numerator differences therefore match the source-named dividend.

All three notes state diluted EPS equals basic because SIBA plans have no
dilutive effect. That statement does not imply potential shares are absent or
that future periods will be nondilutive. The notes' ratio of thousands of
euros to thousands of shares is euros per share. An extra thousand multiplier
would be wrong. The unchanged 2008 comparative denominator and preference
footnote support a numerator explanation; they do not support an inferred
rights-issue adjustment as the cause of this 0.81/0.74 difference.

All 81 full-note occurrences, including headings, units, dates, footnote and
dilution paragraphs, are mapped. The complete 405 page occurrences remain as
context; Notes 13, 15 and 16 visible on the pages have not been newly evaluated
in full. The original statement's 5.58% BES Finance preference dividend label
does not turn the preference shares into prospectus notes or provide standalone
BES Finance accounts. Source scope and unresolved authority remain explicit.

The original 2008 note does not carry the later preference footnote and uses
the full Bank-holder profit as numerator. This observation plus the later
footnote explains the displayed comparative change. It does not decide why
reporting changed, whether IAS 33 was complied with, which edition controls,
or whether an official correction was required. No such authority was read.
