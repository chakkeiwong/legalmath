# Retained page coverage and actual downstream use

The 768-page inventory identified five previously missing Portuguese 2010
report pages, and image review distinguished them from a photographic divider.
The existing OCR and source consumers now retain the consolidated statutory
audit on pages 190–192 and committee report on pages 193–194. The former is
dated 4 March 2011; the latter is dated 7 March 2011. Publication, authenticity
and historical availability remain unknown.

The five-page OCR run retained 1,652 words and passed strict v1 geometry.
Ten supplied body/margin regions preserve every selected nonspace character
exactly once without changing the raw graph. Both reports reached actual
P1/P2/P3 and the final answer. All twelve declared consumer checks passed;
nine adverse controls rejected. The comparison preserves the instrument
questions, contract assembly and dependencies. Hybrid-coverage uncertainty
remains in the final answer. Paragraph 8's separate legal-reporting conclusion
and the committee's knowledge qualification remain visible. Committee assurance
is unknown. Raw OCR noise is retained, including intrusive characters within
the body; region partitioning is not a complete recognition correction.

Three further image-reviewed native regions reached the existing layout
consumer: the 2008 step-up financing row with its first-coupon qualification,
the 2009 foreign-branch list, and the 2009 network-count paragraph. All three
positive regions passed; five altered-qualifier, row, column, count or edition
cases rejected. Supplied regions do not establish automatic table semantics
or label-to-map connectivity.

The twelve inspected images also supplied counterexamples to convenient
triage rules. A low-text image page can be artwork (2008 p6). A large number
of vector drawings can be map dots rather than missing outlined letters (2009
pp21–22). Native-character, image-area and vector counts remain nominations.
All other pages remain unreviewed for semantic completeness unless separate
retained evidence says otherwise.

Post-run review: the strongest alternative explanation is that supplied
image-reviewed classifications make these cases work while automatic semantic
classification remains absent. The result supports assisted consumption only.
A missing qualifier, stale input, omitted selected character or failure to
preserve uncertainty would overturn the corresponding pass. The weakest
coverage evidence is the unreviewed majority of the 768 pages. This is a
bounded source-intake success, not evidence against the research direction
or proof of general OCR accuracy.
