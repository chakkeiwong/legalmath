# Note 27 review of the original images

Original pages 133 and 134 were rendered at 300 dpi and displayed at original
resolution. The separate image transcript was typed directly from those images,
before generating candidate tables. It covers the full two page-133 tables and
the page-134 Note 27 movement table. The rest of page 134 starts Note 28 and is
retained as full-page source context, not claimed as complete semantic coverage.

The financial-information table has 18 named associates, an Others row and an
unlabelled total. The first six columns are assets, liabilities and equity at
June 2010 and December 2009. Income, period profit/loss and acquisition cost have
June 2010 and June 2009 headings. All financial amounts are thousands of euro.
Others and the total have printed acquisition cost only: preceding blank cells
are NOT_PRINTED, distinct from printed dashes represented as unknown. Preserve
the source's qualification that information is adjusted for consolidation purposes.
It does not identify a standalone audit of each associate or current valuation.

The second table has the same 18 named associates plus Others and a total.
Economic interest, book value and share of associates' profit each have June
2010, December 2009 and June 2009 columns. Economic interests are percentages,
not thousands of euro. Blank interest cells for Others/total remain unprinted.
BES VIDA's footnote (b) identifies EUR 267,440 thousand goodwill and EUR 45,052
thousand value-in-force (December 2009: EUR 46,651 thousand). These amounts must
not be added to the reported book value as if excluded. Footnote (a) says the
listed companies with economic interest below 20% use the equity method because
the Group exercises significant influence. This is a source assertion, consistent
with Note 1's distinction; percentage alone does not prove voting power, control
or the accounting treatment's correctness.

UNICRE shows acquisition cost 11,497, book value 19,754 and share of profit 8,260
in June 2010. Those are distinct reported quantities. Note 1 describes the 8,260
as a revaluation gain on initial equity-method application after the additional
holding, so it cannot simply be reclassified as recurring investee earnings.
Its investee-profit cell in the first table is a dash, not zero. The same date
label does not make investee profit and the Group's reported share interchangeable.

The movement table prints 'Six month period ended' above June 2010, December 2009
and June 2009. Its December 2009 share-of-profit entry is 16,792 while the page-133
December 2009 share-of-profit total is 29,977. The difference is 13,185, equal to
the printed June 2009 figure. This is a consistency observation compatible with
different periods, not proof of the report's intended reconciliation. Preserve
both headings and figures rather than replacing either. The June 2010 disposal
dash remains unknown; no balancing calculation establishes it is zero. Signed
fair-value reserves, dividends and foreign-exchange/other amounts remain negative
where printed. The reserve footnote specifically attributes changes to BES Vida.
