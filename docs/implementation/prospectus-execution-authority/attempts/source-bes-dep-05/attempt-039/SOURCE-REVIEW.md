# Related-party disclosures: original source review

The four 200 dpi renders of the retained H1 2010 PDF were displayed and read
before production editing. These are printed pages 154–157. Page 154 introduces
Note 41 with an explicit reference to subsidiaries in Note 1 and IAS 24. The
left column begins with associates and then changes to ESFG's subsidiaries,
associates and related entities; the right column and both columns of page 155
continue that second category. Source occurrences are retained separately.
Similar names, multiple roles and spelling variants do not authorize identity
merges or a conclusion that all related companies are consolidated subsidiaries.
Directory headings, column transitions and endpoints were checked visually;
the machine check proves complete occurrence preservation, not corporate identity.

The image transcript was typed separately from the displayed tables. Page 155
contains ten numeric/unknown columns, first June 2010 and then December 2009,
in the order assets, liabilities, guarantees, income and expenses. It has twenty
named rows and an unlabelled total. The unlabelled total is explicitly identified
as an inferred label. Page 156 has two holding-company tables with separate dates,
nine columns and seventeen named rows plus a printed TOTAL. The four components
under Assets precede Total; guarantees, liabilities, income and expenses are
separate columns. All three tables use thousands of euro. Printed dashes remain
unknown, including the 2009 OTHER bank-advances cell. Numeric positions and row
geometry are checked against the distinct typed transcript, not a transcript
generated from the mapping. A pre-source visual reread corrected the typed 2009
ES CONTACT CENTER guarantee from a dash to the printed 74.

The apparent discrepancy is present in the image. In the 2009 OTHER row the
printed loans, securities and other values are 48,022, 1,219 and 370, while the
printed total is 31,637. The known subtotal is 49,611 and exceeds the displayed
total by 17,974. The full row sum remains unknown because bank advances is a
dash. The aggregate components are 57,055, 690,405, 157,159 and 19,360; these sum
to 923,979 against a printed total of 906,005, the same difference. These are
arithmetic observations in thousands of euro. Their cause is not established;
there is no authorization to replace printed figures with a calculated total.

Page 156 says BES recognises the BES Vida guarantee as a liability at fair value
against income when expected asset return falls below the guaranteed minimum.
The cost is EUR 2.3 million in June 2010 and explicitly nil in December 2009.
This explicit nil is distinct from a table dash. Key-management loans are
28,012 and 30,313 thousand euro, using the management definition quoted in the
report. Page 157 separately reports 5,661 and 5,698 thousand euro for ESFG board
members who are not simultaneously BES board members. The populations are not
silently combined.

Page 157's arm's-length and compliance statements are attributed report claims.
Its company-law exclusion cites article 397; its banking-law discussion cites
article 85 RGICSF, Decree-Law 126/2008 of 21 July and Instruction 13/2008. These
pages do not establish the authoritative text, its complete exceptions, its
temporal applicability or actual compliance. The executive/Fiscal Board lending
restriction includes first-degree relatives; the report gives three alternative
exceptions. Non-executive lending requires at least two-thirds of the remaining
board members AND Fiscal Board approval. The involved member cannot participate
and the report describes market pricing. Replacing AND with OR changes the
reported condition and must be rejected.

No individual impairment at either date does not remove the report's portfolio
assessment or imply absence of credit risk. The explicit Note 2.5 reference is
retained. The pension paragraph compares six months ending June 2010 with the
year ending December 2009; the no-transactions assertion does not make those
periods equal. The full clauses are preserved along with the concise reading.

Source-review decision: proceed to the source consistency checks, and only then
the production edit. Image/text differences in spacing do not change values;
any substantive association mismatch must block implementation and be retained.
Full original-graph admission and normal/isolated installed answers remain
required. This is assisted preservation of a finite supplied interpretation.
It does not establish general extraction, financial truth, current law, holder
recovery or complete source acceptance. Note 1 and remaining notes are still
explicit dependencies. No external adviser opinion is a prerequisite here.
