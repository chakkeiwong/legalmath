The report describes more than 50 percent voting rights as a presumption of
subsidiary control; power can establish control at or below that threshold.
Consolidation begins when control transfers and ends when it ceases. Through
2009, losses exceeding noncontrolling interests were attributed to the Group,
with subsequent profits recouping those attributed losses. From 2010 losses
are allocated proportionately even when the noncontrolling balance becomes
negative. The January 2010 changes for step acquisitions and loss of control
include fair-value remeasurement of retained interests and income effects.
Transactions with noncontrolling shareholders while retaining control are
equity transactions; that rule must not be replaced by the loss-of-control rule.

For associates, more than 20 percent gives a presumption of significant
influence, which may also exist below 20 percent. The equity-method loss cap
has exceptions for legal or constructive obligations and payments on behalf
of the associate. Special-purpose entity control depends on substance rather
than equity share alone; all four criteria across pages 69–70 are retained.
Managed funds are normally excluded except when controlled, with more than
50 percent of units described as a presumption. These are reported policies,
not threshold-based determinations of ownership or creditor rights.

Goodwill treatment distinguishes the pre-2004 transition, 2004–2009 purchase
accounting and the 2010 treatment of transaction costs. The proportional
noncontrolling share excludes goodwill. Goodwill is not amortised and is
reviewed annually for impairment even without indicators; negative goodwill
is recognised in income. The foreign-currency paragraph describes translation
into euro while the bullets say functional currency. Both wordings must
survive without a silent correction. Intercompany and associate eliminations
retain the exceptions for losses evidencing impairment.
