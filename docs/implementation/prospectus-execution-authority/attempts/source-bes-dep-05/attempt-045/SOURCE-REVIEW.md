Policy 2.10 requires both an enforceable offset right and intended net settlement
or simultaneous realization/settlement. An intention alone does not satisfy the
printed test. Policy 2.11 requires principal recovery by sale, immediate
availability and highly probable sale, with current measurement before held-for-
sale classification and subsequently the lower of carrying value and fair value
less selling costs. The report attributes restrictions on non-operational
property acquisition to RGICSF, article 112 of DL 298/92, and an exception for
property acquired in exchange for loans. It states a two-year disposal period
that may be extended by written Bank of Portugal authorization and on that
authority's conditions. This is a historical report assertion, not an independent
verification of legislation, amendments, extension approval or current law.

Acquired property is initially the lower of fair value less selling costs and the
loan's carrying amount; subsequent lower-of measurement uses the property's
carrying amount. It is not depreciated. Later write-downs enter income. Market,
income and cost valuation methods are alternatives selected for the asset. The
printed cost method lists Urbane Ground/Urbanity, Construction and Indirect Costs.
The report says independent specialized entities value the property and the Group
reviews the reports; these statements do not establish the truth of a valuation.

Policy 2.12 uses cost less depreciation and impairment, with the report's January
2004 transition election preserved. Subsequent capitalization requires probable
future benefits; other repairs and maintenance are expenses. Land is not
depreciated. The nine-row table spans the page break and labels useful lives in
years: buildings 35–50, leasehold improvements 10, computer equipment 4–5,
furniture 4–10, fixtures 5–12, security and office equipment 4–10, vehicles 4,
other equipment 5. Native extraction interleaves the first labels and values;
the image and row geometry establish their association. The recoverable amount
uses the greater of net selling price and value in use; impairment occurs when
net book value exceeds that amount. Do not invert greater-of to lower-of.

Policy 2.13 reports software capitalization and straight-line amortization usually
over three to six years. Specific identifiable development requires probable
benefits beyond one year and includes directly associated employee costs; other
IT services are expenses. The stated typical life is not an unconditional limit.

Policy 2.14 uses historical IAS 17 and transaction substance, with substantially
all risks and rewards as the finance-lease test. Other leases are operating.
Operating payments are charged in the related period. As lessee, the report
records an asset and liability at inception, separates principal repayment from
interest cost, and uses a constant periodic interest rate on the remaining
liability. As lessor, net investment is a loan, principal reduces that loan and
interest income reflects a constant periodic return. These accounting statements
do not establish legal title, a bondholder's claim or present accounting rules.
