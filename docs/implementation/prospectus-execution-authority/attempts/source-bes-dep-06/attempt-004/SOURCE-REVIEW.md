# Original 2009 primary statements: complete source reading

Inspected all five full original images, physical pages 75–79 (printed 74–78),
and all 849 retained native occurrences. The source is the original English
2009 report bound by the admitted graph and PDF hash. No extraction, OCR,
digit reconstruction or source rewrite was used. This is the current agent's
finite reading; it is not an independent accounting or legal opinion.

Page 75 has 2009 then 2008 annual columns. All amounts are thousands of euros
except basic and diluted EPS, explicitly euros per share. The value preceding
the interest-income label in native order belongs to 2008. The 2009 tax
subtotal 109,819 and Group profit 575,092 occur at the end of native order;
their image positions resolve the association. Interest expense and operating
costs are printed positive and subtracted; fees paid, trading losses and
deferred-tax credits retain parentheses. The unlabeled tax and profit
attribution subtotals remain unlabeled. Note references 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10,
11, 13, 25/26, 33, 21, 19/20/22, 24/26/28, 1, 27, 34, 38 and 14 are
retained; retention does not mean those notes have all been interpreted.

Page 76 distinguishes profit attribution, exchange OCI with its tax effect,
available-for-sale gains/reclassification/tax, and total comprehensive income
with separate parent/minority attribution. Group profit 575,092 plus exchange
OCI -18,094 and available-for-sale OCI 568,854 gives 1,125,852 in 2009. The
2008 comprehensive loss -513,986 coexists with positive profit 427,145. A
closing fair-value reserve is not the entire year's OCI or comprehensive income.

Page 77 contains complete consolidated balance sheets at 31 December 2009
and 2008. Native comma/space groupings are retained alongside their integer
readings. Current-tax amounts are displaced in the native stream; the image
places assets 20,929/52,721 and liabilities 133,616/89,515 on their current-tax
rows. Note 34 is printed beside each deferred-tax row, with no note numeral
printed beside either current-tax row. The finite mapping does not invent a
current-row reference. The Bank-holder equity subtotal includes seven
components; minority interest is added once to reach Group equity. These are
consolidated amounts, not the individual Bank or BES Finance accounts.

Page 78 is intact, unlike the clipped 2010 interim equity page. Its ten columns
are share capital, premium, treasury stock, preference shares, fair-value
reserve, other reserves/retained earnings, Bank-attributable profit,
Bank-holder equity, minority interest and total equity. Opening 2007 and
closing 2008/2009 balances, both years' movements, both repeated direct-equity
headings, all dashes and both footnotes are retained. Fair-value, exchange and
profit rows are components of the comprehensive subtotal. The 2009 capital
increase 1,187,885 has four indented children: capital decrease, 666,666,666
shares issued, incorporation of reserves and costs net of taxes. Neither
subtotal is added again with its children. Transfers move amounts between
profit and reserves; they are not new income. Embedded note references 37,
12 and 38 remain in their literal labels. Dashes remain unknown even where
known-number sums agree with closing balances.

Footnote (a) states ordinary dividends of EUR 0.16 and EUR 0.48 per outstanding
ordinary share for 2009 and 2008. Footnote (b) states a preferred dividend based
on an annual rate of 5.58%, relating to preference shares issued by BES Finance,
and refers to Note 37. It does not state the coupon of the prospectus notes or
supply standalone BES Finance accounts.

Page 79 retains operating, investing and financing categories, the separate

The literal title says `Dezember`; the 2008 subsidiary-disposal amount literally
prints `6133`. Those are source readings, not silent text corrections. The

The before-tax operating label spans two separate native occurrences. The
2008 financing capital-increase dash is unknown; it cannot be made zero just
to prove the financing subtotal. Notes 1, 15 and 16 and the integral-notes

Cash equivalents in 2009 are 218,595 cash + 1,973,722 central-bank deposits
- 641,802 mandatory deposits + 610,574 bank deposits = 2,161,089. Balance

through the printed composition. Net flows 242,916 are added once to opening
1,923,762 and signed FX -5,589 to reach closing 2,161,089. Accrual profit,
interest income and tax expense are not cash-flow measures; bond proceeds are
financing receipts. No assumption equates annual flows with half-year flows.
