# Shell source-chain review after finance013

The eight retained images were visually inspected: final terms pages1–3 and
base memorandum pages1,26,33,34,50. Their text agrees with the retained native
anchors. Five images were rendered locally; the other three were authenticated
against existing manifests. No original PDF was re-extracted or changed.

The final terms dated8September2020 identify Shell International Finance B.V.,
Royal Dutch Shell plc as guarantor, £500million1% notes due10December2030, and
the13August2020 Information Memorandum. The memorandum's cover names the same
entities and prints that date. This establishes a match between the supplied
documents' printed references. It establishes neither authenticity nor the
first publication date. The final terms themselves state publication on Shell's
website; that is a source statement, not an independently observed2020 retrieval.

Base p33 says the Conditions enter Global Notes by reference. For Definitive
Notes incorporation depends on the listed permission/agreement; otherwise the
Conditions are attached or endorsed. Part A of the final terms supplements the
Conditions. The separate Exempt Note/Pricing Supplement branch can replace or
modify terms to the stated extent. It is wrong to collapse these into a rule
that any later document overrides everything. Page34 gives a narrower rule:
the Trust Deed prevails over the Agency Agreement on inconsistency, and applicable
Final Terms prevail over either agreement on inconsistency. It does not make
final terms superior to mandatory law.

The2005 Trust Deed paragraph names Citicorp Trustee Company Limited and expressly
includes successor trustees. The2020 programme summary and office paragraph name
Deutsche Trustee Company Limited. These statements concern different contexts
and dates, so they do not establish a contradiction. They also do not provide
the appointment/succession instrument. The2020 Agency Agreement is described
with Deutsche Bank AG, London Branch as agent, the other paying agents and the
trustee. Full executed agreements, amendments and succession evidence are not
among the two documents being reviewed. Their absence from this pair is not
proof that no such records exist elsewhere.

Final p2 describes temporary-global to permanent-global exchange, followed by
definitive exchange at the issuer's request and in limited circumstances set out
in the note. A hypothetical definitive calculation does not prove an exchange.
Final p3 distinguishes scheduled redemption from equal-valued early redemption,
and leaves the alternative Payment Day definition not applicable. Base p50 makes
the global holder (or trustee as provided in the Trust Deed) the payment recipient;
persons recorded in clearing systems look to those systems. The optional report
must preserve these conditions and leave the existing conditional arithmetic
untouched.

Finance013's actual complete answer has Q1 UNKNOWN, Q5 conditional financial
results and no source-chain report. Its P5 quotes payment sources but does not
explain the memorandum/Part A relationship or agreement hierarchy. The new review
adds that missing explanation through the normal P2/final path. It is a bounded
named source interpretation, not an automatic general construction algorithm.
