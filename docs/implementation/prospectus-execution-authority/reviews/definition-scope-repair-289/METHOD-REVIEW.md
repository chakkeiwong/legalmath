# Source-application repair: actual method and skeptical review

This note supplements the unchanged preparation plan and its source freeze.
It does not replace the prospectus execution authority.

The package retains the original 62 records, 610 associations and 60 references
and adds a separately versioned supplement. Independently authored expectations
cover all 25 retained occurrences of the five reviewed definition groups:
five early-amount phrases, twelve holder-date spellings (including compound
components), three affiliation spellings and five custodian spellings.
The fifth amount phrase is additional source coverage, not a changed denominator.

The candidate uses supplied forms with exact source offsets. Review found two
material omissions before evaluation: input bounds did not bound output
amplification, and immediate-character boundaries missed a dash followed by a
line break. The staged candidate now caps 4096 distinct proposals and 8192
form associations and conservatively withholds split dash components. Additional
independent boundary and bound cases are frozen before evaluation. These caps
are convenience limits, not language coverage claims.

For legal application, a source-specific profile retains the express purpose
clause, defining relation, contextual extent and per-occurrence judgment. The
method requires all those premises and the exact selected-source identity.
Containment alone cannot establish scope. Naming tokens and statutory referents
are distinguished from operative uses. The tax amount equality applies only
to the tax-call context; the make-whole maximum applies only to its expressly
referenced issuer option. The general capital-reference occurrence remains
unresolved. The holder's fifteenth-day rule cannot govern issuer-call dates.
The section 10 affiliation reference establishes neither affiliation facts nor
the historically applicable statute. The enforcement definition of Depotbank
does not resolve the earlier uses in sections 7 and 11.

The supplement is optional inside the existing exact-source definition packet.
Without it, complete products and answers must equal sealed 028/029. With it,
both request-side and interpretation-side paths must match independently built
complete expectations. Existing dependencies remain unresolved: these partial,
conditional applications do not resolve an entire record or a client question.
The financial and statutory payloads must remain byte-for-byte equivalent as
data structures; only envelope hashes may change with the new P3 product.

Preservation checks retain the payment-day reuse ambiguity, table-call versus
early-call date mismatch, undefined Zinsperiode, calculation-agent placeholder,
historical-law uncertainty, all gross-up exceptions and the section 10 lawyers'
confirmation condition. User instructions remove external advisers as program
validators; they do not remove a condition written into the instrument.

The primary criterion is exact source reconstruction plus 25 independently
fixed application decisions and three complete ordinary product/answer
comparisons. Actual consumer controls use validly resealed products and require
the declared error and origin; a hash mismatch alone is insufficient.
Smoke tests, totals and runtime are explanatory. No unrestricted recall,
automatic legal interpretation, real-holdings acceptance, package-wide
limitation or whole-build completion follows from this finite trial.

Promotion vetoes are accepted forgery, scope leakage, invented meaning,
lost uncertainty/provenance or changed protected payloads. Continuation vetoes
are corrupted sealed evidence, unexplained method drift, missing required
results or exhausted cumulative limits. A rejected candidate triggers a changed,
reviewed repair; it does not by itself reject the direction.

The cumulative 026 onward time ceiling remains 3600 measured seconds.
The 160 MiB storage ceiling is revised to 224 MiB including a 4 MiB reserve
to cover immutable program/proposal/admission copies and the new exact-source
profile and retained answer differences. Prior costs are carried forward,
including source-only preparation. Historical manual/controller time stays
UNKNOWN. Admission ceiling 29 becomes 30 for this changed substantive package.
Every child has a 180-second alarm; no unchanged or completed stage is retried.
The budget review must include a forecast using the observed approximately
50-second four-control batches and allowance for full-answer comparisons,
reconciliation, sealing and controller storage.

Skeptical review: conditional PASS for preparation, with final method/hash and
resource review required before admission. The baseline is the complete checked
028 answer, not a smoke result or a historical storage donor. The strongest
alternative explanation is further source-reading omissions. Exact quotations,
finite independent boundary cases and adverse application controls discriminate
local engineering correctness; they cannot establish universal legal truth.
No unavailable adviser, ACTUS source, CDM receiver or actual holdings are needed.

Final actual-method audit before preparation/admission: PASS. The inspected
implementation registers source premises separately from the 25 expected
decisions, applies a rule only to an operative or declaration occurrence with
one source-reviewed extent, and keeps all other roles unbound. The comparator
constructs complete expected products and answers independently of candidate
execution. Both entry paths use the ordinary service; consumer attacks are
resealed and checked for their exact refusal origin. The unchanged-record
comparison protects all original exceptions and unresolved definitions.
The expected supplement was frozen before the application implementation.
The supplied-form candidate existed before that supplement but was not
evaluated; its original 19 cases predate it, and seven additional cases address
the pre-run boundary review. No heldout-generalization claim is made.

Measured time is accumulated rather than reset. The brief source-only
expectation-freeze command has no retained elapsed-time measurement; it remains
explicitly UNKNOWN alongside manual/controller time. It is not silently zero.
