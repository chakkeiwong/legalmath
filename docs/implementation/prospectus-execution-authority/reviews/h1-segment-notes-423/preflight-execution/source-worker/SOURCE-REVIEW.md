Pages 97–101 describe the Group, the operating segments and internal allocation
rules. Geographic segmentation follows the location of the operating units.
The original list includes the corporate centre, whereas its later description
expressly says that it is not an operating segment. Both statements remain.
The international commercial segment excludes investment banking and asset
management; foreign units of those businesses are included in the international
geographic description. The table uses the literal International retail banking
header. Those distinctions must not be silently harmonised.

The seven BES allocation principles include allocation by origination even after
securitisation, launch-set margins for mass products, negotiated margins for
other products, dedicated direct costs, indirect allocation using drivers and
cost-based allocation, impairment-based credit risk and total Bank equity in
capital markets. Transactions between autonomous units use market prices.
Internal funding and services use the described margin process; other internal
transactions use cost-based allocation without supplier margin. Credit risk is
excepted from the Financial Department's other risk allocation. BES associates
and noncurrent assets have a different allocation basis from those of other
Group entities. Income taxes are excluded from most segment evaluations, not
necessarily all. Deferred taxes and postemployment assets go to capital markets.
The reported IFRS 8 paragraph 23 reference is retained without independent
verification of a current rule.

Page 102 has two operating tables with nine columns and two geographic tables

an of-which component of operating expenses and must not be deducted again.
Intersegment operating income is separately disclosed and is not automatically
an additional component of the reported operating-income total. Parentheses
preserve negative numbers; printed dashes remain unknowns. Geographic net profit
and operating profit before tax are different quantities.
