# Pre-execution review of the unquoted-definition continuation

The source freeze and its seven exact readings precede candidate creation. The
baseline is the complete sealed attempt087 answer, not a smaller reconstruction.
The parser receives source text and a selection/language partition; reviewed
definition records and expected offsets are unavailable to discovery.

Skeptical audit PASS for the finite claim in the frozen plan. The grammar is a
source-motivated hypothesis, not a German legal-definition standard. Unfamiliar
terms, differing restrictions on identical names, raw whitespace, near misses
and selection/language boundaries test the main hidden assumptions. Exact
expression/term/value/scope intervals and source projections are the primary
criteria. Counts and test totals cannot establish completeness or legal truth.

The oracle also checks report identity, source hash and candidate regions. Its
adverse controls must reject deleted/changed names, exchanged or deleted
restrictions, missing applicability conditions, changed values/provenance,
invented definitions, invented legal meaning/completion and changed old answers.
Fresh wheel imports must all resolve inside the retained installed directory,
and the complete ordinary and installed answers must be equal. A passing parser
alone cannot promote installation or downstream integration.

Every frozen stage has one bounded execution. A parser or consumer failure
rejects that candidate and triggers retained failure analysis, not a claim that
PDF/OCR packages or the research direction have failed. Baseline corruption,
method drift or the cumulative bound stops this attempt. The reviewed revision
extends storage explicitly, preserves all prior costs and leaves the original
task and wider general build open. No external adviser, live call or new client
fact is required for this finite conditional check.
