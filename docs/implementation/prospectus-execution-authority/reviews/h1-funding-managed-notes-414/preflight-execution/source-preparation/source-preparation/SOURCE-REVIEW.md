# Original funding and managed-asset notes

Original page images 136, 137 and 153 were displayed and read. Each was rendered
once at 180 dpi and was legible. Four tables with 37 rows and 74 numeric positions,
plus eight passages, were typed from the images. Native text was read beforehand
for selection, so the separate image check is not blind independent evidence.

Note 29 reports deposits from central banks at 30 June 2010 and 31 December 2009.
The table uses thousands of euro. ESCB deposits and other funds are distinct from
other central-bank deposits and repurchase agreements. Subtotals and totals have
no printed text labels; assigned labels are interpretations of the retained
positions. Reported totals are 8,995,743 and 3,817,643 thousand euro.

The collateral paragraph uses millions of euro: 6,730 and 2,000 refer to the
ESCB other funds at the respective dates. Available-for-sale securities are
pledged as collateral, with Note 39 cited. This does not state that all funding
is secured or establish security enforceability or creditor priority. The next
paragraph also uses millions: 862 and 1,083 of deposits from the Central Bank
of Angola are included in other central-bank deposits. They are not the whole
category. Scaling a million by 1,000 can explain agreement with the table but
cannot establish source association or financial truth.

Note 30 separates four domestic and five international categories, their
subtotals and overall totals of 7,112,438 and 6,895,720 thousand euro. These
are deposits from banks, a funding liability; they must not be conflated with
the previously delivered deposits with banks, an asset. Keep loans, deposits,
short-term funds, repurchase agreements and other funds in their printed
domestic/international groups.

Note 31 reports amounts due to customers, with demand deposits, time deposits
and other time balances, pensioner and other savings accounts, and repurchase
agreements and other funds. The total is 26,082,073 and 25,446,450 thousand euro.
Native extraction places the savings and other-funds labels after the numeric
columns, so source order is insufficient. Check each image row against its
original geometry and retain all four section headings.

Note 40 states that fund management companies and the depositary bank are
jointly liable before fund participants for non-fulfilment of obligations
assumed under law and fund management regulations. Preserve both actors,
beneficiaries, joint liability and its trigger. The report does not identify
the applicable law here or state a general guarantee against investment loss;
neither inference is justified. This historical report statement is not a
verified present-law determination.

The six managed-asset categories include securities, real-estate and pension
funds, bancassurance, portfolio management and other assets. Totals are
18,005,646 and 19,122,360 thousand euro at the same respective dates. Managed
assets are not automatically bank-owned assets available to its creditors.
The final sentence says the amounts recognised in these accounts are measured
at fair value determined at the balance-sheet date. Preserve its date and
attribution, without claiming verified valuations or current realizable value.

All 148 page occurrences should be retained, with 139 substantive occurrences
partitioned into eight clauses. Verify these counts against the original graph,
exact quotes, image transcript, geometry and prior source overlap. Numeric sums
are explanatory only. Require complete prior-answer preservation, equality of
ordinary and installed consumers, and adverse cases for date/row/unit changes,
liability reversal, collateral overreach, Angola scope and fund-liability
overstatement. Finite supplied readings do not establish automatic extraction,
financial truth, source authority, incorporation or whole-source acceptance.
