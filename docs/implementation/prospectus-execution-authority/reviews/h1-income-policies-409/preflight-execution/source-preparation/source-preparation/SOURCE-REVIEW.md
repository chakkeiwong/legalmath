# Original tax, provisions, income and cash policies

All four retained original images, pages 90–93, were displayed at original
resolution and read. The 25 passages were separately typed from those images
before mapping native source lines. The image files were reused; no new OCR or
rendering was performed. These are historical assertions in the report.

Income tax follows income or equity recognition, with the stated subsequent
recycling for available-for-sale remeasurement and cash-flow hedges. Current
and deferred tax use enacted or substantively enacted rates by jurisdiction;
deferred tax also considers the rate expected at realization or settlement.
The goodwill, initial-recognition and subsidiary-investment exceptions must
remain distinct. Deferred tax assets require probable future taxable profits.
Offset requires both an enforceable current-tax setoff right and the same tax
authority, tested at each subsidiary. Consolidated presentation sums the
respective subsidiary assets and liabilities; it is not blanket group netting.

All three provision conditions are required. The page break must not detach the
restructuring condition from policy 2.17: a detailed formal approved plan and
either commencement or public announcement. Onerous contracts compare expected
benefits with unavoidable costs; measurement is the present value of the lower
of termination cost and net continuation cost, not the higher amount.

Interest recognition distinguishes non-derivatives from derivatives and the
risk-management exception. The report defines the effective rate by discounting
cash flows to net carrying amount over expected life or an appropriate shorter
period. It states that this rate is calculated at inception and not subsequently
revised. Preserve that statement without silently treating it as an override of
the earlier variable-rate impairment policy. Contractual terms, integral fees,
transaction costs and premiums/discounts are included; future credit losses are
excluded from the stated calculation. For impaired financial assets, interest
uses the rate employed to measure the impairment loss. No present IFRS rule or
universal floating-rate theorem is established by copying this historical text.

The three fee bases are completion of a significant act, the service period,
and effective interest. Dividends use an established right to payment. Segment
reporting retains the stated 1 January 2009 IFRS 8 adoption, business risk/return
definition, management review and geography definition. These are source claims,
not proof that current reporting complies with a standard.

Basic EPS excludes average treasury stock. Potential shares are dilutive when
conversion decreases net EPS; preserving this description is not a recomputed
earnings-per-share measure. On page 93 policy 2.23 is visibly headed
"Non-current assets held for sale", but its body defines cash and equivalents.
Keep that mismatch explicitly. The body says less than three months from
inception, including cash and bank/central-bank deposits; restricted central-bank
balances are excluded. Do not correct the heading silently or replace the strict
inequality with an inclusive one or a balance-sheet-date maturity test.

survive. Source checks require independently typed passages and exact quotes,
order and geometry; consumer checks require actual complete ordinary/installed
