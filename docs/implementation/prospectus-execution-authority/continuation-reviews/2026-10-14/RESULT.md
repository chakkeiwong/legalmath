# Execution diagnosis and reviewed closure design

The repeated checkpoint handoffs were premature assistant termination, reinforced
by incomplete continuation and acceptance design in the local authority. They
are not explained by the allow-list, and the historical stream-disconnection
cause remains unestablished.

The current request asked why execution keeps stopping and how to close the
remaining gaps thoroughly. This package completes that diagnosis and records a
concrete closure design. It does not represent execution or completion of every
product repair in that design, and it is not a whole-program blocked handoff.

Revision 64 binds the reviewed plan, exact 44-task continuation map, read-only
inspector and focused tests into the existing program. The 59 obligations, task
specifications, budgets and previous evidence are unchanged. Existing generated
state still contains 4 COMPLETE, 31 RESULT_RECORDED, 1 BUDGET_EXHAUSTED,
3 WAITING_EXTERNAL and 5 WAITING_DEPENDENCY tasks. All four delivery decisions
remain false.

The live continuation report finds 26 unfinished tasks with local continuation,
design or preparation, and 14 waiting on specific evidence or external inputs.
These are reviewed routes, not 26 admitted execution jobs. Twenty-five partial
tasks have spent their admission allowance. Forty unfinished tasks lack an
implemented task-specific acceptance route. Some require genuine human
acceptance; a generic automated validator would be incorrect.

The inspector returns REVIEW_REQUIRED and whole_program_stop_supported=false.
It preserves the distinction between an empty dispatch queue and absence of
useful authorized work. It exposes incomplete predecessors and required budget
reviews without bypassing them. It checks plan/map/method identities, exact
task/obligation coverage and authority consistency, and refuses a mixed report
if the event history advances during inspection. It cannot keep the agent alive
after a final reply or guarantee that the agent follows the continuation rule.
