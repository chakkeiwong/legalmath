admitted quotation occurs; it does not prove the supplied interpretation of it.

broad hashes must not be narrowed on this evidence. A6 excludes known development
document IDs, but cannot authenticate readers or establish family separation
from self-declared identity strings. A reviewed family/exposure register and
original reader records are needed before a real acceptance exercise.
