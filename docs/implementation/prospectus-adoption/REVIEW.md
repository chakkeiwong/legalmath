The implementation still has important limits. Source binding proves that an
admitted quotation occurs; it does not prove the supplied interpretation of it.
AST v2 has strict runtime field validation, not a standalone JSON schema.
Native subprocess/import reads remain outside the Python capability boundary;
broad hashes must not be narrowed on this evidence. A6 excludes known development
document IDs, but cannot authenticate readers or establish family separation
from self-declared identity strings. A reviewed family/exposure register and
original reader records are needed before a real acceptance exercise.

No trial establishes complete financial settlement or actual legal exercise.
The absent actual coupon admission, unresolved German construction and missing
independent cohort remain substantive gaps. These are not excused by the 115
passing regression tests. The current program can execute the tested mechanisms
and preserve those gaps; completing the legal deliverable requires the work in
NEXT-PROGRAM.md.
