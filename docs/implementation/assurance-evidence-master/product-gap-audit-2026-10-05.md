## Case evidence versus product defects

An actual compliance assessment would additionally require the provider, product,
arrangement, assessment period, applicable product conditions, responsibility and
supervision records, ownership evidence, incidents and remediation. Their absence
currently prevents an actual case conclusion. It does not prevent repairing the
interpreter or checking how it handles explicitly declared premises.

Correct abstention on a genuinely unresolved source or missing fact is intended
product behavior. The defect would be silently resolving that uncertainty,
misstating the source, claiming compliance from supplied Booleans, or failing to
preserve the different consequences of supported rival readings.

## Next product work

1. Correct the active reading and candidate explanations, preserving immutable
   originals. Separate acquired text, uncertain applicability and truly missing
   authority in the operative fields themselves.
2. Build a clause-level semantic specification for the disputed meanings,
   conditions, exceptions, actors, temporal scope and modality. Mark conclusions
   as entailed, conditional, rival or unsupported with their source anchors.
3. Connect each supported branch to its program and evidence requirements. Use
   distinguishing cases such as retained responsibility with defective records,
   good outcomes without retained authority, no request, conflicting ownership,
   provider replacement and incomplete evidence.
4. Check meaning preservation for those stated propositions and retain explicit
   limits. Evaluate amended and unfamiliar text separately from repaired examples.

This sequence targets product interpretation before another final review. Its
success criterion is supported meaning and correctly preserved uncertainty, not
forcing every legal question into a Boolean answer or closing every open item.
