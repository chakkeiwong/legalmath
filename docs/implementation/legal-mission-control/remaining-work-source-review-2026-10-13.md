# Original-question targets and remaining source work

13 October 2026. The four original questions remain the target. The retained
October 9–10 dossiers supply the substantive baseline; this review connects
their conditional conclusions to exact inputs for the installed adapters. It
does not choose an unresolved legal standard merely to make a solver runnable.

For P10, continuing responsibility, competent exercise of responsibility and
achieved operational or record adequacy remain distinct propositions. The words
“remain and be ultimately responsible” and “regardless of any outsourcing” support
continuing provider responsibility. The ownership-record rationale and the
separate proper-records provision support a substantive argument. They do not
alone select a complete conduct or outcome test. A provider with diligent
supervision but established defective records is therefore a useful conditional
case: an outcome conjunction can fail while a narrower allocation predicate
remains satisfied. The legal choice between those predicates remains open.
Required client evidence includes the appointment and delegation terms, actual
powers and information access, record roles, rights, dated incidents and remedial
conduct. Later repair cannot silently erase an earlier deficiency.

For the evidence question, the retained common-error counterexample remains
decisive against a specific shortcut. If H owns ten units under stipulated
applicable law and terms while two relevant records both show zero, agreement
does not establish that those records reflect H's right. The Appendix separately
asks about legal validity, reconciliation and encumbrance. The source-warranted
argument attacks the sufficiency of agreement alone. It does not prove a client's
rights, select a P10 discharge standard or establish that all secondary-record
discrepancies breach a legal requirement. The installed graph tools can evaluate
the explicit undercut once that argument is supplied. They cannot discover or
validate its legal warrant merely by computing extensions.

For P14, the exact main sentence conditions demonstration on an SFC request and
requires confirmation. Footnote 5 uses confirmation/demonstration language
without repeating that condition. The main-text conditional reading and the
standing-footnote rival must both survive. A Boolean conditional
`not request or satisfactory_demonstration` can test a declared reading's
conditional obligation only. With no request it is vacuously true; that value
does not establish actual confirmation, underlying compliance, absence of defects
or SFC satisfaction. An unknown request is evaluated as unknown, not false.
The exact scope/content of a request, the deployed contract version, audit scope,
reliance justification and relevant SFC response remain factual questions.
Paragraphs 15 and 16 concern separate audit/verification and legal-opinion
requests. One does not establish the others. The Appendix's repeated technical
confidence language supports the smart-contract reading, while the wider
footnote attachment remains a supported rival.

For Gatecoin, the 2025 reasons distinguish scenarios one to four from scenarios
five and six and separately discuss scenario seven. The first group's NCC
direction is defeasible on evidence of access/use to effect the transaction.
It supports a narrowly declared predicate: category established and no contrary
evidence under the stated direction. The factual assessment of contrary evidence
is an input, and missing evidence is not a known false input. A post-date entry
is therefore neither invariably conclusive nor invariably irrelevant. The
scenario-seven conflict between the reasons and disposition remains visible;
it is excluded from this restricted predicate, not resolved by exclusion. The
2023 and 2025 holdings also require the actual parties, terms, rights and property
relationships. Cryptocurrency being property capable of trust is not a finding
that a particular customer has a beneficial interest. Transfer to a Product
Provider requires the applicable terms, governing law, record role and an
express explanation of why the reasoning applies to that different relationship.
