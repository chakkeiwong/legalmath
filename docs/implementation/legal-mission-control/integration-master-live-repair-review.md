# First live result and bounded repair review

13 October 2026. This is a repair within the registered integration master,
not a successor program or a new grant. The original proposal, grant, requests,
results and node attempts remain immutable. The saved approved implementation
is under `artifacts/legal-mission-control/2026-10-13/master/approved-code/`.

The first live stage charged 167 calls: eight canaries and 159 acquisition
requests. Seventy-one acquisition responses validated. Fifty-six failed quotation
or source-reference validation, twenty exceeded the output token limit, ten
timed out and two disconnected. All 52 explicit source bridges validated. No
complete answer arm ran and no selected tool gained a complete evaluation.

The software and method have identifiable defects. The bounded route did not
reuse the existing exact source-span transport. Models had to copy long IDs and
literal quotations; inspected failures include both an ID transcription error
and nonliteral quoted text. Requirement-only packets invited observations about
requirements, while validation accepted only quotations from source records.
The numbered-citation check also treated a statement that a provision is missing
as a substantive assertion about that provision. Finally, eight-record reading
packets had no adequate output reservation, and the circular canaries used BM25
hits from Gatecoin instead of the controlling circular passages. Successful
scripted rehearsal did not expose these live-response failures.

## Evidence contract and skeptical audit

The question is whether source and requirement evidence can cross the actual
product/provider boundary faithfully enough to execute the full integration
evaluation. The comparator is the exact first live input and its retained
failed outputs, plus the unchanged original questions and acceptance populations.
The primary engineering criterion is exact source/reference expansion, complete
typed record coverage, preserved negative controls, and bounded actual requests
through `run_product`. Model response acceptance, legal adequacy and matched tool
effects remain separate criteria requiring actual evidence.

Foreign or fabricated references, dropped original records, requirement text
treated as legal authority, unsupported numbered legal assertions, invalid
shared identities, lost attempt history and unapproved service dispatch veto
the affected result. Timeouts and unsatisfactory responses are candidate failures;
they do not establish legal impossibility. Call counts, tests and token estimates
are explanatory diagnostics only. No local check can establish legal truth or
complete tool evaluation.

Short reference aliases are a transport choice, justified by the observed ID
copying failures; round-trip and foreign-ID controls test their integrity.
Smaller reading packets are a capacity hypothesis, justified by observed output
overflow; the measured schema, complete population count and live output check
remain mandatory. A typed missing-source report states a limitation of the
delivered packet, never absence from law or from the complete corpus. Exact
quotation checks and verified legal locators are retained.

Audit: proceed with the local repair and independent source inspection. The
previous qualification was inadequate because unresolved scripted responses
could fit and pass without exercising quotations or findings. New controls must
exercise those mechanisms. A new live request is prohibited until the repaired
implementation and resource migration are concrete and authorized. Merely having
7,933 calls below the global ceiling does not authorize replay of spent stages.
