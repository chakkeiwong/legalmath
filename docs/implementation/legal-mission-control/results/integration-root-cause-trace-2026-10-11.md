# Legal product integration: current-route root causes

11 October 2026. This is a product-code read-only diagnosis of the admitted
`product-contribution` task. The comparator is the **current**
`CompleteInvestigation` → `dossier_answer` route and the sealed 13-call pilot,
not the older 492-pair illustration or the synthetic gift-rule fixture. No
provider call, product repair, source acquisition or legal-obligation decision
was made here. Four questions, 46 original obligations, 35 readings and six
answer-level tool evaluations remain open.

## Skeptical audit of this trace

The audit passes for diagnosis, not dispatch. It starts from the terminal
pilot and the repaired current source, distinguishes measured failures from
code-structural risks, and treats byte fit, call arithmetic, schema validation,
tool agreement and scripted answers as explanatory evidence only. It does not
assume the 78-claim/12-reading hybrid is a complete population, that two roles
using the same model are independent legal adjudicators, or that the grant's
global unused calls are transferable between sealed stages. The stop condition
is the first unsupported claim about actual model response quality, legal
entailment or a matched answer effect. The artifact answers what blocks the
route and what evidence would discriminate the causes; it cannot answer
whether any tool improves the original legal answer.

The bounded verification is an offline diagnostic, not a model experiment:
call the current guard with the three missing constructor names using a
provider that refuses any dispatch; compare joined and individual query
bindings on the retained original questions; and inspect the search repair
constructor for `original_task`. Expected results are precise guard refusals,
no joined-query original-question binding, valid individual bindings, and
preserved generic-repair provenance. Any unexpected dispatch or changed live
ledger invalidates this diagnostic. The zero-call result and per-file hashes
are retained under
`artifacts/legal-mission-control/2026-10-11/integration-root-cause-trace/`.
These checks can confirm structural behavior only; they cannot establish
end-to-end branch reachability, legal quality, or tool contribution.

The diagnostic completed on 11 October 2026 with zero provider dispatches.
All three constructor names were refused as `unbudgeted_constructor`; joined
and unbridged typed queries had no original-question binding, while the two
individual queries bound to their respective original questions. The search
output-repair constructor retained `original_task`. The grant ledger's SHA256
was unchanged. This confirms the local admission/binding behaviors, not that
those branches caused the historical source-inventory failure.

## Path actually traced

The old live runner constructs the baseline and six-tool candidate with the
same source/method configuration except the optional backend
(`scripts/legal_mission_sol_inventory_window.py:136-149,207-208`). The
candidate is contingent on a complete baseline dossier and answer
(`:278-343`). `CompleteInvestigation.run` first calls
`IntegratedInvestigation.run`, which invokes `Assurance`: source extraction,
two inventories, abstraction proposals, candidate search, core fidelity and
criticism (`complete_investigation.py:183-200`, `engine.py:456-735`). Only then
does it assign candidate questions and run the all-claim/all-candidate scoped
review (`complete_investigation.py:200-240`). It adds proofs and replay before
marking a dossier complete (`:280-344`). `dossier_answer.propose` builds
bounded evidence groups, model reviews, an interaction review and synthesis,
then marks the output structurally valid but expressly leaves legal quality
and tool consideration unevaluated (`dossier_answer.py:523-605`).

The sealed live route stopped on baseline source inventory at slot 13, before
generation, scoped judgment, final answer or candidate tools. Its fourth
response was 25,681 bytes before source-span expansion and 475,806 bytes after
expansion, against the old 200,000-byte journal limit. The first four pieces
covered 53/55 units and yielded 78 claims and 45 uncertainties; no second
reader exists. The later local repair accepted that exact expanded response
under separate finite bounds, added aggregate and staged review contracts,
and delivered 189 baseline and 180 prepared candidate inputs through the
transport. Nine candidate requests were not prepared after the 200-action
tool journal filled. None of these local checks supplied actual new-method
responses or a P10/evidence answer. See `results/sol-inventory-window.md`,
`results/inventory-capacity-audit.md` and `results/route-repair.md`.

## Causal findings

1. **The original answer target is weakly enforced before expensive work.**
   `selected_slice` enters the packet, but the search produces proposed
   `Reading`s before `question_request` asks which question each candidate
   answers (`complete_investigation.py:183-227`). The latter validates source
   controls and candidate IDs, not whether the proposition answers P10
   *fulfillment* rather than merely a trigger. `validate_answer` later checks
   question hashes, citations and accounts, not entailment or completeness
   (`investigation_answer.py:279-335`). This explains how a fully accounted
   trigger answer can miss the actual legal question. A question-first warrant
   and counterexample check is needed before candidate expansion and again
   at final review. This is a code/design risk, not an observed new-model
   failure on the original target.

2. **Source-to-rule justification remains outside the mechanical checks.**
   Inventory and quotation validators enforce unit coverage and exact quoted
   text (`semantics.py:128-167`); staged reviews demand support, contrary and
   missing-authority fields (`staged_source_review.py:15-80`). These are useful
   controls, but a model can still attach an authentic quotation to an
   unsupported legal premise. The retained false-warrant trial showed graph
   and Boolean checks accepting the supplied reconciliation shortcut until
   the reviewer supplied a shared-error undercut (`results/product-contribution.md`).
   BM25 locates text, LegalRuleML/Logical English preserve restricted supplied
   formalisms, and PyArg/Carneades/clingo compute a supplied effective graph
   (`scripts/legal_integration_tools.py:48-190`). None derives the applicable
   P10 discharge standard, resolves P14 footnote attachment, or transfers a
   Gatecoin holding to a different party arrangement. Those are legal-warrant
   questions requiring explicit source argument and contrary review. The code
   explicitly disclaims legal-premise verification; this is an unfilled
   product requirement, not evidence that its validators claim legal truth.

3. **The complete legal population and the complete model population are
   different missing inputs.** The 55-unit corpus is a qualified P10/evidence
   pilot; P14 and Gatecoin need their own source and reading populations.
   Even the P10/evidence model inventory is partial: one reader, 53/55 units,
   78 claims and twelve *historical* readings. `merge_inventories` coalesces
   only exactly equal commitments (`semantics.py:180-192`), so a complete
   second reader can add distinct claims. Search can retain deferred and
   repaired candidates (`engine.py:605-636,702-723`). Declaring the hybrid
   to be the future bound would hide claims, rivals and required calls.

4. **The route's computational work grows multiplicatively.** Core fidelity
   covers every non-CONTEXT claim against every candidate; scoped fidelity
   covers *all* claims against every candidate and asks two perspectives
   (`engine.py:227-310`, `complete_investigation.py:200-240`,
   `scoped_investigation.py:72-86,120-160`). The dossier's
   `execution_complete` requires the scoped matrix, even though that matrix
   alone cannot establish a legally sufficient answer
   (`complete_investigation.py:311-314`). On the incomplete 78 × 12 hybrid,
   scoped review already has 936 pairs, or 156 calls at batch 12; core has
   402 model-reviewed and 402 deterministic unsupported pairs. The
   4,096-pair hard limit and bounded answer groups may also bind at complete
   populations. A method revision must either fund these exact denominators
   or explicitly justify a question-directed alternative that retains every
   omitted relation as unassessed. Silent pruning is not a repair.

5. **Local serialization repairs do not qualify real reasoning or timing.**
   The current code separates logical/encoded input and raw/expanded output,
   uses an exact shared-evidence codec, distinct aggregate inventories and
   staged source, interaction and omission reviews (`journal.py:24-49`,
   `evidence_tables.py`, `decomposition.py:12-95`,
   `staged_source_review.py:119-173`). The earlier 475,806-byte failure and
   147 prepared input-size refusals are repaired locally. But source-unit
   accounting is still a model assertion, and a reversible encoding does not
   prove that a model notices a remote qualification. Actual responses at
   64 core pairs and 32/48 scoped pairs, output size, latency, omission
   quality, cross-piece interactions and final synthesis have not been
   qualified on the complete original target.

6. **There is no affordable, live-admitted whole route.** The sealed pilot
   allocates 36 core, 82 scoped and 64 answer-review calls per arm, with 13
   baseline-core calls spent. The optimistic partial-hybrid 64/32 and 64/48
   settings require 33 core calls per arm against the baseline's 23 remaining.
   They fit the paired total only arithmetically; complete populations,
   repairs, tool actions and deadline headroom are missing
   (`results/gap-closure.md`). The current `RouteBudgetProvider.check`
   expressly refuses live providers (`route_admission.py:118-133`), while
   `workload` takes declared counts and `observe` checks only whether actual
   claims/candidates exceed them (`:40-79,148-154`). Only final-answer
   packing recomputes a downstream reservation (`dossier_answer.py:569-582`).
   Thus the local guard is a proof-of-structure, not a reviewed successor
   allocation or a prediction that the full route will fit.

7. **Some conditional branches are not wired into that guard.**
   `Assurance` can emit `REPAIR_SOURCE_INVENTORY` and
   `PROPOSE_DERIVED_MAPPING` (`engine.py:669,796`), but neither name appears
   in `route_admission.TASK_STAGES` (`route_admission.py:13-19,156-163`).
   These branches would fail closed if reached through the guard. More
   seriously, the source-inventory repair sends an unpartitioned `Inventory`
   request and replaces the reviewed inventories with raw revised ones
   (`engine.py:665-701`). It does not rerun piece aggregation, staged review
   or search; `search_view` and candidate generation were built from the
   earlier inventory (`:589-592`). A repaired claim could therefore lack
   the source review and candidate reconsideration required by the new route.
   This is a structural repair-path incompatibility, not an observed failure
   in the sealed run.
   Optional example-query work has a similar unmapped constructor but is
   not reached by the current `CompleteInvestigation` call signature.
   The suspected generic output-repair routing defect was **not confirmed**:
   `Search.call` preserves `original_task` in `REPAIR_OUTPUT`
   (`search/engine.py:151-157`), which is what the guard requires.

8. **Tool invocation is not tool contribution.** The backend is injected
   explicitly from `scripts/legal_integration_tools.py`, not a default
   production dependency (`complete_investigation.py:56-91,179-183`).
   `ToolSession.augment` can add question/candidate-bound retrieval, and
   candidate/criticism checks survive into a request (`optional_tools.py:123-216`),
   but its 200-action journal already stopped nine preparations. The cost
   depends on actual unique targets, candidate revisions, checks and request
   count; `route_admission.workload` is a declared estimate, not an observed
   bound. The answer code now accounts for nested adapter status and rejects
   wrong-question retrieval (`investigation_answer.py:210-228,305-328`),
   but `dossier_answer` passes fallible group summaries to interaction and
   synthesis; raw records are archived rather than wholly supplied at the
   final step (`dossier_answer.py:455-510`). Hash and disposition accounts
   cannot verify genuine consideration or causal answer change. None of the
   six selected tools has a matched source-reviewed answer-level finding.

   There is also a concrete question-binding gap. The pilot supplies both
   original questions as one joined `selected_slice`
   (`investigation_answer.py:101-103`,
   `scripts/legal_mission_sol_inventory_window.py:306`). Ordinary retrieval
   falls back to that joined string (`optional_tools.py:194-198`), but final
   applicability requires equality to an individual original question
   (`optional_tools.py:42-56`). Thus those joined-query results cannot be
   accounted as `USED` for either original answer; account question lists
   cannot be empty (`investigation_answer.py:64-81,315-328`). Scoped retrieval
   instead binds a candidate's typed question, which is deliberately excluded
   without a reviewed bridge. Final dossier dispatch does not call the
   optional augmentor. The guard is correctly preventing false attribution;
   the missing integration is explicit original-question retrieval and a
   reviewed relationship to candidate questions. Retrieval may still influence
   earlier reasoning; no actual answer harm has been measured.

   Atomic checks for several candidates are intentionally withheld when a
   later request lacks any checked candidate or changes its reading
   (`optional_tools.py:202-211`). This protects against stale context. A
   successor must show which checks each receiving stage can consume; it
   must not weaken the hash rule or assume every retained check reached every
   reviewer. Where a result is separable, produce a separately bound result
   for that subset; a whole argument graph cannot be projected without
   checking its dependencies.

   The register contains 26 surveyed entries: six selected tools, nine
   supporting tools, seven deferred tools, three conceptual approaches and
   one target-unsuitable tool (`tools.json`). All six selected tools lack
   complete answer-level evaluation. This does not mean all 26 should be
   installed in the product or that none has been connected.

9. **The legal uncertainties are real and question-specific, not transport
   defects.** P10 distinguishes retained responsibility, actual supervision,
   separate record outcomes and remediation. Matching two records need not
   establish the underlying legal right (`results/p10-standard.md`). P14's
   request condition, demonstration object and footnote-5 confidence scope
   have supported rivals; an audit report is not SFC satisfaction
   (`results/p14-attachment.md`). Gatecoin's 2025 scenario-seven passages
   conflict, and later cases do not give a Product Provider transfer rule
   (`results/gatecoin-transfer.md`, `results/source-applicability.md`). These
   ambiguities permit conditional, source-supported answers now. They veto
   an invented unique standard or client-specific compliance finding, not
   every independent product task.

10. **Service cadence and the fixed clock compound the budget mismatch.**
    `CodexProvider.complete` starts a fresh, isolated CLI process for each
    model request, reserves a call before dispatch and fails closed on timeout
    (`search/providers.py:104-201`). The retained exact-source Sol canary took
    259.430 seconds and the next identical request timed out at 300 seconds
    (`results/sol-canary.md`, `results/live-pilot-sol.md`). Multiplying that
    one canary duration by the optimistic 284-call 64/48 pair gives about
    20.5 hours before retries, review or setup; this is an illustration, not
    a latency prediction. With the grant's 12 October 04:28:01 UTC deadline,
    no complete-route timing headroom has been established. More unused calls
    do not extend the fixed clock.

## Resolution order and decisive checks

1. Freeze a **question-first** P10/evidence acceptance sheet: exact original
   wording, source spans, allocation/conduct/outcome rivals, the shared-error
   and secondary-record counterexamples, missing authority/facts, and a
   trigger-only answer that must fail. Keep P14 and Gatecoin on separate
   source sheets. A legal reviewer must inspect whether each proposed warrant
   follows from its cited source; a quote/solver PASS cannot do that job.
2. Review one coherent method revision. Decide explicitly whether full
   claim×candidate matrices are necessary to answer the original question.
   If retained, bound their complete populations and all repeated stages;
   if narrowed, preserve the full source, contrary/unknown evidence and an
   explicit unassessed denominator, with tests against lost remote qualifiers.
   Fix the repair path and enumerate every reachable request task within this
   same revision, rather than adding another isolated cap patch.
3. Run bounded **offline** end-to-end regression on retained real source
   structures and adversarial legal counterexamples, including both arms,
   candidate-only tool actions, interrupted dispatch, source/claim conservation,
   omission and interaction review, answer packing, and each adapter's
   unsupported/false-warrant result. This verifies engineering reachability,
   not legal benefit.
4. Separately review a bounded live **qualification pilot**, if resources
   permit. Before its first call, declare conservative population ceilings,
   task-specific response and time limits, repair reserves and stop conditions.
   Do not require future actual model inventories as a prerequisite to the
   calls needed to create them. After each measured inventory, generation or
   review stage, recompute remaining work and stop before an unaffordable
   dependency. Qualify real source/contrary preservation, outputs and latency
   before admitting the full comparison. The pilot is a separate reviewed
   method with its own finite reservation, not a bypass of the local-only
   `RouteBudgetProvider.check`. This audit authorizes no such dispatch.
5. Use the qualification evidence to calculate a prospective equal-arm
   reserve for every constructor, context pass, repair, tool action, final
   group and task-specific latency. Keep the 13 spent calls, 23-core baseline
   remainder, 200-action bound and original deadline visible. The old grant's
   unused global slots cannot silently become a new stage allocation. Refuse
   the full route as under-budgeted if a reviewed method cannot fit.
6. Only after that gate, compare one complete P10/evidence baseline with an
   equal-source/equal-method combined-tool candidate, then applicable
   individual-tool controls. Inspect raw tool receipts, actual downstream
   consideration and each final source-to-answer inference. An unchanged or
   harmful answer is a valid finding. P14 and Gatecoin need separate complete
   populations. A single paired trial is descriptive; it cannot establish
   general English correctness, statistical superiority or a new default.

Post-review: the strongest alternative explanation is that the deliberately
strict guards are functioning correctly on unsupported paths. That is true;
the remedy is to supply the missing reviewed method, bindings and reservation,
not delete the guard. A complete current-target run with inspected source
warrants and matched tool effects would overturn the conclusion that product
integration remains unevaluated. The weakest evidence here concerns actual
model behavior after the repaired transport and review stages; it has not run.
The historical pilot failed an engineering boundary. The current audit finds
additional route incompatibilities and missing evaluation; neither result
rejects the tools or the legal research direction.

The causal chain is: incomplete question-to-warrant discipline produces broad
candidate work; uncertain populations multiply that work; local capacity
repairs admit earlier stages without qualifying the entire route; conditional
repair and tool-feedback paths remain incomplete; and no complete matched
answer reaches the point where legal contribution can be judged. Installed
packages operate on the supplied readings and premises, so adding packages
alone does not resolve this chain.

This trace identifies an incomplete execution route and unsupported claims of
full evaluation, not a demonstrated failure of the six tools or of the legal
research direction. The first
discriminating output is a complete, question-correct, source-reviewed P10
answer under an affordable route; if that route cannot be funded before the
fixed deadline, the correct decision is under-budgeted, with conditional legal
analysis retained and no further doomed prefix dispatched.
