## UT 6.8: the referenced provision is the member-register rule

The product circular's footnote 1 says the Product Provider should remain fully
responsible despite outsourcing, giving arrangements envisaged under 6.8 of the
Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds as an example. Its further reference to
Part A of the intermediary Appendix retains “where applicable”. The footnote is
source unit `d996fc27b2d2e.u40.6899`; it must not be confused with paragraph 6.8
of the Handbook's separate overarching principles.

The retained October 2024 Handbook, PDF page 42 (Section II, printed page 19),
states:

> The scheme, or in the case of a unit trust, the trustee or the person so
> appointed by the trustee must maintain a register of holders. The Commission
> must be advised on request of the address(es) where the register is kept.

The retained page extraction matches the rendered page. The Handbook PDF has
SHA-256 `23f4d2f994a6cab493bb54cb2f855cbfa25b8e74cdc56a7e120905adfd35d4fd`.
The separate official-byte edition review supplies provenance; it does not
establish every current applicable rule or actual client facts.

The provision identifies who may maintain the register and an express request
condition for supplying its address. It does not make register maintenance
request-dependent. The product footnote separately preserves Provider
responsibility. Neither text supplies a comprehensive outsourcing contract,
an exhaustive supervisory-adequacy test, or proof that records are accurate.
The text does not make every Product Provider personally perform register
maintenance or turn that actor into the trustee.

Proposed addition to the conditional answer:

> Footnote 1's example refers specifically to UT Code 6.8, concerning the
> member register. Where that provision applies, the scheme—or, for a unit
> trust, the trustee or its appointed person—must maintain the register of
> holders. The Commission is to be advised of the register's address on
> request. That address-notification trigger is separate from paragraph 14's
> demonstration trigger. The product footnote retains Provider responsibility
> despite outsourcing; neither provision establishes actual performance,
> complete outsourcing terms or adequate supervision. Product form, actor,
> appointment and applicable-date facts remain necessary for application.
