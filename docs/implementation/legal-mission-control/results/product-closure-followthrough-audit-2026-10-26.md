## Review of the execution design

The scheduler drains ready answer consumers after each acquisition position.
This avoids requiring every acquisition to succeed before any answer proceeds,
but is not a bounded round-robin fairness guarantee: a long answer can delay
another ready acquisition. Protected call floors prevent spending its budget;
they do not reserve wall-clock time. This is a remaining scheduling risk, not
evidence that P10/evidence depend substantively on P14. Do not introduce an
untested concurrency change during deployment or dispatch beside the supervisor.

Skeptical audit: the repair baselines are actual current failures, not toy
proxies. Fresh objections retain their veto. Old attempts and evidence remain
immutable. Independent branches continue after local failures. Global authority,
unknown dispatch, budget and deadline are real continuation vetoes. Repeated
full-history validation remains a measured cost; no cache has been qualified.
The master cannot be declared complete merely because local tests pass or the
remaining graph becomes blocked.
