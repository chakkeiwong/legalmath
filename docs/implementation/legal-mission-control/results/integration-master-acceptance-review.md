# Master integration acceptance review

13 October 2026. The governing program remains
`docs/plans/legal-integration-master-2026-10-13.md`, registered by
`docs/implementation/legal-mission-control/integration-master.json`.
It covers the October 12 completion contract. This review is its completion-only
assessment, not a successor phase and not a declaration that implementation
or integration is complete.

**Total integration is not delivered.** Local implementation, finite execution,
native controls and source preparation have run. The actual model route failed
before complete acquisition and matched answers. The corpus review also found
material missing standards. All four original legal questions and all six full
tool evaluations remain open. The program's existing dependencies explain which
work cannot yet execute. A report can be complete while its program remains
blocked and its product fails acceptance.

## What executed and what it establishes

The optional bounded route reaches `run_product`, `CompleteInvestigation.run`
and `dossier_answer.propose`. The original-input reference rehearsal reaches
all four answer paths, retaining the 46 obligations, 35 readings and 12 cases
across 64 units and 151 chunks. The corrected rehearsal retains 1,822 scripted
calls and 1,062 source/bridge/observation accounts; a resume creates zero new
scripted calls. Scripted unresolved legal verdicts are not accepted answers.
The 88-test master regression and focused controls are engineering evidence;
overlapping suite counts are not added together.

The first actual campaign charged 167 calls, including 159 acquisition requests.
Only 71 acquisition responses validated; all 52 explicit bridges did validate.
The approved repair then charged eight canaries, bringing the master ledger to
175. Only the P10 reading and P14 review validated. No question passed both
required canaries, so no full repaired acquisition or complete answer arm ran.
The original 41-call expired grant and all earlier attempts remain preserved.
The repaired implementation's all-source review reservation checked 604
pair/singleton-plus-maximum-answer combinations; actual service framing and
semantic behavior remain separate unqualified conditions.

All six installed native profiles ran retained controls. BM25 retrieved for
the retained P10/evidence questions. LegalRuleML executed supplied supported
Boolean candidates. Logical English executed supported restricted translations
and an unsupported case. PyArg, Carneades and clingo executed the supplied
questioned, assumed, false-supported and undercut cases. Those inputs came
from an earlier reviewed preparation, rather than a successful new product
investigation. Their execution is useful narrower evidence and does not fill
the missing original-answer comparisons.

Independent source work continued after the model failure. Five original
judgments now render, selected reasons and orders have been inspected, fresh
official circular content matches retained content, and ten relevant Handbook
passages are retained with edition comparisons. The general diligence,
service-provider and remediation duties were absent from the approved corpus.
They advance the conditional legal analysis and require a reviewed source
revision before a new matched evaluation. Details are in
`integration-master-source-supplement.md`; live failures are in
`integration-master-recovery-result.md`.

## Execution and approval state

The master is executable and the fixed `live` command matches the installed
narrow allow-list rule. The rule's bytes match the reviewed repository copy.
Local checks and source work did not need a new broad approval. The grant has
an 8,100-call ceiling and an unchanged 18 October 2026 19:03:39 UTC deadline;
the approved recovery stage allocations and node-attempt limits also apply.
Unused global calls do not authorize a new stage, source revision or third
acquisition attempt.

The approval preparation also exposed an integer-serialization defect. The
concrete approved proposal was migrated with its preserved erratum, rounding
required seconds upward and available seconds downward. The current runtime
uses that corrected immutable proposal. The approval-generation action itself
still needs its exact-input regression before any future reuse. Do not rerun
`approve-and-run` to resume an existing grant. This is a local tooling defect,
not a fresh permission requirement for already-approved work.

The corpus node's final attempt retains partial qualification and the specific
remaining source/locator work. Release review uses completion dependencies and
can succeed as an assessment while explicitly rejecting product acceptance.
Acquisition remains failed, its descendants remain dependency-blocked, and the
master remains incomplete. The same source and attempt history must govern any
reviewed reopening; no new directory or renamed phase may erase it.
