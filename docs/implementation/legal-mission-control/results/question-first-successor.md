# Question-first successor: executed local repair

11 October 2026. The single reviewed offline repair is complete. The result is
**engineering acceptance with no substantive legal advance**. The mission stays
on `product-contribution`; the next live comparison is **under-budgeted under
the existing stage allocation**. No live provider was dispatched and no historical
grant, source capture or result was changed by this work package.

## What was repaired

The opt-in `original_questions` argument binds the ordered question IDs and exact
text before the complete investigation begins. It enters the method identity,
core transport, scoped requests and final-answer preparation. Both comparison
arms receive the same questions. Changed identities or text are rejected;
existing callers without the argument retain their previous behavior. This
preserves the answer target mechanically; it cannot prove that a model answers
fulfillment rather than merely describing a duty trigger. Optional challenge
and semantic paths have not been qualified under this new binding.

`ToolSession` retrieves each original question separately, with evidence retained
in its journal and attributable in final-answer accounting. Typed candidate
queries still require an explicit reviewed bridge before they can corroborate
an original answer. Multi-candidate checks remain indivisible: all checked
candidate revisions must be present and unchanged before delivery.

The route guard now recognizes `REPAIR_SOURCE_INVENTORY`,
`PROPOSE_DERIVED_MAPPING` and `REGISTERED_EXAMPLE_QUERIES`. Each constructor
requires an explicit reserve; it cannot borrow unrelated core capacity. Unknown
constructors and exhausted reserves remain refused. The new workload parameters
expose these costs and original-question retrieval costs. Tool-action arithmetic
is still an estimate, not a qualified bound for every possible request.

On staged or partitioned inventory routes, a selected source-inventory repair
now stops before replacing the inventory or dispatching stale criticism. The
stop retains the concern and old inventory/candidate hashes and identifies the
reviews, search, fidelity, tools and answer requiring a full restart. The
complete investigation also prevents downstream dispatch after that stop.
**A functional restart is not implemented.** The older non-staged repair path
retains its previous behavior; it is not a qualified substitute for this route.

failed, zero errors and zero skipped** across eight focused and compatibility
modules. The tests cover
exact question identity, early rejection, retrieval attribution and cache
binding, constructor reserves, source-repair stops, compatibility and mission
accounting. No assurance source file changed between those runs. The precise
original pytest invocation was not retained in the supplied handoff; the
closeout rerun's exact command is recorded in the manifest.

The controlled complete-product script ran a synthetic gift-rule source through
both arms. Each arm made 14 scripted investigation calls and three scripted
answer calls. All requests retained the two exact fixture questions. The
candidate invoked all six installed selected tools and delivered separately
attributable original-question retrievals. Both answers remained structurally
valid proposals with legal quality and actual tool consideration unevaluated.
This establishes delivery and accounting behavior, not an answer to P10 or a
measured legal benefit from any tool.

The follow-up skeptical review checked the actual constructor routing, early
bindings, immutable snapshots, test report, original ledger and resource
arithmetic. The reviewed plan's phrase “independent skeptical audit” is too
strong: the retained review explicitly identifies itself as executor self-review.
No independent human or model legal review occurred. The sealed plan and review
are retained unchanged, with that limitation recorded here.

## Return to the substantive task

The four original questions, 46 obligations and 35 readings remain visible; zero
of the six selected tools is fully evaluated at answer level. Nine cumulative
enabling detours are recorded, including this one. The original ledger remains
13/400 charged calls, with deadline 12 October 2026 04:28:01 UTC. The local
repair allowance is consumed once and set to zero.

The next work must address the actual P10/evidence answer: preserve the legal
standard, contrary evidence and the distinction between observed conduct and
fulfillment. Before another product execution, the method still needs (1) a
functional source-revision restart, (2) prospectively bounded complete claim and
candidate populations with stage-by-stage checks, (3) qualified actual responses
and task-specific latency, (4) an affordable reviewed stage allocation and live
authority, and (5) matched complete answers with source-reviewed per-tool effects.
P14 and Gatecoin require their own source and reading populations. No future
inventory is demanded before the qualifying calls that would create it.

The strongest alternative explanation for the apparent local success is the
scripted fixture itself: it supplies valid answers and a tiny source population.
A real response that loses an original question, contrary qualification or
legal warrant would overturn any attempted stronger conclusion. The weakest
evidence remains the absence of a complete original-target answer and measured
tool contribution.
