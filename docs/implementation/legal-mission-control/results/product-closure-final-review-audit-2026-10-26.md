## What the final P14 result establishes

Attempt nine retained 1,759 review results: 529 ACCEPT, 306 REJECT, 916
UNRESOLVED and eight failed account reviews. The rejected reviews contain 528
issue occurrences, comprising 371 distinct texts. The exact census retains
every occurrence and its original review hash. Identical wording is not a
reason to discard an assignment. Lexical flags are navigation aids, with no
authority to waive issues or classify them as substantive failures.

Several different problems coexist. Initial source groups omitted other
answer-cited passages; separate context reviews omitted Handbook passages
already authenticated for historical issue dispositions. The staged source
context repair supplies these records jointly. A different inspected review
asserted that operative provisions were absent while selecting those exact
delivered provisions in its own response. Source delivery is proved in that
case, but the reason for the model's error remains unknown.

The review target is also inconsistent. Review 528 uses REJECT for a dependency
that its reason says is unnecessary because the answer makes no staffing
compliance finding. Other reviews reject a title for not proving the entire
answer. The aggregation code requires every review to ACCEPT, so a rejection
of a proposed dependency becomes an answer failure. Exact request/response
traces preserve this mismatch. New instructions explicitly distinguish an
answer defect from a rejected dependency proposal. They grant no automatic
acceptance and do not relabel the original results. Semantic effectiveness is
untested until an authorized reassessment is completed.

Reviews 12 and 41 allege that the answer still lists the operative P14/P15/P16
and FN5 passages as missing. The exact delivered answer's source-gap list does
not contain those locators. It lists other passages, while its conclusion and
citations discuss the operative provisions. This disproves that specific list
allegation; it does not settle every broader complaint about source completeness.
Some retained successful reviews were replayed from earlier runs. The diagnostic
authenticates their historical request and identical semantic response, rather
than falsely equating their old and current transport request hashes.

There are also potential content defects which delivery and instructions alone
cannot repair. The census identifies omitted rival attachment of the satisfaction
standard; the separate staffing-confirmation direction; conditional consultation
and approval distinctions; the proper-controls requirement and non-exhaustive
network guidance; and the application purpose and satisfactory quality of legal
opinions. Review 803's proposed qualification partly repeats the answer's own
condition that procurement was not requested, so it needs exact wording review
rather than automatic adoption. Remaining source-gap allegations need individual
comparison against the precise answer and source delivery. Extraction, numbering,
applicability and later-treatment questions remain separate.

The existing appended draft preserves the old alternatives and source gaps.
That narrow editing policy protects history but cannot implement every legitimate
correction to those fields. It is therefore a reviewed candidate for further
work, not a complete repair. A later version needs explicitly reviewed changes
and a disposition for every original and intervening material issue. Mere
append-only equality must not become the substantive acceptance criterion.

## Executed engineering work

The actual production planner, with provider transport and dispatch disabled,
hit the five-minute diagnostic bound using incremental greedy grouping. That
is a local planning failure, not a model-capacity failure. The staged opt-in
batch-first partition measures complete blocks, splitting only oversized blocks
and retaining oversized singletons. Every final group is measured; no capacity
monotonicity assumption or history cache is used. The real P14 workload now
produces 73 groups plus eight account requests, preserving 1,751 assignments.
The final replay takes about 74 seconds, including authentication and the
pre-dispatch refusal check. This is a descriptive result for this workload,
not a speed ranking or an optimal partition.

All 81 initial requests were measured after downstream preparation and actual
reference transport. They fit the checked input bounds. Hidden CLI service
framing remains unmeasured. Follow-up contexts, invalid-output repairs, service
retries and objection reassessments are not included. The earlier 83-call
estimate used a different offline representation and must not govern funding.

The local-version selector authenticates the provider predecessor, exact
question/input, accounts, dependency population, candidate, sources and mapping.
It labels local authorship explicitly. The final correction refuses production
resume before any new reviewer call when the 306 predecessor rejections lack
version-bound dispositions. An actual-history replay proves zero dispatch and
preservation of the old evidence. Offline sizing remains possible without
pretending the candidate is ready for a live review.

The staged scheduler gives independent ready acquisitions priority at each
acquisition position and limits intervening answer drains to one completed
consumer. Tests exercise the actual scheduler loop, including failed acquisition,
service deferral and disabled-profile compatibility. This fixes the reproduced
task-boundary starvation pattern; it does not preempt a long individual task.
The previously tested typed budget-yield repair retains every review and defers
undispatched work after a proved same-branch exhaustion. Retained successes and
the deferred population still need a checked continuation.

The latest combined suite passes 227 tests. A prior scheduler fixture had the
wrong expected legacy order; its failed result is retained and the expectation
was corrected against the actual question order. The earlier planner timeout,
trace-path errors and failed test runs also remain in the execution record.
No error is erased to report a clean history.

These changes are staged and archived, not installed. The installed Unicode
predicate, answer-attribution checks, authenticated warrant reuse and narrow
enum-order repair from the earlier packages remain the active repairs. They
must not be confused with the new staged scheduler and review candidates.

## Review and decision

NOT READY for deployment or legal acceptance. Baselines are actual installed
dirty bytes and exact retained answers, not HEAD or a small fixture. Proxy
metrics remain engineering evidence. The latest profile is optional and changes
request identity. Comparison arms must use equivalent editorial, source and
review policies. Twenty-four members is an inherited convenience, not an
