## What changed

separate validated records and counters. A valid negative effect can complete
an evaluation without requiring adoption. Scope rejection requires reviewed
applicability and boundary evidence; missing targets or failed machinery cannot
be relabelled as a completed evaluation. The four original question texts remain
mandatory for claims about those questions.

The new applicability register retains all 24 question/tool pairs. Four BM25
pairs are applicable: each has the actual original question and retained source
units. The other 20 pairs require source-warranted formal candidates or argument
graphs. They remain open. No tool has been rejected merely because its necessary
input is currently absent. Answer/comparison dispatch checks applicability and
budgets the baseline plus actual applicable tool arms.

All 46 obligation and 35 reading IDs remain. The disposition validator permits
reviewed retained, duplicate, irrelevant, unsupported-demand and external-fact
decisions with source evidence. Every current record remains unreviewed; no
blanket reclassification manufactured completion. Missing facts require explicit
conditional analysis and a specific evidence request. Reader count, repeated
whole-source review, capacity limits and batch size are method choices, with
their provenance and early checks documented in the current plan.

R09 is locally repaired: unmet preflight prerequisites are retained as
NOT_DISPATCHED and consume no acquisition attempt. Authority/integrity vetoes
persist for their named consumers across restarts, while independent work stays
ready. Historical attempts are validated against the preserved pre-revision
state. A reviewed retry clears the old undispatched marker when the first actual
attempt begins. The old four wrongly charged attempts remain unchanged in the
historical record; the repair does not refund them.

R12 is locally repaired: a valid matched candidate rejected by complete,
source-supported adverse review can support a harmful-effect assessment. Broken
review output, unsupported native execution and mismatched inputs remain
insufficient evidence. R15's dormant missing import now routes to the actual
binding preparation helper; a dispatch-free test checks it.

The candidate corpus now includes Handbook Chapters 6–7, PDF pages 16–18. It has
80 source units, with original 46/35/12 acceptance populations unchanged. The
old corpus is intact. The supplement provides conditional disclosure/monitoring
language; it does not establish universal P10 fulfillment, source rendering
