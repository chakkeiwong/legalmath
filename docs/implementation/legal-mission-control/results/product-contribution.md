## What the installed tools actually computed

LegalRuleML preserved both the deliberately invalid shortcut and a separate
Boolean checklist of whether rights, record role and comparison coverage were
established. Its mutation control detected a changed representation. The actual
P10 allocation/conduct/outcome reading stayed `UNFORMALIZED`. Thus a successful
Boolean round trip cannot decide whether the English proposition or its legal
standard is supported. The checklist's true value means those inputs have been
supplied; it is expressly not a sufficient compliance result.

PyArg and the installed Carneades adapter agreed on grounded/preferred graph
semantics; clingo agreed with the product's stable extensions. All comparisons
used the effective graph after the product's premise blocking and explicit
defeats. Matching records were a stated hypothetical fact in every case.

| Supplied legal-warrant status and criticism | Observed graph result | Supported interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary QUESTIONED warrant | Shortcut blocked; grounded set empty | The product respects the supplied unknown status. |
| ASSUMPTION / STATED warrant | Shortcut in grounded set and marked conditional | Computation proceeds under an explicit assumption; it does not establish that assumption. |
| Falsely SUPPORTED warrant with real footnote-1 quote; no attack | Shortcut in grounded set; still conditional on the hypothetical matching records | Exact quotation and a SUPPORTED label do not establish entailment. The engine does not detect the false legal warrant. |
| Same falsely supported warrant plus the conditional shared-error UNDERCUT | Counterexample in grounded set; shortcut defeated | Supplied contrary reasoning changes the computation. The example's rights and shared-error facts remain assumptions. |

The underlying evaluator explicitly returned `legal_premises_verified=False`
and `candidate_pruning_authorized=False` throughout; tool-session reports also
returned `legal_premises_established=False`. These flags and the limits in the
delivered context are essential to interpreting the graph results. This is not
a reproduction of an erroneous live model answer: no live model supplied or
endorsed the deliberately false premise.

BM25 used the inherited eight-window configuration without tuning. On the exact
original P10 question, none of the three predeclared targets appeared in the
returned windows: paragraph 11, the Appendix validity/reconciliation note, and
the secondary-record footnote. P10 itself and footnote 1 were retrieved. On the
exact evidential-sufficiency question, paragraph 11 appeared at rank 8 and the
secondary-record footnote at rank 3. The validity/reconciliation note was only
partly returned: the rank-2 window stopped at character 4005 while the target
ended at 4047, omitting its encumbrance qualification. The full source packet
remained available to the receiving request. These are descriptive location
results on this corpus, not proof that retrieval worsens an answer or a general
estimate of legal relevance.

## Product connection and limits of the comparison

The test used the real `question_request` constructor, `ToolSession`, installed
`ProductToolBackend` and `CompactProvider`. Identical baseline and tool requests
reached a local receipt sink. Candidate checks, unsupported status, blocked and
conditional arguments, defeats and source/candidate hashes survived transport.
Changing the shortcut's expression excluded the old atomic candidate and graph
checks; retrieval remained. The input check also verified every source character
against its retained parent and preserved the extraction qualifications.

The development corpus contains 54 source units. One full circular has 20,543
characters, exceeding the product Packet limit of 20,000 per unit. Preparation
therefore made a lossless 55-unit projection with explicit original IDs and
offsets. Both arms received that same projection. This qualifies the tested
checkpoint inputs, not the full acquisition route; using the historical
12-unit excerpt packet as an allegedly equal comparator would have been wrong.

The sink returned no legal answer. This establishes provider-boundary delivery,
not downstream model consideration, an independently generated criticism graph,
completion of `CompleteInvestigation.run`, or an improvement in a final answer.
No P14 or Gatecoin tool trial ran here. Logical English still lacks the reviewed
source-to-controlled-language mapping and product adapter. The other surveyed
tools retain their individual dispositions.

Tracing `CompleteInvestigation.run` also clarifies the next prerequisite. Its
returned dossier collects interpretations, question assignments, scoped
assessments, proofs and replay; `execution_complete` is not a criterion for a
complete answer to the user's legal question. The next comparison needs an
explicit answer consumer and source-grounded assessment of that answer, bound
to this actual dossier and tool context. Another large assessment-pair matrix
alone cannot supply that evidence.

## Review of validity and the resulting decision

Two preliminary executions are preserved in `run` and `run-2`. Their printed
PASS labels are rejected for the planned contribution inference. The first
transported only retrieval feedback; the second corrected that delivery but
did not isolate the false warrant, preserve the exact retrieval question or
run the candidate-revision control. The review records those deviations and
the one final corrected experiment. No production code was repaired. The
preparation check caught the source-unit length constraint before that final
worker execution; no source text was shortened to make the check pass.

These were deterministic computations, not a stochastic comparison. There is
no statistically supported ranking or default-policy change. The next evidence
needed is a complete, matched product answer with cited support, contrary
readings and visible treatment of tool feedback. A single such pilot would
still be descriptive, not population accuracy or general English correctness.

Post-run red team: the strongest alternative explanation for apparent usefulness
is that the executor already supplied every important legal distinction. That
is true of this diagnostic and limits its claim. A product-generated graph and
answer that independently preserve the same distinctions would supply different
evidence. The weakest remaining part is source-to-premise justification and
actual downstream use, not engine execution. The first two attempts invalidated
the diagnostic implementation; the corrected run did not invalidate the tools
or research direction. It exposed their expected limits and triggers the
planned answer-level evaluation, not an unrelated repair campaign.

No live provider calls, network requests, installations, GPU computations,
commits or pushes occurred. Historical grants and deadlines remain unchanged.
