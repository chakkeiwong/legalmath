# Integration audit and current execution

13 October 2026. Governing program: `docs/plans/legal-integration-master-2026-10-13.md`.
This audit traced the selected bounded public product route, source construction,
reference transport, native adapters, final review and comparison, provider
allowance, scheduler and mission admission. Historical full-context paths remain
evidence and optional code; they were not substituted for the admitted route.
This is a scoped code audit, not proof that the repository has no other defects.

The central difficulty is that an installed engine can correctly compute its
supplied Boolean expression or argument graph while the product has omitted a
source, lost a qualification, supplied an unwarranted premise or failed to
review the resulting answer. Those are different failures. Tool installation,
successful invocation and source-delivery coverage cannot close them.

## Source, legal and product coverage still owed

The retained source revision adds material duties; it does not qualify the
entire Handbook. In particular, pages 7–15 include Chapters 1–5 and selected UT
pages. The Overarching Principles disclosure provisions continue on pages 16–17.
The comment describing the complete Overarching Principles is too broad: the
actual page list is authoritative. Chapter 7 also continues on page 18. Review inclusion by original question,
including circular disclosure duties, before claiming corpus completeness.
Keep the sealed source packet unchanged during the live comparison.

The judgment locator implementation currently accepts only two circular HTML
numbering profiles. Main judgment paragraphs, superscript footnotes and annexure
boundaries still require a profile bound to original OOXML and rendered pages.
The retained start-label diagnostic matches 181 of 184 starts; the other three
contain superscript markers absent from the text derivative. A short-prefix
match cannot qualify complete paragraph boundaries. Exact-unit quotes remain
the supported fallback. The same applies to Handbook clause/page identity.

Source-to-rule warrants, actor/product/date applicability, contrary authority,
P14 request/standing-duty alternatives and Gatecoin reasons/order tension remain
our research work. Later-treatment searches remain dated and bounded, not an
exhaustive current-law finding. Private appointments, agreements, actual conduct,
rights, records, timing, requests and remediation require specific client facts.
Those missing facts do not prevent conditional source analysis now.

The original four questions, 46 obligations, 35 readings, 12 cases and 224 premise
records/407 subparts remain unchanged. None of the six selected tools is yet
fully evaluated. All 26 surveyed dispositions remain distinct. No remaining
issue has been established impossible merely because a run or search failed.

## Program review and execution decision

The same 78-node master was migrated with byte-identical historical archives;
all attempt records and results remain. Sources, runtime, product, six native
branches, four qualifications and counted campaign preparation executed. Live
qualification and independent source qualification are the next ready work.
The original 175 charges are preserved; the first new control added slot 176.
The original 8,100-call ceiling and 18 October 2026 19:03:39.626294 UTC deadline
are unchanged. Total finite stage allocations, including prior spending, are
6,718 calls. This is an admission bound, not a prediction or a monetary budget.

Skeptical review: the current local passes do not close R01–R14. Source-page
selection, model review agreement and native PASS are particularly weak places
to overstate progress. The current live controls are justified to discriminate
semantic and service behavior under the frozen implementation; only eligible
dependent branches may proceed. Complete independent source work and preserve
any failed candidate. A successful assessment of failure cannot certify product
completion. Final decisions and live outcomes belong in the execution result
and reset memo; this audit is the issue register, not a completion declaration.
