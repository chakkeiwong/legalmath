### Bounded reviewer recovery design

Do not reject a review merely because a requested source is present: it may
identify an insufficiency, missing incorporated text or a necessary client fact.
The next candidate must distinguish (a) text not delivered, (b) text delivered
but insufficient, and (c) facts or external authority absent. Each delivery claim
should name its exact record/locator and be compared with a source-delivery
receipt. A conflict triggers one fresh reassessment with the original review,
unchanged answer, exact operative passages, all contrary context and a neutral
instruction to resolve the conflict. The result must explain each original
need. Preserve both reviews and any remaining material defect. Do not vote,
auto-accept, treat all missing facts as waivable, or repeat until ACCEPT.

For a discriminating presentation check, compare equivalent original compact
tables with a plainly displayed source packet, holding the answer, assignments,
source bytes and model settings fixed. One paired check can diagnose a possible
presentation problem, but cannot establish a general superiority claim. The
check is not authorized to consume protected unrelated-question calls.
