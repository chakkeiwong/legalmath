## Question, comparator and interpretation

The engineering question is whether explicit case identities and tool keys,
together with one diagnosis-guided argument-graph repair, permit actual matched
answers under the declared output contract. The substantive question is whether
the answers preserve the seven P10/evidence obligations and five independent case
distinctions, and whether any delivered tool result changes a warranted answer.
The fresh baseline receives exactly the same retained source, questions, cases,
four prose readings, repaired graph and executor input review as every tool arm.
The comparison tests the incremental contribution of the tool feedback on top
of those common inputs. It does not compare graph construction against no graph.

The earlier failed baseline is a regression control, not the matched comparator.
The explicit obligations, cases, readings and graph are development material;
this is not a held-out general-English test. All arms retain the 55-unit,
54,910-character selected source projection. Exact preservation of that text does
not recover original paragraph numbering or prove the corpus complete. Both
failed predecessor source readers remain excluded.

The acceptance criterion requires the source distinctions, preserved case facts,
valid fresh review and traceable treatment of actual tool feedback. An invented
applicability premise, unsupported legal inference, lost rival, false citation or
invalid review vetoes acceptance of the affected output. A branch failure does
not veto other arms or the completion-only assessment. Only shared corruption,
invalid authority, the original deadline or a spent bound can stop dispatch.
Wire identity, schema results, engine agreement and durations are explanatory
diagnostics. One response per arm supplies no statistical ranking or default
promotion.

## Findings already established

The repaired answer interface exposes the exact consumer constraints in the
provider schema. The argument repair corrects the P11/UT Code 6.8 confusion and
the sign of the outcome-sensitive conclusion. It removes all eleven unjustified
attacks. The accepted graph has twelve arguments, three blocked by questioned
premises, nine eligible arguments and no attacks. The blocked accountability,
outcome-sensitive and broader-confidence readings remain legal alternatives.
This is a valid input to installed engines, but its extensions cannot demonstrate
conflict resolution.

Baseline and clingo have conditionally acceptable answers and valid
fresh reviews. The reviewed answers preserve allocation versus conduct versus
outcome, conditional P11 applicability, relevant incorporation versus direct
intermediary duties, both P14 attachment axes, reconciliation's evidentiary
limits and case independence. These are scoped source-analysis findings. They
neither identify a complete P10 fulfillment standard nor decide an actual
client's compliance.

Three fresh reviews—BM25, Logical English and PyArg—copy their case-premises
quotation from the answer's limitations list. The consumer accepts only main
answer and case prose. Carneades' review instead invents a summary sentence in
its quotation field. The latter is a fidelity error, not merely an excluded
field. All four positive raw verdicts remain rejected. The answer instructions
expose the quote-location rule explicitly; the review instructions do not. This
remaining interface mismatch warrants a shared, precisely specified citation
interface rather than accepting invalid receipts retrospectively.

The combined answer also selects a quote from its limitations field. Unlike the
review prompts, its answer prompt explicitly forbids that location. The result
therefore shows that clarification alone has not made quotation handling reliable.
All eight outputs preserve exact case-fact and delivered-tool-key identities,
but only seven satisfy the complete answer contract. The combined review needs
a valid answer and was not dispatched; its one unused stage allocation is not
a transferable recovery call.

The LegalRuleML arm has a valid fresh review but an executor-detected citation
error: its answer calls the staff-competence provision P20. The retained official
HTML identifies it as paragraph 19; paragraph 20 concerns new-product prior
consultation. Its source quote is accurate, but the free-prose locator is wrong.
The common packet supplies unit IDs in its locator fields without a verified
paragraph map, and the current validator does not check such prose locators.
The fresh reviewer missed the error. The raw answer requires a citation
correction before reuse. This is not evidence that LegalRuleML caused it.

LegalRuleML and Logical English report unsupported inputs because every reading
has null formalization. Their adapter decisions reach actual answers, which
respect those limits. No external formal engine evaluates a substantive rule in
these arms. PyArg, Carneades and clingo do execute their installed engines on the
effective graph. Carneades also records synthetic CAES controls; those controls
are separate from the target's legal evidence. The engine results and explicit
answer accounts establish delivery and consideration in the focused route, but
no observed baseline omission is corrected. BM25 executes exact-question
retrieval and supplies passages that the answer checks against the complete
retained text. It rejects the valuation passage as a general tokenisation
supervision rule and retains qualifications beyond a truncated retrieval window.
That descriptive caution is not evidence of a causal answer improvement.

The strongest alternative explanation for the absence of a measured tool benefit
is that the shared full text, explicit obligations and reviewed development graph
already give the baseline the necessary reasoning. Conversely, a structurally
trivial graph cannot reveal whether argument engines help on a justified conflict.
A correct exact quote would not by itself show that its legal inference is
warranted: the missed P19/P20 error exposes that review weakness directly.
Independent source verification and an appropriate target could overturn a
limited contribution finding; repeated generic prompts alone would not.

The retained artifacts and shared inputs are valid for this bounded diagnostic;
specific candidate outputs fail acceptance. The result does not invalidate the
legal research question or show that the packages cannot help on supported
inputs. The declared one recovery has now been attempted. The next repair is
not already an executable task in v5, and no unused stage allocation is moved
to it. This distinguishes a consumed attempt from an exhausted 400-call global
ceiling or an expired deadline.
