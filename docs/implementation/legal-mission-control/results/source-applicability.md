The companion `26EC23` circular is dated 20 April 2026 and addresses

principally designed for on-platform secondary trading of SFC-authorised

coordinates with the tokenisation circulars. Those provisions may matter to
a product that actually has secondary trading, but they do not silently
become a primary-dealing P10 discharge standard. No such arrangement or

## Later treatment of Gatecoin

The exact HKLII name search returned seven results: the retained `[2023]
HKCFI 914` and `[2025] HKCFI 493`, plus unrelated 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2026
decisions. An exact `HCCW18/2019` search returned only the two retained
Gatecoin judgments. This is search evidence, not proof that no other appeal,
direction or treatment exists.

Three official later judgments were inspected:

* `[2024] HKDC 1628`, paragraphs 20–30, cites Gatecoin, treats USDT as
  property capable of being held on trust, restates the three certainties,
  and applies them to pleaded JPEX facts. It discusses platform records and
  the parties' objectively assessed relationship. That is a later
  application of a narrow Gatecoin proposition, not a general Product
  Provider rule.
* `[2024] HKCFI 370`, paragraphs 13–14 and 29–30, says each trust case turns
  on its particular facts, the nature and identification of the asset, and
  the context. It recounts Gatecoin's mixed-cryptocurrency/internal-ledger
  reasoning while also recording that Gatecoin found no express trust on its
  own terms and conduct. This reinforces the distinction between fungibility
  or ledger identification and the existence of a trust.
* `[2026] HKDC 1402`, paragraphs 16–23, restates Gatecoin's three-certainties
  framework and applies it to pleaded bank-account and securities facts. It
  contains no tokenisation arrangement, Gatecoin scenario-seven ruling or
  Product Provider analysis.

These decisions strengthen the existing caution that a crypto asset's
capacity to be trust property, a ledger balance, and a party's actual
proprietary interest are different propositions. They do not resolve the
conflict between the 2025 Gatecoin scenario-seven passages, establish later
treatment of that conflict, or justify transferring the Gatecoin result to
26EC22 without the target asset, terms, parties, governing law, record role
and chronology.
