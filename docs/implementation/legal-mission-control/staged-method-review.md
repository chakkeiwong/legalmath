## Staged method obligations

Use the exact-evidence store and distinct reader aggregates already specified in
`results/evidence-route-design.md`. For each reader, partition complete claims
into assigned groups while supplying the entire qualified source. A scoped
source-check response must account for each assigned claim and every source
unit, preserve missing-source issues and unresolved questions, and identify
nonlocal claim IDs. It may not claim to have inspected unassigned claim contents.
This needs a separate response contract; do not relabel the old whole-inventory
cross-check as successful.

All nominated dependencies must be supplied together in an interaction request,
with exact relevant raw claims, quotations and the complete source. The union
of dependency edges and unresolved questions must survive every reduction.
Retain explicit unknown cross-group interactions. An interaction group that
cannot fit is a resource refusal, not a reason to delete an edge. A final
full-source omission review is required to challenge the claim catalogue and
the staged findings; identifier accounting alone cannot detect a missing legal
qualification. Independent inventory readers remain blind to peer outputs.

Generation and refinement consume this reviewed evidence through the same
store and admission policy in both arms. They must retain original questions,
contrary claims, unformalized readings and explicit reduction uncertainty. The
tool arm adds the selected tool output only; every payload is measured after
that augmentation. The current core fidelity and later scoped fidelity assess
different contracts; their work cannot simply be canceled as duplicate to make
a call budget fit. Any consolidation is a reviewed method change requiring
tests of all previous dimensions.

Admission must use actual generated claim/candidate counts and all future
required stages before dispatching the next dependent stage. The configured
search-node limit of twelve is not the total candidate bound: `engine.py` can
also retain up to eight additional frontier proposals. That population must
survive; a planning calculation using twelve is a scenario, not a worst-case
guarantee. No truncation of frontier or unresolved legal readings is authorized.

The source-derived review cases include P10 versus P11, supervision occurrence
versus assessed conduct versus effective mitigation, the 23EC52 actor/activity
bridge, pre-activity assessment timing, shared-error reconciliation, secondary
record status, unknown inputs and unformalized fulfillment readings. A model
response that meets IDs but loses one of these distinctions fails substantive
review. Formal tools cannot replace the missing source-to-standard warrant.
