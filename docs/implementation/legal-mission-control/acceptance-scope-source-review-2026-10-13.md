## Handbook Chapters 6 and 7

The retained extraction's PDF pages 16–18 contain Chapters 6 and 7. Chapter 6.1
requires clear/effective disclosure; 6.4 concerns information necessary for an
informed investment judgment under the applicable product code. Clauses 6.5–6.8
address Product Key Facts Statements; 6.9–6.11 concern advertisements; 6.12 warns
against disclaimers excluding or limiting otherwise applicable obligations.
These are disclosure and product-code conditions, not a universal test proving
that P10 responsibility has been fulfilled.

Clause 7.1 conditions its duties on an ongoing information obligation under the
applicable product code. Its requirements concern effective timely dissemination
and close monitoring of matters requiring notification. Clause 7.2 requires a
competent designated delegate to review an advertisement before issue. Clause
7.3 describes investor education as best practice; 7.4 concerns information on
enquiries and complaints. A supervision-event log alone cannot establish the
specific effectiveness, competence, scope and timing these clauses address.
Their applicability must be established for the actor, product, date and conduct.

Include all three pages in a new retained source revision so applicable duties,
conditions and best-practice language are equally accessible to baseline and
candidate. Keep the former revision intact. Inclusion closes the omission of
these pages from the candidate corpus; it does not qualify extraction against
rendering, full paragraph ownership, current law, or any arrangement's compliance.
The 2024 edition date remains explicit. Gatecoin's original holdings cannot be
changed by silently treating a later Handbook clause as a premise of the court.
