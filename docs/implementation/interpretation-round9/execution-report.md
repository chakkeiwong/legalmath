# Bounded comparison of four interpretation procedures

The comparison investigates a narrower, testable question: given the same public
source, stated question and factual vocabulary, can four procedures recover a
retained reference behavior while exposing uncertainty? The procedures are one
reader, two isolated readers, those readers plus a search for missing readings,
and that search plus source criticism and one reconsideration. This is a
controlled development diagnostic, not a legal-accuracy benchmark.

The four cases come from the family-office and RA13 client-money FAQ families.
Each family has an original retained source and a deliberately altered version.
The reference formulas and scenarios remain hidden from model requests. The
altered versions have no regulatory authority. Declared fact definitions make
paraphrases comparable, but also constrain what a proposed program can express.
The references are authored translations, without an independent legal oracle.

## What the comparison found

All sixteen jobs completed. Each retained at least one proposal matching its authored reference on the hidden scenarios. All sixteen abstained; none was accepted or missing. Two jobs also retained an additional finite-reference behavior. The study used 63 calls: 53 validated tasks and ten rejected responses, all retained. All four assurance jobs executed their one reconsideration cycle.

| Procedure | Jobs | Reference in set | Additional signatures across jobs | Calls | Abstentions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| single-reader | 4 | 4 | 0 | 5 | 4 |
| isolated-readers | 4 | 4 | 0 | 10 | 4 |
| search | 4 | 4 | 1 | 13 | 4 |
| assurance | 4 | 4 | 1 | 35 | 4 |

The final candidate sets produced 7,168 Java/Python evaluations across the declared true, false, unknown and conflict states. All matched. This count excludes earlier pre-reconsideration comparisons and separate hidden-reference scoring. The complete receipt audit checked 36 task queues, all 63 attempts and their preserved allowance history. The grant stands at 144/500; the earlier ledger remains frozen at 97/100.

Every call is recorded, including failed responses. A request produces at most
two readings. A critic judges at most four named proposals while retaining the
full source and all competing proposals as context. Successful tasks survive
resume; validation repairs do not replace their original failed responses.
Each job had the same ceiling of 24 calls and one hour. The study reserved at
most 384 calls within the existing 500-call grant; the reservation was not a
spending target.

The altered client-money case illustrates why matching programs do not settle
English interpretation. Its retained candidates shared one executable behavior,
yet critics distinguished the corporation's general transfer-agent status from
its role in a particular handling of money. They also asked which appointment,
account and time period determine that role, and how the corporation's activity
relates to money held by an associated entity. A single supplied Boolean cannot
express all those distinctions. The critic therefore retained source and
classification questions even for proposals it marked supported. These are
findings about the controlled altered text, not conclusions about the actual
regulatory requirement.

The two extra behaviors also need explanation. The search arm on the altered
family-office text retained both an inclusive disjunction and a stricter
conjunction, alongside a non-executable reading about whether the wording states
only a necessary condition. The assurance arm on the clean client-money text
retained a rival adding transfer-agent status as a required input even though
its own explanation recognized conflict with the phrase “regardless”. Keeping
these proposals makes the disagreement inspectable; it does not give the rival
equal legal support. Candidate-set coverage alone would conceal this difference,
which is why excess behaviors and source criticism accompany the recovery count.

## Review findings and successor repair

During the frozen comparison, isolated adverse tests exposed three defects in
version 1's discrepancy propagation. A question stored only on an individual
reading could miss the merged concern list. Critics could also support two
incompatible programs without triggering reconsideration when no other concern
was reported. Rejected proposals also reached the final record without being
supplied to the semantic reviser, so a structural repair could remove a possible
reading from active consideration. These failures were reproduced by executing
the old method, then repaired and checked in an isolated copy. The original implementation and study
are preserved; these runs must not be relabelled as version 2.

The completed live jobs already abstain on retained concerns. The new adverse
tests demonstrate a missing path that nevertheless matters for future inputs.
They also exposed a mistaken acceptance expectation in one synthetic paraphrase
test: the authored reference itself contained unresolved questions. The repaired
scorer abstains on that unchanged reference. A separate explicitly cleared
synthetic variant checks the narrow paraphrase acceptance behavior.

The next executable plan is `docs/plans/interpretation-h0-reusable-command.md`.
It installs version 2, exposes an ordinary source-question command, runs it on
the disputed family-office clause, verifies exact resume without another paid
call, and performs the full regression. It uses at most 24 further calls from
the same grant. No external consultancy is a prerequisite to executing this
engineering repair.

The strongest alternative explanation for shared reference agreement is that all
readers inherit the same model error or the same incomplete factual vocabulary.
An all-abstention result supplies no accepted decisions from which to estimate
unsafe acceptance. Zero unsafe accepted cases must not become a claim of safety.
The current evidence does not establish a reduction in professional-review cost.
