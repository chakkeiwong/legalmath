# Skeptical review of the legal interpretation master program

5 October 2026. Reviewer: Codex author/executor. Scope: the entire master program,
P0–P8, their dependencies and proposed interfaces, the literature recommendations,
and the new monograph section. This is an author review, not a Claude review or
evidence that an English interpretation is legally correct.

## Decision

Proceed with independent review of the implementation specification. The
corrected program is concrete enough to start P0 and then implement the bounded
pilot. No product phase has been executed. PyArg compatibility, actual case
coverage and source-fidelity guarantees remain open. These are named phase
obligations, not facts established by writing the program.

## Phase-by-phase challenge

- **P0:** Correction is allowed only where the saved record is stale or
  contradictory; no legal issue closes through editorial repair. Changed active
  pointers require reconciliation. The full original denominator survives.
- **P1:** Quote validity, factual support and legal role are different types of
  evidence. A legacy migration must use unresolved fields, not invented facts.
  Unknown and conflict need tests beyond Python object serialization.
- **P2:** The pilot is a disputed reading, not a legal benchmark with an answer
  supplied by the author. The plan retains unsupported structures and requires
  source relations and actual proposals at the input.
- **P3:** Formal semantics is explicitly selected per computation. The independent
  reference must not duplicate production code. Unknown priorities remain legal
  premises, even if a computational profile still returns an extension.
- **P4:** Holding extraction is itself proposed interpretation. Court/opinion
  metadata and exact quotes are necessary but insufficient. No-citation and
  no-search-hit outcomes must not imply no contrary authority.
- **P5:** The hardest step is the evaluative warrant between observation and
  responsibility, adequacy or title. The phase must deliver actual narrow derived
  facts and precise unresolved legal predicates; it cannot rename a supplied
  Boolean as evidence-derived.
- **P6:** Integration must be observed in the full product path. Controlled
  explanation and compiled code can share a source error. Every branch and
  qualification survives the summary and target-selection boundaries.
- **P7:** A theorem about abstract functions is not a parser-correctness theorem.
  Formal scope and executed-runtime correspondence are reported separately.
  Declared mathematical transformations provide checks, not legal labels.
- **P8:** Scope extension must follow the original obligations, not convenient
  successes. Future-source observations cannot be repaired and still treated
  as untouched generalization evidence.

## Remaining uncertainties and strongest objections

1. **Source fidelity remains a research frontier.** The program can make
   assumptions and counterarguments explicit without proving that every relevant
   reading has been found. Its complete-qualified-result objective is compatible
   with that fact; a claim to have solved unrestricted interpretation is not.
2. **The pilot may underrepresent precedent difficulty.** No specific Hong Kong
   judgment was established here as resolving paragraph 10 or footnote 5. P4 must
   acquire and inspect relevant authority or retain that exact gap.
3. **Dependency reuse is unvalidated at runtime.** The licence/source audit
   nominates PyArg; it does not guarantee compatibility, performance or theorem
   preservation. The reference fallback must retain its own independent checks.
4. **Evidence rules may remain too narrow for actual arrangements.** Complete
   event records and source-supported evaluative standards cannot be replaced by
   confidence thresholds. Missing private records prevent an actual case result,
   not local interpreter development.
5. **No resource superiority is claimed.** The proposed limits bound failures;
   they are not measured optimal settings. Any training or stochastic comparison
   needs its own task-specific protocol.

Post-review challenge: the strongest alternative explanation for future success
is that all components consistently implement the same mistaken reading.
A source-bound counterexample or incompatible authority would overturn that
reading even if every compiler and argument engine agrees. The weakest evidence
remains transfer from the inspected US/tax/structured-case methods to the actual
Hong Kong disputes.

The complete build, rendered-page observations, preserved-content checks and
document identities are recorded in the accompanying documentation review and
validation files. They establish document integrity and the scope of author
inspection, not independent reader acceptance, legal truth or implementation
completion.
