## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: which retained official provisions and obtainable judgments supply
premises for responsibility allocation, effective conduct, achieved adequacy,
proper records/title and paragraph 14 / footnote 5 attachment? Which necessary
premises remain missing for each of the 46 original obligations?

Comparator: S1 attempt-2 (SHA256

P0 attempt-2 packet and obligation map. All 35 readings and 70 role records stay
retained. Original formulas are not rewritten from retrieval results.

Candidate mechanism: a source-bound rule inventory with explicit applicability
and sufficiency premises, plus a bounded official-judgment investigation. A rule
may support a conditional consequence only after its required premises are
supplied. No observation, quotation, authority label, model agreement or successful
download is sufficient by itself.

Primary engineering criteria: all 46 obligation identities individually receive
a source/evidence/missing-premise disposition; every proposed rule binds exact
quotes, source edition and dependencies; incomplete support stays unknown; amended
source or scope cannot reuse a receipt; tests exercise actual conditional rule
evaluation. New legal closure needs an independently checked scoped proposition.

Promotion vetoes: unresolved applicability, missing factual coverage, unidentified
holding or subsequent treatment, unproved source-to-rule interpretation.
Continuation vetoes: corrupt baseline/source bytes, false provenance, silently
dropped obligations, unsafe/unbounded retrieval or invalid checker.
Expected missing standards and unsuccessful searches trigger the next justified
repair; they do not establish absence of law. Counts and retrieval hits are
explanatory only. No accuracy, generalization, completeness, liability or overall
compliance claim follows from this phase.
