 KBC in the debt capital markets 


 
 
 

     
         
     
    
     
         
            
         
         The KBC group is a frequent issuer in the debt capital markets. The group and its subsidiaries have issued a variety of bonds with senior and subordinated status in many different currencies. Bond holders wishing to exercise rights attached to their bonds can contact KBC Bank NV. 
 
         
             
    
    


 
    
    
    
 
 
         
     
 




    
     


 
 

    

    
    
     The main debt issuances and programmes are listed aside, by issuer (KBC Group, KBC Bank or KBC IFIMA). 
 Our policy is to issue all MREL instruments (AT1, T2, senior holdco) from KBC group level as a single point of entry (in line with our resolution strategy), which is the highest level of consolidation, and down-stream in the appropriate format to KBC Bank and KBC Insurance. 
KBC Bank issues covered bonds and KBC IFIMA issues retail and wholesale debt instruments. All debt obligations of KBC IFIMA are unconditionally and irrevocably guaranteed by KBC Bank.
