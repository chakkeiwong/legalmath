Home Investors Debt Investors

Compass Group PLC maintains a GBP 6 billion Euro Medium Term Note Programme (EMTN Programme).

The most recent annual update of the EMTN Programme was completed on 18 June 2026.

EMTN Issuer Information

Compass Group PLC

Information on Compass Group PLC is available on its website at: https://www.compass-group.com/

Compass Group Finance Netherlands B.V.

With effect from 13 June 2025, Compass Group Finance Netherlands B.V. ceased to be an issuer in relation to the EMTN Programme, but continues to have guaranteed notes outstanding under the EMTN Programme.
