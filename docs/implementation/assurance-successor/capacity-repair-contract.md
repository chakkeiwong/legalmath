# Capacity repair without losing the question or the source

The interpretation/coverage decomposition below is a proposed continuation,
not an implemented capability or permission to restart an exhausted action.
The later sections distinguish the narrower repairs already executed. The
trigger is measured live evidence: the 26EC35
baseline timed out while returning one reading plus coverage for 263 units;
the 26EC55 scoped requests timed out at 32 and 24 pairs, while twelve-pair
requests returned completed judgments. Those observations establish failures of
the particular workloads. They do not establish that twelve is universally safe.

## Preserve the engineering question

Can a bounded decomposition process the complete selected source and retain all
required interpretive questions when a monolithic response exceeds a call limit?
The comparator is the retained monolithic protocol and its exact failed source
packet. A successful repair must finish its required stages on that source and
on structurally different, frozen stress inputs, without dropping source units,
qualifications, alternatives, prior objections or failed reservations.

An answer-shaped JSON response, a shorter prompt or a passing toy case does not
meet that criterion. A contrary legal conclusion is not a processing failure.
Source loss, inconsistent identities, hidden omitted obligations, unbounded
retries, leaked reference answers or missing final integration veto acceptance.
Elapsed time and request counts explain capacity only; they do not rank legal
accuracy. Preserve the original monolithic result and report unequal actual cost.

## Separate interpretation from repetitive response tables

For a large single-reader task, retain one maintained reading. First ask for its
substantive interpretation, qualifications, declared question and optional typed
program, with the whole selected source available. Do not require the long unit
coverage table in this same response. Give this stage a closed, bounded schema.
Every later revision must name the predecessor reading hash.

Next inspect bounded source-unit sets against that exact reading. Each response
must account for every requested unit once, identify a precise disposition and
retain its source quotation. Merge by unit identity, rejecting missing units,
duplicates and judgments about a different reading. Preserve cross-references,
exceptions and dependencies between pieces. A final whole-source consistency
pass must address every cross-piece concern and either retain the reading or
propose one revised reading. A revision invalidates affected correspondence
judgments; it does not retrospectively change their target.

This remains the single-reader comparator: it sees only its own earlier answers
and produces one maintained interpretation, not a collection of rival readers.
The protocol changes relative to the monolithic arm and must be reported as such.
The ensemble's peer answers and the evaluation's expected outcomes remain absent
from every request. A purely qualitative answer is explicitly unencoded, even
when it accounts well for all clauses. It earns no Java-execution credit.

## Close question coverage as well as response capacity

The completed S9 assessment retained sixteen conditional probes. In thirteen,
neither arm retained the stipulated reference answer. Those outcomes include
missing candidates, different-question programs and unresolved facts. They are
not thirteen proven interpretation errors. They do show that a broad request
followed by a small executable subcondition is insufficient for whole-source
coverage, even when its limited program is correct.

The continuation must bind each retained duty, exception and factual
classification to an explicit question with an actor, unit, temporal basis and
output meaning. For each question, record an executable proposal, a supported
non-executable treatment or an explicit unresolved/unsupported disposition.
Preserve the original broad question and cross-question dependencies. A program
for an approval exemption cannot count as an implementation of a transitional
application process merely because both produce Booleans. Bind revisions to
their exact question and source identities, and invalidate affected mappings.

Exercise the mapping with automatically constructed same-type/different-question
mutations, omitted questions, duplicate coverage and source amendments.
Acceptance requires complete accounting and rejection of semantic-identity
substitution. It does not require every source meaning to become executable or
established. Freeze structurally different inputs before live continuation and
keep the old sixteen cases descriptive. Do not add circular-ID branches or
condition generation on their expected answers. This extends N0 and N3 of the
refreshed successor; a zero count of contrary decisive results cannot skip it.

## Choose work partitions from measured size, not circular identity

Specify separately the maximum input bytes, output items, source units, claim–
candidate pairs and call duration. Unit counts alone cannot predict response
size: one paragraph can contain many interacting duties. Retain a deterministic
partition manifest showing every item and the parent question. On a capacity
failure, split the failed work while carrying its spent reservations, earliest
deadline and per-issue attempt limits. Do not reset those limits by changing a
batch index or directory. A failed request containing twelve issues has consumed
an attempt for those issues even if the subsequent split has only six per request.

Completed identical responses may be reused after checking source, question,
schema, model route and reservation identities. They are not fresh judgments.
Different batch memberships may change a model's reasoning; preservation of
source bytes does not prove identical interpretation under a new decomposition.
Retain that uncertainty and compare consequential outcomes on frozen probes.

If the whole source exceeds the input ceiling, bounded extraction with explicit
cross-piece references is a different, stronger extension. It needs a document
graph, identified unresolved forward/back references and a validated integration
stage. A model summary is not a lossless replacement for the source. Unsupported
cross-document dependencies must stop interpretive promotion while other
independent questions may continue.

## Execute discriminating checks before a new live continuation

The present scoped engine visits batches before rounds. Thus an early batch can
receive both its initial and follow-up judgments before a later batch receives
any judgment. Preserve this executed ordering in the historical record. The
continuation should implement a versioned scheduling option that allocates
initial perspective opportunities across the complete required matrix before
routine follow-ups, or a reviewed fair queue with explicit priority overrides
for consequential objections. Candidate-generation breadth and scoped-review
breadth are different properties; neither should stand in for the other.

Test the schedule with a budget that permits every initial request but not every
follow-up, a decisive late-batch exception, an early consequential objection,
invalid responses and an interruption. Every undispatched or unresolved item
must remain visible. A same-round peer answer must not enter the other initial
perspective's request. Resumption must preserve pair/role spending and the
earliest deadline even when the order changes. Compare coverage and retained
objections under fixed budgets before adopting the option; more initial
coverage alone is not evidence of improved legal accuracy. This scheduling
extension remains unimplemented in the current campaign.

1. Use a synthetic qualification placed at a partition boundary, an exception in
   a footnote, a table continued on another page and a superseded annex. Each
   must remain reachable from the affected proposed rule after merging.
2. Mutate unit IDs, source bytes, reading hashes, coverage membership and the
   cross-piece concern list. The merger must reject every detached or omitted
   record before a completed result is written.
3. Interrupt one response after reservation. Resume the exact completed work,
   retain the interrupted reservation and demonstrate that changed partitions
   cannot replenish its original issue limit or deadline.
4. Present one reader with an initially plausible rule missing the boundary
   exception. Require the integrated result to preserve the objection or issue
   a new reading revision; it must not quietly mutate the original program.
5. Freeze the actual failed public-source workload and an additional structurally
   different workload before live answers. Run only within an explicitly
   reviewed allocation of the remaining grant. Record unfinished tasks if the
   required calls do not fit; a spending ceiling is not a capacity guarantee.

The repair record must state whether failure invalidated the transport,
partition/merge implementation or current candidate, or merely exhausted the
declared workload budget. Engineering acceptance requires completed processing
and the negative checks above. English correctness and a safe review-exemption
rate require separate evidence; this capacity repair supplies neither.

## Additional measured inventory boundary

The eDDA atomic inventory timed out twice on the same 67-unit request containing
14,702 source characters. The authentication reader also timed out while
repairing a 29-unit, 7,755-character piece. These observations refute using
80 source units alone as an established safe transport threshold. Character
count, output claim count and repair-response size need separate limits. They
also leave a service-latency explanation open: request size by itself has not
been shown to cause these particular deadlines. Keep the retained transport
records and compare bounded requests before attributing the failures to size.

Splitting a previously failed inventory must preserve attempt counts per
source-unit identity and reader role, including failed whole-source attempts.
Otherwise hashing a smaller packet would give each child a new three-attempt
allowance and silently replenish the same underlying investigation. Repair 5 implemented this accounting for initial inventory units and their output repairs, with negative tests for repartition and deadlines. It is now used by the eDDA continuation. The later live duplicate-unit response exhausted its remaining source-unit allowance; processing correctly remains incomplete.

## Measured distinction between forecast and actual retained size

The UCITS twelve-pair legacy partition was blocked by a 3,409,937-byte forecast,
computed from seventeen maximum-size responses, above the 2,097,152-byte limit.
This is not observed output size. The opt-in incremental admission repair was
staged under /tmp/legalmath-successor-capacity and reviewed in
incremental-fidelity-review.md. Its 22 staged focused/default checks passed;
the merged repair suite passed 72 checks. It preserves
pair/call limits and checks actual retained bytes and concerns before admitting
each validated part; an excessive part survives outside the incomplete
aggregate. It was merged as an explicit opt-in for S8 attempt nine. On the live
UCITS workload it admitted all seventeen response batches, whose canonical sizes
sum to 204,355 bytes, and retained the twenty-five separate deterministic
unsupported-encoding rows. The complete legacy pass accounts for 225 pairs and
retains 75 concerns. This establishes execution of this capacity repair on that
workload. The later scoped review stopped at its call deadline, with 48 pairs
having both perspective proposals and only 34 having all four dimensions
assessed; it still has 177 pairs without both perspectives. The complete UCITS
investigation is therefore incomplete. The generic split baseline
interpretation/coverage/whole-source reconciliation above remains a separate
unimplemented extension.

The separate `source-reference-protocol-spec.md` defines a future exact-span response protocol for the measured NBSP/NUL quotation and duplicate-coverage failures. It is unimplemented; no current failure is relabelled as a pass.

The later pilot also exposed an incomplete diagnostic for inconsistent
question-relation/correspondence labels (review finding F11). The bounded
response-protocol repair must identify the exact affected claim, candidate and
field for each cross-field rejection. Its tests must preserve all valid rows
and the original invalid response while rejecting both directions of the
inconsistency. A clearer diagnostic cannot replenish the same issue's failed
attempts or convert a rejected response into an accepted legal judgment.
