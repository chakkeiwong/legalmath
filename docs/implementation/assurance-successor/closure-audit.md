## Execution order for the measured continuation

First use N0 to bind the unfinished source, question, candidate and pair sets to
their original failed receipts. Implement the generic response/source-reference
and fair-scheduling repairs before spending the remaining live allowance. Their
stress checks must include structurally different inputs, not circular-ID
branches. Run malformed identity, Unicode, duplicate/missing coverage,
partition-boundary exception and interrupted-resume checks. Any dropped source
obligation vetoes the repair.

Next extend the smallest missing arithmetic/profile or domain feature through
N4, using the retained unsupported expression as a reproducer and generated
structural variations as engineering challenges. Preserve the old result and
open a new method revision. Connect those outputs to the shared qualification
route in N5, declaring the exact propositions and remaining premises. Do not
reinterpret a proof about a constructor tree as proof of natural-language
meaning.

Only then allocate a bounded live continuation with per-issue histories and a
complete denominator. The 37 remaining global calls do not restore exhausted
per-unit or per-pair opportunities or guarantee completion. A revised protocol
needs a recorded identity/attempt policy before it can dispatch. A missing
authoritative source, unsupported formal meaning or exhausted required resource
remains an explicit qualified result. It does not authorize an inferred answer.

perspective slots and UCITS lacks thirty. Their total of forty-eight already
exceeds the remaining thirty-seven calls, before response repairs, follow-ups,
new inventories or the two other frozen tasks. The pilot has also exhausted its
120-call arm ceiling. This is a measured resource veto for the current protocol,
not a reason to discard pairs, merge their questions or reset issue limits.

The frozen prospective window in the concurrent qualification increment is
already implemented. Use its eligibility and source/method bindings; do not
start an easier retrospective window or call repaired development cases future
