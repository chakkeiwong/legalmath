## S9: what the conditional comparison can establish

The comparator is the single maintained reading under the recorded protocol,
with the same frozen source packet and declared arm ceiling. Actual usage is
unequal and is not evidence of a fair-cost performance advantage. The sixteen
conditional reference cases predate these answers but were written by the
implementing agent. They are not independently adjudicated outcomes.

The reviewed evaluator sends case facts, questions and assumptions to the fact
mapping route, withholding expected answers and selected reference passages.
It keeps generated candidate programs unchanged. A mapping must address the
same Boolean question or its negation before actual Java output can be compared.
Missing programs, unaligned questions and failed mappings remain coverage gaps;
they cannot receive credit for correct abstention. Multiple retained answers
include contrary proposals even when another proposal matches the reference.

Successful evaluation means that every selected case is accounted for and its
available conditional execution is retained. It does not mean every answer is
correct. A contrary decisive answer vetoes interpretation promotion and triggers
investigation; it does not prevent recording other cases. A missing or altered
source, reference or candidate identity invalidates the affected evidence.
Resource exhaustion prevents further dispatch while leaving all cases in the
denominator. There is no ranking, error-rate or review-cost promotion in this
four-task descriptive study.

The partial-proposal reader verifies the active investigation revision and its
producer journal before exposing retained candidates. It cannot substitute a
historical successful revision for a failed current investigation. The pilot
and UCITS dossiers will be checked again after S8 becomes terminal. Empty source
inventories in the two failed tasks remain failed source work.
