## Post-run red team

The strongest alternative explanation for valid responses is that readers can
produce well-formed, quoted proposals while sharing the same legal mistake.
The encoding checks cannot rule that out. The weakest evidence is the single
reused circular with incomplete coverage and no independent legal reference.
A demonstrated reconstruction error would overturn the transport result; a
missing or altered recorded action would invalidate the recovered report.
Neither a software test count nor agreement settles the proposed legal premises.

The failures concern transport, service continuity and rejection reporting.
They do not reject the entire interpretation research direction. The next
smallest useful work is to examine the retained alternatives and missing
premises, define a bounded continuation with its original histories, and only
then decide whether additional live evidence is justified.
