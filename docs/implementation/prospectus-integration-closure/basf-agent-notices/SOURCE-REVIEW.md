# BASF §6(2) agent-change source review

The original German page 124 table gives the initial Fiscal Agent and Paying
Agent branch applicable except for a permanent global certificate held by or
for CDS: Deutsche Bank
Aktiengesellschaft, Trust & Agency Services, Taunusanlage 12, 60325 Frankfurt
am Main, Germany. A separate Canadian/CDS table and the calculation-agent
placeholder are conditional template branches. The final terms on page 7 name
Deutsche Bank as Fiscal and Paying Agent and NatWest Markets N.V. as Calculation
Agent. Those final-term facts support this issue's selected agents; they do not
fill the generic `[Name und bezeichnete Geschäftsstelle]` text across every
conditional branch.

The selected paragraph at page 124 lines 45–47 permits the named agent(s) to
replace a designated office with another office in the same country. Paragraph
§6(2), lines 48–52, reserves the issuer's right at any time to change or end the
appointment of the Fiscal Agent or Paying Agent, and conditionally the
Calculation Agent, and to appoint another or additional agent. It requires the
issuer to maintain a Fiscal Agent at all times. The U.S.-dollar
and CDS/Canadian branches are excluded in the frozen construction. That
construction retains calculation-agent wording and its maintenance clause,
whose template condition is "if a Calculation Agent is to be appointed".
The missing B54 name/office authority is separate from that selected wording.

The selected text at lines 63–64 completes the maintenance sentence. The next
sentence begins within line 64 and ends with "wurden." in line 68. It
states that a change is effective only after holders have been informed in
advance under §13 with at least 30 and at most 45 days' notice. A change becomes
effective immediately in insolvency. The rest of line 68 and lines 69–71
define `Vereinigte Staaten` independently of the optional U.S.-dollar branch.
The temporary-global paragraph expressly imports this definition. Its previous
reference is preserved; its meaning is outside this package and it supplies
no New York office.

The final terms select the Luxembourg Stock Exchange website and Clearing System
as notice media and select German and English with German controlling. The
previous notice package supplies the §13 reading; this package references that
complete target without importing a notice date or proving a change occurred.
The conclusion is therefore a source-bound development reading of conditions,
not a finding that any agent change has occurred or become effective.

The placeholder B54 (`[Name und bezeichnete Geschäftsstelle]`) remains
unresolved. The Canadian/CDS and U.S.-dollar branches remain excluded.
Calculation-agent wording retains its template condition. No office is invented from
the final-term name. Existing scalar mappings, four references, 15 markers and
537 unresolved intervals remain unchanged.

The supplied clauses classify these paragraphs as having no direct Q1 principal
loss or Q2 common-conversion effect. Their positive appointment, maintenance,
notice and timing requirements remain in structured metadata. The Q1/Q2 engine
does not determine whether a real agent change or insolvency occurred, whether
the notice was legally delivered, or whether a 30–45-day period was satisfied.
