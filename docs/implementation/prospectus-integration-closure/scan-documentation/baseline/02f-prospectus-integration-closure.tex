\subsection{Integration repairs and remaining evidence}
\label{sec:prospectus-integration-closure}

The next repair program traced accepted package outputs through the application:
did the extracted text still identify its source, did the selected clause apply
to this instrument, and did the calculation actually use its terms?  Several
earlier failures arose between these steps.  A valid layout file had no consumer
in the assessment service.  A quotation could come from an excluded alternative.
A coupon calculation could use a supplied rate without deriving it from the
selected contract.  These were defects in our integration, even when the
underlying parser, solver, or financial library worked as configured.

The closure program therefore separates five decisions: execution, artifact
validity, the engineering criterion, actual consumer integration, and external
acceptance.  It records them in immutable phase receipts under
\path{docs/implementation/prospectus-integration-closure/}.  A failed but
well-formed attempt remains evidence and cannot be retried with unchanged
inputs.  A current assessment revalidates source derivatives and admissions;
an explicitly labelled frozen replay does not silently acquire the guarantees
of the new profile.

\paragraph{What changed in the service.}
The source graph now validates exact OCR page sequences, unique unit identities,
document hashes, raw intervals, and recorded applicability times.  A clause
premise must refer to the selected occurrence and question.  Construction
authority has a separate checked admission: its source must have been consumed
by the selection or override.  Dated-law premises retain their own source,
jurisdiction and time checks.  These checks establish consistency with supplied
authority records; the legal authority itself still requires review.  Supported
references resolve through raw and assembled intervals.  Repeated copies need
an explicit occurrence use; deleted, replaced, or ambiguous targets cannot
silently resolve to the first matching quotation.

The construction and interpretation products use the same bounded predicate
interface.  Finite reasoning remains the compatibility path; an explicit Z3
request reaches construction, clause, and law stages.  Conflicts, witnesses,
unsat cores, limits, and typed unknown reasons survive into the report.  A
large Z3 request now returns \textsc{unknown}/\texttt{DOMAIN\_LIMIT} before
starting the solver rather than raising an unclassified exception.  This is a
resource boundary, not a claim that the legal question is false.

The package adapters now have real consumers.  A retained layout projection is
bound to the source graph and reviewed governing edges; losing a governed unit
makes the affected question unresolved.  Authored XMI spans are decoded with
the actual type system, mapped through raw and assembled occurrences, and
translated into typed clauses.  Authored condition and exception relations
must correspond exactly to the clause links consumed by the interpreter.  A
reviewed cross-document link binds both exports and their group endpoints;
named, relative, external and amendment references have a separate reviewed
admission.  This supplies an assisted route while automatic discovery remains
incomplete.  Bounded acyclic priorities have an independently enumerated
three-rule control; cycles remain unresolved.  Corrected OCR readings preserve
raw quotations, and inserted or uncertain text cannot acquire borrowed source
geometry.  The reading-to-evaluation mapper uses the same correction rules.
A signed administrator record binds the submitted export and assignment; its
signature cannot establish the reader's qualification or independence.

The BASF construction exposed a more specific loss of evidence.  Removing a
branch delimiter changed a line without removing its surviving provision, but
the adapter discarded that line's character map.  A final-term amount inserted
into the selected conditions likewise lost its usable source occurrence.  The
repair carries copied intervals into interpretation and binds an inserted term
to its exact final-term quotation.  A deleted template cannot supply that
quotation.  Older substitutions identified only by page-text offsets remain
unmapped until their exact source occurrences are reviewed.

A second defect kept a recognizer's unresolved proposal active even after its
reference had been reviewed.  The new admission explicitly names the proposal
it replaces, for the selected occurrence and question; other occurrences and
questions retain their own obligations.  On the inspected German BASF pages,
this connects \S5(10)(b) to the make-whole call in \S5(5), \S13(3) to the
ownership-proof provision in \S14(4), and \S1(3) to the United States definition
at the end of \S6(2).  The construction and raw numbering are unchanged.
These three connections establish reference consumption under the supplied
review, while principal-loss and conversion questions remain unknown and
contract completeness remains partial.  The 537 unresolved source intervals
still require individual meaning and authority decisions.

The OCR path records a versioned CPU profile, language-model hashes, page images,
word geometry, and blank or unread dispositions.  A missing Poppler executable,
worker, or language file is a typed extraction failure.  The current default
is project-local and configurable; the historical training environment is no
longer an implicit production dependency.  Native subprocess reads are not
claimed to be confined.  Their receipts therefore cannot be reused as fully
current unless the read set is observed or the invocation is run under a tested
restriction.

For finance, selected terms now include principal, calculation amount, rate,
currency, period dates, maturity, day-count, frequency and rounding.  A scenario
cannot replace them.  The distinction between rounding per holder and per
calculation amount matters: two EUR100.10 units at five percent yield EUR10.01
if rounded after aggregation, but EUR10.02 if each unit's EUR5.005 is rounded
to EUR5.01 first.  The repaired kernel represents both supplied conventions
explicitly, including the published fixed coupon when applicable.

The retained Compass final terms provide a real development example:
XS3232968985 specifies EUR1,000 calculation amounts, a EUR35 annual fixed coupon,
15 January 2026 commencement and 15 January 2035 maturity.  For a hypothetical
entitled EUR100,000 holding, the service calculates EUR3,500 for the first
annual coupon and generates the nine anniversary dates in the final terms.
The dates agree with QuantLib 1.38; the annual fraction is exactly one.  Altered
rate, date and issue-principal values fail their source bindings.  The result
assumes timely presentation, no early redemption or default, and the declared
limited future calendar.  The registered-note record-date provision is not
imported into this bearer-note example.  Incorporated annual-report material
and full-contract completeness remain unresolved, and Compass's related family
is now excluded from any claim of unseen evaluation.

\paragraph{Evidence from the executed phases.}
The combined successor and selected financial regression passes 681 checks.
Standalone XMI and ZIP exports carry an authored exception through the service.
Separate English and German scan controls preserve their designated text and a
blank page.  On retained BASF page 112, all three supplied shared-definition
dependencies resolve; removing one required line makes them unresolved.  Eight
day-count examples and four calendar adjustments agree with independent
fractions and dates and with QuantLib under identical conventions.

A fresh, non-editable wheel runs nine cases through the public assessment
command, constructing all five products inside the isolated installation.
They include Compass, the three reviewed BASF references, an AST-selected
definition, a cross-document exception and a thirteen-fact Z3 construction and
law case.  Relocation and offline
restoration preserve the answers.  Docling's CPU packages and model restore
twice from a verified binary snapshot and preserve fourteen mapped regions on
the BASF page.  Restored Java, INCEpTION, Chromium and Playwright also run twice:
the browser authenticates and the local server exports a synthetic project.
These results establish the recorded host profiles.  Native libraries remain
unconfined and a fresh operating-system image has not been tested.

Failed attempts exposed additional local defects.  The codec and finance
exercises initially imported pytest-only fixtures; dependency-free fixtures
repaired that error.  The first Compass run repeatedly rebuilt the full source
index; a local index removed that unnecessary work.  A controller comparison
loaded different source versions in parent and child processes; the corrected
experiment snapshots the same source.  The first restored authoring request
used a human title where the API required a slug.  The corrected request works.
An installed retry also rejected the older Compass input after Poppler's
executable changed.  The complete source-graph comparison found identical text
and geometry, with only the executable identity changed.  Rebuilding the
request from those sources and repeating the exact coupon, schedule and adverse
controls passed.  The installation harness now verifies the chosen successful
attempt's saved files instead of hardcoding the older request.
Each failed attempt is retained alongside its repair.

\paragraph{What the literature contributes.}
The literature reviewed in Section~\ref{sec:prospectus-delivery} remains relevant
to these repairs.  ContractNLI's self-contained supporting spans and
separate treatment of distant exceptions motivate retaining context and refusing
to infer absence from an unmentioned sentence \cite[Sections 2--5 and
Appendix A.1]{contractnli2021}.  Typed spans, events, and argument relations in
Holzenberger and Van Durme motivate the explicit actor, effect, condition, and
date fields \cite[Sections 3--5 and Appendices B, D and E]{connecting2023}.
LegalRuleML and structured argumentation separate a source, an interpretation,
and the authority selecting between conflicting alternatives
\cite[Sections 3--4]{legalinterpretations2014} and
\cite[Sections 3 and 6]{aspic2010}.
Catala's compiler argument supports preserving a formal execution boundary, but
cannot certify that a local prospectus translation is faithful
\cite[Sections 4.1, 4.4--4.5 and 6.1]{catala2021}.  Date-arithmetic work explains
why calendar rounding and operation order must be selected terms rather than
library defaults \cite[Sections 2, 3.2 and 4.3]{dates2024}.  These sources justify
the architecture and its tests; none supplies a complete prospectus engine or
a transferable accuracy result.

\paragraph{Root-cause disposition.}
The evidence supports local integration and usage defects as the cause of the
reproduced failures.  It also leaves substantial work in our program: broader
reference discovery, actual multilingual and table cases, wider schedule and
entitlement semantics, and native-read restrictions.  These are not all missing
external inputs.  BASF's field meanings, incorporation and calculation-agent
authority additionally require source decisions, while independent acceptance
requires actual readers and an adjudicator.  ACTUS and FINOS CDM still lack their
declared runtime or receiving-profile prerequisites and were not trialled here.
None of these results establishes whole-package unsuitability.

The remaining work therefore begins with named source cases and discriminating
comparisons.  Complete the BASF source and meaning table without guessing away
its unresolved intervals.  Extend the financial comparison to source-reviewed
stubs, transfers and entitlement events.  Test native filesystem or network
restrictions with an undeclared-read counterexample.  Obtain genuine first
readings on a frozen, family-separated cohort and preserve adjudication before
exposing predictions.  The phase controller retains these obligations even
when all implemented checks are current.  The service remains a conditional
assisted system; the full integration program and independent legal acceptance
are incomplete.
