# Portuguese 2010 source and supplied reading, 12 October 2026

The Portuguese 2010 report is now stored locally and has been consumed by the
actual P1 source service. Its two sustainability-assurance pages support the
same limited subject and reliance restrictions identified in the English
version. This is a conditional comparison of inspected wording; independent
edition authentication, historical publication evidence and qualified
translation review remain outstanding.

## Acquisition and validation

The exact URL was already in the retained issuer-archive routes. The sandbox
could not resolve the host; a trusted request returned HTTP 200 but reached the
120-second bound after 5,269,348 of 8,125,384 bytes. The issuer advertised byte
ranges. One request for bytes 5,269,348–8,125,383, guarded by the same ETag,
returned HTTP 206 and exactly 2,856,036 bytes. The original partial bytes,
separate range bytes, response headers and recovery review are preserved.

The combined PDF has SHA-256
`981fa3f96ed65da0d42f3de9c35797f1dfbd676c6f6c11fbcf394b54adff3058` and
256 pages. PyMuPDF opened it without repair. The HTTP Last-Modified value is
31 December 2021; it is not evidence of first publication or availability at
the July 2011 instrument date. The internal assurance date is 4 March 2011.

The contents page names the limited-assurance report at printed page 88, and
the actual images confirm PDF pages 88–89. Their native text contains only
65 and 43 nonspace characters. That location was established from this source,
not inferred from the English page numbers.

The complete transcribed negative-assurance sentence and third-party clause
are retained in `critical-spans.json`, linked to original image hashes.
The source describes sustainability assurance; it does not by that wording
establish financial audit assurance, issuer solvency, bond repayment, or an
investor's right of reliance. The English report's Portuguese-priority clause
is evidenced in the English source, not asserted to be repeated in Portuguese.
