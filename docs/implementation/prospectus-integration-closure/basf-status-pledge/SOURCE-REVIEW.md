# Original-page review: status and negative pledge

On 10 October 2026 the implementer inspected retained original images
base-110, base-111, final-2 and final-7. The cached graph, construction and
scalar-admitted assembly were checked against all three identities in the
amount/holder packet before inspecting the raw and surviving selected lines.
This is development source review, not an independent legal reading.

Final page 2 names BASF SE as issuer and selects Option I. Final page 7 makes
German controlling. The surviving heading at 110:47 is `STATUS,
NEGATIVVERPFLICHTUNG`; its bracketed guarantee continuation is removed.
Selected status at 110:49–53 states unsecured, unsubordinated obligations,
equal ranking with one another and with the issuer's other unsecured,
unsubordinated obligations, subject to mandatory statutory priority. It does
not identify any actual statutory priority or promise equal recovery.

The full issuer negative pledge is 110:54–75. It lasts while notes remain
outstanding, but only until all principal and interest amounts have been made
available to the Fiscal Agent. Its debt scope is present or future
capital-market indebtedness and guarantees or other assurances for that debt.
The restricted security is over the issuer's assets. Holders must participate
simultaneously and with equal rank in that security or in other security
recognized as equivalent by an internationally recognized independent auditor.
The final `lassen.` at line 75 completes the sentence and must survive.
Funding the Fiscal Agent is not proof of receipt by every holder.

The definition at 110:76–80 qualifies the borrowed money, securities, original
maturity of more than one year, and actual or potential listing/trading on a
stock exchange or other recognized securities market. The negative pledge's
use of this defined term therefore needs the complete definition. The bounded
heading recognizer has no proposal in 110:49–80; absence of a proposal does not
erase this semantic dependence. The next admitted implementation should bind
a typed clause-definition edge to this complete supplied definition and test
the unresolved effect when the definition is omitted. The six earlier raw
reference admissions remain separate. Ordinary statutory wording supplies no
identified external legal authority to resolve automatically.

The guarantee for BASF Finance issues is a distinct, excluded alternative:
110:84–85 and 111:5–21 have empty selected text. The source-only side labels
at 110:81–83 and 111:1–4 remain outside these three meanings. In particular,
the guarantor paragraph's `bestehen zu lassen`, equal-proportion wording and
section 328 BGB third-party enforcement language must not be imported into
the selected issuer covenant. The complete image, including the page break,
was inspected to check this distinction.

Actual compliance, security creation, payment, statutory priority, insolvency
recovery and ownership remain unevaluated. This source review admits neither
a guarantee nor an automatic decision on contractual loss or conversion.
B54, incorporation alternatives, the bilingual redemption discrepancy,
fifteen markers and 537 construction intervals remain open as previously
recorded. Final execution admission still requires the completed amount/holder
comparator and a reviewed bounded plan.
