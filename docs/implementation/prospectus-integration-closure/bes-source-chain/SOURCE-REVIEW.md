# BES Series 23: source chain review

The final terms dated 14 July 2011 name Banco Espírito Santo, S.A., acting
through its head office, as issuer of EUR 81.4 million fixed-rate senior notes
due 15 July 2016. Issue and interest commencement are 15 July 2011. This is
Series 23, tranche 1, ISIN PTBEQBOM0010. The introduction selects the
3 November 2010 prospectus's conditions **other than undated deeply subordinated
notes**, together with six dated supplements. All six supplements predate the
final terms. The final reference is printed in brackets, `[13 July 2011]`;
the retained sixth supplement actually bears that date. Brackets are retained
and do not authenticate the document or its delivery.

Source identities, original PDF hashes, retained text hashes and rendered page
hashes are in `source-review-001/inventory.json`. All 31 newly rendered pages
were actually inspected, including all 18 supplement pages. The selected base
pages are 1, 37–38, 40–41, 76–80 and 110–112; printed and PDF numbering agree.
Final-terms review images 1–3 were reinspected alongside the prior six-page
oracle. This is executor development review, not independent legal adjudication.

## What the documents support

| Passage | Reading and consequence |
|---|---|
| Base cover and p76 | Bank and BES Finance are distinct issuers. The Bank's guarantee of BES Finance notes does not describe these Bank-issued notes. Interbolsa notes may be issued only by the Bank through its head office. |
| Final terms pp1–3 | Senior, fixed-rate, book-entry registered Interbolsa notes; New Global Note: No. Credit-linked, redenomination, issuer call and investor put fields are not applicable. Unselected base branches must not supply their rights or losses. |
| Base pp76–78 | Applicable Final Terms supplement or modify conditions only to the stated extent or on inconsistency. For Interbolsa, the Principal Agency Agreement prevails over the Trust Deed on inconsistency; applicable Final Terms prevail over those agreements. This is not a blanket deletion of earlier text. |
| Base p80, Condition 2(i) | Senior unsecured and unsubordinated status preserves the exception for obligations given priority by law. This clause does not prove actual recovery or absence of statutory priority. Subordinated branches immediately below are separate alternatives. |
| Base pp40–41 and 79–80 | Interbolsa title depends on book entries and relevant Portuguese rules. Euroclear/Clearstream participation through depositary or affiliate arrangements is described. This gives a possible relationship between the systems, not proof that the final terms' generic safekeeper wording applies as written. |
| Base pp111–112, Condition 22 | The Interbolsa branch selects Portuguese law while preserving conflict-of-laws qualifications. The Portuguese-courts clause benefits holders and expressly preserves other competent courts. Condition 20 in the later deeply subordinated section is the wrong source. |
| Base pp37–38 | Seven identified financial sources are incorporated only to the listed extent; other material is informational. Later supplements modify or supersede only to the extent applicable. |
| Base pp76–78 and112 | Principal Agency Agreement (18 January 2008), Interbolsa amendment (18 February 2009), and Common Representative Appointment Agreement (3 November 2010) remain onward source requirements. |

inferred from these historical contractual statements.
