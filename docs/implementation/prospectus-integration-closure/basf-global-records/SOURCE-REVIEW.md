# BASF global-note form and ICSD records: source review

The selected final terms (page 3) choose temporary notes exchangeable into
permanent global notes, the NGN form, Clearstream Banking S.A. and Euroclear
Bank SA/NV. They leave Clearstream Banking AG, CDS and the CGN alternative
unselected. Page 7 selects German controlling text. Original base pages
108–110 and these final-term pages were inspected continuously before authoring.

Six source paragraphs form this package. Page 108 lines 49–58 contains the
selected §1(3) heading and all of paragraph (a): temporary form without coupons,
exchange into permanent global form in the specified denomination, signatures
by duly authorized issuer representatives, authentication by or on behalf of
the Fiscal Agent for both forms, and no individual notes or coupons. These
form provisions do not waive the separate certification/time/delivery conditions
in the completed paragraph (b).

The selected §1(4) definition is page 109 lines 1–3 and 5–9. It names the two
ICSDs, with their printed names/addresses, and every functional successor. Raw
line 5 begins inside the excluded CBF branch; raw line 9 continues into the
excluded CDS branch. Whole raw-line copying would introduce excluded language.
Lines 14–15 require NGN custody by a common safekeeper on behalf of both ICSDs.

Lines 23–37 describe the aggregate nominal amount recorded at the relevant time
in both ICSDs' records. The records are defined as those kept by each ICSD for
its customers concerning their shares in the notes. They are stated to be
authoritative evidence of the aggregate represented principal; an ICSD statement
is stated to be authoritative evidence of that ICSD's records at the relevant
time. This is the supplied contractual rule, not an adjudicated procedural-law
finding or proof of any actual account balance.

Lines 38–46 require the issuer to ensure that redemption, interest payments,
and purchase and cancellation are recorded. The subsequent reduction sentence
uses `zurückgekauften bzw. gekauften und entwerteten` (repurchased or purchased
and cancelled). English page 90 lines 11–12 instead says `redeemed or purchased
and cancelled`. The exact German remains controlling and unchanged. Preserve
the discrepancy and leave its effect on redemption accounting unresolved.
Neither interest-payment recording nor cancellation after purchase supplies an
independent power to impose uncompensated principal loss on a holder. No event,
payment, cancellation, balance or actual compliance is supplied.

Lines 47–50 require the issuer to ensure that a partial exchange of the
temporary global note is recorded in the ICSD records. The construction deletes
the instruction prefix on line 47 and the closing brackets on line 50. The CGN
alternative and the following CDS material remain excluded through page 110
line 39. The separately surviving §1(5) holder definition begins at line 40
and is outside these six meanings.

The §1(1) incoming NGN reference remains visible at page 108 line 11. It stays
unresolved pending complete review of that source sentence and its relation to
§1(4). No sixth reviewed reference is claimed. Preserve all five predecessor
references, eleven meanings, six scalar mappings, the fixed construction,
fifteen markers, 537 intervals, unresolved B54 and competing incorporation
readings. Q1/Q2 remain UNKNOWN, Q3 PARTIAL, ASSISTED. Independent source/legal
adjudication remains absent; exact preservation can preserve a supplied mistake.
