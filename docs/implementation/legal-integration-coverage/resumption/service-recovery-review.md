## Evidence contract and decision

The engineering question is whether one separately charged attempt can finish
the same failed assessment after the upstream service error. The comparator is
the exact retained request and response schema, on the unchanged provider route.
The primary pass criterion is a newly validated qualification-first proposal
for batch 21's original twelve pairs, followed by successful verification of
both perspectives and all earlier evidence. HTTP status, elapsed time and tool
counts explain execution; they do not establish legal accuracy or superiority.

The service failure invalidated this attempt's availability, not the input,
legal target, candidate-binding implementation or research direction. It
triggers a bounded recovery. Changed source/request/schema/route, corrupted
evidence, an uncertain dispatch, exhausted allowance or expiry veto execution.
Another returned failure consumes this review's single attempt and requires
a new specific review. A valid disagreement or unavailable representation does
not itself veto continuation. General English and case-law correctness, legal
closure and production readiness remain unestablished.

baseline is the returned service failure, not a rejected legal proposal. A
successful retry cannot explain the service outage or prove that free-text
reasoning is correct. The principal hidden risk is changing the request when
tool evidence is regenerated in another directory: inspected evidence contains
directory-specific paths. Reuse the original verified tool context and enforce
identical request and schema hashes before reserving another call. A missing receipt or selection
pointer must block normal execution rather than silently permit redispatch.
