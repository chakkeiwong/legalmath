# What this instrument increment closes, and what still needs evidence

The baseline report had no populated product observations. The new source-bound
proposal supplies five conditional product premises for the June UBS issue and
connects them to the existing HKMA calculation. They are not independently
verified English-law propositions. All fourteen bank obligations remain.

| Gap | Implemented treatment | Remaining work and its difficulty |

The next engineering extension should select a distinct retained instrument
and inventory its clauses before sharing these terms. The next evidence work
for UBS is complete incorporated/action/notice history and a resolution of the
price conventions. Neither task requires humans to label correct legal answers.
Contractually required issuer, FINMA or adviser acts are factual evidence when
they occur; their existence does not certify this program's interpretation.

The hard boundary is unchanged: independently checked mathematics and source
identity do not prove a complete natural-language interpretation or universal
future-law accuracy. Where that proof is unavailable, the result is qualified.
