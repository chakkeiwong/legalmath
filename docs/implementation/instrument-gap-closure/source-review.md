# Exact issue and source boundaries

The implemented issue is UBS Group AG's 24 June 2024 SGD notes, ISIN
CH1357852636, Annex A of the retained published terms. The original remains at
`docs/prospectus/originals/ubs-sgd-at1-2024-final-published.pdf`; the I0 dossier
binds both the PDF and its complete extraction. The cover's SGD 675 million
combines the June SGD 500 million issue and the July SGD 175 million reopening.
This increment does not silently treat the July issue date as the June date.

During this investigation, official-source searches for CH1357852636 located
the [UBS capital-instruments portal](https://www.ubs.com/global/en/investor-relations/investors/bondholder-information/capital-instruments/capital-instruments-content.html)
and [UBS's SEC-filed Exhibit 4.20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1610520/000161052025000023/exhibit420.htm).
Searches also returned UBS internal capital loans. Those are not substitute
terms for the public notes. Search availability and an absent amendment result
do not establish continuing legal force or complete amendment history. Only the
retained identified edition is implemented here.

PDF page 30 (printed page 28) displays `(A-B)/B` for the extraordinary-
distribution adjustment. A rendered image is retained in I0. The source defines
A as the specified market price and B as the distribution per share. The code
therefore computes the printed expression literally, reports its unusual
result, and qualifies the operational price. No source correction is invented.
An authoritative clarification or amendment, tied to this issue, would trigger
a new source edition and reassessment.

The dependency inventory includes incorporated agency and settlement agreements,
publication snapshots, higher-trigger instruments, notices, actual bank/FX
calendars, corporate actions, amendments, issuer substitution, market prices,
FX rates, contractual determinations and private account/route evidence. Their
absence is explicit. The existing Swiss/Hong Kong/sanctions dossier remains
retained, including the exclusion of the inconsistent Swiss consolidated
edition. No new claim about the final merits of Swiss litigation is made.

Contractual conversion and statutory write-down remain distinct. The source's
reference to FINMA's resolution powers does not establish an applicable current
power, an actual order, finality, implementation or repayment. Those continue
through the separate issuer-law and bank calculations.
