# Evidence audit and decisions

The formal target is the declared conditional interpretation of the selected
UBS terms and HKMA rules. The programs compute those conditional consequences;
they do not compute a certified proposition that every relevant legal condition
for a real transaction is satisfied. The real-document report remains qualified.

The final audit corrected a misleading inherited execution count: evidence
abstentions occur before compilation is executed on those inputs. Report actual
compiled evaluations separately from checked target cases. It also probes the
missing-calendar path so the available product and bank findings survive an
incomplete event input. Repair records identify the actual implementation and
reporting issues; they do not weaken the original criteria.

The strongest alternative explanation for a clean formal run is that the shared
proposed interpretation is mistaken even though its arithmetic and translations
are consistent. The retained English clauses, exact source anomaly and explicit
qualifications constrain that risk but do not eliminate it. A different applicable
edition, an incorporated exception, a counterexample to the declared interpretation
or changed event facts would require reassessment. The weakest evidence remains
complete source applicability and English entailment, not the algebraic threshold.

No stochastic method ranking is attempted. Execution times and test counts are
descriptive engineering facts. No human legal labels, judgments or ratings are
promotion evidence. Acts required by the contract are possible factual inputs,
not a substitute quality oracle.
