# G3 substantive review and G4 execution decision

8 October 2026. G3 attempt 4 completed with 62 of 96 provider reservations and
seven of twelve recorded repairs. All original responses and failed primary
product trials are preserved. Six diagnostic calls were added: four scoped
product checks and two source-identity checks. No provider substitution or
production-default change occurred.

## Findings from the live product

The primary baseline retained twelve candidates, six Boolean and six
unformalized. Its five completed fidelity batches produced 160 model judgments,
all NOT_ESTABLISHED. Review of their distinct rationales, questions and concerns
confirms a repeated loss of question context: the representation describes
TRUE_IS_SATISFIED but omits the candidate statement defining the result as duty
activation. Rejecting the formula as proof of substantive fulfillment is correct.
Attributing a fulfillment claim to the original candidate is unsupported. The
same problem affects constant-true candidates: an unconditional activation under
one proposed reading is not a claim of unconditional performance.

The baseline also timed out during fidelity and did not complete criticism. Its
later exact-question assignment correctly recovered the trigger distinction, but
the run stopped at an incompatible grant interface. The combined trial timed
out during generation, produced no candidates, then issued three unusable
question-assignment requests. Their `no_candidate_supplied` identifiers were
correctly rejected. The missing-prerequisite check should precede those requests.
Neither primary trial produced a final dossier or argument graph.

Offline checks preserved all six actual baseline Boolean expressions through the
official XML schema adapter. Six unformalized candidates remained explicit. No
live graph or XML check succeeded in the primary combined trial. Its eight
retrieval calls initially used stage labels as queries. The corrected adapter
uses the selected substantive question. A same-request offline comparison found
seven of eight development targets in each mode before and after correction;
this establishes corrected routing, with no measured coverage gain on that
small control.

Both scoped diagnostic arms used identical baseline packet, claim, candidate and
validated-question records, because the combined primary trial had no candidates.
Both completed two perspectives over the same two claim/candidate pairs. All four
responses correctly distinguished source support from relevance to the selected
DUTY_TRIGGER question. Continuing responsibility was supported but not a missing
term in the narrow trigger expression. The request-conditioned demonstration
claim was supported, relevant, and preserved by that expression under the
declared reading. All retained the footnote alternative and declined to infer
fulfillment. This supports the grant adapter and explicit question context in
this narrow stage; it does not complete the primary investigations or establish
an advantage from retrieval. Both arms report two internal calls and two shared
ledger reservations, so the anticipated request-hash accounting discrepancy did
not occur in these diagnostics.

## Source identity and direct answers

All 32 direct answers received substantive review against the retained source
set. Thirty-one passed structure and exact-span checks; the second Gatecoin
retrieval answer remains rejected for five source failures. Seven of eight
Gatecoin answers incorrectly compared a combined-packet hash with hashes of
individual DOCX files. Those identify different objects; the original manifests
verify against the actual combined packet.

Both additional source-identity responses pass exact-span checks and correctly
state that distinction. They preserve the non-consenting-customer exception,
different party relationships, the scenario-seven textual tension, unverified
later treatment and the missing bridge to Product Provider compliance. Neither
asserts legal correctness or actual fulfillment. This is evidence for a narrow
representation repair after observing the error, not an unbiased additional
benchmark, a general accuracy result or a statistically supported comparison.

The review covers retained responses, including rejected ones. An unsuccessful
request has no answer to review; its failure record remains evidence of incomplete
execution. This is agent source review, not independent legal adjudication or a
complete subsequent-treatment search. Original obligations closed: zero.

G4 audit: PASS for the already planned, bounded offline coverage work. Its lexical,
whole-unit BM25 and window BM25 comparators use the same corpus; unequal retrieved
text lengths remain explicit. Exact offsets check source identity, not entailment.
The three original paragraph-10 Boolean readings are distinct from the G1
paragraph-14 controls and six new product expressions. Thirty-two original
readings remain unformalized. Proposed target factors test reference/authority
boundaries, not an implemented analogy classifier. G4 adds no model calls.

The observed failures invalidate promotion of the affected answers and completion
of the primary product trials. They do not invalidate the frozen corpus or the
independent offline controls, and they do not reject tool reuse as a direction.
Proceed with the planned coverage phase; do not extend the program or repeat the
primary matrix automatically. The result must remain INCOMPLETE_WITH_EVIDENCE.

Strongest alternative explanation: clear typed question context, rather than
external retrieval or solver results, accounts for the successful scoped answers.
The primary trials provide no completed downstream comparison to distinguish
these explanations. A complete matched product comparison that retains correct
question meanings would be needed to assess that contribution.
