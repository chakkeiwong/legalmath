## Skeptical review of the integration plan

The integration note is a gap inventory, not yet a sufficient execution protocol.
Its original Git baseline predates the completed integration: this run starts at
5bff0065. It also conflates missing documents with documents already downloaded
but awaiting admission. In particular, both BES bases and their listed
supplements already exist. A new download cannot substitute for their review.
The original 4,990 references and 1,467 unresolved records are development
inventory counts, not independent error counts or acceptance criteria.

OCR tool discovery must establish the installed executable, models/languages,
runtime and actual output. ResearchAssistant's Marker/MinerU adapters alone do
not establish availability or page/quotation fidelity. Its reconciled Markdown
does not supply the source-image and page bindings this legal application needs.
Start with installed local tools and a small image/text comparison. Page 287 of
the 2010 BES base may be blank: inspect it before treating empty text as failure.
Preserve all original source and historical derivative bytes.

Independent adjudication, actual private client/bank facts and human acceptance
are genuine external requirements. They block their corresponding production
claims; they do not block OCR, source review, local implementation or test work.
Do not invent a reviewer, mark a machine review as human approval, or silently
transfer those requirements into reasons to stop other executable phases.

Audit verdict: REVISED before execution. The protocol below supplies baseline,
criteria, default hypotheses, artifact locations, repair triggers and stop rules.
No material unexamined default is accepted as a production default.

### Reviewed repair scope after source inspection

The 2013 BES base, printed pp95–96 (PDF pp96–97), supplies the
Actual/Actual (ICMA) formulas, start-inclusive/end-exclusive timing, euro-cent
rounding and the distinction between full outstanding Interbolsa principal and
definitive-note Calculation Amount. Series 35 and 36 select annual ICMA coupons.
Implement a separate conditional Interbolsa calculator with exact rational
arithmetic, one or two explicitly supplied determination periods, and explicit
selection of the printed half-up rule. Unknown or different market convention
must abstain. Do not silently use Actual/365, round each holding before summing,
or extend the two-period formula to arbitrary multi-period stubs.

The final terms deem clearing notices delivered on the second business day after
delivery to Interbolsa. Require an explicit complete calendar for the traversed
dates; a weekday assumption is not a calendar. The tax-redemption notice's
30–60-day interval needs separately confirmed legal time-counting. Unknown
early-redemption/cancellation facts preserve unresolved maturity payment; the
Eurosystem designation alone preserves unknown collateral eligibility.

Skeptical audit: PASS for these bounded repairs. Exact formula examples, leap-year,
long-stub, half-cent, missing-calendar, unknown-event and negation controls are
engineering criteria. They cannot establish actual payments, settlement, tax
eligibility, complete dossiers or original-cohort calculation coverage.
The two conflicting risk factors numbered 1.17 must remain separate records;
numeric section identity alone never permits later-record overwrite.


## Pre-mortem

A run could succeed technically while wrong identifiers, lost negations or
incomplete incorporation mislead the reader. Review those relations explicitly.
A run could fail because an optional GPU parser lacks its models, rather than
because OCR is impossible: test the installed CPU alternative first. Recovered
text can improve extraction without proving a complete issue dossier or legal
outcome. Keep those decisions separate in the result.
