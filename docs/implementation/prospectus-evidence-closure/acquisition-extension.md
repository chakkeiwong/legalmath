# Bounded acquisition extension and fresh-case protocol

Proposed 2026-10-04; additional dispatches require user approval. Preserve the
cumulative ledger and raise its ceiling from 12 to 36 only if approved: 24 extra
public HTTPS dispatches, two per exact URL, 45 seconds and 32 MiB per response.
Allow up to 18 extra requests for missing documents and authority; reserve six
for fresh-case recovery. Preserve the five original unspent recovery requests.
Count every dispatch, retry and redirect. Do not reset historical campaigns.

Fresh case families, declared before reading their PDFs: Rabobank perpetual AT1,
Danske perpetual AT1, BMW senior fixed-rate debt, Sanofi senior fixed-rate debt.
Use final issues dated 2024 or 2025 with issue-specific terms and operative
conditions available from an issuer or regulated exchange. Audit local prior
exposure first. Within each family select the latest eligible issue by date,
then ISIN ascending; retain identity and selection reasoning before classifying.
An unavailable or already-exposed family stays unavailable/exposed, without a
convenient replacement. Six requests may be insufficient; report that shortfall.

Freeze final reader/parser/dependencies before acquiring or inspecting challenge
PDFs. Compare against source judgments recorded independently of classifier
answers, identifying reviewer, anchors and uncertainty. The bounded-case pass
criterion is zero unadjudicated contradictions among available adjudicated cases.
Keep abstentions and unavailability in the denominator; neither counts as a pass.
Four purposive cases cannot establish population accuracy. Without an independent
adjudicator, report exposure and engineering only. Human legal acceptance remains
separate. Repairs after exposure consume this set as development evidence and
require a new untouched set for post-repair validation.

Skeptical review: this finite request extension enables research, not legal
acceptance. Missing adjudication or acquisition blocks the affected conclusion,
not independent engineering. Do not dispatch beyond the original budget until
approval is recorded.
