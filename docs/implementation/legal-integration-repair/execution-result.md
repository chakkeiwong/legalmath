## Validation and actual execution

All **161 distinct regression tests** passed across the recorded revised suite
and focused recheck. The first revised suite had 159 passes and two failures:
one new assertion incorrectly used the financial JSON codec for operational
timestamps, and one sidecar subprocess failed while another product run was
active. The underlying sidecar failure was not diagnosed because the temporary
fixture removed its log. The fixture now preserves failed evidence. A focused
sequential recheck passed both cases. This does not establish concurrency
reliability; the original failure log remains in the program's R1 results.

The revised complete controlled product used ten scripted provider actions,
actual Java/Lean and independent checks, actual optional retrieval/XML/graph
tools, and produced a verified dossier. Reconstructing the runner and reopening
it used zero new model requests. These are engineering controls with scripted
model proposals, not independent legal judgments.

Actual tools checked all twelve retained generated product candidates: six
Boolean ASTs passed XML preservation/mutation controls, and six unformalized
candidates stayed explicitly unsupported. These twelve candidates are distinct
from the original thirty-five readings. Effective-graph controls exercised
blocked premises, ancestor propagation, unaccepted preference proposals and a
twelve-node graph; product grounded/stable results matched the external checks.

Thirty-two of the thirty-five original readings remain unformalized. General
English interpretation is **not solved**: modal force, open-textured standards,
temporal/scope conditions, competing readings, and trigger versus fulfillment
still require source-grounded assessment. LegalRuleML checks selected conditional
or Boolean structure; Logical English parses a restricted controlled language.
Neither establishes that an ordinary English legal passage has one faithful
formal meaning.

Case law remains limited by tested HK/UK citation-recognition failures, absent
automated analogy/consent classification, target-fact distinctions, authority
and later-treatment checks, and missing independent legal adjudication. The
existing source-bound case-factor constructors do not interpret arbitrary cases.
MAPIE lacks labelled target calibration data; SARA-IE lacks qualified reuse and
target training prerequisites; original CLERC deployment/corpus remains
unqualified. Other deferred/overlapping tools and their reasons are individually
listed in the complete inventory. Installing them cannot supply client facts or
missing legal standards.

Delivery gaps remain too: no successful current-method full live product run,
unresolved deadline reliability, and one undiagnosed concurrent sidecar failure.
These limit engineering evidence; they are separate from legal-premise gaps.

with a wrong interpretation. The strongest alternative explanation for the

fragile evidence is legal scope/relevance, not graph computation. Independent
adjudicated questions, contrary sources and dated target facts could overturn
any proposed legal reading. The current failures reject those attempted
executions; they do not reject the overall direction of conditional, traceable
legal computation.
