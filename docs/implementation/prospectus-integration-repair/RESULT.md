| Next evidence | Reviewed complete contract/relationships and actual instrument inputs; real blinded readings and adjudication on a frozen unexposed cohort; pinned ACTUS/CDM runtime trials when their prerequisites are supplied. |

The strongest alternative explanation is shared implementer assumptions. Source
projection deliberately reuses retained words, so its success is evidence about
the integration, not Docling transcription accuracy. A manually supplied margin
target cannot validate itself as independent legal truth. Exact finance can share
incorrect contract premises even when both calculators agree. Password accounts
prove the tested authentication behavior but cannot supply authentic reader
independence. These are the weakest parts of the evidence and remain open in
[NEXT-PROGRAM.md](NEXT-PROGRAM.md).

A source-backed counterexample, an unsupported answer after a changed premise,
a corrupt raw export, or a failed current-state verification overturns the
affected support claim and triggers repair. A valid minimal failure after using
the package's supported configuration would justify a narrower package-defect
claim. We have not established such a fundamental runtime defect here. The results
repair several candidates' integrations and leave specific prerequisites and
interpretation work; they do not reject the research direction.
