\subsection{What the tool trials changed}
\label{sec:prospectus-adoption-executed}

The next investigation, on 7 October 2026, tested whether established tools
could repair specific failures in the prospectus service. It retained the
existing extraction and finite Boolean evaluator as comparators. Nine documents
supplied 326 selected file pages and 3,047 retained text units. For 289 of those
pages, the printed page label remains unresolved; a file index is therefore not
presented as a verified printed citation. These documents had already informed
development and cannot supply an independent acceptance sample.

The construction repair makes omissions visible. Every interval in the selected
BASF body now has a recorded disposition, including unresolved material. The
initial checker reported 1,018 unresolved intervals and 83 retained brackets.
Complete accounting consequently establishes where the work stops,
not a complete German contract. A shared definition can serve several branches:
excluding one use no longer erases another. A field whose rendered value differs
from its cited quotation also remains unresolved until that transformation is
justified. Versioned runtime validation checks the admitted construction; a
separate published JSON schema has not been added.

\begingroup
\clubpenalty=10000
The next source inspection exposed a different failure: retaining every character
does not preserve a sentence if the words appear in the wrong order. In the
printed German terms on pages 120 and 121, the clearing-system sentence ends
before the instruction beginning ``Falls die Schuldverschreibungen in Form
einer NGN'' (if the notes take the form of an NGN). The bold opening word has a
slightly higher bounding box; sorting fragments by their top edges moved it
ahead of the preceding words. A similar error affected a coupon placeholder,
and two Fiscal Agent address blocks interleaved their labels and addresses.
Five occurrence-specific permutations, checked against the original pages,
correct these orders while preserving all 1,578 body occurrences and their text.
The Canadian-agent exclusion now includes its whole label and address field.
The corrected construction has 1,008 unresolved intervals and still 83 brackets;
the change reflects corrected ordering and branch boundaries, not additional
legal adjudication. Other reading-order and clause-scope decisions remain subject
to source review.
\par\endgroup

\begingroup
\clubpenalty=10000
The optional Z3 adapter agrees with finite evaluation on the declared Boolean
test corpus. The checks cover a small grammar with all its partial and conflicting
assignments, and selected formula families across 8,190 complete assignments
using one to twelve facts. This is not an exhaustive test of every formula over
twelve facts. Larger examples exercise the installed interpretation path. An
inconsistent premise set produces a conflict; missing premises and computational
timeouts remain distinguishable forms of uncertainty. Typed reference checks
also retain missing targets and unsupported cycles as unresolved. Translating
German definitions, exceptions and priorities faithfully remains a separate task.
\par\endgroup

The Docling trial failed its declared acceptance criterion. The pinned layout
model processed BASF file page 112 and Deutsche file page 52 on the CPU, with
OCR and table recognition disabled. After correcting the adapter's mixed-font
ordering and list markers, 14 of 53 designated critical source lines still lacked
complete exact character maps. Some failures arose from joining hyphenated text;
others involved geometry and reading order. This result means that the candidate
could not recover those occurrences under the required mapping, not that every
affected line had disappeared from the PDF. The governing relation between a
margin and its clause also remained unresolved. The service keeps its existing
extractor, and the trial does not expand to further pages.

The annotation exchange now works through a running INCEpTION 38.0 server.
The trial used a Unicode example and four retained BASF lines joined into a
development excerpt, with provisional labels. Browser edits to one label in
each example persisted after reloading. Twelve exported annotation files---the
original and subsequent records for an administrator and two synthetic readers
on each example---matched their expected contents exactly. The edited labels
changed in the administrator's records while the two reader records retained
their originals. The checks preserved source editions, quotations, the
supplementary Unicode character and combining mark, repeated text,
discontinuous evidence groups and semantic relations.

The bridge converts between Unicode code-point offsets and UTF-16 code units.
The displayed spans must omit whitespace at their boundaries, so separate
source offsets retain the complete quotations, including trailing spaces and
line breaks.
The importer checks the relation between both sets of offsets and rejects
altered text or broken group links. Editing also requires the server's current
storage timestamp. Ordinary XMI export removes that internal field, so the
tested workflow uses the browser to update existing annotations and REST to
import initial records and retrieve exports. It does not replace existing
records with a newly encoded XMI file.

The exported project contained no recommenders. Opening integrated curation
left both curation records without evidence annotations despite the completed
synthetic reader inputs, confirming the disabled automatic merge in these
trials. Each synthetic reader session could open its own annotations; four
requests for the other reader's or administrator's annotations received explicit
access denials. These checks establish preservation of supplied annotations,
the tested curation behavior and access restrictions within those sessions.
The guest display names establish no human identity or independence. New spans,
discontinuous groups and relations still need an authoring check before the
interface can support first readings. The annotations also need legal review
by separately authenticated readers and a distinct adjudicator. The
server trials and failed attempts are retained under
\path{docs/implementation/prospectus-inception/}.

\begingroup
\clubpenalty=10000
Financial testing supports a conditional fixed-coupon calculation. Nine
independently derived year fractions agree with QuantLib 1.38 within
$10^{-12}$ years, and six holiday-adjusted dates agree exactly. For example,
consider a semiannual ICMA coupon accruing from 31 January to 31 August 2024,
with supplied reference periods 31 August 2023--29 February 2024 and
29 February--31 August 2024. The first 29 days fall in a 182-day reference
period; the remaining 184 days fill the second reference period. Its year
fraction is therefore
\[
 \frac{29}{2\cdot182}+\frac{184}{2\cdot184}
 =\frac{29}{364}+\frac12=\frac{211}{364}.
\]
This derivation corrected an erroneous expected value in the original test
fixture. The failed check is retained. The calculator also checks explicit
calendar coverage, entitlement, event order and rounding after aggregation by
legal holder. The tests supply hypothetical conventions; the complete
issuer-specific calendar, entitlement and event premises have not been admitted.
Tax, foreign exchange and full settlement remain outside this calculation.
\par\endgroup

The controller records observed inputs and their absence, immutable snapshots,
tool identities and the installed sidecar's file hashes. Generated mutation
sequences compare cached results with fresh computation; installed-package
checks verify that changes to construction, law and finance reach the report.
Broad source hashes remain necessary because native process reads are not
confined by the Python input recorder. Each phase refreshes the next plan from
its exact parent results. A completed phase can preserve a rejected candidate
or an unresolved premise; it does not imply acceptance.

The legal-source reconciliation retains 59 records, but current applicability
and actual exercise still need evidence for the particular question. The next
delivery step is a source-complete selected contract and reviewed interpretation,
followed by a frozen cohort with independent first readings and adjudication.
Actual-event questions additionally require dated event and client records.
The executable program, failed attempts, comparisons and remaining work are
preserved under \path{docs/implementation/prospectus-adoption/}.
