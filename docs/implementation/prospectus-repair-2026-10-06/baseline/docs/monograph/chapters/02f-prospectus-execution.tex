\subsection{What the executed service establishes}
\label{sec:prospectus-executed}

The 6 October execution reached the application report, but did not complete
the interpretation of the BASF contract. The source intake retained 43,626 text
units and 489 detected references across four documents and 519 pages. These
counts show the extent of the inventory; German marginal reading order,
document dates and the relevance of individual references still require review.

The assembly recorded ten source-backed operations, including removal of the
unselected scheduled holder put and the initial Finance-only guarantee.
It left 163 bracket branches unresolved and one unmatched opening bracket in
the long-coupon alternative. The candidate therefore remains a partial German
contract. Neither an invented closing bracket nor a blanket deletion of
remaining alternatives would establish the selected terms. Each retained
instruction, substitution and reference needs a justified disposition.

The installed command-line application produced the following answers for
this incomplete BASF bundle:

\end{center}
The historical bank report contained 14 obligations, but a subsequent code
trace found that its BASF request inherited the UBS request's AT1 instrument
classification and assessment dates. The adapter changed the instrument
identifier without establishing a corresponding classification or temporal
mapping. Retaining an inventory count therefore does not establish correct
integration. The report grants no transaction permission.

All 50 deterministic development tests passed. The installed-package check
also confirmed that the service used the worktree implementation, without
injecting its source directory into the installed command's search path.
These checks establish the behavior of their development fixtures and the
installation path. A later adversarial investigation recorded 18 findings and
one passing control. Repeated quotations and clauses split across printed lines
could defeat coverage; a malformed financial input could raise an uncaught
exception. Most seriously, the law evaluator could return yes even when an
additional declared necessary premise was false, because it evaluated a fixed
checklist instead of the supplied premise expression. These are software
failures that independent readings alone cannot correct.
The automatic interpreter remains a bounded English grammar; its inability
to answer BASF's principal-loss and conversion questions is an unresolved
implementation limitation.

In the program's phase terminology, P0--P6 remain partial: question and review
preparation, source intake, contract construction, interpretation, dated law
and facts, financial calculations, and application integration all retain
unfinished obligations. P7 executed the check that prevents evaluation without
independent labels; the paired legal evaluation itself did not occur.
P8 recorded a blocked release because there is no accepted scope,
independently scored cohort or independent sign-off. Resuming the command
verified the current source and result hashes and reused the nine phase
receipts. A reused receipt preserves a result; it does not turn a partial
phase into an accepted one.

The repair must first make the declared legal premises executable and bind
reports to the correct instrument and question context. It must also connect
the phases: the current interpretation and installed-report jobs rebuild from
the initial request instead of consuming the saved construction, law and
financial products. A phase dependency recorded by the scheduler does not
establish that the downstream calculation uses that evidence.

Occurrence-specific source intervals, a structured selected contract,
partial-information evaluation and explicit validity dates have conditional
correctness arguments. An isolated reference implementation passed exhaustive
small-domain Boolean, date-boundary and allocation checks, together with source
and context mutations. Those results establish properties of the supplied
representations; they do not prove that the production service implements them
or that a legal translation is faithful.

After these repairs, the next discriminating step is a complete, reviewed BASF
contract with each selected clause and exception accounted for, followed by
interpretation of the same contract. Other retained offerings still need their executed agreements
and amendments. Dated primary authorities and actual jurisdiction, event and
procedure facts are needed before legal applicability can be decided.
Rounding, calendars, accrued amounts, settlement and delivery conventions
remain necessary for complete financial calculations. Independent readers
must then assess an unexposed cohort and reconcile the existing review records
before any legal release decision.
