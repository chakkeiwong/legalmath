\subsection{A preferred share changes the calculation}
\label{sec:preferred-evidence}

The UBS program cannot simply be reused for every security that supplies bank
capital. Bank of America's Series SS provides a useful contrasting case.
Each depositary share represents one thousandth of a preferred share, with a
USD 25 share of its USD 25,000 liquidation preference. The preferred stock
pays non-cumulative dividends when declared and has no contractual conversion
right. Its equity form therefore changes the source-dependent reading of the
selected HKMA debt-product scope. That conclusion leaves the other selling,
sanctions and bank obligations to be assessed separately
\citep[pp.~S-16--S-17, S-22, S-27]{bofass2022}.

Consider a regular three-month dividend period and a holder of 100 depositary
shares. The annual rate is 4.750 percent. The source specifies a 360-day year
of twelve 30-day months, so a regular quarter contributes $90/360=1/4$ of the
annual amount. Let $n$ be the number of depositary shares and $f$ the fraction
of the regular dividend actually declared, with $0\leq f\leq1$. Before cash
rounding and withholding, the holder's conditional dividend in US dollars is
\begin{equation}
  D_{\mathrm{exact}}=n\times25\times0.04750\times\frac14\times f.
  \label{eq:ss-regular-dividend}
\end{equation}
For $n=100$ and $f=1$, this gives USD 29.6875. Under an explicitly supplied
convention that rounds the holder's aggregate allocation to cents, with half
a cent rounded upward, the gross payment would be USD 29.69. If no dividend
was declared, $f=0$: the missed amount does not accumulate into the next
period. This is a loss of that distribution, not a conversion of principal
into UBS shares. The declaration and legally available funds remain factual
premises, and the calculation does not establish that payment occurred
\citep[pp.~S-17, S-27]{bofass2022}.

The rounding unit deserves attention before money is assigned to an account.
The preferred-share calculation and the depositary distribution both discuss
cent rounding. Rounding a full preferred share first and then allocating its
rounded dividend need not equal rounding a holder's allocation of the exact
amount. For 10,000 depositary shares, these two declared computational scenarios
give USD 2,968.80 and USD 2,968.75 respectively. The implementation exposes both
and requires the applicable allocation convention rather than choosing one
from agreement among models. Initial and irregular dividend periods also
remain qualified when the necessary month-end convention is absent. Taxes,
record ownership and actual depository receipts require their own evidence.

The source hierarchy changes another result. The supplement states that the
Certificate of Designations controls an inconsistency. Its summary describes
notice within 90 days of a capital treatment event followed by redemption;
section 6(a)(ii) of the certificate places the special redemption itself within
90 days. The conditional program follows the latter timing, together with
the 30-to-60-calendar-day notice interval. It also retains the whole-issue
condition for this special branch. The ordinary branch begins on 17 February
2027. Neither branch makes redemption mandatory or removes the issuer's
election, lawful-funds and applicable regulatory-approval requirements
\citep[pp.~S-16, S-19--S-20]{bofass2022}
\citep[sections~6(a)--(b)]{bofasscertificate2022}.

Liquidation supplies a third, distinct calculation. The available amount is
what remains after senior claims, not the issuer's gross assets. The Series SS
claim includes the liquidation preference and the specified declared unpaid
dividends; undeclared dividends are excluded. If the available amount is
insufficient, that claim shares proportionately with the supplied parity
claims. The executable allocation conserves the amount distributed and leaves
only the excess for junior shareholders. It cannot establish the complete
creditor inventory or infer an actual liquidation from a merger
\citep[p.~S-19]{bofass2022}.

\paragraph{Dates belong to a market and a purpose.}
The same discipline applies to calendars. A UBS successor-share calculation
can involve ordinary shares and replacement shares traded on different
exchanges. Each five-day average must use its own preceding dealing days.
The revised arithmetic accepts two covered calendars, dates each exchange
rate, and separately checks the date of the existing conversion price.
A missing observation or an unexplained shared calendar leaves the price
qualified. Contractual adviser or issuer determinations can supply a
conditional factual price only when tied to the issue, event, action history
and applicable exceptions. Such an act is evidence about the contract's
operation; it is not a quality judgment about the program's interpretation
\citep[conditions~8(d)--(e), 8(m)--(n)]{ubsat12024}.

Calendar retrieval itself can fail subtly. The stable SIX banking-holidays
address inspected for this increment returned a schedule of clearing days
for 2027. That document distinguishes SIC and euroSIC, including days on
which their answers differ. It supplies neither a complete inventory of
2026 dates nor proof that the banks and foreign-exchange markets named in
the UBS terms are open. The program therefore binds the downloaded content,
covered interval, market and calendar purpose. It rejects missing dates
instead of treating them as ordinary business days
\citep[one-page schedule]{sixclearing2027}.

\paragraph{A transaction needs facts about the same transaction.}
Private evidence now carries the instrument, client, booking entity,
establishment, service, action and route to which it applies. Its source
content, effective interval, recording time, freshness and declared coverage
are checked before its factual assertions enter the bank calculation.
An assertion about another route cannot satisfy the present route; conflicting
PI assertions remain conflicting. A machine-readable request identifies the
remaining facts while retaining all fourteen obligations. The coverage check
concerns that declared inventory: complete discovery of every applicable law
remains unproved.

The UBS event assessment also assembles the new evidence branches. An
alternative permitted notice method can supply the effective notice date
without inventing a SIX publication. A scoped price determination retains
the earlier reconstructed price and its unresolved conventions. Holder
delivery is checked against the resulting share entitlement, and it cannot
establish completed conversion while the notice sequence, share creation or
depository receipt remains unresolved.

Five additional Lean propositions establish rational floor and half-up
rounding properties and characterize the earliest date selected by a
cumulative count of open days. Separate tests challenge the actual Python
calculations. Four further declared relations are checked against independent
equations and compiled for RuleIR/Java and Catala. These checks establish
formal consequences of the supplied premises. The source-dependent readings,
missing incorporated documents and actual legal force retain their stated
qualifications.

Later observations also require careful dating. A new private fact, an old
law newly added to the collection, and a document published after the method
was frozen are recorded separately. Declared publication dates do not become
externally attested timestamps. The current frozen method has no future
observations, and the prepared full-document model comparison has no fresh
responses under this increment's offline execution. Neither missing result
is replaced by successful development tests.
