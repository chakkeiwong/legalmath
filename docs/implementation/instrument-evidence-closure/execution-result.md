[reviewed plan](../../plans/instrument-evidence-closure.md). The implementation
now assembles scoped UBS notices, contractual price determinations, private
transaction facts and holder-delivery evidence with its existing event
calculation. A separate Bank of America Series SS profile calculates regular
dividends, selected redemption conditions and liquidation allocation. It does
not inherit the UBS note's conversion rules or Swiss-law assumptions.

weaknesses and initially unassembled instrument branches. Repairs bind resumed
responses to exact successful evidence and assemble the new branches with
schedule and entitlement checks. The Series SS certificate places special-event
redemption itself within ninety days; a notice date cannot substitute for it.

contractual determination is supplied; no intended replacement was invented.

no candidate verdict. A later input failure arose from concurrently edited,
unused executable-reference files; a reviewed inventory repair excluded exactly
