Known next engineering work is explicit: model the remaining
Series SS stub/redemption/record-holder branches from its controlling materials;
and extend profiles only after their own clauses and dependencies are retained.
Those are further implementation increments, not claims completed by this one.

The harder boundary is evidentiary and semantic. A proof can establish what
follows from declared formal premises. It cannot establish an unprovided private
fact, discover every unknown future law, or choose an intended clause where the
available authoritative texts are ambiguous. These require new evidence or an
explicitly qualified result, never human-labelled legal answers.
