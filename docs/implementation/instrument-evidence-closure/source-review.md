# Sources inspected and retained

The retained Bank of America Series SS supplement is a 176-page issuer PDF.
The implementation reads the supplement's own pages S-16–S-20, S-22, S-27–S-28,
not an issuer-name template or a generic preferred-share description. Its
certificate-precedence clause is material. The SEC Certificate of Designations
was read through the web retrieval service, including definitions, dividends,
liquidation, redemption, and the remaining voting/rights provisions. The
locally retained extraction is explicitly not original HTML. Direct download
returned HTTP 403, including the trusted retry; issuer certificate PDF mirrors
returned HTTP 404. No missing original was replaced by an invented document.

Section 6(a)(ii) of the certificate places special-event redemption within
ninety days. The supplement's wording instead describes giving notice within
ninety days and subsequently redeeming. The calculation uses the controlling
certificate. The non-cumulative dividend provisions also require care with
declared-but-unpaid dividends preserved for specified liquidation/redemption
purposes. Only the absence of undeclared carry is encoded generically; declared
arrears are not silently cancelled by a Boolean non-cumulative flag.

The forty-page issuer-hosted 8-K PDF was downloaded and extracted. Its issue
announcement and underwriting material are retained. The controlling
certificate is incorporated by reference rather than reproduced there, and
the complete deposit agreement was not obtained. Search absence is not proof
that these materials are unavailable generally or that there are no amendments.

SIX's one-page PDF was downloaded from its stable banking-holidays address.
The actual title and table concern 2027, with boundary rows in 2026 and 2028.
SIC and euroSIC are separate columns; certain Swiss holidays have different
clearing answers. The document cannot supply a complete daily calendar or
certify the bank/FX opening definition in the UBS terms.

UBS Annex A definitions and conditions remain bound to their previous exact
PDF/text identities. Its condition 8 source anomaly is unchanged. A source
dependent adviser determination, if actually supplied and applicable, is a
factual act under the contract. The implementation does not use a human
interpretation or majority model answer to repair the printed denominator.

Every acquired source has a representation, URL, retrieval record and hash in
`docs/prospectus/supplemental/manifest.json`. These preserve what was inspected;
they do not establish current force, complete amendments, authentic future
events, private facts or English entailment.
