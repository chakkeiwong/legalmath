# Teaching and source review plan

Reader: a technically literate finance/legal reader who has just followed the
UBS conversion example. Assumed prerequisites are shares versus debt, a cash
dividend, calendar dates and a conditional calculation. The next unit must
explain why a non-cumulative preferred share needs a different program and why
a new source or a missing private fact changes the conclusion.

Narrative: replace the conversion note with a fractional interest in Series SS;
derive one regular dividend; expose the cash-rounding unit and missing
declaration; follow the controlling certificate to the redemption timing;
return to calendars and evidence in the common bank assessment. Introduce
amounts and units before the equation. One diagram shows the two branches.

Protected baseline: baseline.zip, including prior mathematics, citations and
qualifications. Add a bounded section after the UBS terms and one guide page.
Source review: supplement pages 16–20, 22, 27–28; controlling certificate
sections 3–6; SIX's actual 2027 clearing schedule. The original SEC HTML could
not be downloaded; its retained web extraction is labelled.

Acceptance: exact source/occurrence bindings, compiled documents, no missing
references, page geometry checks and visual inspection. No human quality
labels or human acceptance gate is introduced. The user's explicit quality
requirement takes precedence over the skill's generic human-feedback suggestion.
Source interpretation and reader experience are not certified by compilation.
