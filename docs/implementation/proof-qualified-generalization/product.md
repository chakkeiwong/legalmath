# Proof or qualification, with generalization as a product goal

Controlling product requirement, 28 September 2026.

LegalMath must independently check a stated correctness proposition or issue an
explicitly qualified result. Its central research goal is to carry justified
guarantees into unseen inputs, amended sources and unfamiliar future situations.
Successful execution, familiar examples and agreement between interpreters do
not by themselves establish that goal.

Humans must never supply quality evidence: no human answer keys, adjudication,
ratings, preferences, reviewer approval or human acceptance gates. This applies
to fitting, validation, model selection and claims of correctness. A model's
agreement with a human or another model is not a substitute. Human-benefit
studies and institutional deployment are outside the present scope.

Legal texts and formal specifications are premises. Their human authorship does
not make them quality labels, and their existence does not prove a proposed
reading. Every guarantee identifies the source edition, question, facts,
semantic assumptions, method version and domain to which it applies. An
unresolved premise remains unresolved even after compilation or a formal proof.

The product distinguishes four kinds of evidence:

* A kernel-checked theorem can establish its proposition for every future input
  in its quantified domain. It cannot establish an excluded proposition.
* Automatically generated challenges use executable mathematics or declared
  transformations. They can find engineering errors without legal answer keys.
* Independent runtime checks can expose compiler, codec and instrumentation
  errors. Shared interpretation errors remain possible.
* A frozen prospective source window can retain observations on unfamiliar
  publications. It must include unfinished and unsupported tasks, separate any
  subsequent repair into development, and state provenance and sampling limits.

The current product reports natural-language legal correctness and unrestricted
future legal generalization as NOT_ESTABLISHED. This is a substantive qualified
statement, not a request for human review. Where the sources leave alternatives
unresolved, retain those alternatives and the missing premises.

This requirement supersedes earlier proposals to use independent compliance
readers, adjudicated legal answers or institutional dispositions as quality
criteria. Historical results remain historical; they cannot qualify new claims
under this policy. There is no requirement to collect human feedback before
continuing this research or accepting its machine-checkable engineering results.
