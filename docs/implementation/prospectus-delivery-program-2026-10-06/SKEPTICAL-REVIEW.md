## Pre-mortem and competing explanations

The program could pass while misleading us if a reviewed input were simplified, a dependency were omitted from the denominator, or the same template entered both development and evaluation. P0 exposure records and P2/P3 source maps are the earliest discriminating checks. It could fail because geometry is corrupted even when semantic rules are adequate; compare the automatic and reviewed-input arms. It could fail because authority or facts are unavailable; preserve that missing input and continue independent engineering. It could remain unusable through blanket abstention; mandatory correct positive and negative cases prevent that escape.

Incomplete dossiers are a strong explanation for many existing abstentions. They do not explain the four new semantic/dependency failures. Conversely, fixing seven probes would not establish performance on real prospectuses. A successor that loses conditions on independently reviewed real clauses would refute the proposed repair's adequacy and trigger representation or interpretation repair.
