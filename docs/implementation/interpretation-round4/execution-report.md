## Post-run red-team note

The strongest alternative explanation for the prose result is that the detector
recognizes a small family of development failures. That is its stated purpose;
it cannot certify the rest of the English. New corrupt forms, a legitimate
quotation falsely withheld, an unaccounted pair, or a lost concern would trigger
repair. Controlled variants and negative controls reduce this risk without
measuring its population frequency. The weakest evidence concerns unseen model
prose and future circulars. B1 first supplies missing authoritative context;
the later withheld evaluation must measure missed defects and useful coverage
together, rather than treating universal abstention or fewer questions as success.
