# Smaller interpretation tasks: execution report

The source-inventory blockage was overcome in this attempt. Six smaller reading
tasks and two whole-source checks completed on the retained SFC family-office FAQ.
Their output exposed a separate citation-storage bug, which was repaired and
replayed without paying to repeat the reading. Four candidate interpretations then
reached generated Java. Complete English-to-formula verification remains pending:
four of 204 fidelity pairs have validated, and the remaining 200 exceed the
three calls left in the authorized allowance at the tested four-pair task size.

## What changed

Optional inventory decomposition preserves every source unit and its order.
Each of two independent contexts reads all pieces, then checks the complete text
against its own assembled claims. The merge gives piece-local identifiers
distinct namespaces. Missing pieces, missing context checks, exact-quote errors
or capacity overflow prevent completion. Cross-piece discrepancies become
unresolved findings. The contexts share a model and source; independence of their
errors is not established.

Compact transport removes repeated storage metadata from the model request while
retaining the exact text, unit IDs, original request, wire request and hashes
locally. Manifest-verified replay checks the wire reservation in the persistent
allowance. Replayed work adds no new live call or independent vote. Recognized
availability failures open a circuit; the preliminary probe permits only one
counted transient retry. Permanent errors do not trigger repeated probes.

The real HTML source revealed an import mismatch. Citation spans referred to the
33,887-character CRLF extraction, while database import normalized it to a
different 32,526-character LF derivative. The explicit exact-text import option
now preserves already-anchored derivatives. Normal import behavior is unchanged.
The actual retained page and synthetic mixed line endings now pass source-span
resolution and search creation checks.

Fidelity batches now receive only their required claims and candidate meanings,
alongside the full source text. Pair validation and complete-matrix aggregation
remain mandatory. The 32-pair default is unchanged. Larger and smaller batch
experiments are identified separately in their frozen contracts.

## Interpretation findings and limits

Both whole-source checks identified context supplied in other pieces, including
the three licensing factors and defined references to the Circular and Ordinance.
They retained uncertainty about a negation, upstream activity/business
classifications and missing statutory context. The generation passes preserved
these questions. The alternatives pass described a concrete office scenario in
which different attachments of the negation could matter, without converting the
qualified guidance into a categorical exemption.

The four candidate formulas passed 24 Python/Java probe cases each. Of six
candidate comparisons, five remained incomparable because their fact bindings
were not established as equivalent; one agreed within its declared domain. One
controlled-English reconstruction agreed within its declared domain. These are
formalization and execution results, not proof of source meaning. Output labels
and underlying classification judgments still need source-fidelity scrutiny.

The criticism validator rejected incorrect signed attacks, including an
impermissible attack on a strict inference and a mismatched premise reference.
Those responses remain invalid. D1S's four validated labels were all
`NOT_ESTABLISHED`: the selected claim described the FAQ's purpose and audience,
whereas the formulas evaluated a licensing trigger. This raises a relevance
question for the inventory, rather than demonstrating four erroneous legal
formulas. It must be investigated explicitly before changing a claim's status;
deleting it simply to fit the budget would invalidate the evaluation.

The strongest alternative explanation for improved execution is changing service
conditions. Request contents and output work also changed. No controlled
comparison isolates decomposition's causal effect. The weakest evidence for
English correctness remains the absence of an independently established full
reference interpretation; another same-model judgment does not supply that
reference. The software now preserves disagreements and failed checks more
reliably, which is distinct from establishing the legal answer.
