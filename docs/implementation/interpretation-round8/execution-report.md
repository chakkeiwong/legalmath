# Explicit predicate meaning and deliberate clause interpretation

The live program now distinguishes a licensing-trigger predicate from a compliance
decision. It also deliberately generates competing readings of a disputed clause,
compares their executable consequences, obtains separate source criticisms and
automatically reconsiders unresolved discrepancies twice. The outcome of the
clause study is **UNCERTAINTY_RETAINED**; the program did not select a legal winner.

This is a retained-source study of the SFC Family Offices FAQ. The narrow trigger
asks whether three supplied classifications hold. The disputed clause study asks
about the antecedent of the ordinary-course business guidance. Neither supplies
a complete business classifier, a licensing exemption or a bank-approved rule.

## Interpretation findings and repairs

The seven initial clause proposals reduce to three behaviors over the declared
facts. With a genuine arrangement assumed, the proposed formulas require either
neither additional income nor a profit objective; no additional income or a
profit objective; or no additional income or no profit objective. These are
proposals extracted from the disputed wording. A truth-table difference shows
where those proposals disagree; it does not establish which the source intends.

Source critics favored some readings and challenged others. The parallel-language
critic left some readings unresolved. After reconsideration, the final critic
marked six of twelve proposals supported and six challenged. Those counts include
different explanations of the same formulas and cannot be treated as votes or
independent legal evidence. The report preserves all earlier critiques and the
questions about source editions, precedence, negation, reimbursement and the
scope of the ordinary-course qualification.

The live run exposed two defects beyond the original output-label problem. A
structural repair changed a proposed formula; using only the final valid response
could lose an interpretation. The added recovery stage retained all six proposals
from rejected initial responses and asked a fresh reader to reconsider them.
No invalid scope or assumption was silently supplied by the supervisor.

A later response deliberately left one reading without a formalization because
the supplied vocabulary could not represent it. The revised validator now permits
such a reading only with the same question, exact source evidence, required
qualifications and explicit unresolved questions. It stays available for
criticism, receives no fabricated Java output and forces continuing uncertainty.
The original response and its earlier rejection remain in the record. Eighteen
tests checked history retention before F3; twenty-four checked the combined
non-executable-reading and package boundaries before F4.

authorities. The experiment improves traceability and failure handling; it has
