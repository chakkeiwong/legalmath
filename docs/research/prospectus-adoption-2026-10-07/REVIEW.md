# Final skeptical review

The recommendations are supported as engineering choices to implement or trial.
They are not local accuracy results. The strongest remaining uncertainty is
whether the adapters preserve meaning on unexposed prospectus families and how
qualified independent readers resolve disputed interpretations.

| Review question | Finding and correction |
