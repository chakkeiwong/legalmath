# Bounded original-question response qualification

11 October 2026. This is the response-qualification node of the continuous
execution program, not a replacement for the complete product or its tool trial.

The question is whether the configured gpt-6.1-sol route can return a usable
conditional answer to the exact P10 and evidence questions from all 54 retained
source units (54,910 characters), preserving contrary readings and the shared-error
and secondary-record counterexamples. The comparator is the newly reviewed
P10/evidence contract and the retained source analysis. No answer text or expected
counterexample result is supplied to the proposing call. A second fresh response
reviews the proposal against the same source, questions and cases. Same-model
review is not independent adjudication.

Primary acceptance requires executor inspection of the actual source-to-answer
inferences, fulfillment rather than merely activation, contrary readings,
unknown facts and the concrete counterexamples. Exact quotations, valid JSON,
latency and a model's own verdict are explanatory or necessary validity checks;
they cannot alone establish acceptance. Invented quotations, lost questions,
unsupported compliance conclusions or erased contrary reasoning veto acceptance.
A failed first response triggers one corrected qualification branch, not a stop
of independent source-restart work. The correction must address an observed
defect. No unchanged retry or automatic new method follows a second failure.

The original grant remains 400 calls with deadline 12 October 04:28:01 UTC.
Its frozen ledger contains 13 charged reservations. The successor ledger copies
those entries verbatim and appends at most four calls: proposal and review in
the primary branch and, only after failure, proposal and review in recovery.
It does not append to or reallocate the sealed old stage grants. The continuation
plan expressly admits this separately bounded qualification under the existing
global balance. Maximum aggregate usage is therefore 17/400. Complete-route
allocations remain unadmitted until population, response, tool and time evidence
support them; 23 remaining old baseline-core slots do not describe the total
global balance.

Each call uses at most 600 seconds, 200,000 input bytes and 80,000 output bytes.
The exact request, schema, route, source hashes, code hashes, original ledger and
deadline are recorded before dispatch. The allowance validates those bindings,
the absolute clock and finite stage counters under a lock before reserving.
Interrupted and invalid calls remain charged. The model is explicitly selected
as gpt-6.1-sol because the prior exact-source canary succeeded on that configured
route; remote model attestation is unavailable. Six hundred seconds is a bounded
qualification hypothesis, informed by the prior 259-second success and later
300-second timeout, not a latency prediction or evidence for full-route timing.

Skeptical pre-run review passes for this diagnostic: the full retained source
denominator is conserved; source limitations and unknown applicability remain;
expected answers are withheld from the proposer; a response can fail semantically
despite correct structure; no matrix, tool integration, legal closure, statistical
ranking or default change follows from a pass. A full matrix can still be too
expensive even if this smaller response succeeds. Qualification cannot remove
the whole-route guard or silently serve as its method qualification.

Exact commands:

```
.venv/bin/python scripts/legal_mission_response_qualification.py prepare
.venv/bin/python scripts/legal_mission_response_qualification.py execute primary
.venv/bin/python scripts/legal_mission_response_qualification.py execute recovery
```

Recovery is used only on the recorded failure branch. The execute prefix is
narrow and fixed; it accepts no arbitrary request, script or shell argument.
Artifacts are under `artifacts/legal-mission-control/2026-10-11/continuous-execution/qualification/`.
The run manifest retains commit, interpreter, CPU-only policy, exact command,
data and method versions, wall time, call counts and result. No stochastic ranking
is attempted; provider seeds are unavailable. A final review must distinguish a
usable candidate answer from an integrated, validated legal product.
