| p14 | Request, object, standard and footnote attachment differ across A/B/C. | Preserve each supported rival, distinguish confirmation/demonstration/verification/opinion, and answer the no-request case without collapsing differing standards. |
| gatecoin | Case propositions need fact, party, terms and authority limits before transfer. | Trace a selected proposition through both decisions and contrary passages to the target inference; retain later-treatment and scenario-seven uncertainty where unresolved. |

Product-capability acceptance and actual client compliance are separate. A
conditional answer can be useful without establishing compliance. Merely
restating that premises are missing is no new progress. A genuine ambiguity may
remain after a supported investigation; describe it and its consequence rather
than invent a unique meaning. A qualified answer must still address the question.

Each claimed advance needs an answer before and after, a precise source-supported
inference or reproduced product correction, the affected original identifiers,
the strongest contrary, and the remaining dependency. Tests, invocation counts,
schemas, hashes and reviewer agreement cannot substitute for this evidence.

## Finite substantive work, with refresh between tasks

The register contains these four work packages. They are not automatically
extended when one fails. Each begins with the previous result and ends with a
specific decision about its question and the next task.

1. **P10: establish the missing standard and evidence argument.** Start from the
   existing paragraph-10 dossier and premise register. Examine the precise
   unresolved inference: does a material records defect determine nonfulfillment
   despite effective retention and adequately assessed supervision/remediation,
   or does that conclusion require a separate outcome standard? Reuse the
   existing distinguishing scenario. Identify the exact circular/UT/Appendix
   passages that support or limit each inference; do not import valuation-specific
   monitoring as a universal tokenisation standard. Produce an argument showing
   what the present sources resolve and a precise research request for any
   unavailable authority. Keep private facts as variables. The first work item
   is source analysis, not coding another Boolean or replaying the truth table.
2. **P14: resolve attachment as far as the sources permit.** Extend the existing
   dossier only at its unresolved A/B/C dependency. Trace request, object,
   standard and footnote relations individually, including paragraphs 15/16.
   Retain a no-request counterexample and a requested case where the object or
   standard still differs. Record a supported resolution or an irreducible
   ambiguity with the particular evidence needed next.
3. **CASE: test one concrete case-to-target inference.** Reuse the complete
   Gatecoin rereading and its rejection of the ledger-label shortcut. Examine the
   remaining inference from particular post-change entries to customer acceptance:
   distinguish automatic entries, customer acts, prima facie evidence and proof
   of a contrary instruction. Trace terms, consent, parties and rights,
   contrary reasoning and scenario seven. A current-authority claim needs a
   dated, scoped later-treatment search; absence of that search remains explicit.
   Gatecoin must not become a universal paragraph-10 fulfillment test.
4. **PRODUCT: close the actual integration path and evaluate contribution.**
   Use the supported/conditional propositions from these tasks. Exercise the
   real investigation API from source and original question through proposal,
   tool checks, downstream consideration and final answer. Start with one
   complete paragraph-10/evidence case; stop to correct a reproduced blocking
   failure before funding a full comparison. Then cover all four fixed questions
   with matched baseline, individual applicable tools and combined tools.
   Retain failures and unsupported profiles. Give every surveyed tool an explicit
   target-specific disposition; deferred tools are not integrated tools.

Product work can expose a missing premise that returns to its substantive task;
it cannot replace the target with a more convenient question. One blocked question
does not block independent source work on the others. An additional phase needs
a recorded change to this finite program, a causal reason and a reviewed effort
bound. A user decision is needed only for material scope/resource changes beyond
existing authorization, not for routine refreshes within it.

## What fully integrated means

For each selected applicable tool require evidence of (1) a pinned available
implementation, (2) a target adapter, (3) invocation in the real product path,
(4) preservation of source and exact question, (5) its result reaching downstream
reasoning, (6) negative and unsupported cases, and (7) a matched evaluation of its
effect on the answer. An unchanged answer or a harmful effect is a valid trial
result, not proof of benefit. A tool may be fully connected and still unhelpful.

Source arguments establish or qualify legal premises. Argument engines compute
over those supplied premises. LegalRuleML preserves supported representations.
BM25 locates passages within the supplied packet. None automatically fills the
source-to-standard gap. The current installed backend still lives under scripts
and requires explicit injection. Its optional nature and deployment dependencies
must be part of the real product-path test.

The survey register accounts individually for PyArg, Carneades, clingo,
LegalRuleML, BM25, Logical English, eyecite, HYPO, CATO, AGATHA, SARA-IE, CLERC,
MAPIE, Tweety, s(CASP), KeY, LangGraph, Docling, OCR, Inspect, PIT, Java, Catala,
Lean, Z3 and cvc5. It records inherited evidence, limits and the next discriminating
test or a specific reopening condition. Selected, deferred, unsuitable under the
tested profile, conceptual reuse and retained supporting infrastructure are
different dispositions. They cannot be relabeled as universal integration.

Before any comparative run, freeze inputs, legal question, method, prompts,
source availability, relevant tool versions, effort limits and review criteria.
Compare the same actual product route. Controlled fixtures qualify plumbing;
replayed output cannot establish new live legal benefit. A large matrix is not
required before the first complete substantive case. Generalization claims need
independent target examples; stochastic ranking requires a funded statistical
protocol and uncertainty evidence. No live experiment is launched by this setup.

## Admission, detours and mission status

Before nontrivial legal-interpretation work, run `check`, read `status`, and run
`admit` on its task. The task must identify an original question, exact gap,
retained baseline, counterexample, expected answer change, evidence artifact,
vetoes, tool selection, resource bounds and return condition. A refresh records
the actual result and why the next task is justified. Historical programs are
verification contexts, not automatic work queues.

A necessary engineering detour must name a reproduced blocker of an admitted
substantive task. It gets at most one bounded attempt before returning to that
task, with an explicit failure disposition if unsuccessful. Starting another
file/program or changing a task name does not reset the detour count. Cumulative
detours are counted from the last evidenced substantive advance, not from the
last successful repair. This is a conservative work-selection default, not an
assertion that every bug can be fixed in one attempt. A justified change requires
an explicit program revision and review; it never silently resets old resources.
The initial record carries a lower bound of one inherited detour: the latest
candidate-response replay repair, which established no new legal result. The
actual earlier total may be higher. Consequently the initial admission is source
work; creating mission control does not grant a fresh engineering attempt.

Mission status is derived separately from task status. The control cannot certify
legal truth or grant final acceptance: at most it reports that evidence is ready
for substantive acceptance review. It rejects a claim of mission completion based
on phase completion. Open items and unfinished tool assessments remain visible.
This control is effective only when the executor uses it; repository instructions
make it the normal route, not a security boundary against a bypassing agent.

## Implementation evidence contract and assumption audit

Engineering question: does this small control catch the recorded forms of drift
before work is admitted, while allowing substantive source work with unknown
client facts? Comparator: the prior static plan plus unconstrained selection of
later repairs. The test criterion is rejection of changed original questions,
trigger substitution, premature completion, unsupported integration claims and
repeated engineering detours, with successful admission of the initial P10 task.
These tests establish process checks only, never legal progress.

| Choice | Provenance/status and justification | Failure mode / early diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| Four fixed questions | Original T0 baseline; preserve their exact text. | Narrowing fulfillment to activation; altered-question negative control. |

Promotion vetoes: changed legal target; unsupported source inference; hidden
standard or priority; unknown treated as false; lost rival; stale evidence;
deployment/fixture evidence described as legal benefit. Continuation vetoes:
corrupted baseline, invalid checker, unauthorized action or exhausted declared
resources. Candidate failure is not a continuation veto for independent work.
Repair triggers are reproduced control defects or a product failure that blocks
the next substantive task. Counts/timings/test totals are explanatory only.
