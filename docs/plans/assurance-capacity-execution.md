## Intent and evidence contract

The engineering question is whether a readable, lossless input representation
can carry the entire retained authentication circular, its proposed questions,
and every cross-piece concern into one bounded reconciliation request. The
comparator is the unchanged final request under `split-capacity/run/model/
action-0015/references/wire-request.json`, preserved by the previous final
assessment. It contains 263 source units and 263 synthetic concerns, and measures
382,396 canonical UTF-8 bytes against the existing 200,000-byte limit.

The candidate stores repeated field names in explicit table columns and refers
to exact source quotations by source unit and Unicode codepoint range. It keeps
the full source readable in the same request. A deterministic decoder must
reconstruct the complete prior request, including ordering, identifiers,
rationales, cross-piece links, source/reference identities and response schema.
Any additional deduplication must be similarly explicit, readable and reversible.
The old request and its failed receipt remain unchanged. Encoding is optional
and versioned; it does not silently change existing live or cached routes.

Primary D1 criteria: exact canonical-byte reconstruction against the original
request; malformed/detached representations rejected; retained final request
below 200,000 bytes; actual simulated full-source reconciliation with all units,
questions and concerns accounted; interpretation revision reopens all coverage.
The real text is used for transport testing, with synthetic proposals clearly
identified. Unique long rationales, heterogeneous quote positions and multiple
unrelated concerns must also be exercised. An incompressible oversized request
must stop before provider dispatch and retain its evidence.

Promotion vetoes and repair triggers: lost fields, changed source or proposal,
accepted detached identity, dropped concerns, changed bounds, refunded issue
reservations, incomplete global reconciliation reported complete, or a synthetic
completion represented as legal proof. Continuation vetoes apply to corrupted
baseline evidence, an invalid test environment or an unreviewed live request.
A failed size criterion is a representation failure that may trigger a bounded
repair; it does not invalidate date or source-acquisition work in later phases.
Byte sizes, wall time and test counts are explanatory diagnostics. Passing does
not prove English interpretation, legal sufficiency, arbitrary future capacity,
model comprehension of the encoding, or production readiness.
