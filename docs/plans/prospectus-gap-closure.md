## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: can the reader resolve supported monetary layouts, detect additional
unsupported mechanism language, expose source dependencies and mechanism
conditions, and connect those qualified findings to the existing bank-rule
investigator without turning absent evidence into legal permission?

The comparator is the v3 reader and its 30-row report: 14 positive, 15 negative,
one Enel abstention. Existing dependency, instrument-event and joined-eligibility
implementations are reusable baselines. Their presence does not prove that the
30-row feature reader is already connected correctly. The keyword comparator is
explanatory only. No weak-baseline accuracy contest is planned.

The candidate combines a bounded monetary-field grammar with independently
checked monetary witnesses, additional full-reader semantic contrasts, source-
bound dependency and condition records, a verified feature-to-investigation
adapter, and a fresh source-family challenge. It changes neither the user's
feature definition nor the established formal decision.

## Gaps and concrete obligations

1. **Enel and monetary layouts.** Bind the calculation amount to final redemption
   through explicit fields. Accept justified optional punctuation and parenthetical
   form labels. Reject malformed numbers, currencies, coupons, minimum denominations,
   mixed series, ambiguous duplicate fields and unselected programme forms. Verify
   field boundaries and exact values through a separate monetary-witness checker.
2. **Unfamiliar mechanism language.** Construct full-reader contrasts for subject,
   modality, negation, exceptions, reporting/hypothesis, principal versus interest,
   discharge and loss wording. A missed operative loss formulation must not become
   a negative. Exemptions must not discard an independent mechanism in the same
   context. Preserve unsupported alternatives and explain every changed corpus row.
3. **Document dependencies and editions.** Discover candidate references with exact
   page spans. Maintain typed resolved/open dependencies, immutable source identities,
   and as-of information. Validate dated selected base/supplement links. Investigate
   the identified Unilever and Enel contractual dependencies through official sources;
   financial reports and historical editions are not substitutes for missing terms.
4. **Authority and language.** Retry the Lloyds official filing/original routes;
   preserve failed attempts and compare content/bytes if retrieved. Inspect Deutsche
   Bank's controlling German loss clause and its English counterpart with exact
   anchors and an explicit local bilingual correspondence; do not promote that
   bounded correspondence to a proof of whole-document translation equivalence.
5. **Complete mechanism description.** Produce source-linked mechanism records for
   positive witnesses: actor, action, principal/share target, contractual/statutory
   origin, quoted conditions and referenced clauses. Preserve unresolved triggers,
   amounts, restoration, notice, authority and settlement inputs. Connect supported
   existing instrument calculations where their input contract is met; do not invent
   numerical terms or report a total-loss calculation from a conversion label.
6. **Bank-decision integration.** Connect verified feature reports and retained
   prospectuses to the existing joined investigator. Keep the broad feature predicate
   distinct from HKMA qualifying contingent loss absorption, SPI eligibility and SFC
   complexity. Exercise the actual dispatcher with missing/conflicting client and
   authority evidence. Retain all 14 bank obligations. No private customer facts have
   been supplied, so real transaction permission cannot be a completion criterion.
7. **Unfamiliar sources.** Freeze the repaired implementation before acquiring new
   source families. Target two capital issues and two corporate senior issues. Use
   the ordered candidate families below, preserve every attempted acquisition and
   abstention, and report descriptive conditional coverage only. Exposed Enel and
   other repaired cases remain development data.

## Thorough skeptical review before execution

REVISED AND ACCEPTED FOR BOUNDED EXECUTION. The naive plan of repairing Enel and
calling all remaining gaps closed fails three tests: source completeness cannot
be inferred from scanning pages, matching backends cannot independently validate
English, and the broad feature predicate is not HKMA selling-rule scope. The
revised plan gives those questions separate obligations and checks an actual
integration. A positive witness establishes existence, not a fully specified loss
schedule. Absence of client facts prevents actual permission, but does not prevent
implementing and testing the connection and missing-input workflow.

Wrong-baseline risk is controlled by preserving v3 and treating its answers as
qualified comparisons, not labels. Fair comparison uses identical formal inputs.
Proxy metrics cannot promote legal accuracy. Every numerical/default choice has
provenance, a limit and an early check. No LLM-quality or human-answer labels are
introduced. No continuation stop is triggered merely by an expected abstention;
G6 executes repairs for exactly those failures. Unavailable external evidence
remains explicitly open after bounded recovery, not silently marked resolved.

Pre-mortem: this campaign could pass while proving the wrong encoding, fitting
known layouts, matching the wrong edition, or publishing administrative statuses
without actual source investigations. Counterexample-first tests, exact source
anchors, independent checks, explicit investigated acquisitions, immutable freezes
and full-reader/bank-dispatch evidence discriminate those explanations. Final
review must assess what remains unproved rather than treating compilation as a
legal verdict.
