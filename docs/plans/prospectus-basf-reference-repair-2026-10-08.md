## Evidence contract and research intent

The candidate extends occurrence-bound source decisions to non-bracket text,
unused table cells and, only where justified, labels and their references. The
primary pass criterion is a source-supported transformation with exact target,
premise and governing-scope bindings, unchanged raw-body/source maps, complete
character conservation and equality through the installed service. A full
reference inventory is required before any renumbering, including references
outside brackets. Absence of an office or an authoritative range resolution is
an explicit missing-input result, not permission to insert a plausible value.

Promotion vetoes: deleting the surviving interest definition, editing the wrong
occurrence, unbound margin scope, overlapping operations, unsupported numbering
or office values, contradictory targets, source loss, or a false complete status.
They trigger focused source/implementation repairs. Continuation vetoes are
corrupt sources/comparator, missing required diagnostics, method/input drift
during a run, or exhausted bounds. An unsuccessful candidate does not invalidate
the source-review direction. Counts, test totals and runtimes are explanatory.
No independent legal acceptance, complete contract, general extraction default,
actual-event answer, new live authoring result or ACTUS/CDM runtime result follows.
