## Research intent and evidence contract

Main engineering question: can the current four-document/55-unit P10/evidence
target be represented, validated and carried through both readers, cross-source
checks, dossier review and final answer without deleting a material source,
qualification, rival or uncertainty? The comparator is the frozen timing run's
exact source selection and the current product method; its returned pieces are
observations, not a completed baseline answer. The mechanism under review is a
bounded, source-preserving representation of quotations and aggregate claims.

Promotion criterion for a proposed method is an **offline, exact-data proof or
executable qualification** of every pre-dispatch bound, coupled to a way to
detect and reject loss of material content. It is not enough that a smaller
request fits. A missing source unit, quotation mismatch, omitted uncertainty,
unbounded expansion, false source-to-warrant inference, unequal arms, or an
unqualified downstream consumer vetoes promotion. The observed output cap and
aggregate-schema excess are repair triggers. Runtime, call count and approximate
token savings are explanatory only. A capacity pass would not establish legal
accuracy, model ability, tool benefit, convergence or default readiness. Preserve
the audit and any proposed revision in this plan/result pair.

The protected baseline is all 55 qualified source units, four original questions,
46 obligations and 35 readings, with the retained exact responses for calls
10–13. Do not compare to the old 12-unit excerpt packet or treat the fourth
offline validator pass as an accepted product inventory. No live model calls,
installer, external source changes, tool integrations or production policy
changes occur in this audit. The original grant, deadline, sealed results and
seven-detour count stay fixed.

## Ordered work and stop conditions

1. Reproduce the output expansion and aggregate failure from immutable records;
   record raw, expanded and maximum bytes, per-piece counts and exact source
   identities. Check that no active execution or unresolved reservation exists.
2. Trace the inventory merge, full-source cross-check and all consumers of the
   merged inventory. Enumerate each fixed count/byte/deadline bound and the
   earliest full-target overflow. Separate proven failures from risks that need
   a fresh result, especially the uncalled fifth piece and second reader.
3. Test candidate representations **offline only** on retained data. A quote
   strategy must keep the model's selected source span and exact source text
   auditable; it must not silently shorten a quotation or infer a legal warrant.
   An aggregate strategy must preserve every per-piece claim, authority,
   uncertainty and unit account or explicitly refuse. Check losslessness and
   all downstream request envelopes, including worst-case amplification.
4. Record a skeptical decision: one coherent source-preserving method proposal
   with explicit residual unknowns, or a precise `not yet feasible` verdict.
   A later live method needs its own reviewed revision, matched baseline and
   candidate, original grant accounting and mission refresh. This audit does
   not itself authorize that dispatch.

Stop if exact retained source identity cannot be verified, the proposed
representation loses a qualification, or any required downstream bound cannot
be stated. In that case retain a concrete counterexample and return to source
analysis; do not widen a cap merely to move the failure. After this package,
record its actual result in `results/inventory-capacity-audit.md`, update mission
history and next-task reason, and run the exact mission refresh.

## Skeptical pre-run audit and defaults

The wrong baseline would be an earlier excerpt or a shortened circular; both
are excluded. The tempting proxy is raw wire size (25,681 bytes), which ignores
475,806 bytes after source resolution; the audit counts both. The hidden default
is `Inventory`'s 80-claim/30-uncertainty schema, apparently inherited from a
single-response setting; its provenance is code, not target-specific evidence.
The earliest test is the observed four-piece merge, which already fails. The
200,000-byte output cap is a reviewed transport constraint, not a legal measure.
The 18,000-character source-unit split is an inherited preparation choice; it
facilitates packet validity but makes full-unit citation amplification possible.
The third and fourth responses may overstate or miss source claims despite
passing structural checks; they are diagnostic inputs, not ground truth.

Unfair comparisons are prevented by forbidding a new live arm here and requiring
any future arm pair to receive the same source text, representation, budgets and
validation rules. The stop is before dispatch: if the full route remains
unqualified, no call is made. The artifact answers the engineering-capacity
question only; it cannot answer paragraph-10 fulfillment. Under these limits,
the skeptical audit passes for **offline inspection and bounded diagnostics**.
It would fail for an immediate cap increase or another live wrapper.
