This is one bounded corrective package within the existing common-capacity
implementation and integration master. It addresses the reproduced
`baseline-gatecoin` attempt-2 failure, `E_INTEGRITY: Retained acquisition omits
planned source or bridge requests`. Sol's exact recovery request succeeded at
slot 3486. The worker subsequently settled; no product worker is running.
The four questions, 46 obligations, 35 readings and six matched tool evaluations
remain the substantive target. This package adds no service calls, replacement
allowance or deadline and preserves every old result and attempt.

## Evidence contract and dependency map

Question: does the new capacity planner incorrectly apply its current batching
to previously completed source observations, or is source evidence missing?
The comparator is the exact retained acquisition and the production entry point
used by the failed Gatecoin attempt, under the installed common-capacity profile.
Do not treat a successful direct call to `PhasedProduct.answer` as coverage of
`run_product` and `retained_acquisition.load`.

D1's four question comparisons are independent and use separate output folders.
Test families in D3 are independent. Production adoption, state updates and live
dispatch share the master locks and remain serialized. A genuine missing bridge
blocks its affected consumers. Invalid shared evidence or uncertain dispatch
remains a continuation veto. No subagents are authorized. A failed repair is
retained and does not create an automatic new repair allowance.

Primary engineering pass criterion: the actual entry point accepts an unchanged,
authenticated complete acquisition despite a capacity-only change in independent
observation batching, while rejecting missing or altered duties and responses.
This does not establish legal correctness, accepted answers or tool benefit.
Packet counts and test totals explain behavior only. Source identity, reader and
question scope, complete joint dependency bridges, failed-parent recovery and
absence of provider dispatch can veto the repair.

## Defaults, assumptions and skeptical review

The current planner estimates 512 + 768 tokens per independent observation and
limits READ_SOURCE/REVIEW_REQUIREMENTS to 3000 tokens. These inherited estimates
are planning hypotheses; they must not invalidate an already validated complete
response merely because a new plan would use smaller batches. The earliest
diagnostic compares exact per-reader record duties and bridge requests before
considering any reuse rule.

The proposed repair, if supported by D1, permits only a change of grouping for
independent source/metadata observations. Dependency bridges remain joint and
exact. Every retained request must still reconstruct from the unchanged source
store; every successful response and failed-parent recovery must validate. A
record union by itself is insufficient because it can hide a lost second reader,
changed question scope or missing joint interpretation.
