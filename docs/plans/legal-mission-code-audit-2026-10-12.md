## Evidence contract and intent

The question is which failures still prevent tools and source evidence from
supporting a complete legal answer through the actual product. Trace entry
points, adapters, durable evidence, consumers, validators, budgets and mission
state. Retain exact source locations and distinguish confirmed defects,
unwired features, restricted capabilities, unqualified behavior and substantive
legal questions. A deterministic counterexample confirms only its exercised
invariant. A source trace supports only the inspected call path. Neither is
evidence of legal correctness, model quality, tool benefit or whole-route
completion. Keep the four questions, 46 obligations and 35 readings visible.

Adversarial checks must pair a positive control with the smallest perturbation
that exposes a suspected defect. Use retained answers and scripted providers;
the latter test orchestration only. Proposed checks cover legal-source identity,
answer-review target coverage, continuation after a provider failure, failed
tool evidence at the consumer, and actual production call sites. No ranking or
statistical conclusion is intended. Record hashes, commands, environment,
git commit, elapsed time and outputs under
`artifacts/legal-mission-control/2026-10-12/code-audit/`.

Diagnostic roles: reproducible invariant failures are repair triggers, not
authority to implement repairs; missing callers or legal warrants are integration
and research gaps; test passes are explanatory engineering evidence. A source
mutation, unintended external dispatch or unavailable prerequisite invalidates
that diagnostic. Stop its affected branch and continue independent inspection.
Do not rewrite old evidence to make a check pass.
