## Question and evidence contract

Can a persistent investigation acquire and account for source qualifications,
retain competing factual models and readings, pursue their consequential
disagreements within fixed limits, and deliver source-bound executable proposals
with independently checked evidence and explicit remaining uncertainty?

The engineering comparator is the accepted dossier integration (1,029 tests) and
round 16's opt-in local investigation. The interpretation comparator is a fresh
single-reader route at the same declared invocation budget as the ensemble; it
is not the archived 23EC46 candidate set. The new study uses source families
frozen before any new model answers. Earlier inspected families remain separate
development/backlog work. No downloaded historical circular will be described as
a prospectively published circular.

Primary engineering criteria are executed transitions, preservation of evidence,
correct rejection of invalid evidence, conformance on specified domains, and
complete regression. Primary interpretation criteria are independently supported
material conditions/answers, unsafe accepted decisions, retained correct
alternatives and unknown outcomes. Count all selected tasks, including failed
generation and unsupported encodings. Test counts, agreement, solver speed,
number of models and reduction in open questions are explanatory diagnostics.

A falsely accepted consequential reference case vetoes interpretation promotion;
it triggers investigation and repair rather than ending unrelated engineering
work. Corrupt evidence, unauthorized dispatch, unsafe executable inputs and an
exhausted required resource are continuation vetoes for the affected action.
No result establishes universal English correctness, statistical independence,
production readiness or review-cost savings without the corresponding evidence.

The available reference for this execution consists of sixteen express-clause
cases frozen by the implementing agent before the study's answers. It is
independent of those answers, but **not independently adjudicated legal ground
truth**. These cases can expose contradictions and measure conditional case
coverage; they cannot satisfy the stronger interpretation-promotion criterion.
The report must preserve this distinction even when every case passes.

Artifacts live under `artifacts/assurance-successor/2026-09-28/`; documentation
under `docs/implementation/assurance-successor/`. Every phase stores commands,
input/output hashes, base commit, environment, CPU-only execution, timing,
attempt history, decision and the refreshed next action. Deterministic phases
have no random seed. Model route/version and raw proposals are retained.

## Resource and command policy

The new grant is at most 500 calls, separate from the exhausted historical 500.
Initial ceilings are 160 for inherited scoped judgments, 100 for source/authority
and PDF work, 80 for unfamiliar initial breadth, 100 for investigation/evaluation,
and 60 reserved for repairs. These are planning allocations within one shared
hard ceiling, not five independent grants. Unused calls need not be spent.
Reallocation requires a recorded reason before dispatch; the total never rises.
One invocation means one actual external model request, including a timeout or
invalid output. Cached responses cannot count as a new independent judgment.

pipeline cannot execute, the other selected tasks remain recorded as pending

scheduling choice has been exercised with a failed pilot, a repaired resume and

## Delivery and interpretation

The final report separates engineering correctness, checked formal propositions
and legal interpretation support. For every gap it records the implementation,
executed evidence, failed candidate, remaining uncertainty, next justified action
and whether an external decision is actually required. The decision table states
primary criterion, veto status and unsupported conclusions. The inference table
separates hard vetoes, descriptive differences, any supported statistical ranking,
default readiness and next evidence. Universal correctness and bank release
remain outside this campaign's authority.
