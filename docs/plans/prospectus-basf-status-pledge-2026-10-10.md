## Source review and assumptions

Original base image 110 was inspected during the amount/holder work. Selected
status is 110:49–53: unsecured and unsubordinated obligations, ranking equally
with each other and other unsecured/unsubordinated issuer obligations, except
where mandatory statutory provisions give those obligations priority. No law
has been adjudicated merely by reading this exception.

The complete negative pledge is 110:54–75. Its duration is while notes remain
outstanding, but only until all principal and interest amounts have been made
available to the Fiscal Agent. It addresses securing present/future capital-
market indebtedness and guarantees or other assurances for it by mortgages or
pledges over issuer assets, unless holders participate simultaneously with the
same rank in those securities or other securities recognized as equivalent by
an internationally recognized independent auditor. Do not erase that alternative
or confuse funding the Fiscal Agent with actual receipt by every holder.

The definition is 110:76–80: obligations to repay borrowed amounts represented
or evidenced by bonds or other securities of original maturity greater than
one year that are, or may be, listed or traded on a stock exchange or other
recognized securities market. Preserve `greater than`, `original`, and the
potential-listing/trading alternative; do not reduce the definition to currently
listed bonds or all borrowed money.
