This is one bounded successor to the deployed P14 decision-closure program.
It addresses a reproduced response-validation defect in that same work, not a
new legal investigation. The current worker and its bound implementation stay
unchanged until a tested idle handover. No new provider calls, owner attempts,
replacement allowance or deadline are granted. The cumulative limits remain
8,100 calls and 288 service replacements, five-minute spacing, 24 replacements
per exact request, and 2026-10-24T02:19:15Z. Count this consolidated successor
as detour 65, preserving the prior 64.

## Reviewed implementation

Validate every nonempty answer-evidence pair. Require both a valid pointer and
an exact quotation. Empty pairs remain valid where optional; conditional waivers
continue to require a quotation and source support. Classify newly detected
response quotation errors as response-schema failures, not evidence that the
shared immutable corpus or dispatch history was altered. Tampered requests,
sources, hashes, assignments, grants and uncertain dispatch retain their
existing global integrity/authority vetoes.

Reuse settled requests and responses exactly through the existing authenticated
capacity checkpoint. On stricter validation failure, retain the original failed
continuation evidence and the original UNRESOLVED decision; do not purchase a
new sample. Independent context reviews and supported corrections continue.
The complete fresh corrected-answer review remains required, with the stricter
check active. A result from the old validator cannot be promoted by its old label.

The offline warrant capture also confirms a distinct delivery limitation: the
new Handbook objection receives pages 7 and 9 but not page 8; explanatory
footnotes are not automatically added. The known network objection does receive
the record containing the relevant network paragraphs. Missing whole-record
coverage is not itself proof of unsupported propositions. Preserve these
findings for substantive warrant assessment; this quotation repair does not
change sources, source routing, legal verdicts or correction warrants.

dispatch, exhausted authority/calls or deadline are continuation vetoes.

The primary engineering criterion is exact checking without losing original
decisions, buying duplicate calls or turning one response defect into a global
stop. Secondary diagnostics are delivery coverage, test counts and runtime.

registered source, obligation, reading and fresh-answer requirements.

## Skeptical review before execution

PASS conditional on Q2/Q3 and a safe boundary. Baseline and candidate compare
the same source/answer/decision bytes. The scope excludes retrospective editing
of outcomes, new semantic retries, source-completeness claims and acceptance
relaxation. An earlier candidate used the default E_INTEGRITY for an invalid
optional quotation; deployment in that form would falsely stop independent
work when revalidating a saved model response. Q1 must use a specific E_SCHEMA
for this response field while retaining all shared-state integrity checks.
This is a material pre-deployment revision, not a relaxed quotation rule.

The strongest alternative explanation is that the optional quote is extraneous
to an otherwise supported source disposition. That possibility does not make
the quotation exact. Preserve the decision and report the field failure without
claiming that the legal proposition is false. A production replay showing loss
of another response or new provider calls invalidates the repair. No model
ranking or stochastic superiority claim is made.
