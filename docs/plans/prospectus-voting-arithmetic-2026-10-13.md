## Inspected source and selected scope

The retained official SchVG consolidation, sections 5(4), 6(1), 15(3) and 16(2),
was read alongside attempt-002/SOURCE-REVIEW.md and necessary-model.json.
Section 5(4) uses a simple majority of participating voting rights ordinarily
and at least 75 percent for essential changes, expressly including numbers
1–9. BASF section 11(2), governing German text at physical page 129 lines 63–68,
uses at least 75 percent with its stated simple-majority exception referring
to numbers 1–8. These are separate conditions. Section 15(3) requires at least
half eligible outstanding principal at the first meeting, excludes suspended
votes from the denominator and permits higher contractual requirements.

The numerical targets here are favorable/participating eligible voting weight
> 1/2, favorable/participating eligible voting weight >= 3/4, and represented/
eligible outstanding principal >= 1/2. A properly convened second meeting has
a distinct branch; it is not selected here. The model is a necessary statutory
floor and the selected contract reading, not automatic discovery of all higher
requirements. Section 16(2) incorporates AktG counting rules that have not been
reviewed. Accordingly eligible denominators are explicit supplied observations;
raw ballots, abstentions, affiliates and competing holder claims are not silently
classified. Eligibility, exclusion of suspended votes and procedure remain
separate unknown or hypothetical premises.

## Implementation and default audit

Add an optional list of declared ratio facts to the existing law input. Each
comparison identifies its basis, fact name, instrument, jurisdiction and event;
its source-bound rule states GT or GE and an exact finite decimal threshold.
Each available operand identifies the same instrument/jurisdiction/event, unit,
source, valid/knowledge intervals and observation time. Missing operands remain
UNKNOWN. Source binding proves literal provenance, not that an agent's declared
threshold correctly interprets a statute. That interpretation remains supplied
and visible, as do current Boolean legal bases.

Use exact rational arithmetic on finite decimal strings. Positive denominators
permit cross multiplication without floating tolerances. A zero denominator
leaves the condition UNKNOWN; negative values, numerator exceeding denominator,
untyped values, mixed units/events and invalid periods are refused. Finite
decimal input of at most 128 characters is an input-size safety bound, not a
scientific tolerance. Reject exponent notation and nonfinite values. Test a
large integer boundary to expose binary-float rounding. No dependency install.

Only declared computed names are reserved against simultaneous Boolean facts;
other existing facts retain their behavior. Multiple source-bound tallies with
the same rule may disagree and must reach the existing CONFLICT logic. Different
declared rules for the same name are refused, rather than silently merged.
Rule applicability and operand availability must both hold. No arithmetic
changes to an input that omits the optional field; final Q4 receives calculations
only when requested. Frozen historical evidence is preserved.
