## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: can extracted premises and explanations reject wrong action,
polarity and series interpretations and prevent negatives based on keyword
silence? Baseline: the retained v1 reader and three full-reader failures in
`reader-gap-diagnostic.json`. Candidate: explicit source witnesses, issue scope,
exact monetary relations and independently checked decision derivations.

Primary acceptance: recorded failures and independently constructed variants
obey their semantic obligations; affirmative controls remain useful; invalid
issue/edition/definition/evidence links are rejected; explanation and decision
share checked premises. Report real-corpus coverage loss. The previous 12/13/1
tally is not a promotion target. Formal tests cannot validate English premises.

False binary decisions, stale evidence, tampering or invalid proof veto
promotion and trigger repair. Broken harness/source assumptions veto dependent
execution only until repaired. An unfamiliar-document abstention is a coverage
result, not rejection of the research direction. Counts/runtime are explanatory.
No human/model labels are quality authority. No universal English interpretation,
complete law discovery or unknown-future legal accuracy is claimed.

## Skeptical audit and defaults

The recent maturity-year/phrase filters were presentation repairs. Expanding
those filters would fit examples without solving scope. A supported witness
must bind subject, action, modality, negation, condition, affected amount/share
class, source position and issue identity. Candidate retrieval cannot directly
set a validated fact. A separate derivation checker must reject corrupted links
and fields instead of trusting a self-reported `applicable` flag.

Unrestricted English cannot currently be proved. Use explicit formal
construction families for independent test expectations and qualified real
source readings. Negative coverage cannot follow from cash repayment plus no
recognised unresolved candidate. Unsupported operative terms/dependencies retain
uncertainty. Every remaining source-scope assumption must be reported.

Money uses exact decimal/currency/field identities, never coupon, issue price
or minimum denomination as a principal substitute. An issuer/regulator power
may qualify despite `may`; holder-optional conversion does not. The exclusion
of ordinary creditor restructuring remains disclosed and versioned. Existing
Python/JDK/Catala/Lean are reused; no GPU or external model spend is required.

Review: accepted for bounded execution with these qualifications. The plan
would be invalid if it preserved old labels as targets, treated new tests as
legal truth, hid all-abstention coverage loss, or modified old evidence in place.
Tests exposing precisely the planned failure trigger repair rather than stopping
the program. Do not retry unchanged failures or silently weaken criteria.

## Phases and executable checks

1. **Checkpoint/sync.** Update master summary/reset, inspect commit inventory,
   compile Python sources, commit docs and existing project work. Fetch origin,
   merge origin/main normally, push main, then fast-forward the clean Catala
   worktree. No force-push or overwritten dirty work.
2. **Specify/repair.** Preserve replayable v1 files. Implement bounded semantic
   witnesses, action/polarity attachment, issue-specific scope, scoped definitions,
   exact repayment-unit composition and dependency checks. Derive reasons from
   the same evidence as the decision. Add whole-path regression counterexamples.
3. **Challenge.** Independent construction families change obligation attachment,
   polarity, actor, security, definitions, amount/currency, conflicting clauses,
   template applicability and versions. Check facts, decisions, explanations and
   unresolved states. Preserve every failed attempt and causal repair.
4. **Corpus/formal/freeze.** Re-run all retained bonds into new directories;
   investigate changed results and execute the same formal inputs through
   RuleIR/Java and Catala. Freeze the complete reader/checker/report before
   fresh-source challenges; exposed data becomes development after repair.
5. **Report/integrate.** Publish reasons, qualification, baseline differences and
   remaining limits; build/inspect the updated PDF. Write final decision table,
   reset and next phase. Commit, fetch/merge/push main, advance Catala again,
   verify both worktrees clean and remote/main equal.
