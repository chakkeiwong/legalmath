## Audit and decision rules

The primary criterion is the complete five-route current source/consumer matrix,
exact dispatch/receipt bindings and actual boundary/prelaunch controls. Test
totals, timings and prior replay are explanatory only. Missing source identity,
unexplained change, false provenance, stale tests, unexpected reservation or
failed finalization veto promotion. Corrupt inputs, a violated explicit grant,
storage/time bound or missing required evidence stop the campaign. Candidate
rejection is not evidence against a package's fundamental suitability.

The weakest evidence remains shared interpretation of exposed examples and a
single host. Even complete equality establishes preservation, not independent
financial/legal correctness or universal runtime closure. Keep original task
admissions, supervised reservations and tool-internal descendants separate:
