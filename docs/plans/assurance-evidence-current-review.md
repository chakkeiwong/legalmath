# Current-evidence review preparation and execution

This plan supplements the protected guarded-continuation plan. It does not
authorize additional provider spending. The grouped numerical amendment is
pending: retain the 100-call total and original expiry; raise the cumulative
repair ceiling from 20 to 74 and the two final-disposition issue ceilings from
20 to 36. The remaining allowance is 54 calls. Historical grants, requests,
responses and failures remain immutable.

## Skeptical audit before implementation

The earlier estimate of 26 packets was not a size preflight against the new
guards. Reusing the old builder would review v2 and overwrite protected coverage;
reusing its task directory would also break the old recovery inventory. Use a
separate review run. Preserve predecessor identities and cumulative issue counts.
The original grant remains frozen at its recorded checkpoint during this run.
The new authorization must explicitly bind the prepared packet receipt, original
grant, checkpoint, limits, expiry and user's approval text. A proposed grant is
never an approved grant. No provider call is permitted before that record exists.

The comparator is the complete retained v3 inventory, guarded candidate code
and results, and every predecessor disposition and failed review. A schema pass
is not acceptance of a program repair, a legal interpretation or an obligation.
Existing reviews covered v2. Every refreshed packet says which current code and
evidence it actually contains. Code omitted from a bounded engineering packet
must be named as outside that packet, not silently claimed as reviewed.

Audit verdict: implement and preflight locally; execute provider review only
after explicit numerical approval and a passing bounded-packet preflight.

## Evidence contract and assumptions

Question: do the current conditional guards and preserved dispositions accurately
represent the inspected source, supplied-judgment computation and remaining gaps?
Engineering pass requires immutable current inputs, unchanged complete source
text, exact coverage of 70 role/reading pairs and 20 guarded candidates, retained
predecessor findings, and valid requests within the actual transport limit.
Reviewer disagreement is a repair trigger; missing real evidence is a promotion
veto. It is not by itself a veto on further local engineering work.

Interpretation/applicability and the 46 original obligations stay open. No legal
correctness, release, human acceptance, independent-model agreement or statistical
ranking follows from these reviews. All 960 synthetic checks are diagnostic
software evidence. Their receipts preserve full inputs; packet summaries must
identify the limits of the supplied detail.

Default audit: at most four whole readings per packet is a capacity hypothesis,
bounded by the existing four-finding response schema; use one finding per reading.
Pack whole readings by actual serialized size, at most four per packet and at most 180,000 bytes. Group only byte-equivalent question dispositions and retain their exact IDs, texts and original order. The grouping must round-trip to every complete original disposition.
The transport maximum stays 195,000 bytes; 15,000 bytes are reserved for a bounded
validator-correction instruction. No source truncation is allowed. The selected
packet grouping is operational, not a legal classification. The first complete
partition and worst-case two-attempt costs must fit 52 calls, leaving two of the
54 calls unallocated, and the amended cumulative issue ceilings. Otherwise stop
before spending and revise the partition or scope explicitly.

## Sequence, stop conditions and artifacts

1. Build a separate immutable prepared package from frozen current code. Carry
   all original source units, readings, questions, assumptions, obligations,
   retained findings and predecessor task identities. Add guarded implementations
   and explicitly bounded executed-check summaries. Check complete coverage,
   exact byte limits, cumulative issue limits and the total worst-case budget.
2. Test the scheduler with local fake provider output: pending approval, altered
   checkpoint, expiry, duplicate dispatch, task/issue/total ceilings, interrupted
   requests, two consecutive provider failures and restart. Do not call a live
   provider for a smoke test. Freeze and run through the fixed master commands.
3. Upon explicit approval, append the authorization bound to this exact prepared
   package. Every current-evidence call, including initial refreshed reviews,
   counts as a repair. Reserve before dispatch. A rejected schema/source quote
   may receive one bounded correction; retain both raw outputs. Semantic
   disagreement remains an open finding, not a reason to rewrite reviewer output.
4. Refresh after the engineering phase, then after dispositions. Integrity,
   expiry, allowance exhaustion, missing complete evidence or two consecutive
   provider failures stop further dispatch. A known reservation is never blindly
   replayed after a stream interruption. The master lock prevents overlap.
5. Record attempted versus structurally validated reviews separately from
   substantive acceptance. Save the remaining evidence needs and next justified
   repair; preserve all historical conclusions and receipts.

Commands remain `.venv/bin/python scripts/assurance_evidence_master.py ops-freeze`,
`ops-run`, and `ops-status`. No arbitrary shell wrapper or sandbox disabling is
introduced. Artifacts live under the existing supplemental run's `current-review`
directory. The run manifest records frozen code, plan, git commit, environment,
CPU-only execution, deterministic preflight, packet receipts and elapsed time.


## Capacity repair after actual preflight

Supplying every full synthetic case exceeded even a one-reading packet. Five
specified contrary cases retain their complete inputs and results in disposition
packets; the other cases are represented by explicit aggregate counts and a
receipt to the complete local cases. Their test generator and guard code remain
in the engineering review. Disposition packets do not independently audit
implementation correctness. Published sources and original questions remain
complete. Lossless repeated-disposition grouping and whole-reading packing reduce
request repetition; their round-trip and exact coverage are required checks.
