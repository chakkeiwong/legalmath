## Evidence contract and skeptical audit

The primary substantive criterion remains two complete supported answers
distinguishing P10 allocation, conduct and outcome, preserving P11 separately,
and explaining the gap from observed supervision/reconciliation to adequacy.
Required inventories, full-source checks, unchanged sources/questions, complete
downstream review and honest accounting are continuation conditions. A lost
footnote, unsupported legal warrant, unknown-as-false inference or substituted
trigger blocks answer promotion. Calls, bytes, test counts, source coverage and
solver agreement are explanatory only. One pair supports no statistical ranking
or individual-tool attribution.

| Choice | Provenance/status | Risk | Smallest discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing piece reader | `Assurance._piece_inventories`; repair hypothesis | Separation may obscure a remote qualification | Required cross-piece review over all original units, preserving its issues |
| 20,000 characters | Existing supported bound; convenience | A retained long unit can still dominate latency | Actual per-piece wire sizes and the first returned response |
| 32 units | Reviewed convenience choice balancing output count and call cost | First piece still requires substantial accounting | Exact coverage/IDs and measured completion, not a presumed speed gain |
| 31 core calls | Remaining baseline allowance; equal-effort constraint | Fidelity/repairs may make completion impossible | Actual core admission; retain unsupported/unformalized readings |
| 300-second call cap | Existing transport policy; hypothesis | Transient service latency may reject an otherwise viable request | Retain response/absence and timing; do not attribute a single timeout to size |
| Same-model, baseline-first pair | Original comparison policy | Shared errors and order effects | Source-grounded descriptive assessment; no superiority claim |

**Skeptical audit: PASS for this bounded existing-method test, subject to local
qualification.** The underlying legal problem remains the missing warrant from
source language to fulfillment, not a lack of another solver. Decomposition is
chosen solely to unblock the model-produced legal argument. The timeout itself
does not prove request size caused failure; successful earlier calls had the
same startup plugin warnings. Extending a timeout or building a new answer
engine now would add untested mechanisms. This test changes only the existing
inventory allocation and equal remaining effort, with adverse outcomes retained.

Pre-mortem: all piece inventories could be valid while their merge omits how P10
relates to footnote 1 or imports P11 as its discharge standard. The mandatory
cross-check and final source review must catch that. Conversely, a local schema
failure or another unavailable response rejects this execution policy, not the
legal arguments. The smallest useful artifact is a validated source piece,
followed by the complete cross-checked inventory and original-question answer.
Evidence belongs under `live-pilot-400/decomposed/`, with a separate result note,
the shared unchanged allowance, and mission refresh after each work package.
