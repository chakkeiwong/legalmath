## Question and scope

Can the prospectus investigation distinguish contractual loss absorption,
statutory resolution, an actual administrative order, judicial review and
investor-specific selling restrictions, without inventing a legal conclusion
from a country name, a risk warning or a human-labelled expected answer?

The comparator is the existing prospectus campaign: it extracts statutory
resolution mentions and checks conditional SPI rules but does not establish
the applicable Swiss legal powers or the effect of subsequent judgments.
The addition is a detailed, introductory monograph section, a preserved primary
source dossier and a bounded executable procedure for keeping these questions
separate. It is not a claim to automate unrestricted legal interpretation.

## Research intent and evidence contract

The mechanism under test is explicit scope, time and source-dependency checking,
followed by separate decisions for loss mechanism, legal authority, actual order,
judicial finality and distribution conditions. The expected failure is an
unsupported implication such as Swiss AT1 implies permanent write-down,
possible total loss implies prohibited sale, or annulment implies repayment.

Pass criteria: every substantive legal proposition in the new section has an
inspected primary source and pinpoint; all new source bytes have recorded hashes;
the executable checks reject missing, stale, conflicting and out-of-scope evidence;
the manuscript compiles with resolved citations and inspected new pages.
These are engineering/source-support criteria, not a measured legal-accuracy score.

Promotion vetoes: a blanket country rule, an uninspected legal proposition,
conflation of case outcomes, a missing necessary premise, or a test that treats
a human's interpretation as its truth label. Repair these and repeat the focused
check. Continuation vetoes: corrupt source bytes, an unresolvable identity/version
mismatch, or an inaccessible source essential to a categorical claim. Such a gap
requires qualification rather than an invented answer. Layout and extraction
diagnostics explain failures; they cannot certify legal meaning or reader response.

Results will be retained in docs/monograph/review/coco-legal-risk/ and the legal
source dossier in docs/prospectus/legal/. No universal future-law correctness,
complete global-law coverage, compiler superiority or investment recommendation
will be inferred. Legal texts and judgments are the material being modelled;
neither human output ratings nor author/model agreement is a quality oracle.

## Skeptical audit before implementation

The initial country-wide write-down assumption fails: contractual conversion is
also possible, and the Credit Suisse order has a contested judicial history.
The plan therefore uses instrument/entity scope and legal-event chronology.
The SFC 2018 circular expressly discusses regulator-triggered loss absorption,
but it does not establish a universal retail prohibition merely from total-loss
risk. Investor eligibility must identify the actual applicable restriction.
The 2026 Supreme Court search hit must be read before treating it as a merits
appeal decision. Existing campaign passes concern formal rules and provenance,
not the legal completeness now being investigated. With these revisions, the
plan passes this scoped audit; missing legal support triggers qualification and
targeted repair, not promotion by a proxy test score.
