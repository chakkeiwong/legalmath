# Prospectus continuation: code audit and causal repairs

## Scope and protected comparator

This is a successor protocol authorised by the user's instruction to trace the
code, close gaps in the next plan and continue. The comparator is commit
119f3d0d and the delivered E7 attempt-002 / E8 attempt-001 of the evidence
master. Its 36 issues (18 yes, 16 no, two unresolved), candidate-004 method
archive, 61 requests and two consumed post-exposure repair cycles are historical
evidence. Do not modify their state, inputs, attempts, reviews or budgets.
The original 32-case comparator is the delivered gap-closure campaign.

The audit covers the executable entry point through acquisition, registration,
extraction, document scope, clause witnesses, decision and replay, calculations,
bank integration, reporting and restart checks. It is an enumerated audit of
these paths, not a proof that unrestricted software or legal language has no
other defects. See `docs/implementation/prospectus-evidence-continuation/gaps.md`.

## Intent and evidence contract

Question: can the bounded reader distinguish cash principal paid in redemption
from principal forgiven, preserve distinct loss clauses, and carry every open
requirement into an executable, resumable continuation?

The candidate adds narrow source constructions and validates scope at the reader
boundary. The expected failure is an unresolved ordinary clause or an excluded
loss clause caused by a sentence-wide exemption. Before repair, retain complete
reader counterexamples for both directions, malformed scope, repayment polarity
and issue binding. A green test count alone is not the promotion criterion.

Acceptance requires the targeted counterexamples to fail on the old reader and
pass after repair, the existing prospectus/compliance regression suite to pass,
all 36 exposed cases to replay with source/derivation checks, every answer change
and every newly excluded formerly unresolved passage (including positive issues)
to be explicitly reviewed, and all 14 bank obligations to remain present with
permission false. LVMH may change from unknown to a *qualified* negative only
after source inspection supports all five former unresolved passages. BASF must
stay unknown while its required editions are absent. Counts and timings are
explanatory; no stochastic ranking, unseen accuracy, complete governing law,
actual loss, signed-agreement completeness or transaction permission follows.

Promotion vetoes: failed regression, an unreviewed changed answer/witness, a
missing material source, unresolved changed passage, or missing bank obligation.
These trigger a recorded repair and a fresh dependent run. Continuation vetoes:
corrupt retained baseline, method drift during a phase, exhausted applicable
budget, or repeated identical infrastructure failure. A candidate failure does
not reject the research direction or cancel an independent retrieval phase.

## Reviewed execution and limits

All commands retain the existing literal approved entry point:

```
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_prospectus_evidence_master.py continue check
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_prospectus_evidence_master.py continue run
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_prospectus_evidence_master.py continue status
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_prospectus_evidence_master.py continue verify
```

The successor has its own lock, state, append-only attempts, method/input
fingerprints, receipts and result-bound next-phase plans. The old `run` remains
the historical protocol; it is not the continuation command. Fixed subcommands
accept no arbitrary executable, shell expression or source URL. Source requests
are drawn from a reviewed public queue. No rule installation or new broad
interpreter approval is needed.

| Phase | Work and acceptance artifact | Repair/next phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C0 preserve and inventory | Verify every historical receipt/output, source derivative and frozen method archive; emit per-issue unresolved-clause/reference/obligation inventory and protected hashes | Corruption stops affected inference; exact recovery precedes C1 |
| C1 repair and replay | Retain failing tests, execute reader fixes, full regression, checked 36-case replay, answer-change and five-passage disposition review | Failure produces an explicit repair requirement; no success by returning the old result |
| C2 integrate and expose gaps | Rebuild the real bank investigations, exact obligation keys, current requirements and source-reference counts; inspect calculation interfaces and preserve unsupported profiles | Missing actual inputs stay unknown; no fabricated customer/event evidence |
| C3 recover public evidence | Attempt exact BASF historical terms routes, Unilever route, Lloyds primary filing and the recorded HKMA redirect; retain failures and inspect successful source bytes and dates | Retrieval alone cannot close applicability, amendments, language or law |
| C4 deliver and refresh | Verify successor receipts, report all answer changes and outstanding gaps, publish executable next command per remaining dependency | New semantic repair invalidates affected work; no rebranding exposed cases as unseen |

Resource limits: four hours of successor phase execution, three identical failed
attempts per input, at most 12 HTTP dispatches, 45 seconds and 32 MiB per response,
and two retrieval attempts per URL. Reserve requests before dispatch. CPU only;
no package installation, paid model calls, private-data acquisition, email or
trading. Development repairs use the finite counterexample set and at most three
full replay attempts per identical method/input; a newly observed causal defect
requires a recorded explanation before another method is validated. No new
unseen-case challenge is claimed in this protocol: freeze and predeclare that
separately after these repairs.

Skeptical review, before implementation: the N0–N6 prose plan was insufficient.
It pointed to a completed no-op runner, did not preserve independent execution
budgets, had no executable closure criteria for the five passages, omitted
mixed-clause/scope/polarity tests, and could hide unresolved clauses behind a
positive binary answer. The revised protocol supplies those checks and records
external evidence requirements without pretending they are coding tasks.
Audit passed for bounded implementation: no test or successful retrieval is
used as legal promotion; stale context, CPU environment, actual-fact boundaries,
source editions and independent repairs have explicit checks and stop rules.

After each phase, write its result, manifest (commit, exact command, Python/CPU,
data hashes, N/A deterministic seeds, duration, plan, outputs), executed repairs
and next justified action. Final review must inspect both unchanged and changed
answers, all new exclusion constructions, restart idempotence and integrity
failure tests. Preserve the strongest remaining alternative explanation:
extractor and replay may share the same incomplete English interpretation.
