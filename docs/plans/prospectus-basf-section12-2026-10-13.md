## Source and default audit

The base-131 image and retained selected source text were inspected. Section
12(1) allows an additional issue without holder consent only when the notes
have the same terms, subject to the stated date, interest-start and issue-price
exceptions, so they form one series. Section 12(2) allows purchases at any
price and distinguishes holding, resale or submission to the Fiscal Agent for
cancellation. Its public-offer sentence requires an offer to all holders; it
does not assert an identical transaction price or an actual tender. Section
12(3) requires prompt cancellation of fully repaid notes and bars reissue and
resale. The word *fully* is necessary: purchase alone and partial repayment do
not satisfy it. The selected BASF SE source does not acquire the adjacent
BASF Finance-only guarantee language merely by page adjacency.
