## Live pilot evidence contract — proposed, not authorized

Question: on the same retained law and two exact questions, does optional tool
feedback help the product give the source-supported reconciliation analysis and
preserve P10 alternatives? Comparator: the same complete investigation and answer
consumer with optional backend absent. Treatment: the installed BM25,
LegalRuleML, PyArg/Carneades/clingo bundle. Run one pair first, retaining arm
order and provider settings. This is a descriptive case study; it cannot rank
individual tools or demonstrate general accuracy.

Primary answer criteria, assessed by executor source review and marked as such:

- Addresses allocation, conduct and outcome readings of P10; explains why P11
  cannot silently replace P10 and what authority could resolve the remaining
  discharge/remediation issue.
- Separates a supervision/reconciliation event, agreement of records, validity
  of underlying rights and the evidential/constitutive role of the records.
  In the stipulated shared-error case, two matching zero entries do not prove
  accuracy of an assumed ten-unit entitlement. A mismatch in a secondary copy
  alone does not identify the governing legal consequence.
- Cites the actual Appendix validity/reconciliation and secondary-record
  passages, footnote 1 and relevant adjacent provisions; states the strongest
  contrary and applicability/time qualifications.
- Retains missing premises and supported rivals. Tool agreement, an XML check,
  unknown facts, proposed priorities and an unformalized alternative do not
  become an unconditional compliance verdict.
- Gives a complete answer with a trace of which tool evidence was used, rejected
  or irrelevant. Receipt of feedback alone is not consideration. An unchanged
  answer is a valid result; no benefit or harm must remain reportable.

Promotion vetoes: trigger substitution; omitted decisive contrary; unsupported
source-to-warrant inference; treating unknown as false; hiding unformalized
meanings; mismatched sources/questions; stale dossier/context; accepting the
executor's own rubric as independent legal adjudication. Runtime and test counts
are explanatory. Source corruption, nonmatching inputs, unavailable required
route, missing call accounting, or resource expiry are continuation vetoes.
An arm's legal failure is a candidate failure, not automatic rejection of the
tool research direction. Missing applicable authority is reported as an exact
remaining dependency; a qualified but complete answer can still be assessed.

Illustrative ceiling, subject to the local capacity audit: at most 36 core model
calls, 3 question-assignment attempts, 48 scoped calls and 1 answer call per arm,
or 176 total for two arms. Those are ceilings inherited from existing method
limits, not evidence that this corpus fits them. Source-generated claim/candidate
counts must fit the reserved scoped capacity without silently dropping pairs.
If they do not, the run is under-budgeted and the protocol must be revised before
dispatch. Do not obtain apparent completion by reducing the legal question or
switching to duty activation. Monetary cost is not yet established: provider
pricing, token sizes and an executable payload have not been qualified. No grant
request is made on this incomplete estimate.

After the pair, inspect the complete answers before any expansion. Extend to
