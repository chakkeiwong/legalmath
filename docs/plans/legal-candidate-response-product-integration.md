## Question and evidence contract

Can an ordinary `CompleteInvestigation` use the candidate-specific response
constraints that recovered the coverage run, while preserving every original
target, judgment, quotation, uncertainty and failure boundary?

The exact comparator is the completed coverage runner's
`legalmath.candidate-bound-response.v1` over the retained 492 claim/candidate
pairs and two perspectives. The proposed mechanism is a reusable product codec
with an explicit `response_profile` option. The default remains `literal`.
This is an engineering integration test, not another legal assessment.

## Skeptical audit before execution

The initial idea of combining candidate aliases with all existing input codecs
was flawed: readable tables revalidate original source span IDs, which aliases
replace. The revised plan rejects the incompatible combination before dispatch
and tests that boundary. It does not silently weaken source table validation.

The revised audit passes. The comparator is the committed implementation and
unchanged retained judgments; counts are insufficient without full-content
comparison. Existing validators remain authoritative. Explicit stop conditions
cover corrupted evidence and unavailable required checks; expected response
rejection triggers repair, not abandonment of the program. No ranking, default
promotion, fresh legal evidence or legal closure follows from this integration.
Remaining uncertainty is live performance on other inputs, which this plan does
not claim to evaluate.
