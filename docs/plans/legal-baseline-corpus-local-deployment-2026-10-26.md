## Skeptical review before execution

PASS for local deployment, HOLD for live continuation. The comparator is the
actual stopped worker and hardened candidate, not the superseded patch. A simple
git apply would leave protected hashes stale and therefore is insufficient.
Source changes, program/state projection and current binding must be reviewed
together before the transaction. The strongest misleading outcome would be a
passing unit suite with stale authoritative bindings; D3 and D5 explicitly test
that risk. Another risk is accidentally resetting exhausted allowances; exact
before/after receipts and negative tests veto it. Source-selector validation
does not prove the corresponding legal criticism.

The execution graph is acyclic. Live resumption is independent of local
installation success and requires authority that is not supplied by this user
request. A failed staged validation triggers the bounded local correction, not
an external retry. Completion means deployment verified, with the remaining
legal and service prerequisites reported candidly.
