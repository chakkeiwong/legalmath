## Question and evidence contract

Can the repaired executable-span and readable-table transports support actual
source investigations, while preserving every earlier unsuccessful attempt?
Can a future-source observation be checked against the entire frozen method,
rather than only the final proof checker?

The baselines are the retained UCITS 25EC66 investigation (225 pairs, 60 without
two validated perspectives), the four-task unfamiliar-source study, and D1's
synthetic full-source readable-table check. D1 is a byte-preservation baseline,
not evidence that a live model understands the tables. All sources already seen
are development data; nothing here converts them into prospective observations.

Pass criteria are exact accounting and independently checked identities:
original issues cannot receive excess requests; selected IDs resolve to the
unchanged original source/program text; rejected responses remain rejected;
all required pairs/units/questions and unresolved concerns remain in reports.
A whole-investigation observation must bind source acquisition/extraction,
provider route, search settings, question/authority/scoped stages, transports,
issue limits, implementation and qualification evidence to the frozen method.
Native/proof checks are executed when an observation makes those claims.

Integrity failure, changed source bytes, unavailable authenticated provider,
or an invalid harness veto continuation of the affected command. Exhaustion,
invalid model answers, ambiguity, unsupported programs and disagreement qualify
the candidate and trigger the next eligible repair/work item; they do not
invalidate the research direction. No result establishes English interpretation,
legal accuracy, model independence, statistical superiority or future legal
generalization. No human labels or quality approvals enter the experiment.

question hashes without providing them: one live response supplied all-zero
values. A synthetic provider had computed those hashes itself and therefore
failed to expose this missing-input defect. This is an implementation/input
failure, not evidence against the legal interpretation method.

**F2-table-identity** follows the first bounded-output attempt. Requests now

silently reused under the new prompt. Original failures remain rejected.
