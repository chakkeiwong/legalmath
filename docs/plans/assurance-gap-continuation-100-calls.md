accounted for; missing role assignments are completed or have a specific final
failure record; all 35 reading records receive explicit source-bound assessment;
every proposed formalization is checked against independently stated obligations
and machine semantics or receives a precise unsupported/qualified disposition;
authority conclusions bind exact source editions and dates. Zero unresolved
legal questions is not a software acceptance criterion. No vote, schema pass,
test count, Boolean proof, or successful download establishes legal correctness.

Promotion vetoes: altered source/candidate/response identity, invalid quote,
unaccounted action, changed prior ledger, formal output outside its declared
domain, dropped assumption, or latest-edition evidence described as historical.
These trigger repair before dependent results are accepted. Continuation vetoes:
corrupt baseline, failed integrity check, exhausted 100-call cap, expired new
window, repeated service failures under the declared stop rule, or unavailable
required toolchain. Legal disagreement rejects a candidate or leaves a question
open; it does not stop independent authority or formalization work.
Coverage/counts/runtime are descriptive only; no ranking is statistically supported.

1. The old 48-call phase and three engineering attempts are closed. The new user
authorization supersedes that stopping boundary for this separately registered
100-call increment; it does not rewrite it. Preserve the shared 78/500 ledger,
old 45/48 arm and their hashes. Do not simulate exhaustion to satisfy the old
GrantedAllowance constructor. Use a new authorization-bound 100-call ledger
with immutable predecessor checkpoints and no borrowing from the future arm.
2. Old issue counts and first-start times are evidence, including attempts that
failed before dispatch. Record them in the new task inventory. The new authority
explicitly permits at most two new dispatched attempts per substantive task/role
during a new 24-hour execution window. Every failure consumes its slot. New
identifiers or output partitioning cannot renew that allowance. Historical
deadlines remain reported; this is an explicit supplemental review window.
3. The complete 35-reading challenge packet is 255,618 bytes in the old codec.
Do not drop readings, quotations, source units or raise the 200,000-byte cap.
For expanded assessment use an exact general text-pool encoding, with literal
full source and independently verified reconstruction. Output batches may be
small while the complete reading context remains available. Preflight each
actual request before dispatch. If a request cannot fit, retain a qualified
frontier and repair its representation before any corresponding call.
4. The retained 26EC22 packet explicitly says "20 Apr 2026" and "Revised on
20 April 2026". The inherited notes about a 2023 question refer to separate
historical work and cannot define this live target. Bind the current source
question to its 2026 edition and retain the older authority questions separately.
The Chapter 571 XML declares a 2025-08-24 consolidation; its amendment dates
must be inspected before any historical or 2026 applicability conclusion.
5. The 21 alternatives are records, not established unique meanings. Keep every
record. Group only exact substantive identities automatically; classify broader
relationships as proposals subject to both reader assessments.
6. Many retained meanings require external legal/factual judgment. A useful
Boolean model may represent conditional logical structure, but an arbitrary
Boolean named "compliant" is not a resolved legal test. Require explicit fact
meanings, dates/units, unresolved classifications, source anchors, and independent
positive/negative obligations. Retain the original reading alongside any derived
formalization; never silently replace the source interpretation.
7. The 84 response differences include wording differences. Prioritize actual
status conflicts, follow-up questions and missing evidence; do not treat every
wording difference as a contradiction requiring another expensive round.
8. Review and compilation cannot supply human legal acceptance or prospective
evidence. This development continuation is ineligible as a fresh future observation.

Audit verdict: PASS for the revised staged plan, conditional on the local
integrity/budget/codec checks before the first live call. The premature plan of
repeating the old panel unchanged is rejected for wrong date assumptions, size,
quota and semantic-completeness reasons.

Two consecutive service/stream failures stop live breadth. First inspect saved
errors and run one counted targeted repair probe within the same task ceiling;
if it fails, stop provider-dependent work and complete independent offline work.
Wrong output identity or quote is rejected, retained and eligible for one
targeted correction. Integrity failures stop dependent execution immediately.
Every action writes request, schema, reservation and final success/failure receipt;
interruption after reservation remains spent and is recovered without blind replay.

A formal candidate may fail a premise while the premise itself is disputed.
Record both; never remove the legal reading by a conditional proof. A source
authority may remain inaccessible or temporally uncertain; this is an explicit
remaining dependency, not permission to invent content. Before ending, distinguish
harness invalidity, candidate failure, external evidence gaps and budget exhaustion.
