# Source-report readings and downstream interpretation

## Question and evidence contract

Can the actual prospectus consumer distinguish a statutory financial audit,
committee concurrence and limited sustainability assurance, preserve the
qualifications of each, and explain their scope using exact retained sources?
The baseline is the same frozen P1 graph and unchanged contract request without
report admissions. Four matched readings are selected before implementation:
2009 statutory opinion (PDF 174–175), 2009 committee (176–177), and 2010
sustainability assurance (88–89), in English and Portuguese. These are
development cases already inspected, not independent heldout legal readings.

Promotion criterion for this optional integration: actual P2/P3/final consumers
carry the reviewed classification and all supplied limits with exact source
maps; matched Q1–Q6 answers and original unresolved dependencies do not acquire
favorable changes. All nonspace characters in the selected frozen page units
must survive exactly once, including margins. Omission, changed source/date/
quote, different edition, stale graph/reading and changed region order controls
must reject. Unknown facts remain explicit, including publication dates,
historical availability and independent edition authentication. An English
translation-priority statement belongs to its English source; it is not
invented in the Portuguese report.

Source coverage here means coverage of selected **retained P1 units**, not
proof that OCR captured every original page mark. Source maps and test totals
are engineering criteria. They do not prove the supplied semantic review is
legally correct, authenticate the reviewer, resolve the fourteen original
dependencies, establish investor reliance, or show any package is unsuitable.
No inference is drawn about solvency or bond repayment from favorable assurance.

## Skeptical audit and defaults

The prior repair is verified and sealed. Its nine-receipt snapshot is the
historical engineering baseline. Adding source code will change controller
bindings; do not call old receipts current for the new method. The completed
coordinator's earlier failures remain retained. The shared `.venv` imports main
unless `src` is explicitly selected, so every runner pins this checkout before
imports and focused pytest uses the explicit `PYTHONPATH` prefix.

| Choice | Provenance and justification | Failure risk and earliest check | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen P1 replay | Already verified source/image consumers; avoid new OCR confounding the integration | Recheck product/graph and PDF hashes before any use | Reviewed comparator |
| Three report categories | Actual source titles and conclusions inspected in the retained readings | Swapped category/subject or an unsupported category must reject | Bounded hypothesis, not universal taxonomy |
| Supplied page-89 regions | Image-reviewed prior P1 mappings | Account for every nonspace character; bind exact selected reading/order | Reviewed case-specific input, no automatic detector |
| Facts supplied by reviewer | Existing admission model, exact spans | Structural checks cannot prove a plausible but false semantic interpretation; disclose assisted status | Supplied interpretation, no independent acceptance |
| Missing facts UNKNOWN | Source wording cannot prove absence or historical availability | Missing fields reject; explicit unknowns produce unresolved statements | Reviewed default |
| Additive report readings | Report identity differs from contractual OPERATIVE/UNKNOWN roles | Baseline assembly and Q1–Q6 must remain equal | Reviewed architectural choice |

Audit verdict: proceed with these restrictions. The material flaw in a naive
plan would be promoting construction roles or equating an assurance conclusion
with an unconditional financial assurance. Keep report readings separate from
the assembled contract, and make downstream explanations consume the new facts.
Full-page/source bindings check integrity; they cannot make our own source
classification independent. No new package, network call or GPU is required.
