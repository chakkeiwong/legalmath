## Question and evidence contract

Can we remove the imposed compliance/prohibition labeling error and make the
competing parses of a disputed qualification explicit, executable and reviewable,
while preserving unresolved authority and interpretation? The comparator is
round7's frozen four-candidate, 204-pair matrix and checked criticism. This is
an engineering repair and one-source diagnostic, not an accuracy benchmark.

The observed candidate formulas use the same three-factor licensing conjunction
but outsource disputed business meaning to judgment inputs. A successful
successor must preserve that distinction: predicate satisfaction has an explicit
neutral meaning; a local interpretation experiment encodes alternative
antecedents, not an unconditional licensing exemption. The stage cannot pass
merely because another model agrees with the author or because all candidates
abstain. It must retain the generated readings, actual finite-domain distinctions,
independent criticism, source evidence, omitted-reading concerns and remaining
questions in a replayable result.

| Diagnostic | Role and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Output type, exact source and stable vocabulary | Promotion veto; changed or unverifiable inputs require repair |
| Full T/F/U/conflict Java–Python agreement | Engineering pass criterion; mismatch invalidates that implementation |
| Distinct source-backed interpretation proposals | Descriptive evidence; one defensible proposal may remain one, with diversity unestablished |
| Fresh missing-reading challenge | Required coverage action; agreement does not prove completeness |
| Explicit source/assumption criticism for every surviving reading | Required evidence; contested readings remain visible |
| Parallel official language text | Additional source evidence; no assumed superiority or legal precedence |
| Service interruption, invalid response | Bounded retry/repair trigger; failures are counted |
| Exhausted three-attempt task or missing source authority | Explicit unresolved result; no automatic human-consultancy requirement |
| Corrupted baseline or unavailable service after bounded recovery | Continuation veto for dependent live work |

No comparison of legal accuracy, cost savings, population error rate or model
superiority is admitted. Scores count executed distinctions, not probabilities.
Synthetic scenarios may combine facts that are infeasible in a real business;
their role is to demonstrate program consequences conditional on declared facts.

## Baseline and assumption audit

The new output vocabulary is a schema repair motivated by the observed mismatch,
not a default change to any approved bank rule. Existing candidate bytes remain
frozen; new readings have new commitments. Runtime uncertainty descriptions are
checked against all 64 states of a three-input test in Python and Java.

The clause experiment has at most four Boolean facts and twelve retained
hypotheses. These are convenience limits for exhaustive checks, not a claim that
legal reasoning is Boolean or that twelve hypotheses are complete. Freeze the
fact definitions before asking readers. An independent vocabulary concern can
veto acceptance of the abstraction; it cannot be discarded to preserve coverage.

The disputed issue is the *antecedent* of the qualified ordinary-course business
guidance. Its vocabulary must describe arrangement and business/profit facts,
not contain a Boolean named “legally exempt” or “correct interpretation”. The
question explicitly preserves the surrounding non-exhaustive, case-dependent
assessment. Copy retained qualifications into each reading's assumptions and
explain that this preserves metadata, not an executable classifier for judgment.

The exact Traditional Chinese page linked by the retained English page was
downloaded separately under round7/parallel-source. Its last-update label matches
8 September 2020, but source-version equivalence and any legal precedence remain
unadjudicated. Do not add it to the English-only baseline or retroactively treat
it as material the earlier readers had seen.

## Skeptical audit before admission

Provisional PASS WITH CONDITIONS for isolated development only. The final
baseline results and actual failure classes must inform admission. The strongest
misleading-success scenario is a perfectly tested expression vocabulary that
already assumes the disputed answer; freeze and challenge that vocabulary first.
The second is duplicate model prose counted as independent legal support; use
distinct executable behavior counts and disclose correlation. The third is a
neutral label attached to a program still used as a compliance verdict; bind the
question and descriptor to the exact draft Java bundle and prohibit automatic
polarity normalization. These checks govern promotion, not a reason to abandon
the planned repairs when a candidate fails.
