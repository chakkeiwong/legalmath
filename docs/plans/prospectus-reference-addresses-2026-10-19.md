## Evidence contract and research intent

The comparator is the complete attempt-083 answer and frozen P1–P5 products from
review conditional-cli-311. Primary criterion: every frozen address has the exact
expected section, path, destination heading, interval and source evidence;
adverse cases remain unresolved or are refused; all prior answer fields survive
both normal and installed execution. Expected failure modes are wrong parent
inheritance, Roman/letter confusion, nearest-label guessing, duplicate addresses,
unknown-role promotion and historical auxiliary-map dependence.

Wrong addresses, missing source pieces, changed original answers, invented legal
acceptance and source-checkout imports veto promotion and trigger a reviewed
repair. Corrupted baselines, changed frozen expectations and cumulative resource
exhaustion veto this attempt's continuation. Counts and timings explain coverage
and cost only. No unrestricted recall, exhaustive legal interpretation, statutory
validity, factual default or whole-task completion follows from passing.

The conditional correctness argument is finite: a validated unique address index
binds each key to its exact source interval; explicit addresses are key lookups;
relative addresses supply only the enclosing parents required by a declared
level; sibling ranges enumerate the stated ordered labels inclusively. A missing
or nonunique key cannot establish a destination. This proves the mapping under
the declared hierarchy, not that every possible drafting convention uses it.
