# Qualify every retained UCITS reading without changing its interpretation

This bounded check supplements C5 while the separate C6 live reader runs.
It makes no model requests and edits no implementation or historical evidence.

Question: can every one of the nine unchanged retained UCITS readings receive
either an independently checked formal proposition and target checks, or an
explicit qualification through the new adapter, with its exact question and
source identities preserved? The comparator is the original retained dossier,
including its reading assumptions, missing dependencies and question assignments.

Acceptance: all nine identities accounted for; adapter verification succeeds;
unencoded and unavailable translations remain explicit; reported target-case
counts equal actual executions. A failed integrity check, dropped candidate,
changed question or overwritten historical receipt vetoes acceptance and stops
this diagnostic. A supported `UNENCODED` or unresolved-source disposition is a
qualification, not an implementation failure.

Inputs are the hash-verified `25ec66` packet, candidates and question assignments
in the historical successor dossier. For each encoded all-Boolean reading,
generate four mathematical challenges: every fact false, every fact true,
every fact unknown, and every fact conflicting. These choices are structural
probes, justified by known/missing/conflict boundaries. They are not asserted
bank facts or a complete Boolean truth table. The earliest diagnostic checks
that all formal facts are Boolean; another type is reported rather than assigned
a convenient value. Unencoded readings receive no fabricated runtime cases.

Use `.venv/bin/python` and the same pinned Java/Catala/Lean tools as C4. Record
the exact payload command, source hashes, tool identities, CPU-only setting,
base commit, elapsed time and individual results under
`artifacts/assurance-continuation/2026-09-29/retained-reading-qualification/`.
No random seed applies. Verification and runtime outcomes are engineering
criteria; observed case counts and runtimes are explanatory only. They cannot
rank interpretations, prove natural-language meaning, validate fact definitions
or complete the unfamiliar-source study.

Skeptical audit: PASS for this bounded check. The most misleading possible
success is agreement between backends generated from the same mistaken legal
reading. The adapter retains that reading as a premise and labels its meaning
unestablished. The check therefore cannot become a promotion criterion for
English correctness. It supplies missing actual retained-input evidence for
the qualification integration, rather than relying only on simulated pipeline
tests or the single private-market arithmetic reproducer.
