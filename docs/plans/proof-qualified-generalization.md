## Product goal and research intent

The product must establish a precisely scoped correctness proposition with an
independently executed checker, or issue a qualified statement identifying what
is not established. Human answers, adjudication, ratings, review approval and
acceptance judgments are forbidden as quality evidence. Model agreement is also
not an authority. Legal sources and formal semantic specifications remain
versioned premises, never proof that their proposed interpretation is correct.

The central question is whether source-bound interpretations can retain useful,
checkable guarantees on previously unseen inputs and amended sources without
fitting to a human answer key. Distinguish universal formal-domain guarantees,
generated engineering challenges, untouched prospective source evidence, and
unresolved natural-language meaning. Never promote one category into another.

Expected failures are a shared parsing error, false proof scope, evidence leakage,
missing alternatives, stale source use, unsupported native models and resource
failure. Promotion requires checked propositions or explicit qualification for
every selected candidate, with full denominators. A false certificate, dropped
candidate, stale revision or human quality evidence vetoes promotion and triggers
repair. A changing validation baseline, corrupt evidence or unavailable required
checker vetoes the affected run until repaired. Unsupported language features
produce retained qualified results; they do not end the research direction.

No present run can observe future publications. A frozen prospective protocol and
admission/revision checks are deliverables now; future legal generalization stays
NOT_ESTABLISHED until eligible evidence exists, and cannot become an unrestricted
claim even then. Institutional deployment and human-benefit evaluation are out
of scope by user direction.

## Evidence contract

Comparator: the unchanged shared model and both target translators at this
checkpoint; the pinned original Catala interpreter and plain/instrumented Java;
an independently parsed formal expression and a Lean-kernel-checked shared-model
lowering proposition. Generated cases derive answers by executable mathematics,
not authored legal answers or returned candidate outputs.

Primary engineering criteria: (1) genuine checker execution and target binding;
(2) direct rich Catala assurance without a RuleIR prerequisite; (3) exact runtime
agreement on generated cases, plus retained unsupported/error cases; (4) refusal
of human evidence and overbroad generalization claims; (5) source/method changes
invalidate active qualifications while preserving history; (6) freezing prevents
repaired cases from re-entering the same confirmation window; (7) measured
resource/trace/library limits appear in the qualification.

All correctness mismatches and accepted tampering are hard vetoes. Runtime,
memory, test counts, solver/model agreement and static resource counts are
explanatory only. There is no default-backend or speed-ranking promotion.
Artifacts: `artifacts/proof-qualified-generalization/2026-09-28/`; product and
operator documentation under `docs/implementation/proof-qualified-generalization/`.

## Skeptical review before execution

Initial proposal REVISE. Human adjudication conflicts with the user's quality
contract; fresh model contexts are not independent truth; synthetic perturbations
cannot demonstrate unknown-future legal accuracy; a local timestamp is not a
trusted publication clock; a theorem about parsing is not a JVM/compiler theorem;
changing old lowering would invalidate frozen build verification; simply lifting
recursion/resource restrictions cannot establish support.

Revised plan PASS for bounded execution. It explicitly binds every proposition,
keeps source meaning qualified, executes trusted checkers, retains unsupported
cases, preserves old identities, and separates generated challenges from genuine
prospective evidence. No numerical or stochastic ranking is planned. Every
remaining inherited assumption has a qualification or negative test. The work
continues after candidate failures unless a true harness/evidence veto fires.

Commands use `PYTHONPATH=src:.`, `.venv/bin/python`, pinned JDK 17 and Catala 1.2.1,
the retained trace-v2 toolchain and installed Lean 4.20.0. All runs are CPU-only,
with no GPU framework imported. Record actual commands, seeds, wall times, source
hashes and result paths. No human acceptance step is part of this plan.
