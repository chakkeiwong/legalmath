## Design and default audit

The optional encoding shares identical record metadata, exact regular 256-codepoint
span recipes with explicit irregular exceptions, identical citation schemas and
repeated identity hashes. Integer selectors form an exact bijection to offered
span aliases. It removes no source text or proposed child reading. Historical
profiles remain byte-reproducible. Integer decoding is bound to the original
request, rejects booleans, strings, fractions and out-of-range values, and then
uses the existing citation resolver and legal validators.

The 12,000 leaf and 16,384 two-child combination caps are inherited policy,
retained rather than newly established provider capacity. The 256 step comes
from the existing span protocol and is used only where its exact recipe matches.
Unknown higher parents must still be measured. The largest observed candidate
has only 251 tokens of headroom; hidden provider framing is unmeasured. Exact
reconstruction is engineering correctness, not model comprehension.

Recovery distinguishes historical NOTE, historical typed regex schema and current
typed provider schema explicitly. Selection restores the exact successful
request and schema, validates under its original contract and refuses changed
assignments or source content. Failures select the new optional profile only
after exact predecessor identity checks. No resampling of successes is allowed.

Service deferral is a scheduler prerequisite, not a new call grant. The current
open circuit and spent reserve stay unchanged. Future requalification must
declare a finite allowance charged to the same global ledger and preserve old
circuit evidence. Original owner attempts cannot be restored retrospectively.
