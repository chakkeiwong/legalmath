# Completing integration while preserving unresolved legal questions

12 October 2026. Proposed completion design under
`legal-interpretation-mission-control.md` and its `product-contribution` task.
This document answers how to finish the integration task. It does not activate
an execution graph, grant repairs, renew live calls, change tool dispositions,
or claim that the repairs below have been implemented.

The current evidence does not establish that any remaining legal issue is
impossible to resolve. It establishes software defects, incomplete evaluation,
unfinished source analysis, and conclusions that depend on unavailable facts
or a deciding authority. These require separate decisions. Successful
integration must support a useful conditional legal answer when the evidence
cannot support an unconditional one.

## Classify individual premises and software duties

Do not put an entire obligation in an "external" bucket. The original inventory
contains 224 missing-premise records across 46 obligations; these are not 224
distinct problems. Some obligations combine facts, legal interpretation,
representation defects and a mismatch with the original question. Preserve
their IDs and original hashes; link duplicates without deleting their coverage.
The retained denominator is in the
[original inventory](../../artifacts/legal-integration-assessment/2026-10-08/input-inventory.json).

| Class | Current examples | What we still owe | What can close this item |
|---|---|---|---|
| Our implementation defect | Unused repair wiring; false paragraph attribution; incomplete review population; guard blocks sibling work; inconsistent runtime limits (G1–G8) | Repair the chosen product path and reproduce the relevant failure through it | The old counterexample fails safely and the intended valid case completes, with checked downstream behavior |
| Our unqualified behavior or unfinished evaluation | Reduction across groups (G9); native engines untested through the new backend; weak applicability cases; no complete matched answers | Construct discriminating cases and inspect actual results | A retained supported-scope result, including a negative result where justified; an inconclusive test remains incomplete |
| Our unfinished legal research and modeling | Source-to-rule warrants; P11 applicability; P14 attachment; Gatecoin transfer and later treatment | Inspect sources, explain the inference, retain contrary support, and search for missing authority within a declared scope | A warranted conclusion, or documented remaining uncertainty after the stated research; "not checked" is not an external limit |
| External fact or authoritative determination | Actual appointments, rights, records, supervision, timing, remediation, or SFC satisfaction | Name the exact needed input and its owner; produce the supported conditional alternatives now | Receipt of the fact/decision closes the legal premise; correct conditional handling can be verified before receipt |
| Capability boundary or unsuitable tool | Restricted Boolean/controlled-language profiles; unsupported jurisdiction/corpus; unavailable calibration labels | Test the boundary and fallback; distinguish a tool limit from a broken adapter or absent input | Reviewed scope restriction or disposition with evidence, question impact and reopening condition; this is not full-integration credit |

No fact-dependent conclusion can be derived merely by installing another solver.
No finite test suite can guarantee perfect interpretation of all English or all
future legal disputes. Those are limits on the requested inference. They do
not excuse a lost qualifier, fabricated citation, unwarranted premise or
unattempted research task.

For each original missing-premise ID, record: original obligation/reading links,
the precise unresolved proposition, class, evidence inspected, responsible role,
next action, item blocked, acceptance evidence and reopening condition. Keep a
separate software-handling result. For example, obligation 2 can retain an open
fulfillment standard and missing incident evidence while its source citations,
conditional conduct/outcome distinction and tool-input handling pass verification.
Its legal status does not thereby become fulfilled.

An "external unresolved authority" classification needs a dated search record:
jurisdiction, issues and citations queried, sources accessed, relevant reasons
and orders inspected, later-treatment checks, access limits and stopping rule.
No authority found in that search means unresolved within that search, not that
authority cannot exist. Private facts require a specific evidence request rather
than another search of public literature.

## Three separate completion decisions

| Decision | Required evidence | What remains separate |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering verified for a declared product configuration | Actual entry point selects the repaired components; native tools, provenance, review coverage, failure isolation, restart/accounting and capacity checks pass for the accepted scope | Whether a tool adds value or the law resolves a question |
| Tool applicability and contribution evaluated | Actual product invocation and downstream consideration; supported and unsupported cases; matched source/question baseline and candidate answers; retained assessment of effect | Whether the effect is favorable and whether it generalizes |
| Legal question disposition | Source-supported answer, contrary reasoning, explicit dependencies and handling of the original obligations/readings | Client-specific fulfillment, unresolved interpretation, later authority and general product accuracy |

The [current controller](../../scripts/legal_mission_control.py) already allows
`helps`, `no_benefit`, `harmful` and
`limited`. A positive effect is not required to complete an evaluation. Do not
keep retrying until a tool wins. Conversely, an invalid review or a missing
supported target is not evidence of no benefit. Report it as insufficient
evidence, leaving the evaluation open.

Retain the seven existing stages: `available`, `target_adapter`,
`product_invocation`, `question_preservation`, `downstream_delivery`,
`negative_and_unsupported` and `answer_contribution`. Preserve the current
`fully_integrated` requirements, including live product invocation/delivery,
`actual_product`, matched inputs and retained answers/assessment. Controlled
plumbing tests alone do not satisfy them. Scope each evaluation by questions,
cases, adapter profile, source version and product configuration.

An evaluation can finish with rejection. That closes an evaluation decision,
not successful integration or deployment of the rejected component. A selected
tool cannot be removed to hide a failed test: a disposition change must preserve
the previous selection, reason, evidence, impact on the original question and
reopening condition. Keep all 26 surveyed tools visible and distinguish the six
selected tools from supporting, conceptual, deferred and unsuitable tools.

Each of the 46 obligations needs a handling decision and each of the 35 readings
needs an explicit account. A conditional or external-open legal disposition
must remain visible. These are a retained coverage floor, not proof that all
possible obligations or interpretations have been discovered. Overall legal
mission readiness remains a separate, stricter decision.

For every applicable case, a useful conditional answer gives the supported
conclusion and source, the exact missing premise, supported rival or conditional
alternatives, what follows under each material alternative, and what evidence
would change the result. A bare "unknown" or generic limitation fails this
handling test even where the ultimate legal question must remain open.

| Selected tool | Discriminating applicability test |
|---|---|

Declare applicability before observing the answers. Do not invent legal attacks
to make the graph interesting. If no source-warranted supported target exists
for an original question, record the failed applicability argument and test
unsupported handling. It does not substitute for an engine-executed supported
case or justify marking the tool fully integrated for that question. Earlier
toy or hand-specified tests retain their narrower scope.

## Finite execution structure proposed for the next reviewed revision

These are work packages with dependencies, not successive new grants. One
failed tool must not cancel the baseline, another tool, another legal question
or independent source research. Run ready work within the eventual approved
limits; no additional agents are implied by logical independence.

| Item | Success prerequisites | Deliverable and local stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| S: freeze scope and reconcile state | Existing audit and original inventory | Named entry point, exact acceptance populations, case/tool mapping, prior consumed attempt reconciled, and finite resource/repair proposal. No new implementation is admitted by this design. |
| E: route, citations and review | S | G1–G5 repaired and exercised through the chosen entry point. Stop this branch on an unresolved shared provenance or review-integrity defect. |
| F: failure isolation and accounting | S | G6–G7 verified at adapter, guard and consumer boundaries, including restart. Stop the affected branch on uncertain dispatch or shared integrity failure. |
| C: capacity and reduction | S | G8–G9 checked against the actual configuration, complete case populations and adverse cross-group case. Exceeding a supported envelope requires an explicit refusal or a reviewed justified partition. |
| L(q): source/conditional cases, one branch per question | S | Premise classification, source warrants, contrary reasoning, search limits and accepted handling criteria for that question. Missing private facts leave only the dependent premise open. |
| T(t): native profile, one branch per tool | E and the relevant L(q) inputs | Real selected adapter, supported and negative controls, downstream evidence. Failures affect that tool's accepted scope; evaluate other tools independently. |
| Q(q, arm): exact route qualification and resource proposal | E, F, C, relevant L(q), and relevant successful T(t) for a tool arm | Enumerated request/resource bound, native environment identity, exact command forms and dry controls. A baseline has no optional-tool dependency. A combined arm needs every tool it actually enables. |
| P(q, arm): actual matched product answer | Relevant Q plus valid new live authorization | Complete actual route, answer, fresh source-grounded assessment and retained failure evidence. A failed baseline blocks a benefit comparison for that case, not independent candidate diagnosis or other questions. |
| Z: assessment and closeout | All planned branches terminal; success is not required | Explicit accepted scope, failed or incomplete integration, per-tool verdict, all original coverage decisions and externally open legal premises. No automatic successor phase. |

E, F and C name workstreams, not indivisible global barriers. S must expand their
edges to the specific shared component and source/case profile each arm uses.
For example, a failed Gatecoin locator does not block P10 with a verified circular
locator; a failed PyArg arm does not block clingo or the baseline. Corruption of
a common source identity blocks every arm that consumes that identity. A local
group-review failure blocks its dependent synthesis, while other groups retain
their own work. Z depends on terminal states so it can always report such gaps.

S must enumerate the bounded implementation allowance and node-specific
attempt/recovery caps before execution. Count the partially executed previous
attempt first. The proposed implementation is one consolidated package covering
the identified route, failure and capacity defects; do not create a fresh detour
for each failed assertion. If a branch exhausts its declared allowance, retain
its failure and continue independent branches. Another attempt needs new
discriminating evidence and an explicit reviewed decision, not a renamed phase.

Before P, complete the concrete offline preparation. Count calls from the actual
per-question populations, review groups, synthesis, final assessment and declared
recovery; include stage limits, output sizes, native runtime and deadline
headroom. Do not estimate from the historical seven-obligation pilot or reuse
unspent numbers from an expired grant. Request any necessary new live allowance
and protected-rule installation together for the reviewed commands and budget.
Local document/code edits need no separate permission. Approval rules cannot
renew an expired call grant or turn a failed method into a qualified one.

Use narrow literal command forms backed by the actual reviewed entry point.
Do not promise the nonexistent `legal_mission_integration_repair.py` wrapper or
install a generic Python/edit allowance. A runner is justified only to bind the
chosen route, resources and results; it is not a replacement product. Preserve
sealed historical runners, rules, sources and outputs.

After each package, retain its actual result, append mission history and the
next-task reason, and run the exact mission `refresh`. The final report must
answer whether all selected scope passed; a terminal graph or completed report
cannot make a failed integration successful. After Z, reopen only for a named
defect, new evidence, changed scope, or an explicitly renewed unresolved task.

## Evidence contract and skeptical review

The engineering question is whether the selected installed tools operate
correctly through the actual product and preserve the legal problem. The
evaluation question is what effect their outputs have on supported conditional
answers to the four original questions. Neither asks a solver to invent a
missing fact or decide an unsettled question of law.

The comparator receives the same original questions, full source access,
factual assumptions and any supplied reviewed inputs. Freeze model/settings,
prompt rules, resource allowances and acceptance criteria before a comparison;
record justified arm differences. For isolated contribution tests, change only
the intended tool feedback at a common valid checkpoint. Such a checkpoint
test cannot replace complete baseline/candidate product execution. If tool
feedback changes upstream discovery, report that as a separate whole-route
comparison, not an isolated answer-only effect.

| Diagnostic role | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Engineering acceptance | Actual configured route passes the listed invariants, native controls and complete declared population/resource checks. |
| Evaluation completion | Each selected tool has valid applicability, invocation, downstream and matched-answer evidence within its scope, or an evidence-backed reviewed rejection/disposition. Rejection is not successful integration. |
| Promotion veto | False citations, lost required alternatives, unwarranted legal assertions, incomplete coverage, invalid review, or absent required native/product evidence prohibit acceptance of the affected result. A candidate failure can still support a valid harmful/limited finding when the harness and baseline are valid. |
| Continuation veto | Expired/exhausted authority, shared invalid source/method identity, corrupted evidence or unresolved dispatch stops the dependent work. Unrelated offline source analysis remains eligible. |
| Repair trigger | A reproduced defect in the intended scope uses only its already declared repair allowance. A disliked tool effect is not a repair trigger. |
| Explanatory only | Unit-test totals, tool-call counts, retrieval ranks, token savings, runtimes and single-response differences explain behavior; they do not establish legal truth or superiority. |

Do not infer general English correctness, client compliance, complete legal
research or a statistically supported model/tool ranking. A single matched
case can establish an observed defect or an observed supported contribution.
It cannot establish that a tool generally helps or never helps. Use `limited`
where appropriate, and explain a case-specific `no_benefit` as no added benefit
observed in the tested cases. General performance claims require a separate
predeclared multi-case/replication design with uncertainty evidence.

| Material assumption | Provenance and reason | Failure risk and earliest check | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete investigation plus dossier answer is the acceptance route | Current product trace; tests must qualify the route the product uses | Accidentally testing only the focused runner; record and trace the exact entry point first | Proposed scope to freeze in S |
| Four questions, 46 obligations and 35 readings remain the coverage floor | Original mission/inventory | Subset trials silently become the denominator; verify IDs, hashes and case mappings | Required baseline, not proof of universal coverage |
| Existing source notes define useful conditional cases | Retained inspected-source reasoning | Treating executor opinions as adjudicated truth; audit warrants and contrary passages before freezing expected outcomes | Provisional reference |
| Installed tool profiles constrain the test | Current adapters and retained native evidence | Assuming the full upstream package capability; inspect actual invoked profile and supported control | Hypothesis until T |
| Full source is available to both arms | Existing fair baseline | Manufacturing benefit by weakening the comparator; compare source/question and supplied-input hashes | Required comparator |
| Current batching, capacity and model settings are suitable | Inherited implementation choices | Hidden replay/action/output limit or poor reduction; boundary and cross-group controls before live qualification | Unqualified until C/Q |

Skeptical review of this design: it corrects three possible shortcuts. First,
an external legal dependency does not excuse its software handling. Second,
negative tool findings are allowed, but broken or missing evaluations cannot be
relabeled negative findings. Third, finish decisions attach to the actual
product scope rather than the focused pilot. It preserves original populations,
separates observed from untested gaps, keeps independent branches runnable and
provides a terminal assessment without assuming universal success.
The review also found that treating E/F/C as global prerequisites would recreate
unnecessary blocking; the component/profile-specific dependency rule above
corrects that before any executable revision is created.

The design passes as a completion specification. It is not yet a dispatchable
live program: S must reconcile the consumed repair and freeze the case mappings;
Q must supply the measured route/environment and exact resource proposal.
These are explicit preparation dependencies, not reasons to repeat the audit.

Retain implementation and diagnostic results under the mission's existing
artifact hierarchy, including source/configuration hashes, exact commands,
environment, data versions, relevant seeds/settings, wall time, plan and result
paths. Assessment should state primary criterion, veto, uncertainty and next
justified action. A final rejection separates implementation failure,
unsupported target, uninformative test and evidence against utility. The
strongest alternative explanation for an apparent benefit is that our supplied
source analysis already contained the answer; matched baselines and a later
complete product-generated case are needed to expose that explanation.
