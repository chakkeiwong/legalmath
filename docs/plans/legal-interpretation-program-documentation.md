problem but need not know argumentation theory. The reader should explain why
retrieved citations do not settle meaning, compare the reusable methods, follow
a disputed circular through a proposed interpretation, and identify conditional
claims.

propositions are justified -> rival arguments make uncertainty inspectable ->
