## What the build must establish

For a declared supported domain, an answer must be reproducible from identified
sources, explicit interpretation premises, dated facts and stated financial
conventions. Independently implemented calculations must check the consequence.
The answer must retain materially different supported interpretations and the
facts that distinguish them. Missing information, conflicting statements and
unsupported inputs must not become an unconditional positive answer. Extraction
success and agreement between formulas do not prove the formulas express the
governing law. Source interpretation needs its own argument and counterexamples.

The engineering claim is conditional correctness within the stated domain. It
is not a proof of unrestricted legal truth, source authenticity from a hash, or
complete discovery of all future interpretations. Future cases must pass the
same source, coverage and domain checks; they do not inherit correctness from
having the same issuer or product name. Source conflicts remain explicit until
public controlling evidence resolves them. Lack of private documents or an
external adviser does not stop conditional analysis.
