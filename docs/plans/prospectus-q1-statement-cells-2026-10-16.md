Question: can the retained BES Q1 2011 release be mapped to its printed
statement rows, periods, unit, sign context, unknown dashes, and memorandum
rows without changing the source-bound current answer?

Comparator: the retained exact release-reading.txt from BES-DEP-11 attempt-002
and its corrected current-consumer product. The balance sheet must preserve

parentheses/dashes. The income statement must preserve EUR '000, Mar. 10 /

Primary criterion: every printed numeric cell is assigned to one row, period,
unit, and role; stock versus flow tables remain distinct; (o.w. ECB) rows are
marked memorandum/non-additive; dashes remain unknown; and the current consumer
retains identical questions, dependencies, assembly, source map, Q3 PARTIAL,
and no transaction authority.

Veto diagnostics: missing or extra rows/cells, period swaps, treating a dash as
zero, treating an o.w. row as additive, changed source bytes, changed
current-consumer protected outputs, or any production-method edit. A veto
invalidates this continuation and does not authorize another admission.

Explanatory diagnostics: row counts, table spans, cell-role inventory, and
arithmetic checks. Arithmetic is explanatory only; it does not certify the
accounts or prove a source error.

Not concluded: authenticity, historical availability, legal incorporation,
audited financial correctness, independent acceptance, or general table
extraction. This is finite source-structure evidence.
