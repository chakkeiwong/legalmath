## Reviewed dependency map and evidence contract

One linear local package: retain the exact diagnostic, calculate separately
bound source and selected-meaning coverage, expose the distinction in the real
consumer, verify against an independently enumerated small fixture and the
retained real product, then record and refresh. No step depends on a favorable
legal verdict. Other authority tasks remain independent after this package.

The engineering question is whether the final consumer identifies all source
and meaning gaps without conflating the dossier with the selected contract.
Primary acceptance is exact accounting of every assembly unit and clause span,
with selected-body unknown characters identified separately from other dossier
sources. The actual consumer must preserve all six answers, every existing
clause/dependency, the construction, and both unresolved incorporation readings.
It must reject missing/duplicate source-accounting identities and invalid
semantic intervals. Reconciliation failure, changed parent evidence or any
answer promotion veto the result. Counts are explanatory only, never legal
acceptance or an automatic selection rule.

The tempting fix is to discard every OTHER_DOSSIER_SOURCE from Q1/Q2. The audit
rejects it: that category includes final-term authority, amendments, comparative
language, financial reports and other relevant evidence. Being outside the
German Option I body does not prove legal irrelevance. The reviewed repair
therefore explains the categories while keeping the substantive uncertainty.
No clause becomes understood merely because a source group has been counted.

Default audit: the four construction categories are inherited bookkeeping, not
legal classifications. Their provenance is the protected construction ledger;
the earliest check compares its complete identity set with graph and assembly.
An unknown-effect clause is an engineering meaning gap, and an UNKNOWN source
role is a disposition gap; both are retained separately, including intersections.
Whitespaces are ignored only for character coverage, following existing clause
coverage, with punctuation retained. Per-unit overlap is counted once and all
nonspace selected characters must reconcile.

Audit verdict: PASS after rejecting the blanket source-exclusion shortcut.
The package can clarify and repair misleading coverage reporting but cannot
complete all German meanings, adjudicate bilingual discrepancies, resolve the
537 construction intervals or replace independent legal review. Those remain
explicit in the result. The strongest false success would be a smaller count
reported as legal progress; equality of the actual answer is a mandatory veto.
