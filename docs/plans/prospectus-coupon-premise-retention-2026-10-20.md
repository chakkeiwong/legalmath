## Reproduced code gap and choice of next work

source citations. It does not return the complete typed premises: a direct Q5
answer omits the actual calendar edition/coverage/holidays/weekend, annual rate,
rounding quantum/rule, and reference-period inputs. Selected-term answers retain
some terms, but still omit the structured calendar. The final answer has request
and product hashes; these bind a retained request but do not expose its premises
in the financial answer. Other named financial profiles already retain inputs.

This is a local input-preservation/reporting defect, not a QuantLib defect.

The matched six-period QuantLib1.38 trial in finance009 already passed and must
not be repeated as new evidence. Its limits remain. The current event-order rule
can refuse a preceding-date payment; that is an explicit support restriction,
not authority to weaken ordering or claim a legal payment rule. Early-payment
support requires a separately source-reviewed domain and remains unevaluated.
