# Follow-up program: resolving the legal interpretation problem

Version 1, 6 October 2026. The immediate objective is to understand the product's
remaining interpretation failure and test a justified intervention. This program
replaces automatic continuation of the proposed S5 sequence as a work-selection
policy. S4 remains the unchanged, verified engineering baseline. This document
does not change its recorded method or claim that a new executor already exists.

## Objective and product question

For a specified legal source edition and question, can the product explain and
compute the consequences of competing interpretations while making every
unresolved source, standard, applicability and evidence dependency explicit?
Where can a stronger source-to-rule claim be independently justified, and where
does the present method lack a way to establish it?

The first detailed question is paragraph 10 fulfillment in the outsourced-
registrar example, under the retained allocation, conduct and outcome proposals.
Duty applicability is a separate subquestion. Paragraph 14/footnote 5 attachment
and a specifically identified Gatecoin case-to-target inference are companion
investigations. They challenge different mechanisms rather than add more copies
of the main example.

Assumption: we are developing the interpretation product, not deciding an actual
client's compliance. No particular client, scheme or assessment period has been
supplied. Case-specific inputs remain variables or explicit absences. Discovering
a need for those inputs does not require asking for private documents to finish
the software research.

The product's proof-or-qualification requirement remains controlling. No human
answer labels, model votes, reviewer approval, synthetic score or formalization
count will qualify a natural-language interpretation. A review can find an error;
agreement cannot prove a legal reading.

## Intended output and evidence contract

At the end of this bounded program, retain one reviewed problem specification,
a complete cross-reference of the current inventory, three diagnostic dossiers,
a decision on one chosen intervention, and a final accept/revise/defer decision.
Implementation is conditional on that decision. The output must tell us what
the product can answer, why it cannot answer more, and whether the tested change
actually addresses the identified cause.

Comparator: the current S4 route with its explicit supplied interpretation and
evidence premises. The main engineering criterion is whether a selected change
removes a reproduced unsupported transformation, omitted dependency, lost
alternative or inability to express the specified conditional question.
Every claim of improvement names the before/after behavior and original issue.

Formal claims need an independently checked proposition whose domain matches
the question. A theorem over proposed clauses establishes consequences of those
clauses. Unless the source-to-clause relation is separately established, that
relation remains qualified. Exact spans, grammatical analyses and plausible
arguments supply traceable proposals; they do not alone prove the unique meaning
of unrestricted legal English.

Promotion vetoes: a hidden source-to-rule assumption; a duty-trigger result
reported as fulfillment; renamed opaque legal judgments; a lost rival; invented
legal thresholds; unsupported authority transfer; missing data treated as false;
or performance on familiar fixtures presented as future-source accuracy.

Continuation vetoes: corrupted baseline, invalid checker, inconsistent target,
unbounded execution or action beyond authorized external scope. A rejected
candidate does not reject the whole research direction. Missing substantive
evidence ends or defers the affected item with a specific reason; it does not
prevent other authorized diagnostic work.

Diagnostics such as test totals, branch totals, execution time and counts of
formulas are explanatory only. Enumerated counterexamples are repair triggers.
A dependency trace can establish a software omission, but cannot certify a legal
interpretation merely because all fields are filled.

## Finite work packages and decision points

### A. Reconstruct the questions and their dependencies

Inputs: the complete S4 reading inventory and obligation register, original
questions where retained, source packets, current rules and actual outputs.

Work:
- Trace each of the 46 original obligations and all 35 readings separately.
  Preserve the 21 alternatives without direct original-obligation links.
- Split each of the 224 requested-premise entries into its actual legal or factual
  questions where necessary. Keep the original text and identity.
- For each proposed shared issue, state which readings depend on it and which
  differences prevent equivalence. Never merge readings by embedding similarity,
  repeated wording or existing category alone.
- Classify dependencies as source interpretation, normative standard,
  authority/applicability, case-transfer warrant, factual input, representation,
  execution, or question mismatch. Multiple dependencies are expected.
- Challenge inherited requests. Record when a request is too broad, demands a
  complete test the sources may not contain, or asks for evidence irrelevant to
  the chosen question.
- Trace the current response from source through question, proposed rule, supplied
  premises and result. Locate the first unsupported or unimplemented step.

Deliverables: a premise-level problem register, a question-to-output table and
a short list of specific discriminating questions. The current cross-reference
is the starting annotation, not the completed premise-level register.

Completion: every original item is accounted for; each claim about a common cause
has a cited local record and a distinguishable failure or explicit unresolved
dependency. Missing client facts have an input specification. No legal closure
is inferred from this administrative completeness.

### B. Investigate three mechanisms, with source arguments

Main dossier: paragraph 10. Separate retained power, exercised conduct, achieved
outcome and responsibility for each subject over time. Compare the outsourced-
registrar defect case, nominal responsibility without supervision, and an unknown
assessment interval. Explain which assumed rule changes the result.

Attachment dossier: paragraph 14/footnote 5. Vary request, object and standard
independently. Keep confirmation, demonstration, third-party verification and
legal opinion separate. Preserve A/B/C where no warranted relation resolves them.

Case dossier: select one precise Gatecoin proposition relevant to a dependency
in the main dossier, or conclude that none is sufficiently relevant. Reinspect
full retained reasons, operative conclusions and relevant qualifications before
any new substantive inference. Identify issue, material facts, speaker/opinion
role, holding or proposed characterization, authority and later-treatment limits.
State exactly why the proposition transfers or why the transfer remains missing.
A quotations-only dossier does not pass this source investigation.

For each dossier, retain an argument supporting each material alternative, the
strongest inspected contrary, the required evidence, and a situation where the
difference changes an answer. Distinguishing scenarios are conditional thought
experiments; they do not supply legal truth labels. If a standard cannot be
reduced to observable facts under the available premises, state that precisely.

Source work begins with retained originals and the earlier source findings.
Use new retrieval only for a named question that an identifiable source might
answer. A broad “find more cases” search is not a task specification. If the
needed material is absent, name it and classify its effect before expanding scope.

Literature is selected by mechanism after diagnosis. The earlier dossier points
to source-linked representation, argumentation and case factors. Reinspect a
specific paper's technical sections and original code before adapting it. Its
published scores, curated answer labels or an attractive engine API do not supply
local evidence of English correctness. No new literature result is claimed by
this program.

Completion: each dossier concludes one of:
- a conditional relation can be stated and checked, with legal premises explicit;
- competing source interpretations remain, with exact distinguishing dependencies;
- a named external source or factual input is required;
- the current mechanism fails on a reproduced counterexample;
- the proposed source/method is irrelevant to the question.

### C. Choose one intervention and test it against the diagnosed cause

Use the dossiers to write a causal hypothesis:
“Because step X loses or assumes Y, the product cannot answer question Q.
Changing Z should produce behavior B under these explicit premises.”

Choose at most one intervention for this program version. Examples are possible
choices, not a preapproved shopping list:

| Diagnosis | Candidate intervention | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Duty and fulfillment are conflated | A question/output contract preserving the distinction | Same input shows the former incorrect association and the corrected qualified response. |
| Distinct normative concepts are collapsed | Separate source-linked propositions and observable/interpretive dependencies | A distinguishing scenario no longer collapses retention, conduct and outcome; unchanged cases preserve their meaning. |
| Attachment or exception is lost | A supported relation representation with source spans and alternatives | Specific altered attachment is detected and consequences differ under stated readings. |
| A needed conditional construct cannot execute | Extend the existing IR narrowly | Minimal failing program, declared semantics, appropriate proof/runtime checks and original-question relevance. |
| Applicable authority or facts are absent | A precise evidence request and conditional result | The result identifies the missing input without fabricating a value or blocking unrelated reasoning. |
| The available method cannot justify English fidelity | Retain qualification and evaluate a specifically motivated research alternative | Explicit testable hypothesis; no automatic conversion of plausibility into legal truth. |

The hypothesis must include the cheapest result that would show the proposed
change unnecessary or insufficient. Compare the unchanged S4 behavior, the
smallest optional repair and only then any more elaborate method. Reuse S4's
execution checks. Do not add recursion, a larger argument engine, a new model,
more search or another wrapper without a concrete dependency that demands it.

Before any code edit, independently verify and freeze S4's method-bound
dependencies. Retain a pre-run plan, exact commands/environment, artifact paths,
criteria, failure conditions and default audit. Run a focused diagnostic before
the required affected regressions. The selected proposal may remain conditional
on unresolved legal premises; proving all legal meaning is not a prerequisite
for a justified engineering repair.

Completion: one intervention is accepted for its stated engineering scope,
rejected, or revised within the bounded repair allowance. If there is no
justified coding intervention, record that decision and complete the program's
diagnostic deliverables. Do not build an executor just to make this planning
exercise look implemented.

### D. Decide the product's next scope

Review the repaired behavior and all original items. Record what changed in the
answer to the main product question, which dependencies remain and whether those
dependencies can be addressed by code, source research, actual case facts or a
different research mechanism.

The final decision must be one of:
- accept the bounded capability and its stated qualifications;
- revise the product question or candidate mechanism with a specific reason;
- defer the affected claim pending a named external input;
- reject the candidate and state what would justify a different experiment.

The program completes at this decision, even if legal disagreements remain.
A new program requires a new causal question and an explicit scope/budget
decision; it is not generated just because the old register remains nonempty.
There is no automatic S6, no target of zero unresolved readings, and no requirement
to produce a Boolean test for every legal standard.

A genuinely later-source observation program is separately justified only after
the method and question stabilize. It needs a real future window, retained
failures and exclusions, and an honest sampling claim. The three development
dossiers here cannot become prospective evidence.

## Effort bounds and refresh rules

These are proposed effort caps for the follow-up, not a renewed live-call grant:
one complete pass over the finite existing inventory; three diagnostic dossiers;
one selected intervention; at most two causal repairs of that intervention; and
one final scope decision. No extra dossier or intervention is added automatically.
A necessary scope expansion must state what new fact warrants it, the proposed
work and the actual resource cost.

Current planning uses existing local evidence and zero new provider calls,
HTTP requests, installations or GPU actions. Historical 100-call and repair grants
are not reused. Any future external work is prepared as a concrete bounded set of
sources/actions and handled under the existing permission policy. Missing inputs
may be recorded without requesting approval to perform ordinary local research.

Between packages, refresh the remaining tasks by recording:
the new finding, its effect on the main question, the hypothesis rejected or
retained, tasks removed as unnecessary, and the next discriminating action.
Keep the objective and completion criteria fixed unless an explicit reason
changes them. A refresh can shorten, defer or terminate work; it must not merely
append tasks. Preserve previous plans and results.

## Assumptions, pre-mortem and skeptical review

| Choice | Provenance and status | Risk | Earliest useful check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start with paragraph 10 | All 35 retained reading statements concern this dispute; reviewed development focus | Overfit a single circular | Two companion mechanisms and an explicit reusable input/output design; no generalization claim. |
| Use existing comparison categories | S1/S4 organizational labels, hypothesis only | Treat different readings as equivalent | Preserve every identity and write the difference before any grouping. |

| Preserve supplied standards as assumptions | Current formal contract | Rename the answer as an input and claim success | Expand the first opaque predicate in each dossier. |
| Three dossiers, one intervention | New convenience budget, not an optimal scientific default | Too little coverage | Report the uncovered dependency and request a justified new scope; do not infer broad success. |
| S4 as execution baseline | Selected checked runtime | Mistake its proof for source interpretation | Trace the exact theorem's assumptions back to the source question. |
| No supplied answer labels | Controlling product requirement | A plausible source reading masquerades as a validated answer | Record source claims, interpretations, deductions and unresolved premises separately. |

Pre-mortem: this program could reproduce the old problem by creating a larger
register and calling that progress. Prevent that by requiring three concrete
source-to-answer traces and a decision that can reject an unnecessary feature.
It could also stop all useful work while waiting for impossible legal certainty.
Prevent that by computing conditional consequences and finishing each item with
a specific disposition.

The strongest alternative explanation for a successful diagnostic is that
author-written rules and examples share the same interpretation error. Independent
runtime agreement does not defeat that explanation. A source argument or exact
counterexample may weaken it; the unresolved bridge must still be reported.

Initial audit verdict: PASS_FOR_BOUNDED_DIAGNOSIS. Wrong target, proxy promotion,
stale baseline, hidden defaults, unfair comparisons, missing stop conditions and
unhelpful artifacts were explicitly reviewed above. No implementation or
scientific promotion verdict is issued before packages A and B select a cause.
