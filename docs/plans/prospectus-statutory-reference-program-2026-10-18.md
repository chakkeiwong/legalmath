## Question, diagnosis and protected comparator

Can the engine distinguish a contractual citation, an identified legal source,
the source's edition and temporal application, the citation's complete or open
scope, and the rule's conditional consequence through its ordinary answer?

The combined021/022 evidence is the behavioral comparator, not a storage donor
or a current-law website. Its47 reviewed links cover48 of60 original records. Twelve records
have no admitted target. These are WpÜG30(2), BGB801(1) sentence1, AktG15,
SchVG5(3) numbers1–8, two SchVG5 et seq. references (one also names18), FATCA,
the tax-haven act/regulations, representative rights/duties/removal, stock-voting
procedure, mandatory statutory priorities, and U.S. tax certification. Further
unqualified defined terms and general discovery remain original obligations.

The inspected implementation explains the boundary. `reviewed_references.admit`
requires selected OPERATIVE targets (or UNKNOWN in v3); another document alone
does not prove a legal edition or scope. A graph of contract occurrences must
not acquire a resolved legal meaning by treating a current statute as another
operative contract clause. `law_facts.assess` already checks explicit legal
bases, instrument/jurisdiction, valid and knowledge dates and supplied premises;
it does not discover or prove the supplied construction. Source-specific
reference validators and replay at consumption protect reviewed finite readings,
but do not establish complete discovery. These are local model/integration gaps;
no package limitation has been demonstrated.

## Evidence contract and research intent

The mechanism under test is an explicit, source-bound representation of legal
citations and their unresolved dimensions, followed by independent reconstruction
and actual ordinary consumption. The expected failure is premature resolution:
a current consolidation, one selected paragraph or a true supplied premise may
hide an unknown edition, open onward citation or missing application condition.

The primary criterion is the complete independently checked source-to-answer
behavior for the declared supported cases. Reconcile all60 original records
losslessly and preserve the47 prior links. New discovery findings extend the
denominator; they do not replace it. Tests, counts, downloaded bytes and timing
are explanatory. A matching hash proves identity, not source interpretation.

Wrong source/edition/scope, hidden unknowns, false closure, a changed unrelated
answer or acceptance of a validly resealed forgery veto promotion and trigger a
named repair. Corrupt comparators, missing required evidence, frozen drift or
exhausted reviewed resources veto that package's continuation. A refusal caused
by genuinely open law is an expected answer and does not stop other ready work.
No actual holder entitlement, statutory applicability, automatic discovery,
unrestricted legal correctness or whole-build completion follows from a finite
source or conditional-model pass.

The retained artifacts are the original citation/source reconciliation, primary
source bytes and acquisition receipts, edition/change-chain review, complete
source-scope readings, independent expected cases, actual products and ordinary
answers saved before verification, adverse receipts, result decision table,
run manifest and output hashes. Missing evidence remains explicitly missing.

## Discriminating controls

Controls must include a statutory citation redirected to contract section5;
a wrong law, section, language or edition; omission of one numbered subitem;
"ff." silently converted to a closed range; a later amendment used at an earlier
date; source acquisition backdated as prior knowledge; missing onward regulations;
an institution exception replaced by residence; a contractual75% threshold used
with the wrong denominator; a notice override ignored; and an unknown mandatory
priority set asserted complete. Preserve each actual refusal or conditional
answer. An upstream source check cannot substitute for a resealed-consumer test.
Do not require every control to run before its representation exists; bind the
specific positive/adverse matrix in the implemented trial's review.
