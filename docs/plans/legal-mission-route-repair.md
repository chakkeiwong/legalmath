## Question and evidence contract

Can the actual product carry independent readings, contrary evidence, source
qualifications and installed-tool results through a complete answer without
silently dropping material or spending a known insufficient call envelope?
The substantive return task is the original P10/evidence comparison. P10/P11,
conduct/outcome, reconciliation/rights, P14 attachment and Gatecoin transfer
distinctions remain explicit legal review obligations. All four questions,

The engineering comparator is the retained rejected fourth inventory response
(25,681 raw / 475,806 expanded bytes) and the actual production constructors.
The 78 partial claims plus twelve historical readings are a labeled hybrid
fixture, never a completed same-run dossier. Installed adapters must execute;
scripted model responses establish plumbing and accounting only.

Primary engineering acceptance requires exact evidence reconstruction, complete
occurrence/pair accounting, staged review with honest scope, question-bound tool
use, and refusal before dispatch when the complete declared workload cannot fit.
Changed sources, ambiguous references, lost rows, unknown dependencies, stale
tool bindings, unresolved dispatches and resource overruns veto acceptance of
the affected stage. A failed candidate triggers repair within this one attempt;
it does not invalidate the legal research. Byte counts and test counts explain
engineering behavior and cannot establish legal truth or tool benefit.

Legal promotion requires completed matched baseline/candidate answers, actual
downstream consideration, source-grounded comparison and contrary-reading review.
No local PASS selects a default, establishes English interpretation correctness,
closes an obligation, or ranks stochastic tools. Live qualification must precede
any such comparison; an absent live qualification is a promotion veto, not a
reason to abandon local implementation.

## Phases and return conditions

1. **Register and preserve.** Record the reviewed revision, snapshots of files
   touched, original ledger prefix and absolute deadline. Count the eighth
   detour at admission, not after success. Prepare fixed commands and narrowly
   scoped rules. Refresh mission state before implementation.
2. **Evidence boundaries.** Add an optional exact shared-evidence encoding and
   separate logical/encoded/raw/expanded bounds. Preflight expansion before
   allocating decoded objects; trust expected hashes from callers, never from
   model output. Measure the actually prepared request and schema separately.
   Keep source-context qualification checks and raw/resolved records.
3. **Independent inventories and source review.** Preserve response limits;
   use separate finite aggregates, occurrence provenance and cross-review
   records. Review assigned claims against the whole qualified source, then
   exact nominated interactions and omissions. The partial-view prompt must
   never assert review of unassigned claim contents. Feed reviewed findings and
   exact record bindings into search, preserving unresolved reductions.
4. **Whole-route admission and attribution.** Account for inventory/context
   passes, search/repairs/criticism, both fidelity stages, two scoped perspectives,
   question assignment, optional-tool actions, dossier review, interaction and
   synthesis. Recompute at transitions. Preserve per-adapter status and bind
   retrieval use to the answer's question/target. No receipt alone proves effect.
5. **Local qualification.** Run focused adversarial tests, retained-response
   regression and production-constructor checks, including real installed tools.
   Retain exact results and unmet requirements; exercise a scripted full route
   where feasible without representing it as legal evaluation. Review actual
   diffs and test results; stop the attempt on a material unresolved flaw.
6. **Live admission decision and substantive return.** Only a qualified complete
   route with an affordable reviewed equal-arm allocation can make live calls.
   If it is not qualified, preserve the candidate and exact blockers, return to
   `product-contribution`, and do not create another repair program automatically.
   Subsequent live work starts with actual-response qualification, then the
   matched P10/evidence pair, then individual-tool/remaining-question evaluation.

Each completed phase writes an immutable result and a next-phase decision.
Restart verifies source/test bindings; it cannot rerun a failed live dispatch or
erase earlier results. Mission refresh follows work-package results. This is
one repair, with internal tests/corrections, not six additional repair grants.

## Skeptical review before implementation

The previous design omitted a feasible whole-core budget and treated input fit
as too close to qualification. This revision corrects both: budget refusal is
an expected possible result and legal promotion remains blocked without real
responses. We must not require future model answers before building the candidate.
Independent source readers remain independent; a shared evidence table deduplicates
bytes, not meanings. Exactly equal claims may coalesce only with reader provenance.
Search summaries remain fallible and cannot replace exact evidence in fidelity.
Core and scoped judgments retain their separate semantics and denominators.

The strongest false-success explanation is faithful serialization followed by
unfaithful legal reduction. The earliest discriminator is an adversarial nonlocal
dependency/contrary-evidence case; the ultimate discriminator is the source-reviewed
matched answer. A source hash, solver result, local full-route fixture or test count
cannot settle that question. The plan is accepted for **one local candidate
implementation and qualification attempt**, with no current live admission or
default promotion. Unfinished boundaries must be recorded as unfinished.
