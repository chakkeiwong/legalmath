## Evidence contract, assumptions and skeptical review

The account profile remains opt-in: 12,000 input tokens for ordinary calls and
typed leaves, 16,384 only for explicit typed two-child account combinations.
The larger bound is a measured local capacity hypothesis, not a claim about
vendor limits. Source bytes, every partial reading, every citation occurrence,
output bounds and the original assignments must survive exact reconstruction.
Use the actual largest retained request and controls close to the candidate cap
to test live schema support, compact input comprehension and conditional
consequences before any acquisition owner resumes. A schema-valid but wrong
control is a failed qualification. A later oversized parent remains blocked;
there is no automatic cap increase or another owner attempt.

Reviewer clarification must not weaken the evidence criterion. ACCEPT means the
delivered text suffices to assess the answer as written. Context needed to judge
that answer belongs in `context_needs` and requires UNRESOLVED. Information needed
only to choose between correctly stated conditional alternatives belongs in the
explanation and answer limitations. Fresh positive/adverse tests discriminate
that distinction; exact old adverse results remain failures.

Promotion vetoes: lost source/assignment, changed successful request, invalid
citation, incorrect conditional consequence, unsupported acceptance, failed
mandatory member, or oversized input. These block their dependent branch.
Continuation vetoes: corrupted shared evidence, unknown outstanding dispatch,
invalid authority, expired deadline or exhausted global budget. Counts, timing
and descriptive model variation are explanatory only; no stochastic ranking is
proposed. Passing synthetic controls does not establish general legal accuracy,
complete accounts, complete answers or any of six tools' matched effects.

Skeptical pre-execution review: PASS for the finite amendment and its local
implementation. It uses the complete settled population instead of the former
two-request proxy; preserves the real failed baseline; separates two independent
failures; forbids hidden limit increases, resampling successes and case certainty
as a condition of accepting a conditional answer. Live execution additionally
requires passing local controls and a recorded adoption review. The main residual
risks are unknown parent sizes, hidden provider framing, semantic loss despite
exact input reconstruction, and unaffordable downstream review. Recompute the
remaining call budget after preparation; the old 4,655-call optimistic floor is
not a funded promise to finish the full matrix.
