## Evidence contract and skeptical audit

Primary admission criterion for an asserted legal-event example: retained original offering document plus inspected primary law/measure/judgment, with exact claim or instrument identity, dates, relevant passages and distinction between contractual terms, legal power, actual intervention, recognition and eventual payment. Controls may be admitted for a source-supported boundary without establishing an adverse intervention. No source-count target overrides these conditions.

Promotion vetoes: missing incorporated terms, wrong issuer/series, wrong law/version/date, inaccessible primary legal source, unreviewed translation, no independent adjudication. These block strong labels, not continued acquisition or retaining a provisional test.
Continuation vetoes: source corruption, changed frozen method/protected history, unsafe retrieval, or 212-request cumulative ceiling. A host challenge triggers an accessible official route or separately labelled mirror, not attempts to defeat access controls.
Repair triggers: issuer-name false match, scan with empty text, unresolved final-terms dependencies, mixed court merits/procedure and regulation effective dates.
Explanatory diagnostics: HTTP success, PDF count, keyword hits, abstention counts, build/test pass and runtime. None alone establish legal correctness, failure rates or superiority.
Not concluded: repayment actually occurred, market-wide loss rates, default engine readiness, or all-jurisdiction purchase permission.
