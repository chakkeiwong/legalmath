## Skeptical review before execution

The successor was directionally appropriate but not executable. Five repairs are
required and adopted here. First, a full claim/candidate matrix mistakes partial
controls for rival whole-document interpretations. Introduce typed questions,
explicit units and true/false meanings; two separately requested assignments may
nominate irrelevant comparisons, but disagreement retains the comparison. Every
excluded pair remains recorded, and controls without candidates remain missing.
Second, a conservative maximum concern count blocked all model batches. Execute
bounded small batches incrementally, preserving every completed and pending pair;
split a failed batch before retrying, with persistent limits. Third, grammar
repair can alter meaning. Preserve the original reading and factual vocabulary,
allow only new expression strings, backtranslate, and require a separate
correspondence judgment. Null formalizations remain unencoded. Fourth, absent
reference adjudicators cannot be replaced by author labels: evaluation will
report source-explicit development cases and unknowns, without method ranking.
Fifth, fetching a current Code or a translated title cannot establish effective
priority or bilingual equivalence. Require anchored provisions and retain unknown
dates, cycles and disputed meaning.

With those changes, the engineering plan passes pre-run review. Legal promotion
and cost-saving claims do not pass. Existing dirty work, prior journals, allowance
history and the other Catala worktree are protected. No package installation,
private bank data, external messages or deployment is authorized by this plan.
