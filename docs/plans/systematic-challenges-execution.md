## Research intent and evidence contract

The question is whether systematic challenges, checked conditional elimination,
different checking mechanisms and bounded complete-investigator processing can
expose omissions that survive repeated model readings. The target is the
interpretation process over new source editions and explicit formal premises.
The comparator is the delivered 933-file F4 snapshot, with 2,097 passing tests,
223/225 UCITS pairs and no prospective observations. Its reports are retained.

Primary engineering acceptance requires an executed complete investigation that
retains every candidate, challenge, response, failed action and missing premise;
rechecks every proposed conditional elimination; binds the source, question,
method and actual stage files; and preserves global and issue spending. Mutation
tests must demonstrate that lost exceptions, altered questions, stale evidence,
missing responses, forged elimination witnesses and inconsistent batches are
detected at the appropriate boundary. Logical tests derive expectations from
declared mathematics, never supplied human legal answers.

A method receives no legal grade. Passing tests, response accounting, agreement,
numbers of readings and fault-detection counts are explanatory diagnostics only.
An actual violated invariant or malformed evidence is a promotion veto and a
repair trigger. Corrupt inputs, unaccounted spending, changed code during a
claimed frozen run, or unavailable required toolchains veto that run. A model
timeout, unresolved legal question, exhausted old issue or unavailable second
language-model family qualifies that work item and does not stop unrelated work.
The remaining natural-language interpretation and universal completeness claims
remain NOT_ESTABLISHED even when this plan's engineering criteria pass.

## Skeptical pre-execution audit

**PASS for bounded implementation after the following corrections to the initial
idea.** An ensemble cannot certify English meaning by voting; additional roles
from one model are not additional independent families; a semantic counterexample
rejects a candidate only relative to a declared formal premise; lexical cues do
not supply that premise; and future-study dates cannot be fabricated. The plan
therefore preserves separate source proposals, formal relations, executed checks
and unresolved premises. It does not promise closure of the fundamental research
claims, a whole-compiler proof or discovery of every applicable law.

Baseline parity requires the same source bytes, questions, candidates and
mathematical mutation definitions for compared checks. First responses do not
see each other's answers. Follow-ups receive the same retained prior round.
Candidate retention and first-response blindness are tested. Descriptive
detection counts never establish superiority or statistical independence.

Pre-mortem: all model responses could be syntactically valid while omitting the
same exception. The earliest discriminating check injects an exception omission
into a declared formal model, preserves the source cue, and checks that its
challenge and logical witness survive the complete dispatcher. This establishes
the mechanism on that declared example; legal source entailment stays separate.
Another failure is a passing helper whose new profile is never invoked by the
product: the acceptance test uses CompleteInvestigation and verifies its dossier.
