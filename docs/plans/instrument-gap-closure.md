# Instrument-specific gap closure: UBS CH1357852636

## Question and protected comparator

Can the retained UBS issue populate a conditional Hong Kong product analysis and
execute its loss-absorption terms, without treating missing notices, legal
authority, calendars or private-client evidence as satisfied? The comparator is
the completed G0–G5 increment: its three real reports retain fourteen bank
obligations but have no populated product premises and no instrument-specific
event calculation. `docs/implementation/instrument-gap-closure/baseline.zip`
preserves the dirty working baseline; a Git commit alone is not that baseline.

The candidate uses the 24 June 2024 issue in Annex A of the retained published
terms. The same PDF also contains the July reopening. They must not be combined
silently. Literal terms, proposed legal interpretations, contractual acts and
actual client facts have different evidence roles. No human answer label is a
quality criterion. A contractually required FINMA notice or adviser determination
is an event to evidence, not an interpretation-quality oracle.

## Evidence contract and intent ledger

Promotion requires source/hash/page binding, executable source-dependent product
premises, exact arithmetic, correct date/event distinctions, explicit unresolved
dependencies and complete retention of the existing bank obligations. Independent
equations, counterexamples and real RuleIR/Java and Catala executions check the
declared formal relations. Neither backend agreement, quotation matching nor a
test count proves that an interpretation is legally correct.

Expected failures include absent market/action history, unavailable calendars,
unissued notices, source anomalies, ambiguous rounding and missing private
facts. They should qualify the affected calculation. A missing input must not
be interpreted as a negative event. Positive settlement is never inferred from
a deadline or from a ratio breach alone.

Promotion vetoes and repair triggers: a substituted issue, stale receipt, omitted
bank layer, wrong arithmetic/deadline, invented event, silently corrected source,
or source-dependent premise presented as independently established legal truth.
Retain a failed attempt, repair its cause and rerun the smallest relevant check
before dependent phases. Continuation vetoes: corrupt required sources, an
unavailable necessary tool or an unauthorized action. A failed candidate is not
evidence against the whole research direction; later independent work continues.

Diagnostic roles: source identity and invariant failures veto promotion; mutation
survival triggers repair; model/execution agreement is an engineering criterion
only; test counts, runtime and filled-premise counts are explanatory. No stochastic
ranking, universal future-law guarantee or legal clearance will be concluded.
Artifacts live in `docs/implementation/instrument-gap-closure/`.

## Skeptical review before implementation

The tempting plan to mark every prospectus observation known and run the old
generic loss engine fails: source proposals are not verified legal facts, a
breach is not the defined Trigger Event, the two calendars differ, viability
uses different deadline anchors, and the printed price formula has an unresolved
anomaly. This revised plan explicitly separates those matters. It also avoids
claiming complete Condition 8 implementation where contractual discretion,
unavailable market inputs or a source ambiguity prevents a unique calculation.

The source packet will account for every selected dependency, including those
not executable. Synthetic events are labelled and actual-document results keep
unknown events unknown. No criterion rewards a larger count of apparent passes.
The plan passes this bounded audit. The first meaningful diagnostic is source
binding plus the seven-percent equality/add-back and Friday viability examples;
a full native run follows only once those distinctions work.

## Remaining-gap dispositions

This increment closes specific implementation gaps, not every jurisdiction's
law. Known engineering remedies are implemented for one issue first. Additional
instrument families require their own retained terms and formalizations; the
schema rejects unsupported profiles. Currentness requires dated source history,
not a successful fetch. Actual mandates/account/booking/counterparty data require
private evidence; no task completion can stand in for it. Large-source transport
already preserves the complete request, while real-model comprehension still
lacks authorized fresh evidence. Universal English-law equivalence and behavior
under unknowable future laws remain research limits; the appropriate output is
a qualified result and a mechanism for reassessment.
