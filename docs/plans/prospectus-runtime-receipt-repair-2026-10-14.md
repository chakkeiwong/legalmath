observe every process in the kernel. Direct boundary-test children from003 are
already retained. Any new tool that launches its own explicit Python subprocess
must share the ledger. No native-boundary retest or unchanged OCR repeat is used
as a promotion shortcut. The one OCR repeat exercises the changed public receipt.
