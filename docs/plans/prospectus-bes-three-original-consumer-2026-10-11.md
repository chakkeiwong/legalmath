# BES three-original conditional consumer, 11 October 2026

## Research question and comparator

Can the actual prospectus service add the three retrieved issuer annual reports
to the eight-document BES source-chain request without changing its previous
source occurrences, supplied meanings or unresolved legal questions? The
comparator is the sealed
`bes-onward-sources/attempt-002/request.json` and its retained report. The new
inputs are the exact 2008, 2009 and 2010 issuer English PDFs reviewed in
`bes-original-acquisition/SOURCE-REVIEW.md`. English reports are translations;
Portuguese originals are retained for comparison but not substituted silently.

This is a **partial original candidate** phase. It does not claim all fourteen
dependencies are resolved. The exact dates when these annual reports became
public and historically known are not established. The 2008 and 2009 selected
audit pages need image-bound corrections for three OCR accent errors and do not
establish full-document coverage. Source authenticity is not independently
established. Do not give these candidates operative legal status or infer
entitlement, eligibility or agreement contents.

The wrong baseline would be the standalone three-report source graph: that
cannot show the eight prior documents surviving. The wrong promotion metric
would be an unchanged Q1/Q2 status or extra OCR text: both can coexist with
missing legal evidence. Retain the exact old report as comparator and verify
its manifest. The source graph presently emits native headers while omitting
image bodies; the reviewed `hybrid_profile` restores six specified original
pages but cannot discover all mixed pages. Preserve both native and OCR layers
with explicit duplicate disposition. The 2010 page 191 Audit Committee report
is not relabelled a second audit opinion.

The baseline request's graph and assembly hashes must change when documents
are added. Rebind only those declarative hashes after deriving the new graph
and assembly from the same old correction, layout, operation and annotation
packets. Compare every substantive packet field before and after. Hash rebinding
must not become a shortcut for changing interpretations. Keep the fourteen
historical dependency IDs and statuses, updating candidate metadata only for
BES-DEP-06/07/09. Adverse controls use the same consumer boundary.

First run the 11-document P1 preflight under 240 seconds; inspect its outputs
and compare the old source-unit prefix exactly. If it passes, run the full
consumer once under 1,200 seconds; compare the matched omission/source-hash
effects and prior 14 unresolved dependencies. No unchanged failed retry. The
preflight is a diagnostic, not legal or phase completion. Retain every failed
attempt. Actual CPU mode uses `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`; no GPU is required.

Audit verdict: PASS for conditional source-consumer work. The comparator,
invariants, resource ceiling, earliest failure check and nonclaims are explicit.
The strongest uncertainty is missing historical document dates and independent
authenticity; those are source-admission limits rather than reasons to skip
conditional analysis. The active R4 controller method remains untouched by
this separate source package.
