# Evidence-dependent follow-up of the retained 697 P14 decisions

This implements D1/D5 of the deployment amendment. The exhaustive ledger is
`artifacts/legal-mission-control/2026-10-23/p14-decisions/decisions.json` and retains
each original assignment, complete decision, source/answer hash and exact review
request/result references. The 672 unresolved decisions contain 600 with a source
request (566 solely source requests), 33 with only client/authority requests, and
39 with no typed need. There are 410 distinct full rationales across the 697
decisions. Shared prose does not establish equivalent legal issues.

The 25 rejected decisions have 43 issue occurrences. Fifteen explain why an
additional dependency is unnecessary for the answer actually written; nine
identify inaccurate descriptions of available evidence; one says UNRESOLVED in
its reason despite a REJECT label. The nine identify four connected corrections:
the delivered non-bearer issuance expectation, the optional burning/re-issuance
example, the delivered intermediary network-risk discussion, and completeness of
retained Appendix text. Completeness does not prove extraction fidelity,
applicability, ownership, actual risk mitigation, or legal sufficiency.

## Mechanisms and evidence contract

1. Retain every original result and identify what each decision actually reviews:
   source identity, necessity of a proposed dependency, or a statement in the
   answer. A valid response schema alone cannot resolve disagreement between a
   verdict label and its reasons. Do not relabel decisions by a keyword rule.
2. Continue each unresolved assignment once with its unchanged original answer,
   complete assignment/origin readings, prior decision and jointly delivered
   requested sources. Include actual answer citations as source context. For the
   specifically enumerated requests for the missing assignment crosswalk,
   deliver the repaired explicit mapping as implementation evidence; do not
   search the legal corpus for schema keys. Preserve the original request.
3. Record an explicit disposition for each previous context need: still required,
   supplied by delivered source, mapping clarified, or unnecessary for the
   conditional answer. The last disposition must quote the actual answer and
   explain why it requires no unconditional client finding. Missing client facts
   cannot be marked supplied by source text. Any still-required need vetoes
   ACCEPT. All source support, numbered assertions and verdict invariants retain
   their existing strict checks. A missing-source failure cannot be discarded.
4. Query-based source selection must filter to the declared question scope and
   retain excluded hits in a receipt. An explicit out-of-scope source request
   remains a real scope block. A query with no in-scope hits remains unresolved.
   Retrieval relevance is assessed by the reviewer, never by the ranking alone.
5. Assess all 43 adverse issue occurrences through the existing issue-specific
   warrant path. A criticism of an unnecessary dependency may be UNWARRANTED as
   a criticism of the answer, with reasoning. Correct the answer only for
   supported warranted defects; retain unresolved issues independently. Apply
   the existing single correction round and re-review the resulting answer.

The baseline is the exact retained answer and review population. Engineering
criteria are complete target/need coverage, exact import, no fabricated facts,
no lost constraints, and working production wiring. Legal criteria are the
existing scoped acceptance requirements applied to the actual answer. A model's
new ACCEPT is review evidence, not a guarantee of legal correctness. The legacy
ID ambiguity remains a limitation of old decisions, including favorable ones;
their preservation does not prove their semantic assignment was correct.

## Dependencies, finite recovery and review

Source-context follow-ups, need-necessity assessments and adverse-issue warrants
are independent after exact answer/assignment import. A failed source selection
does not stop other assignments or issue warrants. The correction requires at
least one supported warrant; final acceptance requires all required reviews and
the existing acceptance checks. The common provider ledger serializes live calls.
Every unresolved original assignment receives at most one new follow-up; every
old failed assignment receives at most one contract continuation. Provider
replacements consume the cumulative 288 reserve and the unchanged 8,100 global
ceiling, deadline and exact-request limit. No repetition until a favorable verdict.

Skeptical review: PASS for implementation and bounded local testing. The earlier
automatic bridge treated all typed needs as legal retrieval requests and preserved
every client need as a permanent blocker even when the question was conditional.
The repair replaces those assumptions with explicit reviewed need dispositions,
not blanket acceptance. Adversarial tests must retain a truly missing fact or
authority, reject an unsupported waiver of a need, reject an invented answer
quote, and preserve an adverse source finding. Exact source scope is retained;
only incidental query hits are filtered. Format failures remain failures. Live
promotion requires a valid actual contract canary and authenticated service
availability; failures leave independent local analysis and other owners ready.

New source-extraction or current-law acquisition is not presumed authorized by
these counts. Classify any remaining exact public-source need before dispatching
additional acquisition. Actual client conduct, correspondence, satisfaction,
ownership and report scope remain explicitly conditional where unavailable.

Resumption audit: the mapping-only subset is 95 occurrences across 11 exact
queries, not the earlier exploratory keyword count of 115/31. The latter included
20 substantive source requests and must not govern routing. Exact needs are
enumerated and hashed in the follow-up checkpoint. The one request combining a
missing assignment with operative provisions keeps its source requirements.
The new continuation also covers the 33 solely factual/authority decisions and
39 untyped decisions. An untyped decision receives an explicit review-question
disposition, not an invented source or factual request. Every continuation keeps
the original decision, exact assignment and answer. Every source-selection
failure stays visible without preventing independent continuations or warrants.

The retained REJECT rationales also reveal wrong-assignment reasoning: several
staffing rationales were attached to footnote/request-trigger dependencies. The
new warrant request therefore includes the actual assignment and its original
readings, and asks explicitly whether each issue criticizes the answer or merely
an unnecessary dependency. Preserve the label and reasoning as historical
evidence; do not move them between assignments. Assess existing adverse issues
before the large unresolved queue, caching each warrant by its exact answer,
decision and assignment content. Newly adverse context results get their own
assessment. A corrected answer then receives the complete fresh review already
required by the product; reused old ACCEPT decisions do not validate that answer.
Qualify the first actual context-disposition contract before its bulk queue.
An invalid contract withholds that queue while independent warrants and account
work continue. This is a format prerequisite, not a demand for an ACCEPT verdict.

Final pre-deployment audit found one incorrectly typed authority need:

Audit verdict: PASS for this narrow correction, conditional on tests rejecting
changed decisions, unreviewed authority waivers and out-of-scope records, and on
measuring the changed actual request. Source selection for the other 671 requests
is unchanged, but the explicit instruction changes their request hashes; repeat
the exhaustive preparation and measurement for final deployment evidence. Deployment still
requires the reviewed registration and complete local result. This is an
engineering remedy to a reproduced routing error, not a legal acceptance.
