## Question and inspected mechanism

The claimed target is verification of the complete conditional answer against
its pinned ordinary answer and profile. The independent certificate checks the
Boolean decisions and several scope fields. Inspection of
`basf_default_f_current.verify_answer` and
`default_f_certificates.certificate` shows that neither explicitly checks all
review metadata: unresolved-status text/evidence, reviewer attribution,
products/ordinary-answer identity labels and explanatory temporal/causal text.
The expected certificate context is reconstructed from the supplied review.
Consequently a newly generated certificate might bless changed labels even
though the original decisions are correct. A hash proves identity only against
an independently supplied expected identity; recalculating it is no authority.

This is an implementation hypothesis pending reproduction. It says nothing
about a third-party package or the legal exhaustiveness of the three readings.
