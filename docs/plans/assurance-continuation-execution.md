## Question and evidence contract

Can the implementation process the whole selected source under bounded response
capacity, retain competing questions and readings, and check explicitly scoped
formal propositions without silently dropping work or converting missing
evidence into certainty?

The exact engineering baseline is the source tree bound by
`artifacts/assurance-successor/2026-09-28/final-report.json`. The comparison for
scheduling is the existing batch-first policy on the same deterministic workload
and action budget. The quotation comparator is the existing literal-quotation
validator, applied to identical Unicode source strings. Old frozen conditional
cases remain descriptive; they are not legal answer keys.

Acceptance requires preservation of source units, question identities, reading
revisions, rejected responses and spent actions; rejection of detached evidence;
executed integrated processing on structurally different challenges; and actual
Lean/Java/Catala checks for the supported semantic extension. All required
stages must execute or have an explicit limitation. Compilation, test counts,
model agreement, coverage percentages and speed cannot establish English
correctness or future legal accuracy.

Promotion vetoes: a lost obligation, changed question treated as identical,
accepted foreign source span, same-round peer leakage, replenished issue limit,
false proof or executable mismatch. These trigger engineering repair. A contrary
proposed interpretation retains its competing explanation and triggers the next
bounded investigation; it is not a reason to abandon unrelated implementation.
Continuation vetoes for the affected action: corrupt evidence, active concurrent
writer on owned paths, unsafe executable input, unavailable necessary tool/source,
or exhausted authorized global/issue/time limit. No automatic retry bypasses a
veto. Missing source meaning produces a qualification, not a human quality gate.

implementation exists; `status` reports the exact accepted receipts. The runner
executes tests/checks rather than granting completion from a checklist. Each
attempt records source hashes, command, environment, elapsed time, output and
decision. A failed phase preserves its receipt and refreshes a concrete repair
task. Up to three attempts per phase; later retries require a documented change
and a passing focused reproducer. Successful predecessors are rechecked against
their recorded relevant inputs. A final full regression follows integration.

fabricating a failed command. It consumes the same three-attempt limit. The
application checks use `.venv`; manuscript checks use the separately probed
`LEGALMATH_DOC_PY` interpreter, or the recorded `python3` document environment,
