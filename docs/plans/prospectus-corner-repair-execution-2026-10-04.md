Phases: prepare, diagnose, test, intake, legal, fetch, compare, finalize, verify.

NEXT-PHASE.md under docs/implementation/prospectus-corner-repair-2026-10-04/.

a reviewable patch; the original method stays unchanged.

1. diagnose: inspect original segments and exact cancellation/exception sources.
   Implement the smallest action/segmentation repair; test focused controls.

   the three BES dependency records; retain unresolved financial-report and
   precedence requirements.
3. legal: implement and exercise typed source-bound rules for the retained legal

   receipts and use a new index. Refresh source conclusions without changing old

5. compare: rerun identical original offerings/probes and prior controls against

6. finalize: write result/decision/uncertainty tables, patch, manifest and reset

7. verify: check original archive and candidate receipts/results/report bindings.
