Can the product preserve all 35 distinct retained readings, their 70 role records,
source warrants, objections, qualifications and 46 open obligations while actually
evaluating the available structured criticism in CompleteInvestigation?

The comparator is P8 attempt-2: 35 legacy identities retained, seven separate

00787202085da9ee4942a34bf87252a9be5192fe1333075b843edede74bb788d.
Inputs are P0 attempt-2 packet, overlay and obligation map. Preserve their bytes.

Engineering pass requires individual source-bound reconciliation for every reading,
lossless retention of role evidence and original formulas, no unauthorized merges,
and a real CompleteInvestigation run whose criticism, spans, attacks, extension
membership and branch receipts are verified from original inputs. Faults in a
quote, record hash, relation, criticism or receipt must fail verification.
Preferred theories above the existing eight-argument limit remain incomplete.
No source interpretation, duty standard, court authority, legal correctness,
English fidelity or release readiness is established by this pass.

Quote checks, identity checks, Java agreement and tests are engineering criteria.
Invalid provenance, missing candidates, unexplained formula changes and false
completion are continuation vetoes. Unknown interpretation, missing authority and
unformalized readings are repair triggers and promotion vetoes, not continuation
vetoes. Counts and pilot overlaps are explanatory only.

## Skeptical audit and revisions

1. Wrong baseline: HEAD has unrelated changes and earlier modifications. Bind
   the actual pre-edit worktree and selected immutable completion, not HEAD alone.
2. The 70 records contain only 12 non-null candidate repairs; related_readings is
   empty throughout. A missing repair is not an empty legal theory. Preserve it
   explicitly and use the original distinction/citations plus operative questions.
3. Several readings share local_id. Use retained candidate identities; never merge
   by local_id, matching formulas, family membership or shared citations.
4. All 35 are paragraph-10 responsibility proposals. Mapping to the allocation,
   conduct, adequacy and combined pilots is a declared partial comparison, never
   legal equivalence. Ambiguous discharge proposals remain explicitly ambiguous;
   no invented mapping to demonstration A/B/C.
5. Raw role criticism is prose, not a signed contrary relation. Preserve each
   objection as an open question. Only the existing typed criticism schema can
   supply checked UNDERMINE/REBUT/UNDERCUT edges. Model premise statuses and
   preferences stay conditional proposals, not established legal premises.
6. Product criticism already exists in core/criticism.json but is not passed to
   semantics. Pass it through the actual stage, bind it in the journal, verify
   against that core file, and invalidate prior method/window identities.
7. Historical packets have no locator field. Use the original unit ID as the
   explicit fallback locator and keep metadata unresolved.
8. Branch receipts currently receive every extension ID, regardless of argument
   membership. Report candidate membership separately and attach only extensions
   containing a supporting argument; conditional formulas remain executable for
   all retained branches regardless of argumentative acceptance.
9. More source premises increase graph size. Keep explicit limits, no silent
   pruning or extension-cap increases. Cycles and resource limits are incomplete.
10. No keyword classifier, inferred authority hierarchy, reviewer vote, live
    provider grant, or fabricated future-source observation is justified here.

Audit verdict: PASS_FOR_BOUNDED_IMPLEMENTATION after these revisions. The plan
answers a preservation and integration question; it does not close legal meaning.

## Executable phases

1. Freeze pre-edit code/test hashes, git commit/status, selected input hashes.
2. Add a source-span binder and per-reading reconciliation. Store exact role
   records and proposed clause comparisons; keep original and operative texts
   separate. Add each assumption/question and cited warrant to the semantic branch.
3. Adapt checked typed criticism to the finite argument evaluator, preserving
   support parents, attack targets, conditional premises and ignored proposed
   preferences/cases. Expose missing coverage and unfinished relations. Do not
   prune candidates or manufacture signed attacks from questions.
4. Integrate the new semantic profile and bind core criticism through run/verify,
   stage journal and observation verification. Keep the v1 qualification adapter
   and unrelated product paths untouched.
5. Run focused preservation, contradiction, stale-source, missing-receipt and
   cutoff tests, plus existing semantic/integration regressions. Execute the 35
   retained branches and a CompleteInvestigation fixture with a real objection.
6. Write an immutable result, preservation audit, readable execution note and
   refreshed next-phase plan. Re-run only after a repair or failed check.

Commands use existing approved forms, with no shell substitution:
`python3 -m scripts.run_legal_interpretation_successor baseline`
