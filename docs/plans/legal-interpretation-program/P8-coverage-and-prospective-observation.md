## Discriminating challenges

Pilot success becomes all-English coverage; unencoded readings disappear; failed observations are excluded; future data are retrospectively selected; an amended method reuses an old prospective label.

## Acceptance and failure decisions

All original obligations have traceable honest dispositions, unsupported work is retained, and future-window admission/invalidation works. Actual future evidence is reported only when it exists; no legal-accuracy or superiority claim follows from counts.

Coverage omission triggers repair; contamination invalidates that prospective observation and records the reason. A missing future source leaves only that component pending while local development continues.
