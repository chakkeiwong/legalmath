# Qualification-aware refusal for the BES interim-review profile

## Question and evidence contract

Question: does the current source-report consumer preserve an explicit Scope
qualification and refuse a truncated or structurally changed interim-review
reading without promoting it to a complete finding?

Comparator: the retained exact BES pp172–173 reading and the accepted
LIMITED_FINANCIAL_REVIEW profile from BES-DEP-05 attempt-002. The positive
comparator must remain CONDITIONAL_SUPPLIED_READING; the two changed source
variants must remain INCOMPLETE_SOURCE_READING.

Primary criterion: both variants are rejected end to end by the current public
consumer, while the exact comparator preserves the prior report category,
moderate assurance, negative-assurance conclusion, closing date, and all six
instrument questions, dependencies, and assembly outputs.

Veto diagnostics: changed source hashes, changed protected outputs, a complete
finding for either incomplete variant, missing qualification text, or any
unrelated production-method edit. A veto invalidates this continuation and
does not justify a fourth admission.

Explanatory diagnostics: the exact unresolved field list, the structural
failure status, and the retained source spans. These explain refusal but do
not establish legal truth, authenticity, financial accuracy, or package
generality.

Not concluded: the report is authentic, historically available, legally
incorporated, independently accepted, or representative of all assurance
reports. A passing refusal check only establishes this reviewed local profile.

## Execution

Use the final method and retained source fixtures from attempt-002. Run a
focused pytest subset plus a direct public-consumer replay for the exact,
truncated-Scope, and extra-Scope-qualification variants. Pin imports to the
attempt method, CPU only, 120-second timeout, and a 20 MiB evidence cap. Do
not install, call the network, edit shared production code, or rerun prior
successful stages. Retain the commands, hashes, result, and decision table in
attempt-003, then record and refresh the authority.

## Skeptical audit

This is a distinct discriminating check because attempt-002 established the
classification repair but retained two incomplete-source variants without an
end-to-end continuation record. The baseline is the exact accepted profile,
not a synthetic pass count. The run can fail because of fixture drift or a
consumer wiring defect; the focused comparator and variant checks distinguish
those causes. One extra admission is sufficient and the stop condition is
reached after this attempt regardless of outcome.
