## Substantive question and evidence contract

Can the actual P5 and final consumer calculate the source-selected scheduled
principal alongside all ten Shell coupons, preserving the conditions that make
those amounts payable? Current011 calculates all coupons and explicitly excludes
redemption. Final Terms paragraph21 specifies GBP1000 per Calculation Amount.
Base Condition4(a) makes scheduled redemption conditional on no previous
redemption or purchase and cancellation. Conditions5(a)–(c) govern payment,
presentation, global-holder capacity and payment days. Condition3(c) separately
addresses improperly withheld or refused principal. Arithmetic alone cannot
establish these premises.

The comparator is each complete sealed011 ordinary answer and a fresh baseline
on any newly extended hypothesis document. Original PDF units must be identical
to finance002; their original bytes and relevant page images are checked.
The new optional calculation contains the original full coupon request unchanged
and a separately evidenced maturity packet. Its contract profile binds complete
selected source groups, distinguishing paragraph21 from paragraph22 even though
both print GBP1000. The final amount and calculation unit are derived from the
selected text. Restrict this first profile to the supplied GBP Shell2030 terms,
annual aligned maturity and integral calculation amounts.

Principal entitlement is separately declared per account: a coupon entitlement
flag must not silently become entitlement to principal. Unknown or false required
payment hypotheses refuse this explicitly conditional route. Retain the entire
validated input. Principal and coupon payments remain separate cash-flow rows,
as required by Condition5(b), with exact totals per declared legal recipient.
Global mode still means one aggregate recipient; beneficial ownership and
clearing-system allocation are outside this calculation.

Primary criterion: source-derived principal, all coupon rows, independently
computed payment dates and integer amounts match through real P5/consume and an
isolated offline installation. Nested coupon results and legacy complete answers
must be unchanged; unrelated question answers must be unchanged relative to the
same-input baseline. Withheld entitlement, altered source selection, early versus
final redemption, missing qualifiers, wrong dates/events, fabricated assumptions,
over-issue holdings and rehashed corrupt outputs must not pass validation.

Promotion vetoes are wrong source/amount/date/recipient, missing condition,
unjustified equality of coupon and principal entitlement, lost original answer,
accepted malformed input or changed installed result/import origin. A valid
candidate failure triggers diagnosis and a reviewed changed repair; it does not
reject the direction. Shared continuation vetoes are invalid authority, corrupted
common evidence or exhausted cumulative allowance. Time, storage and test counts
are explanatory. Passing establishes this conditional source-to-answer behavior,
not actual payment, statutory applicability, default recovery, full settlement,
arbitrary prospectus interpretation or whole-task/build completion.
