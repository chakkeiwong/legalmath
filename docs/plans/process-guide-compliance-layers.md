# Bring the illustrated process guide up to the transaction implementation

Completed 29 September 2026. The standalone guide contains 18 pages and 20
flowcharts; the monograph has 337 pages and the companion 78. The
[execution review](../monograph/review/process-layers/review.md) records source
preservation, all-page rendering inspection and 32 checked navigation links.

## Question and scope

The user wants the complete two-CoCo example illustrated and all current layers
represented in the process guide. The guide must connect retained documents,
proposed legal meaning, a shared formal model, translation, execution, the seven
transaction assessment categories, and reassessment after changes. Readers know
what a bank transaction is but need QIB, Regulation S, RuleIR, Catala and the
proof tools introduced before those terms carry an argument.

The immediate baseline is the current dirty checkout, including the 13-page
guide and the completed paired prospectus example. Preserve that source and PDF
under `docs/monograph/review/process-layers/baseline/` with SHA-256 identities.
Do not overwrite or reinterpret the historical campaign records. Existing
chapters, mathematical displays, labels and citation occurrences must survive.

## Skeptical audit before execution

The old guide has three material defects: a RuleIR-first diagram contradicts
the shared frontend; the bank transaction categories and lifecycle are largely
absent; an old human-reference evaluation proposal appears in the current path.
Revise these before adding the case charts. An attractive diagram preserving
those defects would fail the task even if LaTeX compiled.

The exact implementation baseline is `transaction/catalog.py`, `engine.py`,
`native.py`, `lifecycle.py`, the integrated implementation guide and the retained
CoCo attempt-002 results. Fourteen obligations cover seven categories; five
have conditional models and nine retain missing families or coverage. This is
neither fourteen implemented laws nor five cleared obligations. The separate
purchaser-route calculation is attached to the bank assessment; it does not
close the broad `product.other` obligation. The issuer-law survey is not a
complete executable foreign-law discovery system.

Both offering dates and the synthetic investor must remain explicit. The pair
compares original purchaser conditions, not simultaneous live offers, regulatory
approval, suitability or principal protection. A US bank is not automatically
the beneficial purchaser; its identity does not establish its client's QIB or
Regulation S status. Both overall receipts remain qualified. Missing inputs,
unknown applicability, unsupported targets and invalidated results must have
visible paths. No diagram may invent an implemented execution-authority path.

Formal equivalence, independent evaluation, native replay, mutation sensitivity
and source hashes have separate meanings. None is an English-law oracle.
Human labels, consensus and bank approval cannot establish product quality.
Institutional authorizations can still be factual inputs to a legal condition.
The current requirement is proof of the named proposition or explicit
qualification. New unknown documents require a frozen-method observation;
the current examples do not establish future generalization.

The plan passes with those corrections. No new legal research, model calls,
compiler benchmark or empirical ranking is needed. The existing deterministic
results are being explained, not rerun or promoted. PDF geometry and link checks
are document acceptance criteria only, not legal or human-readability metrics.

## Execution and evidence contract

2. Revise the overview and shared-model/target diagrams. Add readable charts for
   all seven categories and the actual paired case, including issuer law,
   account/beneficial-owner scope, different purchaser results and common gaps.
3. Replace the outdated final human-quality route with qualified delivery,
   lifecycle invalidation, proof-or-qualification evaluation and the explicit
   repair/refresh phase loop. Preserve the separate Stipula research route and
   bounded interpretation-search explanations.
4. Compile the monograph and companion and export the standalone guide using
   `python3 scripts/build_reader_facing_monograph.py`. Update navigation and
   README counts. Inspect every changed guide page at readable resolution.
5. Record preservation, source-to-diagram checks, final page counts, link
   destinations, build diagnostics and remaining qualifications in
   `docs/monograph/review/process-layers/review.md` and `RESET-MEMO.md`.

Primary pass criterion: a reader can trace the same investor through both
prospectuses, all seven assessment categories, both executable targets, the
independent formal checks and the qualified final results without treating a
conditional pass as transaction permission. Each implemented/conditional/
missing/research distinction must match code and retained evidence.

Vetoes: lost prior scientific content, false architecture, hidden unresolved
layers, human quality authority, fabricated bank facts, wrong legal-time scope,
undefined references, clipping or unreadable diagrams. Repair these and rebuild;
do not accept a PDF merely because a command exits successfully. Stop only for
an unresolved source/implementation contradiction or an unavailable necessary
build dependency. Page count is a layout consequence, not a success metric.

No claim of complete legal interpretation, all-law coverage, production
clearance, backend superiority or future-document accuracy follows from this
documentation change. No human quality feedback or labels are requested.
