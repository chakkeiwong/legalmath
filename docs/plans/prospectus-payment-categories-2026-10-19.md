## Source problem and intended answer

The selected German terms expressly expand references to principal and interest
in section4(5), without putting those names in quotation marks. Principal
includes, where applicable, the redemption amount, early redemption amount,
call redemption amount, premiums and other amounts payable on or in respect of
the notes. Interest includes applicable additional amounts payable under
section7. That section retains its German-tax limitation, legally required
withholding, net-amount purpose, five exceptions, and the separate exclusion of
Abgeltungssteuer and its solidarity surcharge. Section9(1)(a) concerns failure
to pay principal or interest within30days of the relevant due date.

The actual code already retains these source readings in `basf_payment_terms`.
However, `payment_deadline.analyze` still takes `qualified_note_payment` as a
supplied Boolean/unknown/conflict premise; it does not derive a qualifying route
from the unquoted definitions. This is an integration gap in our program, not
evidence of a defective external package. Original records U02-capital and
U03-interest and all other62 definition records must remain visible.

The next optional profile will explain whether a specified hypothetical payment
qualifies through one of those express inclusions, and pass that result into the
existing30-day calculation. The result is conditional on the source reading,
the payment's classification, applicability and timing. A failed inclusion
route alone cannot prove that no principal or interest is payable: a separate
`other_qualifying_note_payment` premise remains UNKNOWN unless explicitly
stipulated. The five principal inclusions and the section7 interest inclusion
are overlapping routes, not disjoint monetary buckets. Do not sum them, create
a payment obligation by relabeling it, or infer actual tax law from the contract.
Each scenario identifies one payment obligation and, for section7, one specified
tax or charge. Aggregation across taxes, amounts and tax-on-tax calculations is
outside this trial; their qualifications cannot be combined without further rules.
