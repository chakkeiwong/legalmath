## Question, target and evidence contract

The question is whether BM25, LegalRuleML, PyArg, Carneades, clingo and
restricted Logical English cause a **source-supported change in a complete
answer** to the original legal questions. Compare the existing product with
the optional backend off (baseline) and on (candidate), using the same
qualified sources, questions, review effort and model method. The first
matched live target is P10/evidence; P14 and Gatecoin require their own
complete source and reading populations before claiming four-question
coverage. Individual-tool controls follow a qualified combined pair.

The primary promotion criterion is a source-reviewed answer change attributable
to an applicable tool, with contrary readings and missing premises preserved.
Unequal inputs, an incomplete inventory, unsupported legal premise, wrong-
question retrieval, omitted interaction, invalid actual response or
unaffordable route veto promotion. A missing source, corrupt artifact,
unrepresentable dependency, exhausted authorization or insufficient whole-
route reservation vetoes continuation of the **live branch**. A candidate
failure alone triggers the next stated diagnosis, not rejection of the
research direction. Byte fit, retrieval rank, graph agreement, test totals,
call counts and latency explain behavior; none alone promotes legal quality.
Even a passing pair will not establish general English correctness, current
law, a superior stochastic method, default readiness or completion of the
other questions.

## Assumption and pre-mortem audit

| Choice and provenance | Status | How it could mislead | Earliest discriminating check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55 pilot units from qualified source context | Pilot source identity | Missing Gatecoin and question-specific source population | Per-question source ledger before any four-question claim |
| 78 claims/12 readings from hybrid fixture | Convenience baseline | Complete readers may produce more claims or readings | Independent inventories and population recount |
| 64/32 and 64/48 from retained input screens | Hypotheses only | Responses may truncate, omit rivals or miss the deadline | Bounded actual response/output/latency screen under a reviewed method |
| Zero repairs/one context pass in arithmetic | Optimistic screen | A repair or repeat can defeat the allocation | Explicit reserve for each permitted repair and repeated constructor |
| Existing 200-action journal | Hard candidate boundary | Nine unprepared requests may recur | Candidate-only whole-route action bound before dispatch |
| Baseline first, same model and review | Descriptive paired design | Time/provider changes or unequal sources confound effect | Frozen input hashes and matched response manifests |

The cheapest false-success check is to ask whether a syntactically complete
answer states who held which right, under which terms, at which time, and why
the cited source supports that conclusion. A 64-pair batch that fits bytes
but loses a contrary reading fails. A timeout or malformed response can be
an implementation/method failure; it does not establish that the legal tools
cannot help. A successful offline ledger cannot establish model behavior.
