## Question, comparator and evidence contract

Can the installed product tools carry the new reconciliation distinctions into
the actual request transport, and can their successful checks distinguish a
warranted premise from an exact but non-entailing quotation?

Use the unchanged 54-unit source packet in successor-s4's
`development-inputs.json` and the two exact original P10/evidence questions.
Inputs are executor-authored diagnostic proposals derived from the reviewed P10
result. They are not model-generated readings, accepted law, independent test
labels or the original 35-reading inventory. Preserve original obligation 2.

The comparator is the same product request through `CompactProvider` with no
optional tool session. The candidate uses the installed `ProductToolBackend`
and actual `ToolSession` checks, then the same transport. The receiving provider
is an explicitly non-live receipt sink: it saves what arrives and returns no
legal answer. This can establish delivery to the provider boundary, not model
consideration or answer benefit. Full `CompleteInvestigation` execution remains
outside this check.

Primary engineering criteria:

1. LegalRuleML preserves two supplied Boolean expressions, including a
   deliberately invalid legal shortcut, and retains an unformalized substantive
   reading as unsupported. A structural PASS must not be described as legal
   approval. The alternative Boolean is an evidence-dependency checklist only,
   expressly not a sufficient compliance rule.
2. On separate, source-identical sessions, the argument profile blocks an ordinary
   QUESTIONED warrant; keeps a STATED assumption conditional; and applies an
   explicit conditional counterexample as an UNDERCUT of the shortcut. The real
   PyArg/Carneades and clingo outputs must agree on their declared effective
   graph targets. A fourth, adversarial input falsely labels the shortcut warrant
   SUPPORTED while citing footnote 1. Acceptance of that graph is an expected
   demonstration of the semantic limit, not evidence that the premise is true.
3. The exact questions, source text, candidate identities, unsupported results
   and conditional/blocking/defeat results reach the transport boundary. A
   revised candidate must exclude the previous atomic candidate/graph check.
   No legal answer or obligation closure may be inferred from the receipt.

Source identity, incorrect graph target, an engine disagreement, dropped tool
feedback or stale candidate feedback veto a successful engineering result.
Invalid inputs, corrupt source evidence, an unexpected provider/network action
or exhausted 90-minute local task bound veto continuation of the affected run.
A reproduced defect is retained for a separately reviewed, bounded repair;
there are zero repair attempts in this investigation. An unsupported profile or
the expected false-warrant acceptance is not a continuation veto.

Retrieval ranks, timings and invocation counts are explanatory only. Predeclare
three discriminating P10 passage targets: paragraph 11, Appendix A(c)(iii)'s
separate ownership-validity/reconciliation questions, and Appendix footnote 1's
secondary-record qualification. Report whether their exact spans are among the
eight retrieved windows for each original question. This small source-selected
set is not a legal-relevance benchmark. Do not retune retrieval after inspecting
the outcome.

| Receipt sink and explicit optional backend | Local boundary diagnostic | Delivery can look like actual reasoning; no generated answer, live call or full-workflow claim is permitted. |
| CPU-only installed environments | Existing workers, GPU intentionally hidden | Environment drift or missing dependency invalidates the check; retain identities, exact subprocess commands and outputs. |

Skeptical audit: **PASS FOR THIS BOUNDED CHECK**. The comparator has identical
sources, questions and proposals. Acceptance concerns actual declared tool
behavior and provider-boundary delivery; no proxy is promoted to legal accuracy.
The intentionally false legal warrant directly challenges an overly favorable
interpretation of tool PASS results. Separate sessions prevent incompatible
premise-status variants from contaminating each other's feedback. Unsupported
cases are retained, not repaired into easier meanings. No installation, live
budget renewal or controller change is required.

Pre-mortem: every engine may pass while the legal answer remains wrong. The
false-warrant case makes that failure visible. A source-selected counterexample
could make the tools seem clever even though the executor supplied the insight;
the result must credit source analysis and treat computation as conditional.
