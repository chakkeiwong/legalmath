public product answer with its exact source, periods and qualifications, while
missing/changed readings and rehashed forged phase products refuse?

losslessly retained answer, plus the corrected attempt004 map. Baseline

the explicitly declared supplied reading may change. All questions,
dependencies, assembly, source map, source reports and legal/transaction

The product accepts one finite reviewed profile initially. Its protected map

the named source document. Preserve exact word anchors and all source

Add `source_statement_cells` to request validation, validate and retain it in

Primary pass criteria: full real source-map consumption; exact protected

Missing real-source evidence, changed qualifications, silent omission,

Counts, runtime and unit-test totals are explanatory only. General parsing,

## Reviewed defaults and skeptical audit

The protected hash is derived from the corrected, image-reviewed original
map, not from a proposed output. Exact finite-source matching is the reviewed
initial scope. It will refuse different extraction geometry/segmentation;
that is explicit unsupported input, not proof of a PDF-library defect. The
smallest exposure diagnostic is a rehashed source graph with one changed
numeric word and one changed bounding box. No silent tolerance is introduced.
The complete page inventory also detects omitted nonnumeric labels/headers.

The P2/P3 digest pattern follows existing report/discrepancy admissions, but
those patterns alone do not establish safety: the final consumer must
reconstruct the optional evidence from the request and reject both absence
and unexpected extra evidence. Review metadata is provenance, never an
independent financial or legal attestation. Keep source context unresolved.

Pre-mortem: a map could merely be echoed into the output, or a forged P2 could
be accepted after ordinary resealing. The complete source-profile validation
and matched public-consumer controls distinguish those failures. Another risk
is treating a supplied reading as a complete financial answer; existing Q3
and dependencies remain unchanged and independently compared.

Skeptical audit: PASS for this finite interface after tracing request validation,
P2 construction, P3 interpretation and final consumption. The map is genuinely
absent today; another detached validation would not close that gap. Preserve
the original 59 obligations, 44 tasks, all four prior admissions and their
unresolved acceptance. This is one explicit cumulative 4-to-5 revision for
BES-DEP-01, with no native budget extension or implied sixth admission.
