## Question, comparator and evidence

Can we convert particular retained English readings into source-supported,
question-specific executable controls while reducing uncertainty about their
scope? The comparator is the complete round-13 investigation, including its
failed encodings and excluded comparisons. A higher number of generated rules
or recorded outcomes is not the promotion criterion. The next result must show
which precise condition was recovered, which interpretation remains possible,
and what source evidence distinguishes them.

Separate three denominators from the outset: model assessments of executable
meaning, deterministic encoding failures, and pairs excluded by proposed
control assignment. Preserve the complete claim/reading universe. Freeze any
new reference cases before the corresponding proposals are generated, record
who supplied the expected answers, and keep those answers out of prompts.

## Defaults requiring review before activation

The 12-pair fidelity batch is a convenience limit, not a measure of interpretive
quality. Check input/output sizes and observed failure rates before changing it.
The two control assignments use fresh contexts within one model family; their
agreement remains potentially correlated. Scope and actor classifications are
substantive assumptions. A new fact mapping must record how it follows from the
source and how an unknown classification propagates into the decision.

Budget from the actual pending work. The initial round-13 bound was too small
because most STR comparisons survived the routing step. Do not assume routing
will reduce the matrix. Count calls already consumed against the unchanged
500-call allowance, declare an absolute next ceiling, reserve before dispatch,
and retain old journals. At most three phase attempts, smaller bounded repairs
after structural failures, and an explicit wall-time limit are required.

## Skeptical review and stop conditions

The principal promotion vetoes are lost source conditions, unsupported extra
restrictions, changed question identity, hidden encoding failures, mismatched
source or response hashes, invented event deadlines and Java disagreement.
These usually trigger the named repair rather than abandonment of the method.
Corrupted evidence, unavailable necessary authority after bounded retrieval,
an invalid harness, or exhausted declared resources stop dependent work.

A proposed split can look successful by making every child trivially narrow;
retain the parent relation and missing-control inventory to expose this. A new
formalization can look accurate by copying the reference answers; keep those
answers withheld and preserve the prompt receipts. Two readers can agree on the
same mistaken scope; retain excluded-pair evidence and challenge cross-cutting
qualifications rather than multiplying votes into a probability of correctness.

The engineering continuation can proceed without commissioning a comprehensive
external legal review. An independently supported accuracy rate, an empirical
claim of reduced common errors across model families, or a quantified saving in
review cost needs its own evidence. Use targeted reference judgments and actual
review-time observations for those evaluations; do not manufacture those data
from the author-created examples. General case-law interpretation and bank
integration remain outside this increment's demonstrated scope.
