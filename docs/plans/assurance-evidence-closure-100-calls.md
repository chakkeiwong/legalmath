## Research intent and evidence contract

Main question: which remaining obligations can be resolved by corrected source
identity, new applicable authority, explicit semantic mappings or actual facts,
and which must remain conditional interpretations?

Baseline: final-result.json, remaining-obligations.json, all 70 assessments,
24 conditional programs, followup-premise-checks.json and the failed/accepted
authority history under artifacts/assurance-gap-continuation/2026-10-01.
Original unresolved count is 46. All original perspectives and reading
assessments are already present; repeating coverage is not an objective.

Mechanism: acquire discriminating evidence, bind it to exact affected questions,
propagate the result to all relevant readings, and test the resulting programs.
Expected failure: available sources prescribe broad standards without deciding
every disputed boundary; official technical materials or case records may be
unavailable. A failed retrieval or unresolved norm must not become an invented
fact or an artificial Boolean.

Primary pass criteria:
- Every claimed repair has an independently replayable input/output/source
  record and addresses the actual diagnosed failure.
- Every claimed source resolution binds exact provision, edition and applicable
  date, and answers the original question rather than a convenient substitute.
- Every affected reading receives an explicit updated disposition or a precise
  unchanged/open reason. No record silently disappears.
- Formal checks preserve assumptions, unknown/conflict semantics and time scope.
  Production target executions are counted only if actually executed.
- No illegal attempt reset, missing action, altered predecessor or unaccounted
  provider use occurs.

Closure must be reported by level: engineering repair; document acquired;
temporal applicability established; interpretation supported under stated
premises; case facts established; conditional runtime checked; or still open.
A passed earlier level never implies a later one. Grouping duplicates does not
reduce the original unresolved count unless each original obligation qualifies.

Promotion vetoes: wrong/ambiguous source identity, unchecked date applicability,
foreign quotation, undisclosed semantic bridge, factual judgment disguised as a
Boolean input, omitted uncertainty, or missing runtime evidence. These require
repair before the affected conclusion is accepted.

Continuation vetoes: corrupt baseline or ledger, exhausted new budget/window,
repeated provider failure, or unavailable required toolchain for a dependent
stage. An unobtainable source blocks its dependent conclusion, not unrelated
repairs. An ambiguous legal reading does not invalidate the research direction.

Explanatory only: counts of model agreement, formal passes, downloads, reviewer
differences, timings and remaining questions. They do not establish legal truth,
a quality ranking, model-family independence or production readiness.

No statistical ranking is planned. Local finite checks are deterministic.
Remote generation is uncontrolled and uses the existing configured provider.
This remains development on retained material, not a prospective observation.
