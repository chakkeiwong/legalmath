## Teaching contract for the revised argument

Reader: compliance sponsor and implementing technical lead/agent. Initial
knowledge: banking workflows, basic programming and ordinary mathematical logic;
no assumption of familiarity with default calculus, deontic logic or SMT.
Spine: a circular's meaning is lost at specific boundaries; each literature family
supplies a partial remedy; choose a bounded combination; show its exact execution
and review behavior; allocate implementation; test the business hypothesis.
Before each formal object introduce its banking question, units, actors and dates.
After it give a decision or failure that the representation makes visible.
Machine contracts and detailed commands remain separately usable, while the
manuscript explains the consequential choices without requiring appendix hunting.
