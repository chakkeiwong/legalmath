## Question and evidence contract

Can the retained official sources update all 35 readings under both existing
roles, correct the four diagnosed historical scope errors, and identify the
precise unresolved legal and factual frontier? The comparator is the saved
2026-10-01 assessment and the halted 2026-10-02 campaign at 40 calls.

Acceptance requires exact coverage of every retained question identity, exact
source quotations, valid reading identities, preserved assumptions and historical
questions, and executed checks for any retained or repaired conditional program.
Source acquisition alone cannot close a compound interpretation question. The 46
original obligations are kept open unless complete evidence supports their full
scope; this continuation conservatively records proposed support for subsequent
substantive consideration, without releasing production qualification.

Missing/altered evidence, unaccounted spending, incomplete phase coverage, expired
authorization, or two consecutive provider failures veto continued dispatch.
The initial two-role propagation pilot must validate both responses before
breadth. A schema or quotation error triggers at most one correction on the same
issues. A concrete program defect triggers a counted formalization repair when
the same two-attempt amendment limit permits; otherwise it remains explicitly
blocked. A failed legal candidate vetoes retaining that program, not continuing
reviews of other readings. Byte size, latency, finite Boolean passes and role
agreement are explanatory diagnostics, not evidence of legal correctness.

   preflight every real propagation and historical-scope input, and record exact

2. Execute the first paired reading review under both roles. Both must pass
   identity, complete-question coverage, source quotation and disposition checks.
3. Execute four historical scope corrections using their own complete source
   families and original questions. Do not infer an assessment date from the
   unrelated April 2026 circular.
4. Execute the remaining 34 propagation tasks (two readings per task, final
   singleton), with exact targeted correction or formal repair when diagnosed.
5. Rebuild formal evidence and execute conditional finite/native/Java and
   unknown/conflict checks. Record challenged programs separately; do not promote
   unchanged programs that a review rejected.
6. Generate final engineering and disposition reviews from the completed new
   results, two roles per subject. Prior premature final reviews are superseded.
7. Verify every call receipt, write the result and remaining-evidence frontier,
   and replay the command to demonstrate zero new spending on restart.

Every phase completion and repair checkpoint refreshes the next-phase plan from
validated results, remaining budget and the actual unresolved frontier. Failed or
incomplete phases cannot advance. Source/fact gaps can remain open after a
completed review phase and are recorded as substantive work, not hidden errors.

## Skeptical audit before implementation

The earlier failed protocol and premature-final-phase baseline were inspected.
The revised design does not reuse those final approvals. It avoids treating
smaller packets or successful JSON as legal acceptance, preserves all original
questions, distinguishes provider invalidity from candidate rejection, and has
explicit budget, retry, timeout, source, and phase stop conditions. The principal
remaining risks are provider latency and subjective source entailment; the pilot
tests execution and the final reviews inspect accepted changes. Tests will attack
restart spending, tampering, incomplete coverage and premature phase advancement.
Audit passes for implementation and bounded validation under these limits.
