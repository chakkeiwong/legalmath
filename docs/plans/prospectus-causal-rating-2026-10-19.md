## Causal evidence and assumptions

Causal relation input distinguishes disclosed stipulation,denial/conflict and
absence. This phase accepts HYPOTHETICAL_CAUSAL_LINK only and rejects a claim of
actual source-supported attribution. A future real-source attribution must bind a
statement to the precise control and rating events,agency,issuer/instrument,
dates and document span. A statement that an event followed control change
does not establish "due to". Until actual attribution evidence is supplied,the
adapter accepts only disclosed hypothetical causal premises and returns their
conditional consequences. An unknown causal premise remains unknown; a denied
causal link blocks this rating alternative; conflicting inputs remain conflict.

The goal is to establish that the program correctly preserves and applies the
source's causal requirement,not to prove a real causal link from chronology.
Any proposed automated extraction/classification needs a separate reviewed
evidence contract and source cases. Reuse existing default(f) causal distinctions
as design lessons,not as authority that its factual causes transfer here.
