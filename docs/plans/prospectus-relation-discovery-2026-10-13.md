# Automatic source-relation candidates

The admitted relation-discovery/attempt-001 asks whether raw extraction can
propose complete row and qualification endpoints without receiving the reviewed
answers. The existing BES consumer accepts eight supplied relations, whereas
the raw route produces zero. Docling projects exact text on BASF p112 and
Deutsche AT1 p52 but does not itself supply governing scope. These are separate
capabilities and separate comparators.

Implement an optional, deterministic candidate generator for enumerated
two-column rows. Infer repeated value-column alignment from raw geometry; use
printed enumeration, colon-ended labels and the next row boundary. A following
`Note that` paragraph is a qualification candidate with exact raw spans. Keep
OCR mistakes, ambiguous regions and unsupported structures visible. Return
candidates through the actual service as review suggestions; they cannot
resolve dependencies, supply meanings, change any question or confer legal
acceptance. The default request remains unchanged.

Freeze the existing BES eight endpoint targets and BASF margin target by hash
before evaluating this method. Neither the generator nor its service path may
read targets, corrected text, source-specific coordinates or document IDs.
Compare raw BES, the candidate on the same raw source, and the separately
supplied-relation route. Also run BASF's German margin family and Deutsche's
English column page. Both are exposed development sources, not heldout data.
No expected relation target exists for the latter: report it as exploratory,
never as a passed legal test. Missing BASF margin scope is an anticipated
candidate limitation, not a reason to stop independent tasks.

Primary engineering criteria: exact source bindings, complete-target matching
when credit is claimed, refusal of missing/invalid geometry and duplicate
identities, isolation of pages/documents, determinism under input permutation,
and identical legal answers/dependencies with this option on and off. The
candidate-quality screen requires all eight BES endpoints and the BASF margin
scope; failure vetoes automatic promotion, not further planned repair. Extra
proposals outside this incomplete target inventory are unreviewed, not assumed
false. Report exact matches, missing targets and unreviewed proposals separately.
No precision estimate is justified by this target inventory.

Assumption audit: numbered two-column forms and colon-ended labels are a
restricted grammar, not a universal legal-document format. Repeated alignment
(at least two seed rows), a half-line-height alignment tolerance, and bounded
line gaps are engineering hypotheses derived from ordinary table geometry, not
validated defaults. Early diagnostics are the retained BES fragmented OCR,
multiline names, full qualification, missing geometry, overlapping alternatives,
and source permutation. English `Note that` is an explicit language restriction;
German margins and long-distance ratings qualifications need another mechanism.
No threshold tuning on these targets after observing scores is authorized by
this attempt. Preserve any failure and propose a reviewed continuation instead.

Skeptical audit PASS with scope correction: geometric adjacency cannot establish
legal scope, and supplied target regions cannot be inputs to an automatic trial.
The optional report is therefore a candidate-discovery facility. Successful
execution does not establish package suitability, complete interpretation,
independent legal acceptance, OCR accuracy or automatic governing scope.
Corrupted sources, target leakage, missing diagnostics or a changed legal answer
veto the run. Expected candidate misses trigger source-family expansion and
independent review; they do not invalidate the harness by themselves.

Sequence: retain targets/source/method bindings; implement and run focused
boundary tests; run the matched actual consumer and additional families; retain
the decision, strongest alternative explanation and next justified work; record
the partial result, refresh and continue the authority's next ready task.
CPU only; GPU intentionally hidden; no new model or network calls. Commands use
the worktree interpreter with src pinned, a 120-second timeout, one-second tool
yields and at most 30 MB of new evidence. Retain actual commands, git commit,
environment, wall time and source/method snapshots in the admitted attempt.
