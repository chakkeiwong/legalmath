## Skeptical audit and evidence contract

The original dossier has a stale baseline and a regex inventory that is not a
complete linguistic graph. The repaired procedure inventories the 47 recorded
candidate lines and reads the whole selected prose for relative and implicit
references. Each entry records its precise source, target or missing target,
selection uncertainty and statutory-source boundary. A split citation must be
read across its source lines. A statutory section must not resolve to a
similarly numbered contract section. Unselected alternatives, missing originals
and unknown target dispositions cannot be made operative merely to reduce the
unresolved count. Retained numbering is unchanged.

Pass criterion: every newly admitted edge binds the reviewed source occurrence
and the complete intended operative target, appears in the actual P3
dependencies, and changes that dependency in a matched omission. The original
meanings, scalar provenance, construction and unresolved legal questions must
remain equal. Source, language, missing target and replaced-discovery adverse
controls must reject invalid packets. The inventory is evidence of explicit
review coverage, not automatic linguistic recall or independent legal truth.

Continuation vetoes: changed source hashes, wrong worktree imports, corrupted
parent products, or an actual protected-baseline mismatch. Missing originals
or unknown target dispositions veto the affected edge only; retain them and
continue independent valid edges and later admitted tasks. Failed admission
is a repair trigger, not evidence that the package is fundamentally unsuitable.
Counts and timings are explanatory diagnostics only.
