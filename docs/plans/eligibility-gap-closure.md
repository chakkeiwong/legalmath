# Close known implementation gaps without inventing legal certainty

## Research and engineering intent

Question: can retained prospectus terms, issuer-law conditions, Hong Kong
loss-absorption selling requirements, the existing SFC calculations and bank
assessment be executed together, with independently checked formal consequences
and explicit unknowns? A second question is whether the known evidence-reference
and date-proof limits can be repaired without changing the legal question.

Baseline: the current working sources and retained bank and purchaser campaigns,
plus the newer D0–D1 capacity increment. The capacity repair already preserves
the complete 382,396-byte request in 175,016 bytes and completes its simulation;
it is not unfinished work and does not establish real-model comprehension.
Preserve the current material files/hashes before editing. The dirty Git base
alone is not the baseline. Existing historical attempts and exhausted live
allowances must remain unchanged.

Candidate mechanism: explicit source-bound rule dependencies; a composed
conditional product investigation; identity-bound executable quotations;
declared calendar semantics and checked constructor preservation; independently
written equations, boundary/counterexample challenges and real execution in
both RuleIR/Java and Catala. No human expected-answer labels are inputs.

Primary promotion criteria are exact evidence binding, complete accounting of
the declared obligations, equality with independently specified formal relations,
kernel-checked propositions for the stated proof domain, correct abstention and
successful execution of the actual targets. Test counts, compacted size, source
matches and model agreement are explanatory only, never legal accuracy.

Promotion vetoes/repair triggers: a dropped obligation, source substitution,
incorrect derived fact, unsupported premise treated as true, stale evidence
accepted, wrong mathematical result, or a mutation undetected by its declared
check. These trigger a causal repair and a preserved retry. Continuation vetoes
apply only to corrupted required evidence, unavailable necessary tools or an
unauthorized/exhausted live action. Other offline phases continue independently.

## Skeptical pre-execution review

The initial gap list contained stale capacity context. This plan corrects that
baseline and preserves its remaining unique-text/comprehension limitations.
The nine unresolved bank entries cannot honestly be closed by manufacturing
facts or substituting a smaller inventory. The real paired offering test cannot
be promoted into a full SFC eligibility comparison. The plan therefore joins
the actual supported components and records the remaining propositions.

An issuer-law survey is not an executable interpretation; a constructor-tree
proof is not a calendar/codec/English theorem; and a generated example is not a
prospective observation. Each phase must expose these distinctions in its
output. The same predicates must apply to structurally different generated
cases, with issuer-name invariance and counterexamples to tempting shortcuts.

The plan passes this audit. No current material default is being promoted on
test counts or source matches. A failed engineering candidate triggers repair,
not abandonment of a later phase designed to address it. Unknown legal meaning
continues to produce a qualified conclusion.

## Execution, repair and records

The executable entry point will be
`.venv/bin/python scripts/run_eligibility_gap_closure.py`. It will expose phase
selection and a complete local run, reserve attempts before work, bind each
phase to inputs/predecessors, retain failures and refresh a machine-readable
next-phase plan after each result. A changed method invalidates dependent
acceptance. Before a retry, record the failure, repair and focused reproducer;
do not relax the criterion to accept the failure. Phase reuse requires verified
identities and unchanged outputs.

Artifacts: `docs/implementation/eligibility-gap-closure/`, including plan review,
attempt manifests, source/proof/native results, final result, remaining gaps and
reset memo. Tests run through the existing `.venv`; the runner sets
`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`. The document command remains
`python3 scripts/build_reader_facing_monograph.py`. A focused check precedes the
affected regression; extra repeats require an actual change or unresolved risk.

The final interpretation must state what was closed in software, what was
proved mathematically, what still depends on factual/source premises, and what
we do not currently know how to prove. Human judgments are never the substitute.
