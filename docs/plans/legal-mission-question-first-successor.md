## Research intent and evidence contract

**Main question.** Can the current source-to-answer route preserve the exact
P10/evidence questions, all material contrary sources, and genuine downstream
tool feedback while remaining reachable and affordable? The proposed mechanism
is a question-bound local method with explicit task routing and fail-closed
source-repair invalidation. It is a candidate method, not the product default.

**Comparator.** The current dirty-worktree `CompleteInvestigation` →
`dossier_answer` implementation, identified by per-file hashes at execution,
plus the sealed 13-call pilot and retained route-repair diagnostics. A gift-rule
scripted fixture or 78-claim/12-historical-reading hybrid cannot stand in for a
completed P10/evidence answer. For later tool contribution, the comparator must
be a complete no-tool baseline with the same exact sources, questions, method,
model and effort policy as the candidate arm.

**Primary local pass criterion.** Exact original question identities are bound
before dispatch; a separate original-question retrieval can reach downstream
accounting; wrong-question, unbridged typed and stale-reading checks remain
excluded; all reachable request types are admitted only against an explicit
stage reserve; source-inventory repair cannot silently reuse old staged reviews
or candidate search; and focused local tests pass. This criterion establishes
engineering reachability only. It does not require a model to assert that its
answer is legally correct.

**Promotion vetoes.** A trigger-only P10 answer, false reconciliation warrant,
lost source qualification, lost rival or contrary, unsupported legal premise,
stale tool receipt, unequal baseline/candidate sources, incomplete answer, or
missing actual downstream tool consideration vetoes a legal or tool-benefit
claim. **Continuation vetoes** are changed sealed evidence, a broken route
guard, lost original question/obligation/reading denominator, exhausted local
attempt window, an unauthorized live call, an unaffordable dependency, or an
invalid/corrupt run artifact. A failed candidate or descriptive tool result is
not by itself a continuation veto for other source analysis. **Repair triggers**
are the reproduced binding and task-routing defects or an observed source
revision invalidating old review. Call arithmetic, pass counts, byte fit and
one-off timing are explanatory diagnostics, not promotion criteria.

**Nonclaims.** Neither local acceptance nor a future one-pair live pilot proves
general English interpretation, legal compliance, stochastic superiority, a
new default, or full evaluation of any of the six selected packages. Preserve
the actual result in a per-file-hashed manifest and a `no_advance` mission row
unless a reviewed original-answer change supports a stronger disposition.

## Skeptical pre-mortem and execution rules

The most dangerous false success is a structurally complete answer that quotes
real P10 text yet substitutes activation for fulfillment or treats matching
records as proof of underlying rights. Human legal review of source-to-warrant
steps and explicit counterexamples is mandatory before a legal contribution
claim. A second false success is an apparently affordable 78-by-12 run whose
second reader or revised search expands the population. A third is a tool
receipt that reaches a prompt but is not actually considered or is attached to
the wrong original question. The earliest discriminating checks are exact
binding controls, source-repair invalidation, population re-estimation and an
answer-level source review, in that order.

Run from the repository root with `.venv/bin/python`. Use ordinary sandboxed
commands and existing narrow approvals; no allowlist edit is needed for local
edits/tests. Avoid pip, network, Claude, GPU and the sealed live runner in this
package. Preserve unrelated dirty work. Record failed tests and bounded fixes
inside the one repair attempt rather than opening another program. The absolute
stop is the earlier of its two-hour expiry and a continuation veto. A future
live qualification needs a new reviewed evidence contract and an affordable
stage allocation; this plan itself grants none.
