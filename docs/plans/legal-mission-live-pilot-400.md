## Question and evidence contract

Does the actual product, with the six selected tools, produce a more useful
source-supported answer to the original P10 and evidence questions than the same
product without those optional tools? Generate each investigation independently
from the same qualified retained source packet. Neither arm receives the prior
executor's answers or the assessment rubric as proposed conclusions.

The baseline is `CompleteInvestigation` plus the whole-dossier answer consumer,
without the optional backend. The candidate differs only by selection of BM25,
LegalRuleML, PyArg, Carneades, clingo and restricted Logical English. This is one
descriptive comparison, with baseline first; order and same-model dependence are
limitations. It cannot establish a statistical ranking or attribute a combined
effect to an individual tool.

The primary criterion is two complete, source-grounded answers addressing
allocation, conduct and outcome readings of P10 and the inference from observed
supervision/reconciliation to legally adequate supervision/proper ownership
records. Review exact quotations, source applicability, rival readings, contrary
evidence, factual assumptions and the tool evidence actually considered. Preserve
P10 separately from P11. Examine the shared-error and secondary-record examples
in the retained substantive dossier when assessing the answers.

## Skeptical audit before implementation

**PASS for this bounded execution package, subject to its local checks.** The
audit corrected a material flaw in the inherited proposal: a 3,000-second core
deadline was inconsistent with its 36-call envelope. The grant adapter must
accept an expired nonexhausted specialized predecessor without editing it. Both
arms are reserved before execution, and dynamic capacity is checked before
downstream spending. Source access and method differ only by the optional tools.
The source-only capacity result and synthetic legally empty answer are not
promotion evidence. Automatic retries, default drift and wider matrix expansion
are bounded explicitly.

Pre-mortem: a run can finish yet teach us little if it formalizes activation only,
compresses away contrary readings, or treats solver agreement as legal warrant.
Those fail the substantive assessment even with valid JSON. A call timeout or
invalid reference instead diagnoses implementation/transport, not the usefulness
of the packages. The earliest discriminating evidence is the baseline's actual
generated candidates and retained source criticism, followed by the complete
answer. Publish the adverse result if that is what the evidence supports.
