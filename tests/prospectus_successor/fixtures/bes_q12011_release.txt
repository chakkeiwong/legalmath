MATERIAL EVENT
BANCO ESPÍRITO SANTO GROUP ACTIVITY AND RESULTS IN 1Q11
(Unaudited financial information under IFRS as implemented by the European Union)
(BES; Bloomberg: BES PL; Reuters: BES.LS)
Lisbon, 2 May 2011
•
Portugal’s financial conditions aggravated during 1Q11, especially in March, when a political crisis led to
anticipation of the general elections to 5 June 2011. Rating agencies reacted with downgrades of the
Portuguese Republic and consequently of the banking sector. Increasing barriers to access the capital
markets produced inevitable effects on the Portuguese banks’ activity and results.
•
During 1Q11 the BES Group continued to implement its deleveraging plan, started in 3Q10, aiming to reach a
loan to deposits ratio of 120% by the end of 2012. The bank decreased its net assets by EUR 3.0 billion (from
EUR 83.7 billion at 2010YE to EUR 80.7 billion at 1Q11): with a special emphasis on the AFS portfolio (EUR 1.0
billion lower), trading portfolio (EUR 0.5 billion lower) and customer loans (ca. EUR 1.0 billion lower).
•
The 1Q11 net income totalled EUR 60.9 million, backed by international business, and impacted by the
deleveraging costs of EUR 35 million (EUR 41 million gross) and the new tax on the banking sector (EUR 7.6
million).
•
Deposits grew 15.2% YoY in the 1Q11, driving the loan to deposits ratio down to 163% from 188% in 1Q10.
•
Commercial banking income (CBI) increased by 3.5% YoY, driven by the international area and the
consolidated NII growth of 7% YoY. International commercial banking income was up 26.7% YoY, while the
domestic CBI was down 9.5% YoY. International area’s net income reached EUR 56 million.
•
1Q11 operating costs increased by 8.6% YoY, namely reflecting the incorporation of Execution Noble. On a
comparable basis, operating costs increased by 1% YoY.
•
The overdue loans ratio (>90 days) was 2.17% (1.67% in 1Q10 and 1.95% in 4Q10). The quarter’s provision
charge of 63 bp lifted the credit provisions to EUR 1.79 billion (+11.3% YoY), or 3.47% of gross customer loans
(3.12% in 1Q10; 3.38% in 4Q10).
•
MLT debt that maturing in 1Q11 amounted to EUR 2.6 billion, representing 60.5% of the total maturing in
2011. However, only EUR 1.1 billion of the international loan portfolio was sold during 1Q11 (i.e. 44% of the
total subject to deleverage), leading to an increase of the use of ECB facilities as the market conditions have
worsened.
•
The Bank continued to strengthen the portfolio of assets eligible for rediscount with Central Banks or in the
repo market, which reached EUR 14.3 billion in March, o.w. EUR 9.7 billion eligible for rediscount with the
ECB.
•
At the end of 1Q11 the bank had a Core Tier I of 7.9%, Tier I of 8.8% and total capital ratio of 11.4%, all of
which will be further strengthened by 30 bp, 60 bp and 90 bp, respectively, by the already announced sale of
4.1% of the voting capital of Banco Bradesco.
•
AFS portfolio’s main equity exposures have a potential gain of EUR 113 million (vs. EUR 120 million at 2010YE).
